Same method as rounds 1–3 ([2026-10-02-sample.md](2026-10-02-sample.md), [2026-10-03-round3.md](2026-10-03-round3.md)):
one Opus 5.5 agent per paper reads every page image in scope and compares the published JSON field by field (and up to
six figure crops); a second agent per paper tries to refute each reported defect; only confirmed defects count. Every
confirmed defect, with path, printed vs published text and page evidence:
[2026-10-03-round4-confirmed-defects.json](2026-10-03-round4-confirmed-defects.json).

604 confirmed defects in 221 papers; 62 papers have a critical or major one. By kind: altered wording 379 (mostly
single words), wrong value 56, missing text 57, extra text 53, wrong formula 33, structure 14, garbled 5, wrong

**Conclusion.** gpt-6-astra's output is mostly faithful but slips often: four papers in ten have a confirmed defect,
nearly all single-word changes; one in eight has a serious one. The serious ones cluster in a few patterns:

- exponent signs dropped in constants tables (electron mass 9.109·10³¹ kg in iao-2010-theory-alpha/beta-en;
  hydrogen atom mass 1.67·10²⁷ kg in ioaa-2015-theory-long-final-bg/en);
- digits changed in data tables (iao-2010-practical-en: seven meteor-log rows; iao-2008-practical 02:57 → 00:57;
  ioaa-2016-team-bg; ioaa-2015-team-competion-0731);
- formula structure (a radical's extent, a squared term dropped, a ρ₃ that became ρ₂);
- values "corrected" away from the print (Sun's radius 696 000 → 690 000 km in nao-2013-iii-11-12; 10 см/с → 10 м/с
  in vserusiyska-2007-iii-region07t; OIII → OII with an invented source note in nao-2020-iv-practical-ml).

gpt-5.6-luna (48 papers) had only minor slips.

The other 18 are pinned in Codex's problem-editions/consolidations and are left to Codex's pipeline. The 159 papers
with minor defects only are not repaired in this round.
