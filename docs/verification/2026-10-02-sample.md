# Independent verification sample — 2026-10-02 (Claude)

Read-only check of 52 published papers against their original page images, by one Opus 5.5 agent per paper, then a
second agent per paper that tried to refute every reported defect. Only defects the second agent confirmed are
counted. All confirmed defects (with paths, printed vs published text and evidence) are in
[2026-10-02-sample-confirmed-defects.json](2026-10-02-sample-confirmed-defects.json).

Sample: small (≤ 6 problems), PDF-sourced papers, deterministic, spread across competitions; weighted to the readers
whose output has had no second-model check.

structure, problem parts misattached; minor = a single word or punctuation slip.

## Recommendations

1. Re-read the ten papers above from the page (a repair round: from-final, fix only the listed fields, mechanical
   check, promote).
2. **glm-5.3-flash**: all five sampled papers had confirmed defects — re-read the whole cohort (77 papers, 213
   problems) with a current reader.
3. Readers must never add a "source note" asserting that the print lacks or differs from what is shown without page
   evidence; a faded sign is still printed (both usapho-2006 and wopho-2013 defects came with such a note).
4. A wider astra/sol sample would sharpen the rates (22 and 16 papers are small samples); the astra critical defects
   cluster in two papers.
