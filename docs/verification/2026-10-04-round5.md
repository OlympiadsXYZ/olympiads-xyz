# Independent verification, round 5 — 2026-10-04 (Claude)

Same method as rounds 1–4 ([2026-10-03-round4.md](2026-10-03-round4.md)): one Opus 5.5 agent per paper reads every page
image in scope and compares the published JSON field by field (and up to six figure crops); a second agent per paper
tries to refute each reported defect; only confirmed defects count. Every confirmed defect, with path, printed vs
published text and page evidence: [2026-10-04-round5-confirmed-defects.json](2026-10-04-round5-confirmed-defects.json).

Sample (702 papers): every gpt-6.1-sol and claude-opus-5 paper not checked in rounds 1–4 (JSON ≤ 150 KB). The run was
interrupted by a usage limit after 675 of 949 agents and resumed from its cache; the figures below are complete.

| Reader | Checked | With confirmed defects | Critical | Major | Minor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gpt-6.1-sol | 271 | 158 | 44 | 91 | 388 |
| claude-opus-5 | 431 | 87 | 18 | 42 | 88 |

671 confirmed defects in 245 papers; 105 papers have a critical or major one. By kind: altered wording 308, missing
text 112, extra text 64, wrong formula 52, figure cut off 37, wrong value 31, structure 22, wrong figure 20, garbled
10, missing figure 3, other 12.

**gpt-6.1-sol** (Codex's current reader): six papers in ten have a confirmed defect, one in two a serious one — more
than rounds 1–2 suggested (41 papers sampled then). Typical serious defects: exponent signs dropped
(icho-1995 2.6·10³ mol dm⁻³ for 10⁻³; icho-2004 6.3·10⁶ for 10⁻⁶; icho-1981 mmol dm³), formula subscripts or factors
changed (belpho-2008 h₁/v₂ for h₁/v₁; vserusiyska-2017 U₀−U₁ for U₀−U₂; vserusiyska-2018 three formulas), table
values transposed (gecaa-2020 Saturn radius 58322 for 58232), and "source notes" that claim the print differs where it
does not (icho-2007: a positive exponent "literally" printed — the page prints the minus).

**claude-opus-5** (the earlier Claude reader): one paper in five has a confirmed defect, one in seven a serious one;
the serious ones are mostly figure crops (a crop showing body text instead of the drawing, figures cut in half) and a
few values (nao-2017-ii-9-10 Moon radius 1378 km printed, 1738 km published — the print's own typo was "corrected").

**Repairs.** 105 serious papers: 25 gpt-6.1-sol papers are inside Codex's reservations and 1 claude-opus-5 paper is
pinned — both left to Codex. Of the 79 free ones, the 43 gpt-6.1-sol papers use Codex's figure-object conventions,
which Claude's pipeline does not yet carry through (round 4 lesson); they are proposed to Codex. The 36 free
claude-opus-5 papers are proposed for Claude's `olympiads-verify-fix` route (work/claude-codex/notes.md).
