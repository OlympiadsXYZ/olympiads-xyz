# Independent verification, round 3 — 2026-10-03 (Claude)

Same method as [2026-10-02-sample.md](2026-10-02-sample.md): one Opus 5.5 agent per paper reads every page image in
scope and compares the published JSON field by field (and up to six figure crops); a second agent per paper tries to
refute each reported defect; only confirmed defects count. Every confirmed defect, with path, printed vs published text
and page evidence: [2026-10-03-round3-confirmed-defects.json](2026-10-03-round3-confirmed-defects.json).

Sample (343 papers): every claude-sonnet-5, claude-fable-5-1 and gpt-6-sol paper not checked in rounds 1–2, plus
deterministic samples of 100 opus-5-5 and 60 claude-opus-5 papers.

292 confirmed defects in 111 papers; 66 papers have a critical or major one. By kind: missing text 60, figure cut off
44, altered wording 41, structure (parts merged/misattached) 35, extra text 33, wrong figure 21, missing figure 18,
wrong formula 13, wrong value 12, garbled 10.

**Conclusion.** The rest of the claude-sonnet-5 cohort confirms rounds 1–2: half its papers have a defect, and a third
a critical one. Its typical failures are figure crops (a crop showing body text or another figure, photos swapped, a
figure cut in half) and dropped blocks (reference data, equipment lists, a whole problem in o3a2-2020-test-round, part
б of nof-2023-iv-k30). claude-fable-5-1 is in between; opus-5-5 and claude-opus-5 have one serious defect in roughly
every 15–20 papers (e.g. nof-1993-ii-8-9 printed m = 60 kg, published 80 kg; belpho-2001-iii-3-etap problem 10.2 shows
another problem's figure). The two gpt-6-sol critical defects are solution formulas (balkanski-1998/1999-cgp).

**Repairs.** 63 of the 66 papers go through `olympiads-verify-fix` (ids appended to
`docs/handoff-2026-09-22/queue-verify-fix.json`; defect files `tmp/verify-fix/<id>.json`); results below once shipped.
The three old RMPh papers (rmph-2012-experiment-exp-bg-1, rmph-2018-theory-t2, rmph-2018-theory-t3) are left to Codex,
whose rule is not to ordinary-promote old RMPh papers (requested in work/claude-codex/notes.md). Papers with minor
defects only (45) are not repaired in this round.
