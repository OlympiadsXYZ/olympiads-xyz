## Условие

**Study Source E4 in the Source Booklet.**

Explain how potholes are formed.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**2 marks**

Explanation key here.

- Potholes are circular shaped; cylindrical; vary in depth; some merge with each other; some are exposed above the river level. The water can be seen to be swirling/eddying. This creates a shallow hollow that is deepened by the same process **1**
- The water contains some of the load being carried by the river and this hits the base and sides of the hole to both widen and especially deepen it, smoothing the edges. This is an example of the abrasion process **1**
- It is most effective at times of high velocity and discharge. As vertical erosion is the dominant process, potholes tend to form well above base level where potential energy is relatively high **1**


</Spoiler>
