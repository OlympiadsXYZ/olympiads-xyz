**1.1** For all transects (purple line) as shown in Map 1a, identify the following features:

- Building types and building height (in number of storey)
- Ground floor retail services and restaurants types

Summarise your observations on Map 2.

Helpful hints:

You will draft your data collection on the draft map sheet provided. Later you will organise and annotate your observations on Map 2. **Map 2 is the only page that will be graded**. A score will be awarded for:

- The accuracy of data collected;
- The design of a classification scheme; and
- Clarity of presentation.

Attention should be paid to the target customers of specific shops/ restaurants as well as to whether clusters exist. If so, where and why do they occur? This information is important to the test that follows.
