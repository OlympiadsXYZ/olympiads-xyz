## Условие

About 20 of the 24 species of fish in lake Aral have disappeared. Fishing, which amounted to 44,000 tons in the fifties and which provided about 60,000 jobs, has gone down to zero. Make a connection between these facts and the subsidence of lake Aral. (2 marks)
