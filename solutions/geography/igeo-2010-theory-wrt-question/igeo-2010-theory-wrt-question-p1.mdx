## Условие

*Read the article in the Source material booklet Section I.*

The United Nations Millennium Development Goals aim to reduce global poverty by the year 2015. This micro-loans project will help to achieve those goals.

On the list below circle **three** targets of the MDGs that are targeted by this project.

- A global partnership for development
- Achieve universal primary education
- Combat HIV/AIDS, malaria and other diseases
- Ensure environmental sustainability
- Eradicate extreme poverty and hunger
- Improve maternal health
- Promote gender equality and empower women

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**3 marks (any 3 of four)**

- Achieve universal primary education
- Eradicate extreme poverty and hunger
- Promote gender equality and empower women
- Global partnership for development


</Spoiler>
