## Условие

*Take a close look at Source 1.*

Compare the ranks of the peak discharge (R) with the years in which they occur. What do you notice about the two sets of figures?

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**1 mark**

Any interpretation of **[1]**

- Observation: 7 of the top 10 discharges occur during the last decade.
- The majority of the higher discharges occur in the most recent years.
