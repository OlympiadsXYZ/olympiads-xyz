*2002 iGeo - Durban, South Africa Multimedia Test Questions · 3 т.*

## Условие

Name three countries that are founder members of the United Nations. (3)

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- Any three of: South Africa, Australia, Turkey, Greece, France, USA, Norway, India, Ethiopia

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

1.
    1. South Africa
    2. Australia
    3. Turkey
2.
    1. Greece
    2. France
    3. USA
3.
    1. Norway
    2. India
    3. Ethiopia (any three of the above)


</Spoiler>
