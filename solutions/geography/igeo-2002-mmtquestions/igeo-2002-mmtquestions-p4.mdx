*2002 iGeo - Durban, South Africa Multimedia Test Questions · 2 т.*

## Условие

What is the name of the country?

**1.** This Asian country has a population of 47 million people. When it received independence from Britain in 1948, it was one of the few countries not to join the Commonwealth. Buddhism is the main religion of the population. (1) **[1 т.]**

**2.** What is the name of a landlocked country with 11 million people. Tobacco was its major product and export until very recently. The name of its capital has changed. (1) **[1 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **1.** Burma
- **2.** Zimbabwe

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

1. Burma
2. Zimbabwe


</Spoiler>
