## Условие

Imagine, that you are asked to plan an open-air ice skating rink to be placed at the Pioneer City close to the running pad. Assuming that the rink is a circle with a diameter of 75 m, find an appropriate place for this rink on Map 2C, and encircle it with a pen. As the skating rink should be flat, with no relative elevation, estimate the volume of ground to be removed. Take in consideration, that the place you have chosen requires minimal effort for make it flat. **(3m)**

*Place for calculations. Please show all of your work*

Answer: Volume of ground to be removed ________________ cubic meters (m$^3$)
