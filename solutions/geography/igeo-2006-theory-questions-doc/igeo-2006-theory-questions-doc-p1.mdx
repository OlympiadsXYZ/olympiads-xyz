## Условие

*Weather in the world*

Look at Source 1 which is **worksheet 1**.  
Write your name and country on worksheet 1.

**1a** In what **time** of the year (month) was this satellite image taken? (1m) **[1 т.]**

**1b** Justify your choice by using information on this image. (2m) **[2 т.]**

**2a** Mark the **Inter Tropical Convergence Zone** (ITCZ) by drawing **two** (2) lines on the world map on worksheet 1. (1m) **[1 т.]**

**2b** How can this zone be identified on the map. (1m)  
Write down the **three** (3) atmospheric processes that take place in this zone. (2m) **[3 т.]**

**3a** Draw a circle on the image on worksheet 1 to indicate

- **one** (1) low pressure area (with a L) (1m)
- **one** (1) high pressure area (with a H) (1m) **[2 т.]**

**3b** Justify your choice for the area of **high pressure**. (1m) **[1 т.]**

*Pressure areas in the world*

**4** The **subtropical zone of high pressure** in the Southern Hemisphere is much more distinct than the same kind of zone in the Northern Hemisphere.  
Explain why. (2m) **[2 т.]**

*Climates and type of vegetation in Africa (sources 2 and 3 of Section 1)*

There are four images (1 - 4) that show the natural vegetation of four African regions There are also two climate graphs (A and B) of two climate stations in Africa.

**5** The climate graphs match **two** of the four vegetation images. What is the **type** of vegetation that is related to:

- climate station A? (1m)
- climate station B? (1m) **[2 т.]**

**6** Justify your answer to the following.

- A and image … : because …    (1m)
- B and image … : because …    (1m) **[2 т.]**

**7** Write your name and country on **worksheet 2**.  
Clearly show the location of the climate stations by writing the letters A and B on the map of Africa. (2m) **[2 т.]**

- the higher temperatures on the Northern Hemisphere
- the prevailing Monsoon in SE Asia.

**2a** (1m) See satellite image with example.

**2b** (3m)

The concentration of clouds in a band from West tot East (1m)

The three elements of a correct answer are:

- heating of the earth surface by the sun
- rising of the air
- condensation (forming of clouds)

(3 correct = 2m, 2 correct = 1m, 1 correct = 0m)
