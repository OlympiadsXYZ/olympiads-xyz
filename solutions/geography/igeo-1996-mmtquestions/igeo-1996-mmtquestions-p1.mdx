**1.** What is shown in this diagram? (analysing diagrams)

a. Gross national product
b. Oil production
c. Agricultural production
d. Number of inhabitants

**2.** Which is the best characterisation of this landscape? (analysing maps)

a. Plain
b. Table land
c. Low mountains
d. High mountains

**3.** Newcastle is at the lowest bridging point on the Thyne, and stands on its northern bank. The city covers a roughly semi-circular area extending for four kilometres in all directions. Across the river lies Newcastle's twin - Gateshead - covering about half the area of Newcastle.

Which map fits the description in the text? (analysing maps)

a. Map 1
b. Map 2
c. Both maps
d. Neither of the two maps

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **1.** a
- **2.** b
- **3.** a

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Theme Maps

1. a
2. b
3. a
