**(d)** Answer (point marking – any two factors (0.5 marks each) with a correct description of how that factor affects velocity):

Stream velocity is influenced by a number of factors including:

- The **shape of the channel** – the speed of flow is faster in channels that are as deep as they are wide compared with channels that are wide but very shallow or very deep and narrow
- The **roughness of the channel’s bed and banks** – water flowing through a channel full of large rocks is more turbulent but slower than water flowing through a channel lined with fine silt
- **Discharge** – the greater the volume of water flowing the greater the velocity.
  (discharge at point Y has increased volume due to the contributions of tributaries)
- **The gradient, or slope, of the stream** – although the gradient decreases downstream, the volume (or discharge) is greater
