## Условие

Once the natural resource phosphate got exhausted on the Pacific island Nauru, the economy became worse than it was before discovery of the resource. One cause of this so-called ‘Dutch disease’ is the unemployed manpower.

What is seen to be the second cause of the 'Dutch disease'?

A. As a low-lying country, Nauru suffered increase subsidence by mining

B. Increasing wealth diseases (e.g. heart attacks and obesity-levels up to 71%), decreasing productivity.

C. The island inherits an import-oriented infrastructure from the mining industry.

D. The boom period has either retarded or killed non-phosphate industries.
