## Условие

In which environments is honeycomb weathering most abundant?

A. Environments where lichen occurs, such as tundra or alpine areas.

B. Environments with temperatures around 0°C such as taiga and tundra

C. Salt-rich environments such as deserts and coastal zones

D. Monsoon dominated environments such as tropical savannas and pampas
