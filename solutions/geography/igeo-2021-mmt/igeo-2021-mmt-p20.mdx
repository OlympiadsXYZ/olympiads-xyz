## Условие

In both pictures the grass strip has been replaced by rocks.

To what purpose are grass strips replaced by rocks in these cities?

A. To improve infiltration rates to cope with more intensive rainfall.

B. To get local dwellers more involved in their local geology.

C. To reduce reflection of sun rays and thereby reduce the urban heat island.

D. To prevent unsheltered people to pitch their tents.
