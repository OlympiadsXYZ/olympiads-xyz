## Условие

Study the diagram below: **Cross-section of a tropical storm**.  
Label the diagram below using letters (A–F) for the following phrases:

A – Warm, moist air drawn into the area of low pressure,

B – Central eye where there is no cloud,

C – Water vapour evaporated from the warm sea surface,

D – Spiralling winds around the low pressure,

E – Descending dry air drawn into the centre of the system,

F – Bands of rain and thick cloud.
