## Условие

Using percentages, construct an age-sex population pyramid for the data presented in Table 1. Complete the age-sex pyramid on the graph paper provided in the answer booklet.

Ensure that you use appropriate geographical conventions in the construction of the age-sex pyramid.

Table 1. Expected population distribution in Kelvin Grove Urban Village (KGUV) on completion in 2010

| Age group | Male | Female | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0-14 | 98 | 126 | 224 |
| 15-24 | 241 | 303 | 544 |
| 25-34 | 244 | 256 | 500 |
| 35-44 | 173 | 177 | 350 |
| 45-54 | 178 | 183 | 361 |
| 55-64 | 122 | 134 | 256 |
| 65-74 | 43 | 56 | 99 |
| 75+ | 23 | 32 | 55 |
| Total | 1122 | 1267 | 2389 |

(based on projections from KGUV)

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Question 2 - Population Pyramid - 5 Marks**
