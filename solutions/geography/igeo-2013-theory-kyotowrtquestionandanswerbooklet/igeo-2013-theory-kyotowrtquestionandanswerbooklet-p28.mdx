## Условие

Complete the table below to make a ranking of development based on the 6 different indices for the 16 selected countries. Each of the indices has been ranked using the 5 divisions of the key shown below the maps. The rankings for Life expectancy, Infant mortality, Food supply, Availability of drinking water and Gender-related illiteracy have been completed for you.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 5 marks:  
1 mark for getting Literacy ranking correct,  
1 mark for sum of rankings,  
1 mark for the categories of development (making the 4 categories **systematically**),  
1 mark for correct shading of countries,  
1 mark for a map title and key.

The map produced must be a **choropleth map**. The students can use shading using only one colour or several colours as long as there is a gradation that means the countries in the lowest category have the lightest shading and the countries in the highest category have the darkest shading.
