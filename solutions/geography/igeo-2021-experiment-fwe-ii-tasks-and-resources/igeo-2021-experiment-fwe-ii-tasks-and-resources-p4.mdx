*17th International Geography Olympiad, Istanbul, Turkey, 10-16 August 2021 — Fieldwork Exercise Part II — Tasks and Resources · 10 август 2021 г. · 4 т.*

## Условие

The local government proposes to have a new housing project in the non-built-up area near the Ataturk urban forest. The possible site of the new housing project is marked as “**A**” in the satellite image. The area of the site is approximately 25,000 m$^{2}$. The site is targeted to accommodate 10,000 people after completion.
