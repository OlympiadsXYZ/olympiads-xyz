Design the layout of the new housing project ‘A’. You have to consider:

i) spatial arrangements,

ii) building density,

iii) height of buildings,

iv) types of housing, and

v) the target population to be accommodated in the site.

In addition, you have to provide some written justifications of your proposed layout design.

**New housing project A**

Area: about 25,000 m$^{2}$

Target population to be accommodated after completion: 10,000 persons.
