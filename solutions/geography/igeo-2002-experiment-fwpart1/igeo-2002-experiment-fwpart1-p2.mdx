*2002 iGeo - Durban, South Africa Fieldwork Part 1*

## Условие

Visit the MTN Science Centre between 12.00 and 13.30 hour. Complete the following tasks in the field: (1 hour maximum). Buses will be waiting in the car park ready to leave by 2.30 pm.

**•** Walk around the outside of the shopping centre. Take some notes on what you observe. For example, what kinds of land use surround the shopping centre? Why was this area chosen for a shopping centre?

**•** What kinds of architecture characterizes the inside of the shopping centre? What has influenced the architecture of the building?

**•** What kind of customers is the shopping centre trying to attract?

**•** How does this shopping centre differ from the shops and commercial activities in West Street (CBD of Durban).

**•** What are the advantages of regional shopping centres?

**•** What are the problems of regional shopping centres? Is the Gateway shopping likely to cause problems? Give reasons.
