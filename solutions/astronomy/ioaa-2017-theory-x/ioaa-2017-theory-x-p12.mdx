<summary>Original Examination Instructions</summary>

1. The theoretical examination lasts for 5 hours and is worth a total of 300 marks.

2. Dedicated IOAA **Summary Answer Sheets** are provided for writing your answers. Enter the final answers into the appropriate boxes in the corresponding **Summary Answer Sheet**. On each **Summary Answer Sheet**, please fill in
   - Student Code (Country Code and 1 digit)

3. There are **Answer Sheets** for carrying out detailed work/rough work. On each **Answer Sheet**, please fill in
   - Student Code (Country Code and 1 digit)
   - Question no.
   - Page no. and total number of pages.

4. Start each problem on a separate Answer Sheet. Please write only on the printed side of the sheet. Do not use the reverse side. If you have written something on any sheet which you do not want to be evaluated, cross it out.

5. Use as many mathematical expressions as you think may help the evaluator to better understand your solutions. The evaluator may not understand your language. If it is necessary to explain something in words, please use short phrases (if possible in English).

6. You are not allowed to leave your work desk without permission. If you need any assistance (malfunctioning calculator, need to visit a restroom, need more Answer Sheets, etc.), please draw the attention of the proctor using the Help card.

7. The beginning and end of the examination will be indicated by a long sound signal. Additionally, there will be a buzzer sound, fifteen minutes before the end of the examination (before the final sound signal).

8. At the end of the examination you must stop writing immediately. Sort and put your sheets in separate stacks,
   a) Stack 1: Summary Answer Sheets, Answer Sheets of part 1  
   b) Stack 2: Summary Answer Sheets, Answer Sheets of part 2  
   c) Stack 3: Summary Answer Sheets, Answer Sheets of part 3  
   d) Stack 4: question papers and paper sheets you do not want to be graded.

9. Wait at your table until your envelope is collected. Once all envelopes are collected, your student guide will escort you out of the examination room.

10. A list of constants and a table of the mark distribution for this exam are given on the next two pages.

## Условие

You are shipwrecked on an island. Fortunately, you are still wearing a watch that is set to Bangkok time, and you also have a compass, an atlas and a calculator. You are initially unconscious, but wake up to find that it has recently become dark. Unfortunately, it is cloudy. An hour or so later you see Orion through a gap in the clouds. You estimate that the star “Rigel” is about $52.5^\circ$ above the horizon and with your compass you find that it has an astronomical azimuth of $109^\circ$. Your watch says it is currently 01:00 on the 21st November 2017. You happen to remember from your astronomy class that Greenwich Sidereal Time (GST) at 00h UT 1st January 2017 is about 6h 43min and that the R.A. and Dec of Rigel are 5h 15min and $-8^\circ11'$, respectively. Bangkok is in the UT+7 time zone.

**a)** Find the Local Hour Angle (LHA) of Rigel. **[10 т.]**

**b)** Find the current Greenwich sidereal time (GST). **[10 т.]**

**c)** Find the longitude of the island. **[5 т.]**

**d)** Find, to the nearest arcminute, the Latitude of the island. **[15 т.]**

### a) Solution

Draw a correct celestial sphere. [4.0]

$$
\frac{\sin\mathrm{LHA}^*}{\sin z}=\frac{\sin A}{\sin(90^\circ-\delta)}.
$$

[2.0]

$$
\mathrm{LHA}^*=\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{\sin A\sin z}{\sin(90^\circ-\delta)}\right)=\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{\sin109^\circ\sin37.5^\circ}{\sin98.18^\circ}\right).
$$

[2.0]

$$
\mathrm{LHA}^*=35.56^\circ=2\mathrm h\ 22\ \mathrm{min},
$$

$$
\mathrm{LHA}=24\mathrm h-2\mathrm h\ 22\ \mathrm{min}=21\mathrm h\ 38\ \mathrm{min}.
$$

[2.0]

### b) Solution

Bangkok time of 01:00 21st November is Universal Time (UT) 18:00 20th November.

Day number + Time = 323 day 18 h. [1.0]

GST = GST of 1st Jan + The angle that Υ moves away from 1st Jan. [2.0]

$$
\mathrm{GST}=6\mathrm h\ 43\mathrm{min}+\left(323\frac{18}{24}\ \mathrm{days}\right)\left(\frac1{365.2422}\right)\times24\frac{\mathrm h}{\mathrm{day}}+\left[18\mathrm h\times\left(\frac{24}{23.9344}\right)\right].
$$

[5.0]

GST = 6h 43min + 21h 16min + 18h 3min

GST = 46h 2min

GST = 21h 58min. [2.0]

Any other correct alternative methods that provide the correct answer also acceptable.

$$
24\mathrm h-\mathrm{LHA}=\mathrm{RA}-\mathrm{LST},
$$

$$
\mathrm{LST}=\mathrm{RA}-24\mathrm h+\mathrm{LHA},
$$

$$
L=\mathrm{RA}+\mathrm{LHA}-\mathrm{GST},

### d) Solution

$$
\cos(90^\circ-\delta)=\cos z\cos(90^\circ-\phi)+\sin z\sin(90^\circ-\phi)\cos A,
$$

$$
\sin\delta=\sin(h)\sin\phi+\cos(h)\cos A\cos\phi.
$$

[5.0]

$$
\alpha=\sin\delta,\quad\beta=\sin(h),\quad\gamma=\cos(h)\cos A,\quad x=\sin\phi,
$$

$$
\alpha=\beta x+\gamma\sqrt{1-x^2},
$$

$$
(\beta^2+\gamma^2)x^2-2\alpha\beta x+(\alpha^2-\gamma^2)=0,
$$

[1.0]

$$
x=\frac{\alpha\beta\pm\gamma\sqrt{\gamma^2+\beta^2-\alpha^2}}{\beta^2+\gamma^2},
$$

[1.0]

$$
\sin\phi=\frac{\sin\delta\sin(h)\pm\cos(h)\cos A\sqrt{(\cos(h)\cos A)^2+\sin^2(h)-\sin^2\delta}}{\sin^2(h)+(\cos(h)\cos A)^2}.
$$

[1.0]

$$
\phi=-24.05^\circ,\ 4.01^\circ.
$$

[2.0]

For $\phi=-24.05^\circ\to A=71.0^\circ$. [2.0]

For $\phi=4.01^\circ\to A=109^\circ$. [2.0]

The Latitude of the island is $4^\circ1'\mathrm N$. [1.0]

### Alternative Solutions — b)

−LMT (Local Mean Time) → UT. [1.0]

Calculate sidereal day using given tropical year

$$
\gamma=\frac{\text{Sidereal time}}{\text{Solar time}}=\frac{24^h\times\left(1+\frac1{T_{\mathrm{tropic}}}\right)}{24^h}=1+\frac1{T_{\mathrm{tropic}}}=1.002737909.
$$

[3.0]

On the 21st November 2017, GST can be calculated from GST on 1st January 2017

Day number + Time = 323 d 18 h. [2.0]

$$
\mathrm{GST}=\mathrm{GST}_0+\Delta\theta=\mathrm{GST}_0+\gamma\square\Delta T.
$$

[2.0]

$$
\Delta\theta=\left(323^d\frac{18}{24}\right)\square1.002737909=324^d.6363981=15^h16^m24^s.8,
$$

$$
\mathrm{GST}=\mathrm{GST}_0+15^h16^m24^s.8=6^h43^m+15^h16^m24^s.8=21^h59^m24^s.8.
$$

[2.0]

### Alternative Solutions — d)

From Cosine law:

$$
\sin\delta=(\cos z)\square\sin\phi+(\sin z\cos A)\square\cos\phi,
$$

$$
\cos z=(\sin\delta)\square\sin\phi+(\cos\delta\cos\mathrm{LHA}^*)\square\cos\phi.
$$

[5.0]

Correct calculation steps that can lead to the final answers. [5.0]

**Final answers**

$$
\sin\phi=0.0698,\quad\phi=4.00^\circ
$$

OR

$$
\cos\phi=0.9976,\quad\phi=4.00^\circ.
$$
