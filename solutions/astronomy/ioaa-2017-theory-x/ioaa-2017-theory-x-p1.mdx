## Условие

The coordinates of the Large Magellanic Cloud (LMC) are R.A. = 5h 24min and Dec = $-70^\circ00'$. The latitude and longitude of Phuket are $7^\circ53'$ N and $98^\circ24'$ E, respectively. What is the date when the LMC culminates at 9pm as seen from Phuket in the same year? You may note that the Greenwich Sidereal Time, GST, at 00h UT 1st January is about 6h 43min, and Phuket is in the UT+7 time zone.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

### Solution

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '61%', '--fig-max': '391px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ioaa-2017-theory-x/s01-sidereal-time-v3.png" alt="Complete original sidereal-time circles, all arcs/arrows, GST/GST*, RA/LST/LHA, local and Greenwich meridians and Aries." width="1222" height="818" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


When it culminates, $\mathrm{LHA}=0$, $\mathrm{RA}=-\mathrm{LST}$. [1.0]

$$
\mathrm{LST}=-(L-\mathrm{GST}^*)=-\mathrm{RA}.
$$

$$
\mathrm{GST}^*=L-\mathrm{RA}=6\mathrm h\ 34\mathrm{min}-5\mathrm h\ 24\mathrm{min}=1\mathrm h\ 10\mathrm{min}.
$$

[2.0]

$$
\mathrm{GST}=24\mathrm h-1\mathrm h\ 10\mathrm{min},
$$

$$
\mathrm{GST}=22\mathrm h\ 50\mathrm{min}.
$$

[1.0]

Phuket time of 21:00 is UT14:00. [1.0]

GST = GST of 1st Jan + The angle that ♈ moves away from 1st Jan + UT. [1.0]

$$
22\mathrm h\ 50\mathrm{min}=6\mathrm h\ 43\mathrm{min}+\left[\left(\frac{\text{Days that away from 1st Jan}}{365.2422\ \mathrm{days}}\right)\times24\mathrm h\right]+\left[14\mathrm h\times\left(\frac{24}{23.9344}\right)\right].
$$

$$
\text{Days that away from 1st Jan}=(22\mathrm h\ 50\mathrm{min}-6\mathrm h\ 43\mathrm{min}-14\mathrm h\ 2\mathrm{min})\left(\frac{365.2422\ \mathrm{days}}{24\mathrm h}\right).
$$

[2.0]

Days that away from 1st Jan = 31.7 days from 1st Jan (at Greenwich). [1.0]

The time of the year that the LMC culminates in Phuket is on 2nd February. [1.0]
