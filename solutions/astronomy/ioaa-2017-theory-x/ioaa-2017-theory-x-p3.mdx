## Условие

In 2011, Dame and Thaddeus found a new part of the outer arm of the Milky Way by studying the CO line using the CfA 1.2m telescope. They found that the CO line was detected at galactic longitude $\ell=13.25^\circ$ (marked A in the figure) where it had a radial velocity of $20.9 \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ towards the Sun. Assume that the galactic rotation curve is flat beyond 5 kpc from the Galactic centre. The distance between the Sun and the Galactic centre is 8.5 kpc. The velocity of the Sun around the Galactic centre is $220 \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$.

**a)** Find the distance from the start of the arm (point A) to the Galactic centre. **[7 т.]**

**b)** Find the distance from the start of the arm (point A) to the Sun. **[3 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

### a) Solution

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '58.1%', '--fig-max': '372px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ioaa-2017-theory-x/s04-arm-distance-geometry.png" alt="Full original annotated Galactic arm geometry, dashed distant arm, A/S/G, d/x/R0 and both angles." width="1163" height="918" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


If there is the flat rotation curve beyond 2 kpc from the Galactic centre, the orbital velocity of the Sun and the start of the arm are both equal to

$$
v_r=-v_{\mathrm{LSR}}\sin(\ell)+v_{\mathrm{LSR}}\sin(\alpha).
$$

[3.0]

Sine law ($\triangle AGS$):

$$
\frac{\sin(\ell)}d=\frac{\sin(\alpha)}{R_0}.
$$

[1.0]

$$
v_r=-v_{\mathrm{LSR}}\sin(\ell)+v_{\mathrm{LSR}}R_0\frac{\sin(\ell)}d.
$$

[1.0]

$$
20.9=220\sin(13.25^\circ)-220\times8.5\times\frac{\sin(13.25^\circ)}d.
$$

[1.0]

**Correctly substitute numerical values.**

$$
d=14.5\ \mathrm{kpc}.
$$

[1.0]

### b) Solution

Cosine law ($\triangle AGS$):

$$
d^2=x^2+R_0^2-2xR_0\cos(\ell).
$$

[1.0]

$$
14.5^2=x^2+8.5^2-2\times8.5x\cos(13.25^\circ).
$$

[1.0]

**Correctly substitute numerical values.**

$$
x=\frac{17\cos(13.25^\circ)+\sqrt{17^2\cos^2(13.25^\circ)-4\times(8.5^2-14.5^2)}}2.
$$

$$
x=22.6\ \mathrm{kpc}.
$$

[1.0]

*Source discrepancy: the question and the repeated question in the key give a flat rotation curve beyond 5 kpc; the worked key paragraph prints 2 kpc. Both values remain as printed.*
