## Условие

The IOAA2021 logo is formed by the acronym IOAA, where the first letter is represented by the silhouette of the building of the National Astronomical Observatory (OAN) of Colombia, the oldest observatory in America. This observatory is located in Bogota, where it was founded in 1803. The capital city of Colombia is bordered by two famous hills, Monserrate and its neighbor Guadalupe, which are icons of Bogota's cityscape that decorate the logo's background.

## Отговори

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

### 8.1

Assuming $R=6378\ \mathrm{km}$ (as per the table of constants)

$$
\Delta x=R\cdot[(4^\circ36'18'')-(4^\circ35'30'')]\cdot\frac{\pi}{180}=1.4842\ \mathrm{km}
$$

$$
\Delta y=R\cdot[(74^\circ3'19'')-(74^\circ3'15'')]\cdot\frac{\pi\cdot\cos(4{,}35{,}54)}{180}=0.1237\ \mathrm{km}
$$

$$
\Delta z=(3.100-3.296)=-0.196\ \mathrm{km}
$$

[2 points]

$$
d_{2-3}=\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}=1.502\ \mathrm{km}
$$

[1 point]

### 8.2

Estimate the angular separation (in degrees) between Guadalupe (2) and Monserrate (3) as observed from the National Astronomical Observatory of Colombia (1). Take point 1 as the vertex.

Using the same method as part 8.1:

$$
d_{1-2}=2.722\ \mathrm{km}
$$

[2 points]

$$
d_{1-3}=2.580\ \mathrm{km}
$$

[2 points]

Using Cosine rule for spherical triangle,

$$
\cos(A)=\frac{d_{1-2}^2+d_{1-3}^2-d_{2-3}^2}{2d_{1-2}d_{1-2}}=0.841
$$

$$
A=32.78^\circ
$$

[2 points]

### 8.3

$$
\alpha=\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{-\sin(\beta)\cdot\sin(\varepsilon)+\cos(\beta)\cdot\cos(\varepsilon)\cdot\sin(\lambda)}{\cos(\beta)\cdot\cos(\lambda)}\right)
$$

$$
\alpha=12^\circ43'3''=0h\ 50m\ 52s
$$

[3 points]

$$
\delta=\sin^{-1}(\sin(\beta)\cdot\cos(\varepsilon)+\cos(\beta)\cdot\sin(\varepsilon)\cdot\sin(\lambda))
$$

$$
\delta=1^\circ33'43''
$$

[3 points]


</Spoiler>
