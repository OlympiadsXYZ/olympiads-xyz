*Theory — Q8 — IOAA Logo (15 points). — English (Official) · 15 т.*

## Условие

The IOAA2021 logo is formed by the acronym IOAA, where the first letter is represented by the silhouette of the building of the National Astronomical Observatory (OAN) of Colombia, the oldest observatory in America. This observatory is located in Bogota, where it was founded in 1803. The capital city of Colombia is bordered by two famous hills, Monserrate and its neighbor Guadalupe, which are icons of Bogota's cityscape that decorate the logo's background.
