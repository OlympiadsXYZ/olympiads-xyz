*XXII International Astronomy Olympiad · Observational round. Questions · Clear sky · 27 октомври 2017 г. · 10 т.*

<details>
<summary>Бележка от източника</summary>

You are provided with a headlight (with white & red lights).

</details>

## Условие

You are provided with a small telescope (80EQ). (Focal length of the telescope: $F = 900\ \mathrm{mm}$; focal length of the eyepiece: $f = 20\ \mathrm{mm}$, and apparent field of view: $\mathrm{aFoV} \approx 40^{\circ}$). Target and center the telescope on a bright star Markab ($\alpha$ Peg), which magnitude is $2.5^m$. Then observe for the second bright star, HIP 114031, in the field of view (FoV).

**9.1.** Right is the FoV of the telescope. Plot the position of HIP 114031 relative to $\alpha$ Peg, and mark the directions by Latin letters N, S, E, W (North, South, East and West respectively). **[5 т.]**

**9.2.** Estimate the angular distance between these two stars. **[3 т.]**

**9.3.** Estimate the magnitude of HIP114031. **[2 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **9.2.** 20 arcmin
- **9.3.** 6.8

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

9. (points: 10(5/3/2))

9.1

Plot the correct distance (3 points), correct direction (2 points)

9.2, $0.3333^{\circ}$ ($20\prime$) (+- 2′: 3 points, +-5′: 2 points, +- 8′: 1 point)

9.3, 6.8 (+-1: 2 points, +-2: 1 point)
