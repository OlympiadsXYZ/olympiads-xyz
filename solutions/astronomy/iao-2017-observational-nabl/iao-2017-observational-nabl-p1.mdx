*XXII International Astronomy Olympiad · Observational round. Questions · Clear sky · 27 октомври 2017 г. · 10 т.*

<details>
<summary>Бележка от източника</summary>

You are provided with a headlight (with white & red lights).

</details>

## Условие

Determine (for the time of 1 minute after the beginning of your work at the round) and write down in the right boxes:

**7.1.** Astronomical azimuth of the Moon (assume the $0^{\circ}$ azimuth corresponds to South direction, and the counting is clockwise). **[3 т.]**

**7.2.** The height of the Moon above the horizon. **[2 т.]**

**7.3.** Constellation(s) in which the Moon is located (write the standard three-letter designation). **[2 т.]**

**7.4.** The angular distance between the Moon and $\alpha$ Cas (Shedir). **[3 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **7.3.** Aqr/Aqu
- **7.4.** 71 °

</Spoiler>
