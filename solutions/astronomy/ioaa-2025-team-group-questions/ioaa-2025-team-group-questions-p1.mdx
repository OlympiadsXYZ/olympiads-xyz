**(G01.6)** Plot the rotation velocity versus Galacto-centric radius on the graph-sheet provided as a part of Summary Answersheet and draw a smooth curve going through these points. **[6 т.]**

**(G01.6a)** Assuming a spherically symmetric mass distribution, estimate the enclosed mass within the corresponding radius of your observations using the formula:

**(G01.1a)**

**Solution:**

**1. Given Parameters**

- **Frequency ($f$)**: 1420.40575 MHz or $1.42040575 \times 10^9$ Hz
- **Horn Dimensions**:
  - ($a$): 56.5 cm or 0.565 m
  - ($b$): 41.5 cm or 0.415 m
- **Speed of light ($c$)**: $299,792,458$ m/s

**2. Calculate the Wavelength ($\lambda$)**

$$\lambda = \frac{c}{f}$$

$$\lambda = \frac{299,792,458\ \mathrm{m/s}}{1,420,405,750\ \mathrm{Hz}} \approx \mathbf{0.2111}\ \mathrm{meters}$$

**3. Calculate the Angular Resolution (HPBW)**

**Resolution along Larger Dimension ($\theta_a$)**

This resolution is associated with the larger dimension, $a$.

$$\theta_a \approx \frac{180}{\pi}\left(\frac{\lambda}{a}\right)$$

$$\theta_a \approx \frac{180}{\pi}\left(\frac{0.2111\ \mathrm{m}}{0.565\ \mathrm{m}}\right) \approx \mathbf{21.4^{\circ}}$$

**Resolution along Smaller dimension ($\theta_b$)**

This resolution is associated with the smaller dimension, $b$.

$$\theta_b \approx \frac{180}{\pi}\left(\frac{\lambda}{b}\right)$$

$$\theta_b \approx \frac{180}{3.14} \times \left(\frac{0.2111\ \mathrm{m}}{0.415\ \mathrm{m}}\right) \approx \mathbf{29.2^{\circ}}$$

**(G01.7)**

**Solution:**

**1. Calculate Channel Bandwidth ($\Delta f$)**

Determine the frequency range, or bandwidth, covered by a single ”bin” or channel in the antenna.

- **Formula**: $\Delta f = \frac{\text{Total Frequency Range}}{\text{Number of Bins}}$
- **Given Data**:
  - Total Frequency Range $=$ 2.048 MHz or $2,048,000$ Hz
  - Number of Bins $= 512$
- **Calculation**:

$$\Delta f = \frac{2,048,000\ \mathrm{Hz}}{512} = \mathbf{4,000\ Hz}\ (\text{or } 4\ \mathrm{kHz})$$

**2. Calculate Sensitivity ($\sigma_T$)**

Next, we use the radiometer equation with the channel bandwidth, integration time and the system temperature.

- **Formula**:

$$\sigma_T = \frac{T_{sys}}{\sqrt{\Delta f \cdot t_{int}}}$$

- **Given Data**:
  - $T_{sys} = 115$ K (Measured $T_{\mathrm{recv}}$ + assumed $T_{\mathrm{ant}}$ of 5 K)
  - $\Delta f = 4,000$ Hz
  - Integration Time ($\tau$) $= 60$ s
- **Calculation**:

Sensitivity

$$= \sigma_T = \frac{115\ \mathrm{K}}{\sqrt{4000\ \mathrm{Hz} \times 60\ \mathrm{s}}} = \frac{115\ \mathrm{K}}{\sqrt{240,000}} \approx \mathbf{0.235\ K}$$

| Determination of $T_{sys}$ from calibration tab, using $T_{\mathrm{recv}}$ | 4.0 |
|---|---|
| Calculation of $\Delta f$ | 2.0 |
| Using correct $t_{int}$ | 1.0 |
| Calculation of RMS noise accurate to two significant digits (i.e., sensitivity) with unit | 2.0 |
