*ΩWAO 2022 ◇ Theoretical Round ◇ Problems*

## Условие

Occultation of a star by the Moon begins on Earth at the North Pole, and ends at a point with latitude $33^{\circ}$ North on the prime meridian ($0^{\circ}$ longitude) at $0^{\mathrm{h}}$ local time. Find the star’s equatorial coordinates and the occultation date.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- The Moon (and the star) is near the descending node of its orbit, which coincides with the autumnal equinox point; the occultation date is June 22.

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Let us look at the occultation of the star by the Moon from the star itself:

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '96.9%', '--fig-max': '620px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/owao-2022-theory-theoretical/p5-sol-fig1.png" alt="View from the star: the Earth's disk with centre O and the North Pole P on top; a radius from O to point E at angle φ = 33° above the horizontal line. The Moon's disk is shown at two positions M1 (touching P) and M2 (touching E); the Moon's path (dotted line through M1 and M2) and the parallel line through P and E are inclined at β = 28.5° to the horizontal line." width="1938" height="1527" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

The Moon moves at an angle

$$
\beta = \frac{90^{\circ} - \phi}{2} = 28.5^{\circ} = \varepsilon + i_{\text{☾}}.
$$

to the celestial equator. Then it is clear that the Moon is near the descending node of its orbit, which coincided with the point of the autumnal equinox.

At the end of the occultation, the Sun is over the meridian opposite to the meridian of point E, because at the point E it is midnight at that moment. Therefore, the Sun is at the point of the summer solstice. The date is June 22.

**Marking Scheme:**

- The Moon is near the autumnal equinox (without justification, taking into account the inclination of the lunar orbit to the ecliptic) — **2 pt.**
- The Moon is near a descending node of the orbit (as justification of the previous statement) — **2 pt.**
- The Moon is in the first quarter (or equivalent statement) — **2 pt.**
- The coordinates of the star — **2 pt.**
- The occultation date — **2 pt.**


</Spoiler>
