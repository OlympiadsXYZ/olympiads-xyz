## Условие

The spiral galaxies which are seen edge-on, are very suitable for determining their masses. A catalogue of such galaxies was created by I. D. Karachencev and his collaborators and mass spectroscopy was performed. The spectrum of one of them, FGC 1908, is shown below. It was obtained on 4.03.1997 using a spectrograph placed in the prime focus of the 6-meter telescope in the SAO. The spectrograph's slit, as the figure shows, was oriented along the galaxy's major axis. The straight lines going across the spectrum are the night sky's emissions. The other emissions belong to the galaxy and their laboratory wave lengths are specified. For calculating of the galaxy's mass in SAO the value of the Hubble's constant $H=74\,\mathrm{km\,sec^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$ was used.

You are proposed to repeat the estimate of the galaxy's mass. We remind that $1\,\mathrm{pc}=3.09\times10^{18}\,\mathrm{cm}$, the Sun's mass is $M=2\times10^{33}\,\mathrm g$ and the gravitation constant is $G=6.67\times10^{-8}\,\mathrm{dyn\,cm^2\,g^2}$.

**1** Explain why the two-dimensional spectrum of the galaxy looks like this.

**2** Estimate the mass of FGC 1908 and compare it with the mass of our own Galaxy.

**3** Specify the possible inaccuracies of your estimate.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">


<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '100%', '--fig-max': '984px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/iao-1998-practical-en/p2-sol-fig1.png" alt="Official annotated spectrum with wavelength shifts and orbital-velocity sketch." width="3074" height="3038" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


The explaining figures show that for the point on the galaxy periphery we have:

$$
\frac{mV_R^2}{R_g}=\frac{GmM_g}{R_g^2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad M_g=\frac{V_R^2R_g}{G},
$$

where

$$
V_R=c\cdot\Delta\lambda_1/\lambda_0\cdot2,\qquad R_g=d\cdot20''/(2\cdot10^5),\qquad d=V_{rG}/H,
$$

and

$$
V_{rG}=c\cdot\Delta\lambda_2/\lambda_0.
$$

The $\mathrm H_\alpha$ line with $\lambda_0=6563\,\text{Å}$ is selected for measurements as the most extended and therefore it fixes the significant part of the galaxy mass. $M_g\sim10^{10}M_\odot$, i.e. it is less than our Galaxy both by the size and by the mass.


</Spoiler>
