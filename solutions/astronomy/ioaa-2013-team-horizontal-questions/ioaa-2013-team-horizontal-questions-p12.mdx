**ii.** What is the wavelength of the spectral line $H\beta$? (integer) **[4 т.]**

**iii.** First planet to be discovered using Telescope (Mercury is 1, Neptune is 8) **[1 т.]**

**iv.** Consider an eclipsing binary, with central eclipses. Time between the first and fourth contact of primary eclipse is 1.5 hours and time between the 2nd and 3rd contact is 1 hour. Find the ratio of their radii. (integer) **[1 т.]**

**v.** The final Apollo lunar mission is Apollo \_. **[2 т.]**

**vi.** How many times greater is the escape velocity of a satellite compared to the velocity that is required for an orbit with radius equal to the celestial body’s radius? (one decimal) **[2 т.]**

**vii.** A star is behind the Coalsack Nebula in a distance of 200pc. If its apparent magnitude is m=18mag and the optical depth of the Nebula is $\tau$=1.38, what is its Absolute Magnitude? (integer) **[2 т.]**
