1. Betelguese
2. Vega
3. Canopus
4. $\alpha$ Centauri
5. Capella

7. Altair
8. Rigel
9. Arcturus **[3 т.]**

**v.** Leo is Nth zodiacal sign. N is **[1 т.]**
