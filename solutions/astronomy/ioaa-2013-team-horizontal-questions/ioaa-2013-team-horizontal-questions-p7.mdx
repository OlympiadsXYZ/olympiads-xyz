**v.** Hipparchus described a Solar Eclipse which was seen as Total in the Hellespont (40° 25’N, 29° 43’E) but Partial in Alexandria (31° 12’N, 29° 55’E), where at maximum 4/5 of the Sun was hidden. Hipparchus calculated the parallax of the limb of the Moon between the Hellespont and Alexandria, assuming that the parallax of the Sun is 0. He also knew that the Moon has the same apparent angular diameter as the Sun, with a value of 1/650 of the circle. Finally, by applying simple trigonometry, he calculated the approximate distance between the Earth and the Moon in Earth radii. How many Earth radii is the Earth-Moon distance? (integer) **[2 т.]**
