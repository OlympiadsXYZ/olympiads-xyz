## Условие

The low-resolution spectra of 15 different regions in an interacting galaxy **Arp 86** were observed by Multi-Object Spectrograph (MOS) on the 2.16-m telescope of Xinglong Station near Beijing, National Astronomical Observatories of China. The data is analyzed by Zhou et al. (2014, RAA, 14, 1393). The measured fluxes of the [OIII], Hβ, [NII], Hα emission lines are shown in Table β7 (see separate sheet). The fluxes are in units of $10^{-16}\ \mathrm{erg/s/cm^2}$.

**7.1** Plot the scatter diagram between $\log_{10}(\mathrm{[OIII]}/\mathrm{H}\beta)$ vs. $\log_{10}(\mathrm{[NII]}/\mathrm{H}\alpha)$ for regions in the galaxy. **[2 т.]**

**7.2** Draw a dashed curve on the diagram for the diagnostic function:

$$
\log_{10}(\mathrm{[OIII]}/\mathrm{H}\beta)=1.3+\frac{0.61}{\log_{10}(\mathrm{[NII]}/\mathrm{H}\alpha)-0.05},
$$

given by Kauffmann et al. (2003, MNRAS, 346, 1055). If the point of a region in the diagram is above this curve, the region can be classified as “AGN” (Active Galactic Nuclei). Determine which regions can be classified as “AGN”, write the answer in form of «AGN = “Region ID” …». (“Region ID” are given in the 1st column of Table β7.) **[2 т.]**

**7.3** In your answer book draw a table in form of the following example. Calculate the angular distance between each region to the center ($\alpha=23^h47^m04.8^s$, $\delta=+29^\circ29'00.6''$) of the galaxy (in units of arcsec). Calculate the luminosities of Hα ($L(\mathrm{H}\alpha)$) for each region (in units of erg/s), and then estimate the Star Formation Rate (SFR) for each region based on Hα luminosities, following the formula:

$$
\mathrm{SFR}\ (M_\odot/\mathrm{yr})=7.9\times10^{-42}[L(\mathrm{H}\alpha)]\ (\mathrm{erg/s}).
$$

Calculate the metallicities (oxygen abundance in units of $\log_{10}(\mathrm{O/H})$) of interstellar medium (ISM) for each region, following the formula:

$$
12+\log_{10}(\mathrm{O/H})=8.9+0.57\times\log_{10}(\mathrm{[NII]}/\mathrm{H}\alpha).
$$

The redshift of **Arp 86** is: 0.016.
