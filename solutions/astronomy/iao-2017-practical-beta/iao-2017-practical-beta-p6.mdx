## Условие

The transit of an extra-solar planet (TrES-3b) across a star (TrES-3) was observed by the 1-m telescope of Weihai Observatory (WHO) on April 16, 2009, using the broad-band V filter and a PI CCD. The photometric data (selected part from the original one) are given in the Table 6 (see separate sheet), the time is in Julian days, and $m_V$ is the V-band magnitude.

**6.1** Plot the transit light curve (magnitude vs time), determine the moment of mid-transit (the deepest transit) and write result (in Julian days) in form of «$T_{\mathrm{mid}}=\ldots$», estimate the duration of the transit (in days) in form of «$T_d=\ldots$», determine the depth of the transit (in magnitudes) in form of «$D=\ldots$». **[5 т.]**

**6.2** Give the formula and calculate the radius of the extra-solar planet TrES-3b $R_p$ (in units of the radius of Jupiter: $R_{\mathrm{Jupiter}}$, write the answer in form of «$R_p=\ldots$»). The radius of the star (TrES-3) is known to be 0.813 radius of the Sun, $R_s=0.813R_{\mathrm{sun}}$. **[2 т.]**

**6.3** Estimate the approximate value of the angle $i$, in unit of degrees (°) between the axis of this planet’s orbiting and the line of sight of the observer. Write the answer in form of «$i\approx\ldots$». **[1 т.]**

**6.4** The next mid-transit moment after the above transit event was: JD = 2454939.578. The mass of the star (TrES-3) is known to be 0.924 mass of the Sun, $M_s=0.924M_{\mathrm{sun}}$. Calculate the orbital period of TrES-3b (in units of days, in form of «$P=\ldots$»), and the semi-major axis of the orbit (in units of au, in form of «$a=\ldots$»). **[2 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **6.1** Tmid = …8.272 JD; Td = 0.055 days; D = 0.027 mag (as printed).
- **6.2** $R_p=1.305R_J$
- **6.3** Approximately 90° (or calculated value approximately 80°).
- **6.4** P = 1.3062 days; a = 0.0226 au.

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

<ProblemSection id="solution-solution-6-1">

### 6.1

Plot the transit light curve properly, should care about the scales, and the magnitudes axis (y-axis) should be reversed (i.e. from high values to low values).

$$
T_{\mathrm{mid}}=\ldots8.272\ \mathrm{JD};\quad T_d=0.055\ \mathrm{days};\quad D=0.027^{\mathrm m}.
$$

(some errors in above values accepted)

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-solution-6-2">

### 6.2

$$
D=(R_p/R_s)^2\quad\text{(or other similar ones)};
$$

$$
R_p=1.305R_J\quad\text{(some errors accepted)}.
$$

Source note: the key prints the same symbol $D$ here as for the magnitude depth in 6.1; the notation is retained without silently converting its definition.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-solution-6-3">

### 6.3

$\sim90^\circ$ (or calculated value $\sim80^\circ$)

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-solution-6-4">

### 6.4

$$
P=1.3062\ \mathrm{days};\qquad a=0.0226\ \mathrm{au}.
$$

(some errors in above values accepted)

</ProblemSection>


</Spoiler>
