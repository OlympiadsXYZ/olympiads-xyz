## Условие

*Your telescope has already been Polar aligned!*

**9.1.** Point the telescope to the Zenith manually as accurately as possible.

**9.2.** Adjust the DEC axis setting circle approximately to $\delta=+7^\circ$.

**9.3.** Point the telescope to the celestial equator using the DEC axis setting circle.
