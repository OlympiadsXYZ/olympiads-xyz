*XVIII International Astronomy Olympiad — Vilnius, Lithuania, 6–14.IX.2013 — Practical round. Problems to solve · 6 септември 2013 г.*

## Условие

Analysis of observations of a near earth asteroid.

Astronomers of two observatories, which are located at a distance of 3172 km from each other, took CCD images of a certain region of the sky for the search of a near earth asteroid. Two images were obtained by Observatory 1 during the same night at $4^{h}53^{m}$ UT and at $7^{h}16^{m}$ UT. These images (negatives) are shown in Figs. 7.1 and 7.2, respectively. The next two images obtained on the same night were made at Observatory 1 .and Observatory 2 simultaneously. These images (negatives) are shown in Figs. 7.3 and 7.4. The scale of all the images is the same as shown in Fig. 7.1.

Note: You are provided a transparency for measurements of angular displacements of the asteroid.

**7.1.** Identify and mark the asteroid in the given Figs.

**7.2.** Measure the angular displacement (in arcsec) of the asteroid as seen from Observatory 1 and calculate its angular velocity in arcsec/s.

**7.3.** Measure the parallax of the asteroid (in arcsec) and calculate its distance from the earth.

**7.4.** Calculate the tangential linear velocity (velocity perpendicular to the line of sight) of the asteroid.
