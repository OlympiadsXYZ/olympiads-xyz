### Русский

Каков сейчас реальный физический диаметр (в км и астрономических единицах) Крабовидной туманности? Крабовидная туманность – остатки сверхновой, вспышку которой на Земле зафиксировали 4 июля 1054 года. Она находится в созвездии Тельца и в настоящее время её видимый угловой размер составляет около 5′.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="en">

### English

What is the actual physical diameter (in km and astronomical units) of the Crab Nebula right now? The crab nebula is the remnants of a supernova, the outbreak of which was recorded on Earth on July 4, 1054. It is located in the constellation of Taurus and currently its apparent angular size is about 5′.

</ProblemSection>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The distance to an object having a parallax of 0.0005″ (this value can be found in the table of stars and objects) is equal to:

$$
L=\frac1p=\frac1{0.0005}=2000\ pc\approx6500\ ly.
$$

The apparent angular size of 5′ at this distance corresponds to the physical size

$$
2000\ pc\times\frac{5'}{3438'}=2{,}9\ pc\approx3\ pc\quad\text{или}\quad6500\ ly\times\frac{5'}{3438'}\approx9.5\ ly,
$$

which is

$$
2{,}9\ pc\times206265\frac{au}{pc}\approx6\cdot10^5\ au\approx9\cdot10^{13}\ km\quad\text{or}\quad9.5\times365\times86400\times300000\approx9\cdot10^{13}\ km.
$$

However, this is the size of the nebula at the time when the light from it began to come to us, that is, 6500 years ago. Thus, the total age of the Crab Nebula is currently 969 + 6500 ≈ 7500 years. And if in 969 years it expanded to $6\cdot10^5$ au or $9\cdot10^{13}$ km, then, assuming a linear expansion, in 7500 years it expanded by

$$
6\cdot10^5\ au\times\frac{7500\ year}{969\ year}=4{,}6\cdot10^6\ au\approx5\cdot10^6\ au\quad\text{или}\quad9\cdot10^{13}\ km\times\frac{7500\ year}{969\ year}\approx7\cdot10^{14}\ km.
$$

Or, calculating differently, in astronomical units it will be

$$
9\cdot10^{13}\ km\times\frac{7500\ year}{969\ year}\approx7\cdot10^{14}\ km\bigg/150000000\frac{km}{au}\approx4.7\cdot10^6\ au.
$$

Answer: $7\cdot10^{14}$ km, $5\cdot10^6$ au Accuracy with more than 1 significant digit is inappropriate.
