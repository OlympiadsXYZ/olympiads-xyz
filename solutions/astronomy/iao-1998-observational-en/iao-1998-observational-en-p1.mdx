*III International Astronomy Olympiad*

## Условие

With the help of a school telescope examine and draw the details visible on the Sun's disk. Mark the cardinal points on your drawing.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '88.1%', '--fig-max': '564px'}}>

</figure>


Identify the details of your drawing with the details of the one-dimensional radio profiles of the Sun obtained in the previous days by RATAN-600. Mark them by the corresponding letters.

The radio observations were carried out at noon, the knife beam was directed vertically and covered all the Sun's disk.
