*III International Astronomy Olympiad*

## Условие

> ...That telescope was christened the Star-Splitter,  
> Because it didn't do a thing but split  
> A star in two or three, the way you split  
> A globule of quicksilver in your hand  
> With one stroke of your finger in the middle...  

Robert Frost, “The Star-Splitter”

Using a school telescope find in the sky and resolve several ($\leq5$) binary stars into their components.

Notice the brightnesses and the colours of the components.

Explain the relationship between the brightnesses and the colours of the components.

## Решение
