## Условие

Consider a sun-planet-moon system. The rotation period of the planet is 2 days. The period of revolution of the moon around the planet is 42 days while that of the planet around the sun is 420 days. What is the length of the lunar cycle as seen from the planet? You can assume the the direction of planetary rotation, planetary revolution and lunar revolution is the same.

(a) 42.1 days

(b) 44.3 days

(c) 46.7 days

(d) 50.5 days

(e) 53.1 days

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- C

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Solution:**

Synodic period i.e. length of lunar cycle is given as $\frac{1}{T_s} = \frac{1}{42} - \frac{1}{420}$.

**Answer: C**
