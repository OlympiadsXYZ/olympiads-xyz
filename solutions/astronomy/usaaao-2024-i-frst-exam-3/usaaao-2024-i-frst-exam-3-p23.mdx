## Условие

VOIDED A recently discovered star *Recentus* in a nearby galaxy has been confirmed to have a luminosity of $4\mathrm{L}_{\odot}$ with a radius of $4\mathrm{R}_{\odot}$. What is the approximate frequency of peak emission of *Recentus*?

(a) $2 \times 10^{14}$ Hz

(b) $4 \times 10^{14}$ Hz

(c) $6 \times 10^{14}$ Hz

(d) $8 \times 10^{14}$ Hz

(e) $10 \times 10^{14}$ Hz

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- B

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Solution:**

The luminosity $L$ of a star in terms of its radius $R$ and temperature $T$ can be written using Stefan-Boltzmann’s Law:

$$
L = 4\pi R^2 \sigma T^4
$$

$\frac{L}{(R^2T^4)}$ is constant, and we know that the luminosity and radius of *Recentus* are $L_R = 4L_{\odot}$ and $R_R = 4R_{\odot}$:

$$
\frac{L_R}{(R_R^2 T_R^4)} = \frac{L_{\odot}}{(R_{\odot}^2 T_{\odot}^4)}
$$

$$
T_R = \frac{T_{\odot}}{\sqrt{2}}
$$

Using Wein’s law, the peak emission wavelength of this star is $\lambda_R = \lambda_{\odot}\sqrt{2} \approx 710$ nm and the corresponding peak emission frequency is $4.2 \times 10^{14}$ Hz. Note that $\lambda_{\odot} = 502$ nm can be calculated from Wein’s constant and $T_{\odot}$ which are in the TOC.

**Answer: B**


</Spoiler>
