## Условие

Imagine a very long cylindrical planet that has a satellite orbiting around it. Considering that the average density of the planet is $\rho$ and the radius is $R$, find the expression that relates the period $P$ of the satellite with its distance $d$ to the center of the planet.

(a) $\frac{2d}{R}\sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{G\rho}}$

(b) $\frac{d}{R}\sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{G\rho}}$

(c) $\frac{d}{2R}\sqrt{\frac{4\pi}{G\rho}}$

(d) $\frac{d}{R}\sqrt{\frac{4\pi}{G\rho}}$

(e) $\frac{d}{R}\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{G\rho}}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- B

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Solution:**

Due to the symmetry, and using Gauss’ Law for gravitation, we know that the gravity at a distance $d$ from the center is given by $g \cdot S = -4\pi G M_{in}$. Our Gaussian shape will thus be a cylinder of radius $d$ and length $L$. So, $S = 2\pi d L$, and the internal mass is given by $M_{in} = \rho \pi R^2 L$. Therefore, we have that:

$$
g \cdot 2\pi d L = -4\pi G \rho \pi R^2 L \Rightarrow g(\vec{r}) = -\frac{2\pi G \rho R^2}{d}\hat{r}
$$

Now, knowing that $F_g = F_{cp} \Rightarrow mg = m\omega^2 d = m(2\pi/P)^2 d$, we can find the period:

$$
P = \frac{d}{R}\sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{G\rho}}
$$

**Answer: B**


</Spoiler>
