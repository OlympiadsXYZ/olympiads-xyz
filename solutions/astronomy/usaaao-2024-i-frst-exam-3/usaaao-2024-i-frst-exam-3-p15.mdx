## Условие

Two protons A and B lie in the solar interior. In the rest frame of proton A, the proton B approaches it radially from a large distance with speed $0.9c$. In the rest frame of proton A, identify the radius of the “classically forbidden” region for proton B (i.e. the region in which proton B cannot enter).

(a) $6.6 \times 10^{-12}$ m

(b) $4.1 \times 10^{-15}$ m

(c) $2.3 \times 10^{-15}$ m

(d) $3.8 \times 10^{-18}$ m

(e) $1.2 \times 10^{-18}$ m

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- E

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Solution:**

Let $r_c$ denote the radius of the classically forbidden region for proton B in the rest frame of proton A. This is also the radial position of the classical turning point of motion for proton B. Applying conservation of (relativistic) energy, we find

$$
(\gamma(v) - 1)m_p c^2 = \frac{e^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0 r_c}, \qquad (2)
$$

where $\gamma(v) = 1/\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}$ denotes the Lorentz factor.

**Answer: E**


</Spoiler>
