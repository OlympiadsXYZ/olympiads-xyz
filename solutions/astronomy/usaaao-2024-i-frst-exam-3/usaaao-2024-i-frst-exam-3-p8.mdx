## Условие

The mass density of the Milky Way galaxy determines the orbital velocity of planets, stars, and other objects orbiting around its center. Assuming a constant surface mass density $\sigma$ for the Milky Way and modeling it as a perfect circular disk, identify the dependence of the circular orbital velocity $v(r)$ of a point mass at radius $r$ from the galaxy’s center.

(a) $1/\sqrt{r}$

(b) $1/r$

(c) $\sqrt{r}$

(d) $r$

(e) $r^{3/2}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- C

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Solution:** From Newton’s law, we have

$$
\frac{G\sigma(\pi r^2)}{r^2} = \frac{v^2}{r} \qquad (1)
$$

which gives $v(r) \propto \sqrt{r}$.

**Answer: C**


</Spoiler>
