Using the 2.16m telescope of The National Astronomical Observatories of China (NAOC), their R-band image in 2009 has been obtained, which is presented at the fig.1. <ProblemSection id="solution-part-5-1">

### 5.1.

In April and September, the right ascension of the sun is about 0.5–2.5 h and 10.5–12.5 h respectively. An… the right ascension of the galaxy is $\mathrm{RA}\approx147^\circ\sim10\mathrm h$. Thus, April is the better choice because i… September the visual position of the galaxies is too close to the sun. Answer: **Apr**.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-5-2">

### 5.2.

According to the DECs of the two galaxies, it's easy to find that the upper one is IC564.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-5-3">

### 5.3.

The wavelength of H$\alpha$ emission line measured in figure 2 is approximately 6680–6700 Å.

Taking into account (see table of constants) that the laboratory wavelength of H$\alpha$ emission is 6563 Å, th… redshift of the galaxy is approximately:

$$
z=(\lambda-\lambda_0)/\lambda_0=(6700-6563)/6563\approx0.02.
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-5-4">

### 5.4.

Since $z\ll1$, the recession velocity of the galaxy is:

$$
v=c\cdot z=6000\ \mathrm{km/s}
$$

We should choose the filter whose peak value of redshift is close to 6000 km/s. The suitable one is № C4.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-5-5">

### 5.5.

According to Hubble's law, the distance to the galaxies is:

$$
r=v/H_0=6000\ \mathrm{km/s}/70\ (\mathrm{km/s})/\mathrm{Mpc}\approx85\ \mathrm{Mpc},\ \text{i.e. }85\,000\,000\ \text{parsec}.
$$

The two galaxies are close to each other and the curvature of the celestial sphere can be neglected. Th… angular distance of the centers of the galaxies is:

$$
\alpha=(\Delta\mathrm{RA}^2+\Delta\mathrm{DEC}^2)^{0.5}
$$

Where, $\Delta\mathrm{RA}=(\mathrm{RA1}-\mathrm{RA2})\times\cos(\mathrm{DEC})=0.00303^\circ$, $\Delta\mathrm{DEC}=(\mathrm{DEC1}-\mathrm{DEC2})=0.02579^\circ$. As a result:

$$
\alpha=0.02597^\circ=93.5''.
$$

The distance between the two galaxies is:

$$
d=r\cdot\alpha=85\,000\,000\cdot93.5/3600/57.3=38.5\ \mathrm{kpc}.
$$

</ProblemSection>
