*III International Astronomy Olympiad*

## Условие

Same as for the 14–16 years old students.

Is it possible the lunar Mare Crisium whose diameter is $520\,\mathrm{km}$, to be resolved by naked eye?

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The Moon diameter is $3476\,\mathrm{km}$, and the visible angular diameter of the lunar disk is $31'$. Therefore, the maximum visible diameter of the Mare Crisium is

$$
31'\times520/3476=4.6'.
$$

This is 3–5 times more than the angular resolution limit of a naked eye. Indeed, good observers can see the Mare Crisium, that was proofed by the pretelescopic draws of the Moon.


</Spoiler>
