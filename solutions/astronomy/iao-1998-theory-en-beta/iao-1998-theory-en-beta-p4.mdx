*III International Astronomy Olympiad*

## Условие

An artificial Earth's satellite moves by $6.9\,\mathrm{km/sec}$ along a circular orbit in the equatorial plane and in the direction of the Earth's rotation. What is the period of the satellite's appearance in the zenith for a point located on the equator?

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

$$
\frac{mV^2}{R}=\frac{GmM_0}{R^2},\qquad V=\left(\frac{GM_0}{R}\right)^{1/2}.
$$

Taking into account $GM_0=gR_0^2$:

$$
P=\frac{2\pi R}{V}=\frac{2\pi GM_0}{V^3}=\frac{2\pi gR_0^2}{V^3}\approx127\,\mathrm{min}.
$$

Then:

$$
\frac1T=\frac1P-\frac1{P_0},\qquad P_0=24^{\mathrm h}.
$$

So:

$$
T=\frac{P_0P}{P_0-P}\approx139\,\mathrm{min}.
$$


</Spoiler>
