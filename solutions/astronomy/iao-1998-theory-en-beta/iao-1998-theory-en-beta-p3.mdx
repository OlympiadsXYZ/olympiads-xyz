*III International Astronomy Olympiad*

## Условие

It is known that the precession is a slow ($50''$ per year) retrograde motion of the vernal equinox points. Which of the celestial circles do these points move along – the equator or the ecliptic?

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Along ecliptic one.


</Spoiler>
