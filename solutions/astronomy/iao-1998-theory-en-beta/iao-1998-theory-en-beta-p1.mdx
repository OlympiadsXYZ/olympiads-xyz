*III International Astronomy Olympiad*

## Условие

Is it possible from the Moon's surface to observe solar eclipses, meteors, comets, aurorae, rainbows, noctilucent clouds, artificial satellites?

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

In principle, from the Moon surface, it is possible to observe all nonatmospheric astronomical events: comets, artificial satellites and solar eclipses (by the Earth). But one can not observe auroras, rainbows, noctilucent clouds and meteors, because they take place only in an atmosphere.


</Spoiler>
