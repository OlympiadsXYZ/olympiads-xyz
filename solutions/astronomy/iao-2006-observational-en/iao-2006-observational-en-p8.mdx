## Условие

Given in a separate “answer sheet” are 10 of the brighter stars in the night sky.

**8.1.** In the table (see answer sheet), mark by X the squares in the last column for those stars that **are visible to you now**.

**8.2.** Show them to the examiner with the help of a torch beam.

**8.3.** Point out the Vernal Equinox in the sky.

## Решение

<Warning title="Непълно решение">
These are on-site observing tasks. The archive supplies the questions, sky chart and blank examiner report, but no completed official answers.
</Warning>
