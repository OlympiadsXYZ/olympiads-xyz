You have been given a 125-mm Newtonian reflecting telescope, focal length $F=1025$ mm, on an azimuth mount with a set of 3 eye pieces. The focal lengths of the eyepieces are: 12 mm, 25 mm and 38 mm.

The target for you depends on group:

(α): Pleiades – Плеяды

(β): Albireo (binary) – Альбирео (двойная)

Choose suitable eyepiece, bring the target to the centre of the field of view of the telescope and show it to the examiner.
