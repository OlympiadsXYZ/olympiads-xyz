## Условие

Determine if each of the following statements is True or False. In the Summary Answersheet, tick the correct answer (TRUE / FALSE) for each statement. No justifications are necessary for this question.

**T1.1** In a photograph of the clear sky on a Full Moon night with a sufficiently long exposure, the colour of the sky would appear blue as in daytime. **[2 т.]**

**T1.2** An astronomer at Bhubaneswar marks the position of the Sun on the sky at 05:00 UT every day of the year. If the Earth's axis were perpendicular to its orbital plane, these positions would trace an arc of a great circle. **[2 т.]**

**T1.3** If the orbital period of a certain minor body around the Sun in the ecliptic plane is less than the orbital period of Uranus, then its orbit must necessarily be fully inside the orbit of Uranus. **[2 т.]**

**T1.4** The centre of mass of the solar system is inside the Sun at all times. **[2 т.]**

**T1.5** A photon is moving in free space. As the Universe expands, its momentum decreases. **[2 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **T1.1** True
- **T1.2** True
- **T1.3** False
- **T1.4** False
- **T1.5** True

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

<ProblemSection id="solution-solution-1">

### T1.1

**True**. The colour of the clear sky during night is the same as during daytime, since the spectrum of sunlight reflected by the Moon is almost the same as the spectrum of sunlight. Only the intensity is lower. **[2.0]**

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-solution-2">

### T1.2

**True**. If the Earth’s axis were perpendicular to its orbital plane, the celestial equator will coincide with ecliptic and the Sun will remain along the celestial equator every day. However, as the Earth’s orbit is elliptical, the true sun would still lead or lag mean sun by a few minutes on different days of year. **[2.0]**

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-solution-3">

### T1.3

**False**. The semi-major axis of the orbit of the body will be less than that of Uranus. However the minor body’s orbit may have a high eccentricity, in which case it may go outside that of Uranus. **[2.0]**

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-solution-4">

### T1.4

**False**. The centre of mass of Sun-Jupiter pair is just outside the Sun. Thus, if all gas giants are on same side of the Sun, the centre of mass of Solar system is definitely outside the Sun. **[2.0]**

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-solution-5">

### T1.5

**True**. For photons the wavelength increases when the Universe expands. **[2.0]**

</ProblemSection>


</Spoiler>
