## Условие



**a)** Определете хоризонталните координати на Тубан ($\alpha$ Dra).

**b)** Определете зенитното отстояние на Алкор (близо до $\zeta$ Ursae Majoris).

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

<ProblemSection id="solution-horizontal-coordinates">

### a) Horizontal coordinates

The official key gives values for four observing groups (mid-round times). Both printed azimuth conventions are retained.

| Group | Time | Azimuth | Parenthesized azimuth | Altitude $h$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | 22:10 | $320^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ | $140^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ | $47^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ |
| II | 22:45 | $324^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ | $144^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ | $44^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ |
| III | 23:35 | $328^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ | $148^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ | $40^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ |
| IV | 00:10 | $332^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ | $152^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ | $37^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ |

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-zenith-distance">

### b) Zenith distance

| Group | Time | Zenith distance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 22:10 | $53^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ |
| II | 22:45 | $56^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ |
| III | 23:35 | $60^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ |
| IV | 00:10 | $63^{\circ}\pm5^{\circ}$ |

</ProblemSection>
