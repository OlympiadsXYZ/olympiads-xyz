## Условие

Асансьор се изкачва от повърхността на Земята до геостационарен спътник. Оценете (поне грубо) на какво разстояние от спътника силата на тежестта, действаща на човека, ще е равна на една стотна от обикновената.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- Около 6000 km под спътника (линейната оценка дава 6150 km).

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

*Official English sketches for solutions.*

Finally necessary to find the distance $x$ where $g_{\mathrm{eff}}=0.01g_0$.

$$
g_{\mathrm{eff}}=GM/R^2-\omega^2R=g_0R_0^2/R^2-\omega^2R,
$$

where $g_0$ and $R_0$ are values near the earth surface. But at first necessary to find the radius $R_S$ of the geostationary satellite's orbit; at this position $g_{\mathrm{eff}}=0$:

$$
GM/R_S^2=\omega^2R_S,
$$

$$
R_S^3=GM/\omega^2=g_0R_0^2/\omega^2=g_0(R_0T/2\pi)^2,
$$

where $T$ is period of the Earth revolution.

$$
R_S=\{g_0R_0^2/\omega^2\}^{1/3}\approx42\,200\ \mathrm{km}.
$$

For small variations one may use formula

$$
g_{\mathrm{eff}}(A+x)=g_{\mathrm{eff}}(A)+g_{\mathrm{eff}}'x.
$$

So in measuring gravitation very near the satellite (the satellite is zero point and $g_{\mathrm{eff}}=0$), for the small variations of the distance $x$ directed from Earth,

$$
g_{\mathrm{eff}}(x)=g_{\mathrm{eff}}'x=(-2GM/R_S^3-\omega^2)x.
$$

So for $g_{\mathrm{eff}}=0.01g_0$ one can calculate

$$
x=\frac{0.01g_0}{-2GM/R_S^3-\omega^2}=\frac{0.01g_0}{-2g_0R_0^2/R_S^3-(2\pi/T)^2}.
$$

Taking into account that $R_S^3=g_0(R_0T/2\pi)^2$,

$$
x=\frac{0.01g_0}{-3(2\pi/T)^2}\approx-6150\ \mathrm{km}.
$$

The sign 'minus' means that these 6150 km are in a direction opposite to our axis direction: the position of the point before the satellite. Of course, the value 6150 km already is not a 'small variation', but it is enough to make desirable estimation: about **6000 km**.

Бележка за източника: английският текст нарича $T$ „period of the Earth revolution“; руският уточнява, че е периодът на въртене около собствената ос.


</Spoiler>
