## Условие

For the following problem find the range in which the answer lies: on the day of summer solstice, on which geographic latitude is the sun culminating at the angle of $+72^{\circ}50'$ north of the equator?

A. $0^{\circ} - 15^{\circ}$
B. $15^{\circ} - 30^{\circ}$
C. $30^{\circ} - 45^{\circ}$
D. $45^{\circ} - 60^{\circ}$
E. $60^{\circ} - 75^{\circ}$

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: A

Actual value is $+6^{\circ}17'$.


</Spoiler>
