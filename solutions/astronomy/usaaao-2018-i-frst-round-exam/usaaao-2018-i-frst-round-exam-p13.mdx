*USAAAO National Astronomy Olympiad First Round 02/12/2018-02/16/2018 Time Limit: 75 Minutes · 12 февруари 2018 г. · 1 т.*

## Условие

Star A has a surface temperature of 10,000 K while Star B has a surface temperature of 4,000 K. Star B is 10 times larger than Star A and its distance from Earth is half that of Star A. What is the magnitude difference between Stars A and B?

A. -10.48  
B. -7.47  
C. -0.48  
D. 2.53  
E. 7.47

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: D

$$
\Delta m = -2.5\log\left(\frac{F_1}{F_2}\right) = -2.5\log\left[\left(\frac{R_1}{R_2}\right)^{2}\left(\frac{T_1}{T_2}\right)^{4}\left(\frac{d_2}{d_1}\right)^{2}\right] = 2.53
$$


</Spoiler>
