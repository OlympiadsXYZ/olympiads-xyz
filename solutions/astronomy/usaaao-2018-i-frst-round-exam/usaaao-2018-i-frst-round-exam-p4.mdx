*USAAAO National Astronomy Olympiad First Round 02/12/2018-02/16/2018 Time Limit: 75 Minutes · 12 февруари 2018 г. · 1 т.*

## Условие

Determine the azimuth of Capella (Aurigae) in its upper culmination as seen from geographic latitude of $\phi = +45^{\circ}58'$

A. $0^{\circ}$  
B. $90^{\circ}$  
C. Undefined  
D. $180^{\circ}$  
E. $360^{\circ}$

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: C


</Spoiler>
