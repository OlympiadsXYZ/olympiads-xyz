*USAAAO National Astronomy Olympiad First Round 02/12/2018-02/16/2018 Time Limit: 75 Minutes · 12 февруари 2018 г. · 1 т.*

## Условие

A planet's orbit around a star has a semimajor axis of 16 AU. What is the period of the planet's orbit?

A. 6 years  
B. 32 years  
C. 64 years  
D. 256 years  
E. 4096 years

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: C

Use simplified version of Kepler's Law.


</Spoiler>
