## Условие

What is the main energy transport process in the core of the Sun?

A. Radiation
B. Convection
C. Conduction
D. Diffusion
E. Advection

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: A

The inner regions of the sun is radiative, and the outer layers are convective.


</Spoiler>
