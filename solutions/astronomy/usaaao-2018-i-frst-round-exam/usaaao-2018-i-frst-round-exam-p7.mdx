*USAAAO National Astronomy Olympiad First Round 02/12/2018-02/16/2018 Time Limit: 75 Minutes · 12 февруари 2018 г. · 1 т.*

## Условие

For the following problem, find the range in which the answer lies: looking from Greenwich on February 10th ($s_o = 9^h17^m48^s$) at what time is Pollux ($\alpha = 7^h42^m16^s$) at its upper culmination?

A. 12 am - 6 am  
B. 6 am - 12 pm  
C. 12 pm - 6 pm  
D. 6 pm - 10 pm  
E. 10 pm - 12 am

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: E

Ans=$22^{h}20^{m}48^{s}$


</Spoiler>
