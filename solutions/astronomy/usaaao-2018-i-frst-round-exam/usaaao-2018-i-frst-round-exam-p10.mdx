*USAAAO National Astronomy Olympiad First Round 02/12/2018-02/16/2018 Time Limit: 75 Minutes · 12 февруари 2018 г. · 1 т.*

## Условие

When seen from Earth, what is Venus' phase when it is at greatest elongation?

A. New  
B. Crescent  
C. Quarter  
D. Gibbous  
E. Full

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: C

At greatest elongation the Sun-Venus-Earth system forms a right angle, therefore Venus will be seen from Earth as half illuminated which is a quarter phase.


</Spoiler>
