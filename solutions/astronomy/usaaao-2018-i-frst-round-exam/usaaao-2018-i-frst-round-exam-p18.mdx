## Условие

What do the cores of low-mass and high-mass main-sequence stars have in common?

A. Both have a uniform composition  
B. Both are burning hydrogen  
C. Both are convective  
D. Both are radiative  
E. Both are contracting

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: B


</Spoiler>
