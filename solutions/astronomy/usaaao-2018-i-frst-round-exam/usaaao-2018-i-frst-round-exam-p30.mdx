## Условие

Find the total sum of the binary system of the star Capella, if semi-major axis between them is 0.85 AU, and period of 0.285 years.

A. 5.5 solar masses
B. 6.5 solar masses
C. 7.5 solar masses
D. 8.5 solar masses
E. 9.5 solar masses

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: C


</Spoiler>
