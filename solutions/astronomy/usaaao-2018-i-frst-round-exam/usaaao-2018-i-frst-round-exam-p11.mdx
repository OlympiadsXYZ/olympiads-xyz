*USAAAO National Astronomy Olympiad First Round 02/12/2018-02/16/2018 Time Limit: 75 Minutes · 12 февруари 2018 г. · 1 т.*

## Условие

Which planets have primary atmospheres?

A. Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune  
B. Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars  
C. Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn  
D. Venus and Earth  
E. Mercury and Mars

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: A

Primary atmospheres are formed of accreted gaseous material during the formation of the solar system.


</Spoiler>
