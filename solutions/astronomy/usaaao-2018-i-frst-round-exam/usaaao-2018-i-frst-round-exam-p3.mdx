*USAAAO National Astronomy Olympiad First Round 02/12/2018-02/16/2018 Time Limit: 75 Minutes · 12 февруари 2018 г. · 1 т.*

## Условие

What is the peak wavelength in Angstroms ($\mathrm{\AA}$) of electromagnetic radiation emitted by a star at a temperature of 10,000 K, assuming a blackbody spectrum? (Wien's constant $\sigma_w \approx 2.9*10^7\ \mathrm{\AA*K}$)

A. 290 $\mathrm{\AA}$  
B. 2,900 $\mathrm{\AA}$  
C. 5,800 $\mathrm{\AA}$  
D. 29,000 $\mathrm{\AA}$  
E. 58,000 $\mathrm{\AA}$

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: B

$$
\lambda[\mathring{A}] = \frac{2.9\times 10^{7}}{T[K]}
$$


</Spoiler>
