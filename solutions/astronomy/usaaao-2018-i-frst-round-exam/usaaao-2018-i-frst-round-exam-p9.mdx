## Условие

What are the bright regions on the solar photosphere called?

A. Sunspots  
B. Limbs  
C. Faculae  
D. Prominences  
E. Flares

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: C

Sunspots are the dark regions, faculae the bright regions.


</Spoiler>
