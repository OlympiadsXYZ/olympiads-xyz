## Условие

In the 1920s Harlow Shapely estimated the size of the Milky Way galaxy using which of the following?

A. RR Lyrae stars
B. Pulsating asymptotic giant branch stars
C. Type Ia supernovae
D. Type II supernovae
E. white dwarf stars

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: A

Shapely used RR Lyrae stars to estimate the size of the Milky Way galaxy.


</Spoiler>
