## Условие

As is known, the Republic of Lithuania (see map) uses zone with winter time UT+02 and summer time UT+03. Calculate and draw a conclusion about the following:

**5.1.** Are there any places in Lithuania, where today (September 8, 2013) the Sun will be exactly in the south at a time when the watches of residents will show just 12:00? (**«да-yes»** or **«нет-no»**).

**5.2.** And in general, on the other days of the year, are there such places? (**«да-yes»** or **«нет-no»**). If "yes", then calculate in what dates, if "no", then justify it by calculations.
