## Условие

An observer in Moletai recorded that a star culminated at 02:54 and set at 05:45 on September 8, 2013. Effects of irregularities of the horizon should not be taken into account.

**1.1.** At what time will the star rise on September 9, 2013?

**1.2.** In approximately which direction do you need to wait for the rising of the star? Choose one of the alternatives: N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW. Draw a picture with an explanation.
