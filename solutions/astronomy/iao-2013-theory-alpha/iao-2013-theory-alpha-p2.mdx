## Условие

This celestial body in the system of the star Gliese 581 is the most Earth-like planet found outside the Solar System, and the exoplanet with the greatest recognized potential for harboring albuminous based life.

Estimate orbital period $\tau$ of Gliese 581 g. Consider the orbit to be circular.
