*USAAAO 2022 - First Round · 5 февруари 2022 г.*

## Условие

The resolution of a space telescope is theoretically limited by diffraction from its primary mirror. In this problem, we will compare the diffraction limit of the Hubble Space Telescope (HST) (primary mirror diameter $d = 2.4\,\mathrm{m}$) and the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) ($d = 6.5\,\mathrm{m}$). The operating wavelengths for the two telescopes are $500\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $10\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ respectively. Calculate the ratio of the diffraction limited angular resolution $\frac{\theta(\mathrm{HST})}{\theta(\mathrm{JWST})}$. Which telescope can resolve smaller angular features if limited only by diffraction?

(a) 0.014, JWST

(b) 0.14, HST

(c) 1.4, JWST

(d) 14, HST

(e) 140, JWST

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- B

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The diffraction limit is given as $\theta = \frac{1.22\lambda}{d}$. Using the given values, we get the answer as $\frac{\theta(\mathrm{HST})}{\theta(\mathrm{JWST})} \approx 0.14$. Hence, HST can resolve smaller angular features.

**Answer: B**


</Spoiler>
