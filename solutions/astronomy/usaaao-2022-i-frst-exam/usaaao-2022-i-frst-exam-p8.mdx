*USAAAO 2022 - First Round · 5 февруари 2022 г.*

## Условие

An exoplanet was observed during its transit across the surface of a bright star. Estimate the variation of the apparent magnitude ($\Delta$m) of the star caused by exoplanet’s transit. During the transit, assume an Earth-based astronomer observes that the area covered by the exoplanet on the projected surface of the star represents $\eta = 2\%$ of the star’s projected surface.

(a) -4.247

(b) 0.003

(c) 0.022

(d) 0.679

(e) -0.003

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- C

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Using Pogson’s formula for apparent magnitudes:

$$
\log\frac{F}{F_0} = -0.4(m - m_0)
$$

$$
F = (1 - \eta)F_0
$$

$$
\log(1 - \eta) = -0.4\Delta m
$$

$$
\Delta m = -2.5\log(0.98)
$$

$$
\Delta m = 0.022
$$

**Answer: C**


</Spoiler>
