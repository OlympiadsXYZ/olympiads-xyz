*USAAAO 2022 - First Round · 5 февруари 2022 г.*

## Условие

An astronomer observes a galaxy in very foggy weather. So far, she has an image of signal-to-noise ratio of approximately 1, imaging for about 5 seconds. if she wants to reach a signal-to-noise ratio of 10. How long, in total, must she observe the galaxy for?

(a) 15 seconds

(b) 20 seconds

(c) 25 seconds

(d) 50 seconds

(e) 500 seconds

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- E

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Signal-to-noise ratio scales with the square root of time, so she would need 100 times more time to reach a 10 times greater signal-to-noise ratio. Hence, the answer is 500 seconds.

**Answer: E**


</Spoiler>
