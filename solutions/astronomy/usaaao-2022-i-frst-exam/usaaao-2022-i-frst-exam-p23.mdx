*USAAAO 2022 - First Round · 5 февруари 2022 г.*

## Условие

An exoplanet discovered by the radial velocity method is found to have an orbital period of 2.45 days around a Sun-like star. Assuming the planet has zero albedo (i.e., absorbs all incoming starlight) and perfectly transports heat across its surface, estimate the temperature at the photosphere of the planet.

(a) 395 K

(b) 954 K

(c) 1231 K

(d) 1476 K

(e) 2071 K

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- D

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

This is the equilibrium temperature assuming zero albedo and full redistribution.

$L_{\star}/(4\pi d^2)\pi R_p^2 = 4\pi R_p^2\sigma T_p^4$.

First, solve for d using Kepler’s third law, assuming the planet’s mass is much smaller than the Sun:

$d(au)^3 = T(year)^2$

$d = 0.036AU$.

Solve for $T_p$ in the above expression:

$T_p = \left(\frac{L}{16\sigma\pi d^2}\right)^{1/4}$.

Plug in for $d$ and $L = 3.827 \times 10^{26}$ Watts to find the temperature:

$T_p = 1476K$

**Answer: D**


</Spoiler>
