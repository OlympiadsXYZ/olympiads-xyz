*USAAAO 2022 - First Round · 5 февруари 2022 г.*

## Условие

An astronomer took the following picture while observing the night sky:

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '91.8%', '--fig-max': '588px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usaaao-2022-i-frst-exam/p11-fig1.png" alt="Simulated wide-field view of a night sky with scattered stars and a faint band of the Milky Way at upper left; a green curved line near the bottom is labelled 'Horizon'." width="1836" height="941" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

What is the latitude of the place where the astronomer took the picture?

(a) $70^{\circ}$ S

(b) $20^{\circ}$ S

(c) $2^{\circ}$ N

(d) $20^{\circ}$ N

(e) $70^{\circ}$ N

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- D

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '76.6%', '--fig-max': '491px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usaaao-2022-i-frst-exam/p11-sol-fig1.png" alt="The same simulated night-sky view as in the statement, with the green curved horizon line labelled 'Horizon' at the bottom; a star near the centre is circled in red and labelled 'Polaris'." width="1534" height="790" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

The latitude of the observer corresponds to the altitude of the celestial pole above the horizon. The North Celestial Pole is very close to Polaris ($\alpha$ UMi).

Since the North Celestial Pole is above the horizon, the observer is in the Northern hemisphere.

Polaris is clearly much higher than $2^{\circ}$ above the horizon, but lower than $70^{\circ}$, so the only possible answer is $20^{\circ}$ N.

**Answer: D**


</Spoiler>
