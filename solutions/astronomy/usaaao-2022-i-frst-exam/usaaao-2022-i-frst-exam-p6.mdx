*USAAAO 2022 - First Round · 5 февруари 2022 г.*

## Условие

Consider a f/9 telescope with focal length $f = 1.0$ m that operates at visible wavelength $\lambda =$ 5000 Å. What is the farthest distance at which an open cluster of radius $R_C = 4.1$ pc can be resolved by this telescope?

(a) $1.2 \times 10^6$ pc

(b) $1.5 \times 10^6$ pc

(c) $3.0 \times 10^6$ pc

(d) $4.2 \times 10^6$ pc

(e) $5.8 \times 10^6$ pc

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- B

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

First, note that the angular diameter of the cluster is

$$
\theta = \frac{2R_C}{d}
$$

where $d$ is the distance to it.

Equating this angular diameter to the resolution limit of the telescope, we find that:

$$
1.22\frac{\lambda}{D} = \frac{2R_C}{d}
$$

$$
d = \frac{2R_CD}{1.22\lambda}
$$

$$
= \frac{2R_Cf}{9 * 1.22\lambda}
$$

$$
\approx 1.5 \times 10^6\ \mathrm{pc}
$$

**Answer: B**


</Spoiler>
