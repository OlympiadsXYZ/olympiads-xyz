*USAAAO 2022 - First Round · 5 февруари 2022 г.*

## Условие

An astronomer observes an eclipsing binary star system from Earth, and he plots the following light curve.

Suppose that both stars have circular orbits and the distance between the stars is 14.8 AU. What is the total mass of the binary star system in terms of solar masses?

(a) $2.3M_{\odot}$

(b) $5.7M_{\odot}$

(c) $6.8M_{\odot}$

(d) $23M_{\odot}$

(e) $46M_{\odot}$

## Отговори

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The time difference between the primary and secondary eclipses is 4.2 years. These eclipses when one star covers the other. Thus, we observe that the period of orbit is twice this, or 8.4 years. The semimajor axis is 14.8 AU, so by Kepler’s Third Law, we have

$$
T^2 = \frac{a^3}{M} \implies M = \frac{a^3}{T^2} = \frac{14.8^3}{8.4^2} = 46\,M_{\odot}.
$$

**Answer: E**


</Spoiler>
