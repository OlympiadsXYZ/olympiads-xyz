*USAAAO 2022 - First Round · 5 февруари 2022 г.*

## Условие

A star with mass $M$ goes through an energy generating nuclear reaction $4\ {}^1H \rightarrow\ {}^4He + Energy$. Here, the burning efficiency of the p-p(proton-proton) chain is 0.007, meaning that each mass $m$ yields $0.007mc^2$ of energy. Assuming that the has a total available hydrogen mass for nuclear reaction amounts to half of its original mass, and the luminosity($L$) stays constant throughout the burning phase, get an expression of the hydrogen burning lifetime of the star.

(a) $1.625 \times 10^{18}s(\frac{M}{M_{\odot}})^{-2}$

(b) $3.15 \times 10^{14}s(\frac{M}{M_{\odot}})^{-2}$

(c) $1.625 \times 10^{18}s(\frac{M}{M_{\odot}})^{2}$

(d) $3.15 \times 10^{14}s(\frac{M}{M_{\odot}})^{2}$

(e) None of the above

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- A

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Total amount of energy available for burning is

$$
0.007(0.5M)c^2.
$$

Since $L$ remains constant, the burning lifetime is

$$
\frac{0.0035Mc^2}{L}
$$

Now using the mass-luminosity relationship

$$
\frac{L}{L_{\odot}} = (\frac{M}{M_{\odot}})^3
$$

we obtain

$$
\frac{0.0035Mc^2}{L} = 0.0035c^2\frac{(M_{\odot})^3}{L_{\odot}M^2}
$$

$$
= 0.0035c^2(\frac{M_{\odot}}{L_{\odot}})(\frac{M}{M_{\odot}})^{-2}
$$

$$
= 1.625 \times 10^{18}s(\frac{M}{M_{\odot}})^{-2}
$$

**Answer: A**


</Spoiler>
