*USAAAO 2022 - First Round · 5 февруари 2022 г.*

## Условие

Consider the following elliptical orbit of a comet around a star:

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '83.6%', '--fig-max': '536px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usaaao-2022-i-frst-exam/p3-fig1.png" alt="An ellipse with its major axis drawn horizontally and its minor axis vertically; a yellow disc labelled 'Star' lies on the major axis left of the centre (at a focus); blue points B at the top and A at the bottom end of the minor axis." width="1674" height="941" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

Which of the following expressions corresponds to the time that the comet takes to go from point A to point B as a function of the period of the comet ($T$) and the eccentricity of the orbit ($e$)?

Assume that the direction of the orbit is **counterclockwise**.

(a) $\frac{T}{2}$

(b) $\left(\frac{e}{\pi} + \frac{1}{2}\right) * T$

(c) $\left(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{e}{\pi}\right) * T$

(d) $(1 + e) * \frac{T}{2}$

(e) $\frac{T * e}{2}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- B

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

When the comet goes from point A to point B, it sweeps out the area marked in red in the the following figure:

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '83.5%', '--fig-max': '535px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usaaao-2022-i-frst-exam/p3-sol-fig1.png" alt="The same ellipse with the star at the left focus and points B (top) and A (bottom) at the ends of the minor axis; the region swept from A to B counterclockwise is filled red: the triangle star–A–B plus the right half of the ellipse." width="1672" height="938" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

Using Kepler’s Second Law:

$$
\frac{\Delta t}{T} = \frac{A_{swept}}{A_{total}}
$$

$$
\frac{\Delta t}{T} = \frac{2 * \frac{aeb}{2} + \frac{\pi ab}{2}}{\pi ab}
$$

$$
\frac{\Delta t}{T} = \frac{aeb + \frac{\pi ab}{2}}{\pi ab}
$$

$$
\frac{\Delta t}{T} = \frac{e}{\pi} + \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\Delta t = \left(\frac{e}{\pi} + \frac{1}{2}\right) * T
$$

**Note:** In the calculations, the semi-major axis was denoted by $a$ and the semi-minor axis was denoted by $b$.

**Answer: B**


</Spoiler>
