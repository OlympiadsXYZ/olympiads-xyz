*USAAAO 2022 - First Round · 5 февруари 2022 г.*

## Условие

Dubhe (declination $\delta = 61.75\,{}^{\circ}$) is a star in the constellation of Ursa Major. Is it circumpolar from the city of San Francisco (latitude $\lambda = 37.7\,{}^{\circ}$ N)? How about from the city of Miami (latitude $\lambda = 25.8\,{}^{\circ}$ N)?

(a) Yes, Yes

(b) Yes, No

(c) No, Yes

(d) No, No

(e) Need more information

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- B

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

A star is circumpolar in the northern hemisphere if $\delta > 90 - \lambda$.

**Answer: B**


</Spoiler>
