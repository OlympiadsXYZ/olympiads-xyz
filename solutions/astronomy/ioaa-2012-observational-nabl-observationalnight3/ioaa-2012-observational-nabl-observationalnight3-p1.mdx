*6th International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics — OBSERVATIONAL ROUND – 3RD NIGHT · 4 август 2012 г.*

<details>
<summary>General instructions — Observational Exam</summary>

- The duration of the exam is 20 min for each student.
- There will be a copy of these instructions inside the envelope with the exam that will be given to you.
- The exam is individual, and **no conversation** with anybody except with the guide who will be helping you with the telescope is allowed.
- The necessary sky charts are included in the exam that will be handed to you.
- In questions that involve pointing the telescope, the student can ask help of assistant at any time, at the cost of not getting the full marks for that question.
- **Material provided for each student:** red flashlight, laser pointer, pencil, pen, eraser and clipboard.
- **WRITE YOUR ANSWERS IN INK.** The organization will give you pens.

</details>

This exam consist of 3 tables and 4 full sky charts in Equatorial coordinates, showing the sky for the four seasons. On those charts different objects are marked in different colours: constellations are noted in blue capital letters; stars are noted in red arabic algarisms and deep sky objects are noted in green small caps. The charts contain 20 stars, 20 constellations and 10 deep sky objects to be marked on tables I, II and III. Your task is correctly address the objects in the tables.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

On those charts some objects are marked in capital letters for constellations; arabic numerals for stars and small letters for deep sky objects. There are 20 stars, 20 constellations and 10 deep sky objects to be marked on tables I, II and III.
