*6th International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics — OBSERVATIONAL ROUND – 3RD NIGHT · 4 август 2012 г.*

## Условие

This exam consist of 3 tables and 4 full sky charts in Equatorial coordinates, showing the sky for the four seasons. On those charts different objects are marked in different colours: constellations are noted in blue capital letters; stars are noted in red arabic algarisms and deep sky objects are noted in green small caps. The charts contain 20 stars, 20 constellations and 10 deep sky objects to be marked on tables I, II and III. Your task is to correctly address the objects in the tables.
