## Условие

The diagram **(Fig.3)** shows how the altitude of three stars above the horizon changes during a night near the winter solstice as observed from the Rozhen observatory (41°41′N, 24°44′E). The one-digit numbers on the chart are the numbers of the stars, while the two-digit numbers are angular distances from the moon in degrees.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">
