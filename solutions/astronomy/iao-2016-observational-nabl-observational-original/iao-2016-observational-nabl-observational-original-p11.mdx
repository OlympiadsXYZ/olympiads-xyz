**11.1.** Which quasar is on the image? Identify the field and circle the correct answer.

*Answer:* *3C 66A / OJ 287 / 3C 454.3 / PKS 1510-08* **[1 т.]**

**11.2.** What changes have to be made in the equatorial coordinates of the telescope in order to put the quasar in the center of the field?

*Answer:* $\Delta\alpha =$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ $''$ $\Delta\delta =$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ $''$ **[2 т.]**

**11.3.** Approximate distances to some field stars are indicated on the charts in parsecs. Estimate roughly the distance to the quasar:

*Answer:* $d =$ \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ *pc* **[1 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **11.1.** OJ 287
- **11.2.** Δα = -91″, Δδ = +48″ (the signs matter!)
- **11.3.** d = 100 Mpc – 15 Gpc for full points (the actual quasar is at z=0.306, ~1 Gpc)

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**11.1.** Which quasar is on the image? Identify the field and circle the correct answer.

*Answer:* *OJ 287* *1 pt*

**11.2.** What changes have to be made in the equatorial coordinates of the telescope in order to put the quasar in the center of the field?

*Answer:* $\Delta\alpha = -91''$ $\Delta\delta = +48''$ *(the signs matter!)* *2 pts*

**11.3.** Approximate distances to some field stars are indicated on the charts in parsecs. Estimate roughly the distance to the quasar:

*0 pts:* $d < 1$ *Mpc or* $d > 15$ *Gpc*

*0.5 pts:* $d = 1 – 99$ *Mpc*

*1 pt:* $d = 100$ *Mpc – 15 Gpc*

*A trick question. The actual quasar is at z=0.306, corresponding to a distance of ~1 Gpc, which cannot be derived from the charts. The nearest active quasar known is at a distance of ~180 Mpc.*


</Spoiler>
