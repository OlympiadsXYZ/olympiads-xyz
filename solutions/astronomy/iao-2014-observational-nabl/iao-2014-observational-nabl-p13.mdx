*XIX International Astronomy Olympiad. Observational round. Questions. Clear sky · 12 октомври 2014 г.*

## Условие

Point your telescope to the binary star ***π Pegasus*** using 10 mm eyepiece and ***star map*** as a guide. Estimate the distance between the stars in arc minutes.
