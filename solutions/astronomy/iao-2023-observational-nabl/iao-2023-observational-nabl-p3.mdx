*XXVII International Astronomy Olympiad — Observational round*

<details>
<summary>Observational round. Outdoor — Instructions</summary>

1. This part of the observational round consists of 2 questions (#9 and #10).

2. Your task is to observe several glowing screens (smartphones, tablets, LED panels, etc.) at a far distance.

3. You have 6 minutes to work on #9, and 10 minutes on #10. You will hear signals for: #9 beginning, #9 ending, #10 beginning and #10 ending.

4. Please do not touch the telescope before the beginning signals and after the ending signals. Please hand over your first answer sheet to telescope assistant instantly after #9 ends.

5. Please use the telescope safely and correctly. Telescope assistant might stop your dangerous action. Any kind of misoperation, including disobedience on the rules or incorrect operation on telescope, might cause a negative effect on your score.

</details>

## Условие

The middle screen shows an image of the same deep-sky object that you observed in #9, but on a different night. Also, a new star appeared on the screen.

Draw this deep-sky object in the circle on the answer sheet, assuming that the circle is the field of view of your telescope. Mark the new star with the symbol ‘×’.
