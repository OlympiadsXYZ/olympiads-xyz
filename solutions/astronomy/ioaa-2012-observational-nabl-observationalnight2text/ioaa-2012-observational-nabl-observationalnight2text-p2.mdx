Star charts 4–6 are supplied from the corresponding Bulgarian observational packet. Its filename is English, but its task sheet and captions are Bulgarian. The original English text-only edition and its inline key are retained: task2 gives only a)1.5 and b)0.5 without explaining these values; no missing drawing is invented. ## Условие

Point to $\nu$ Scorpii (***star chart-4***). If you are not able to point at the star you can ask the instructor to do it for you but you will not get points for this. (3 min)

Use 2x barlow + 10mm eyepiece to draw what you see through the eyepiece. You don´t need to mark North . (2 min)
