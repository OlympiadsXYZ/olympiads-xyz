## Условие

The Triangulum Galaxy has coordinates (RA: 1.564h, Dec: 30.660°). Which answer is closest to the local time at which it reaches its maximum elevation above the horizon on the night of September 18-19? Ignore daylight savings.

**a.** 11:54 pm

**b.** 12:21 am

**c.** 12:48 am

**d.** 1:15 am

**e.** 1:42 am

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- e

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: e


</Spoiler>
