## Условие

Jupiter has a mass of $1.9 \times 10^{27}$ kg and a semimajor axis of 5.2 AU. Assume someone "out there" can resolve star positions to 10 microarcseconds. What is the maximum distance from the Sun at which the planet Jupiter could be discovered by observing the wobble of the Sun?

**a.** 5 pc

**b.** 15 pc

**c.** 50 pc

**d.** 150 pc

**e.** 500 pc

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- e

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: e


</Spoiler>
