## Условие

In a distant solar system, two planets, A and B, orbit a central main sequence star, like our sun. A day on Planet A lasts 20 Earth hours, and there are 240 such days in a year on Planet A. Likewise, a day on Planet B lasts 38 hours, and there are 220 such days in a year on Planet B. What is the synodic period with respect to these two planets, in Earth years?

**a.** 1.1

**b.** 1.2

**c.** 1.3

**d.** 1.4

**e.** 1.5

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- c

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: c


</Spoiler>
