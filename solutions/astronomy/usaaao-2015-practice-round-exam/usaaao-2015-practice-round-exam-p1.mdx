## Условие

Light from distant galaxies appears to be:

**a.** Shifted to longer wavelengths

**b.** Shifted to shorter wavelengths

**c.** Increased in energy

**d.** Increased in intensity

**e.** Completely polarized

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- a

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: a


</Spoiler>
