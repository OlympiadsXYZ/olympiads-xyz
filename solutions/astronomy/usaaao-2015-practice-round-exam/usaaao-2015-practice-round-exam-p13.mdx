## Условие

Fifteen minutes after sunset, you look west and see the moon rising. What phase is the moon in?

**a.** New

**b.** First Quarter

**c.** Waxing Gibbous

**d.** Full

**e.** Waning Crescent

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- d

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: d


</Spoiler>
