## Условие

Which of the following Messier objects is grouped incorrectly with its type and constellation?

**a.** M4, globular cluster, Scorpius

**b.** M27, spiral galaxy, Sagittarius

**c.** M42, emission nebula, Orion

**d.** M57, planetary nebula, Lyra

**e.** M87, elliptical galaxy, Virgo

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- b

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: b


</Spoiler>
