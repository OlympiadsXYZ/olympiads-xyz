## Условие

Star X has an apparent magnitude of 5.7 and is located 26.3 pc from the Sun. If there are 2.0 magnitudes of extinction to Star X, what its luminosity?

**a.** 1.6 solar luminosities

**b.** 8.0 solar luminosities

**c.** 9.8 solar luminosities

**d.** 19.6 solar luminosities

**e.** 25.6 solar luminosities

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- d

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: d


</Spoiler>
