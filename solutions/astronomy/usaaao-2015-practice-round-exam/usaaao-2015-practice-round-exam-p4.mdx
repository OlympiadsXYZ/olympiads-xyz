## Условие

The latitude and longitude of LA are 34.05 degrees N and 118.25 degrees W, respectively. A star at which of the following declinations never rises over the horizon in LA?

**a.** 60 degrees

**b.** 45 degrees

**c.** 0 degrees

**d.** -45 degrees

**e.** -60 degrees

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- e

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: e


</Spoiler>
