## Условие

The peak wavelength of radiation from a certain star is 644 nm. If the absolute magnitude of this star is -0.17, what is its radius?

**a.** 13.4 solar radii

**b.** 16.5 solar radii

**c.** 17.8 solar radii

**d.** 19.8 solar radii

**e.** 20.5 solar radii

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- b

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: b


</Spoiler>
