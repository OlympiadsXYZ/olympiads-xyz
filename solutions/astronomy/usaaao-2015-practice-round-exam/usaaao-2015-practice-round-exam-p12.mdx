## Условие

What size telescope is needed to resolve Hellas Planitia (a 2,300 km wide crater) on Mars through a v-band filter (effective wavelength = 550 nm) when Mars is 54.6 million km from Earth?

**a.** 34.8 mm

**b.** 26.1 mm

**c.** 15.9 mm

**d.** 10.6 mm

**e.** 7.7 mm

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- c

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: c


</Spoiler>
