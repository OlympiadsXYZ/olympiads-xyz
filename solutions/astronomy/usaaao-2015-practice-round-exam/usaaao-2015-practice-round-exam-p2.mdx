## Условие

Given a star of 70 solar masses and 20 solar radii, what type of star is this?

**a.** Main Sequence

**b.** Giant

**c.** Dwarf

**d.** Neutron Star

**e.** Supergiant

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- e

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: e


</Spoiler>
