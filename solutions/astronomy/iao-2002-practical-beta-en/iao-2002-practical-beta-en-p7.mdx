Look at the maps of the nearby Universe. A circle within radius of 5 Mpc is clipped out from the "pancake" of Local supercluster. Fig.1 demonstrates a view in its plane (top view). Fig.2 demonstrates a view in its edge (front view). Each point is a single galaxy. Majority of galaxies are dwarfs, mostly detected and investigated in the Special Astrophysical observatory by I.D.Karachentsev with colleagues. One can separate several group in this supercluster: Local group including our Galaxy Milky Way (MW) and M31 and M33 galaxies, a cluster of galaxies in Centaurus and M81 with environment. A Local Void is visible on the upper part of Fig.2, where no galaxy was detected.

### Tasks

- Using the tabular data, plot the profile $V_r$ vs $(d)$ on the cross-bar paper.
- By fitting the function on the straight line, find the Hubble's constant from the law of Hubble.
- If your profile includes systematic deviation of points from the line (take into account the intervals of $d$ from 0 up to 1 Mpc and from 2.5 up to 4.5 Mpc), please, explain them.

</ProblemSection>
