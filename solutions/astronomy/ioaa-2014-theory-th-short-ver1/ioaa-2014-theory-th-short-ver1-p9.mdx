## Условие

One particle of star dust is in static equilibrium at a certain distance from Sun. Assuming that the particle is spherical and its density is $\rho$, calculate the diameter of the particle.

The following assumption may be useful for solving the problem:

The pressure of electromagnetic radiation is equal with the volume density of the electromagnetic radiations

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- $d = \frac{3}{2}\cdot\frac{\sigma}{\rho K}\cdot\frac{T_S^4R_S^2}{M_S}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

***Problem 9. Marking scheme .  Pressure of light***

- Correct use of the formula (1) for the pressure of light — 3 points
- Correct identify of the equilibrium condition — 3 points
- Correct solution and reasoning — 4 points

The pressure of the emitted radiation is

$$
p_{rad} = w = \frac{\phi_{planet,D}}{c}
$$

$$
p_{rad} = \frac{\sigma T_{planet}^4R_{planet}^2}{cD^2} \qquad (1)
$$

As seen in the below image, the pressure due to the solar radiation is effectively acting on an equivalent plane disc with the diameter *d* of the spherical star dust particle

<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ioaa-2014-theory-th-short-ver1/p9-sol-fig1.png" alt="Two sketches. Top: the Sun (SUN) emitting radiation P_emis toward a spherical dust particle of diameter d, with the gravitational force F_g pointing toward the Sun and the radiation force F_rad,S pointing away; a dashed arrow labelled p_absorbed. Bottom: the Sun and the equivalent flat disc of diameter d on which the radiation pressure p_rad,S acts, with the force F_rad,S." width="1191" height="654" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<figcaption>Fig.12 b</figcaption>
</figure>

Thus the force acting by the Sun radiation on the star-dust particle is:

$$
F_{rad,S} = p_{rad,S}\cdot\pi r^2 = p_{rad,S}\cdot\frac{\pi d^2}{4} = \frac{\sigma T_S^4R_S^2}{cL_S^2}\cdot\frac{\pi d^2}{4},
$$

The equilibrium condition is

$$
F_{rad,S} = F_g,
$$

Where  $F_g$  is the gravitational attraction force between  Sun and the star-dust particle.

$$
\frac{\sigma T_S^4R_S^2}{cD_S^2}\cdot\frac{\pi d^2}{4} = K\frac{mM_S}{D_S^2};
$$

$$
m = \rho V = \rho\frac{4\pi r^3}{3} = \rho\frac{4\pi}{3}\frac{d^3}{8} = \rho\frac{\pi d^3}{6};
$$

$$
\frac{\sigma T_S^4R_S^2}{cD_S^2}\cdot\frac{\pi d^2}{4} = K\rho\frac{\pi d^3}{6}\frac{M_S}{D_S^2};
$$

$$
d = \frac{3}{2}\cdot\frac{\sigma}{\rho K}\cdot\frac{T_S^4R_S^2}{M_S}.
$$


</Spoiler>
