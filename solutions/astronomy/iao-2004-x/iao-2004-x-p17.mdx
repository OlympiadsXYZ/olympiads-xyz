## Условие

Circumpolar region of the sky.

**1.1.** The latitude of the region where the Olympiad is taking place is 45° North. Contour by your hand the circumpolar region of the sky.

**1.2.** Indicate (by hand) the circumpolar constellations and say their Latin names.

(6p)
