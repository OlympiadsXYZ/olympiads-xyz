## Условие

Venus makes a central occultation of a star at the time of maximum elongation. Please find the duration of the occultation. The orbits of Venus and Earth may be considered as circular ones.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 9.8 min

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

At the time of Venus's maximum elongation the angular velocity of its sky motion is equal to the angular velocity of the Sun motion by the ecliptic:
$$
\omega = (2\pi/T) = 0.986^{\circ}/\mathrm{day}.
$$
The distance $l$ between Venus and Earth at the time of maximum elongation can be calculated using Pythagoras' theorem since the angle (Sun – Venus – Earth) is equal to 90°.
$$
l = (r_2^2 - r_1^2)^{1/2} = 103.4\ \mathrm{mill.\ km}.
$$
Here $r_1$ and $r_2$ are the radii of Venus and the Earth orbits, respectively. Let's denote Venus radius by $r$, then its angular diameter from this distance will be equal to
$$
d = 2r/l = 1.17\cdot10^{-4} = 24.1''.
$$
The duration of the central occultation will be
$$
t = d/\omega = rT/\pi(r_2^2 - r_1^2)^{1/2} = 9.8\ \mathrm{minutes}.
$$
Note: numerical values for the orbit radii of Earth and Venus, radius of Venus should be taken from the Solar System data.
