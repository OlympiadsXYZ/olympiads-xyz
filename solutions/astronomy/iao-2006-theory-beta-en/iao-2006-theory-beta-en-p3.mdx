## Условие

Find the boundaries of the region of the polar day and the region of polar night at the dates when the Sun may be in Zenith in Mumbai, HBCSE. The latitude and longitude of HBCSE are $\lambda_C=72^\circ56'$, $\varphi_C=19^\circ03'$. Take as many effects as possible into account.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

If the Sun is in zenith in any point, its declination is equal to the latitude of this point. Therefore those dates when the Sun can be in Bombay (HBCSE) in zenith, declination of the Sun is equal to $\delta=\varphi_C=19^\circ03'$. In this case (firstly refraction not taken into account) the center of the Sun during the current day never be under the horizon at northern latitudes above $(90^\circ-\delta)=(90^\circ-\varphi_C)$ and also never be above the horizon at latitudes to the south from $(-90^\circ+\delta)=(-90^\circ+\varphi_C)$. Taking into account refraction ($35'$ at horizon) and radius of the Sun ($16'$), it is possible to receive, that the top edge of the Sun during the current day never set under the horizon at northern latitudes above $(90^\circ-\varphi_C-51')$ and also never rise from the horizon at latitudes to the south from $(-90^\circ+\varphi_C-51')$. These latitudes are the real boundaries of polar day and polar night. For HBCSE $\varphi_C=19^\circ03'$ and the boundaries appear on $71^\circ06'$ N (polar day) and $72^\circ48'$ S (polar night).


</Spoiler>
