## Условие

A spherical molecular cloud has a radius of 5 pc and a mass of $2 \cdot 10^3\,\mathfrak{M}_\odot$. The temperature of the gas is 30 K, the cloud is homogeneous.

**a)** Estimate the mean free path and the mean free time (between successive collisions) of the hydrogen molecules in the cloud. The cross section radius of $\mathrm{H}_2$ is 2.7 Å.

**b)** Let a star pass behind the cloud along its apparent diameter with a tangential velocity of 70 km/s. For how long will the absorption be greater than $0.5^{\mathrm{m}}$? Absorption in the V band is related to the atomic column density by formula $A_V = 5.2 \cdot 10^{-22}\ N_H$ in magnitudes, where $N_H$ is measured in $\mathrm{cm}^{-2}$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a)** Mean free path ≈ 4·10¹⁰ m ≈ 0.3 au; mean free time ∼ 5·10⁷ s ∼ 1–2 yr
- **b)** 140000 yr

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

a) The mean free path of the molecules is $l_{\mathrm{H}_2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\pi d^2 n}$, where $\pi d^2$ is the collisional cross section of a molecule, $d = 2.7$ Å is the cross section radius. The $\sqrt{2}$ coefficient arises from taking into account the motion of molecules relative to each other. Note, however, that taking the latter into account does not critically affect the estimations of the values needed for the solution.

The concentration of the molecules in the cloud

$$n = \frac{N}{V} = \frac{M/m_{\mathrm{H}_2}}{\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3} = \frac{2 \cdot 10^3 \times 2 \cdot 10^{30}\ \mathrm{kg}/(2 \times 1.67 \cdot 10^{-27}\ \mathrm{kg})}{\frac{4}{3}\pi \times (5 \times 206\,265 \times 1.49 \cdot 10^{11}\ \mathrm{m})^3} \approx 8 \cdot 10^7\ \mathrm{m}^{-3} = 8 \cdot 10^1\ \mathrm{cm}^{-3}.$$

Here the mass of $\mathrm{H}_2$ is estimated as $2m_p$.

$$l_{\mathrm{H}_2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\pi \times \left(2.7 \cdot 10^{-10}\ \mathrm{m}\right)^2 \times 8 \cdot 10^7\ \mathrm{m}^{-3}} \approx 4 \cdot 10^{10}\ \mathrm{m} \approx 0.3\ \mathrm{au}.$$

The characteristic thermal velocity of the molecules is

$$v_t = \sqrt{\frac{3k_BT}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{3\mathfrak{R}T}{\mu}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 \times 8.31\ \mathrm{J/(mol \cdot K)} \times 30\ \mathrm{K}}{0.002\ \mathrm{kg/mol}}} \approx 6 \cdot 10^2\ \mathrm{m/s}.$$

We can also estimate the characteristic velocities associated with the gravitational field of the cloud. We may use the virial theorem assuming that the cloud is not collapsing nowadays ($2\langle E_k\rangle + \langle U\rangle = 0$), or calculate the circular velocity at the cloud boundary

$$v_g \sim \sqrt{\frac{G\mathfrak{M}}{R}} = \sqrt{\frac{G\mathfrak{M}_\odot}{a_\oplus} \cdot \frac{2 \cdot 10^3}{206\,265 \times 5}} = v_\oplus \times \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 10^3}{206\,265 \times 5}} = 30\ \mathrm{km/s} \times 0.044 \sim 1.3\ \mathrm{km/s}.$$

As a result, to estimate the mean free time we may take the value of velocity of $1.0 \div 1.5$ km/s. The mean free time is

$$t_{\mathrm{H}_2} = \frac{l_{\mathrm{H}_2}}{v_g} \sim \frac{0.3\ \mathrm{au}}{1\ \mathrm{km/s}} \sim 5 \cdot 10^7\ \mathrm{s} \sim 1 \div 2\ \mathrm{yr}.$$

b) We estimate the absorption in the direction of the cloud center, that is, the maximum possible absorption in the cloud:

$$\begin{aligned} \max A_V &= 5.2 \cdot 10^{-22}\ \mathrm{cm}^2 \cdot N_H = 5.2 \cdot 10^{-22}\ \mathrm{cm}^2 \cdot 2n \cdot 2R =\\ &= 5.2 \cdot 10^{-22}\ \mathrm{cm}^2 \times (2 \times 8 \cdot 10^1\ \mathrm{cm}^{-2}) \cdot (2 \times 5 \times 206\,265 \times 1.49 \cdot 10^{13}\ \mathrm{cm}) \approx 2.6^{\mathrm{m}}. \end{aligned}$$

The minimal path of light through the cloud for the absorption to be at least $0.5^{\mathrm{m}}$ is

$$AB = 2R \cdot \frac{0.5}{2.6} \approx 2\ \mathrm{pc}.$$

The star has to travel the distance

$$2CH = 2 \cdot \sqrt{5^2 - 0.95^2} \approx 10\ \mathrm{pc},$$

which is approximately the cloud’s diameter.

The time needed for that travel

$$\tau \approx \frac{2R}{v_s} = \frac{10 \times 206\,265 \times 1.49 \cdot 10^{11}\ \mathrm{km}}{70\ \mathrm{km/s}} = 4.4 \cdot 10^{12}\ \mathrm{s} \approx 1.4 \cdot 10^5\ \mathrm{yr}.$$

- Question (a)
    1. Concentration of the molecules — **2 pt. + 1 pt.** (formula + estimation)
    2. Mean free path — **2 pt. + 1 pt.**
    3. Characteristic velocity of molecules — **2 pt. + 1 pt.**

    9. Star’s travel time — **1 pt.**
