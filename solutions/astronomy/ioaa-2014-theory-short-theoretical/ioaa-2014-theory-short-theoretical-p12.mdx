## Условие

The cepheids are variable stars, whose luminosities vary due to stellar pulsations. The period of the oscillations of a cepheid star is:

$$
P=2\pi R\sqrt{\frac{R}{GM}},
$$

where: R – the mean radius of the cepheid; M – the mass of the cepheid (remains constant during oscillation), you can assume that the temperature is constant during the pulsation;

Express the mean absolute magnitude of the cepheid $M_{\mathrm{cep}}$, in the following form:

$$
M_{\mathrm{cep}}=-2.5^{\mathrm m}\cdot\log k-\left(\frac{10}{3}\right)^{\mathrm m}\cdot\log P,
$$

where P is the period of cepheid’s pulsation.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

***Problem 12. Marking scheme Apparent magnitude of the Moon***

$$
P = 2\pi R\sqrt{\frac{R}{GM}},
$$

rezults

$$
P^2 = \frac{4\pi^2R^3}{KM};\ R = \sqrt[3]{\frac{KMP^2}{4\pi^2}} = \left(\frac{KM}{4\pi^2}\right)^{1/3}\cdot P^{2/3};
$$

$$
R^2 = \left(\frac{KM}{4\pi^2}\right)^{2/3}\cdot P^{4/3}.
$$

**1p**

The absolute brightness is:

$$
L_{\mathrm{cef}} = \sigma T_{\mathrm{cef}}^4\cdot 4\pi R^2,
$$

**1.5p**

And the apparent brightness :

$$
E_{\mathrm{cef}} = \frac{L_{\mathrm{cef}}}{4\pi d_{\mathrm{P,cef}}^2} = \frac{\sigma T_{\mathrm{cef}}^4\cdot 4\pi R^2}{4\pi d_{\mathrm{P,cef}}^2},
$$

**1.5p**

$d_{\mathrm{P,cef}}$ is the distance between the observer on Erath and the cepheide

$$
E_{\mathrm{cef}} = \frac{\sigma T_{\mathrm{cef}}^4\cdot 4\pi\cdot\left(\frac{KM}{4\pi^2}\right)^{2/3}\cdot P^{4/3}}{4\pi d_{\mathrm{P,cef}}^2}.
$$

**1p**

Similarly for Sun

$$
E_{\mathrm{S}} = \frac{L_{\mathrm{S}}}{4\pi d_{\mathrm{PS}}^2} = \frac{\sigma T_{\mathrm{S}}^4\cdot 4\pi R_{\mathrm{S}}^2}{4\pi d_{\mathrm{PS}}^2}.
$$

**1p**

By using the Pogson formula:

$$
\log\frac{E_{\mathrm{cef}}}{E_{\mathrm{S}}} = -0{,}4\left(m_{\mathrm{cef}} - m_{\mathrm{S}}\right);
$$

**1p**

$$
M_{\mathrm{cef}} = M_{\mathrm{S}} - 5^{\mathrm{m}}\log\frac{\left|d_{\mathrm{P,cef}}\right|}{\left|d_{\mathrm{PS}}\right|} - 2{,}5\cdot\log\frac{E_{\mathrm{cef}}}{E_{\mathrm{S}}};
$$

**1p**

$$
\frac{T_{\mathrm{cef}}^4\cdot\left(\frac{KM}{4\pi^2}\right)^{3/2}\cdot d_{\mathrm{PS}}^2}{T_{\mathrm{S}}^4\cdot R_{\mathrm{S}}^2\cdot d_{\mathrm{P,cef}}^2} = k_1 = \mathrm{constant};
$$

**1p**

$$
M_{\mathrm{cef}} = M_{\mathrm{S}} - 5^{\mathrm{m}}\log\frac{\left|d_{\mathrm{P,cef}}\right|}{\left|d_{\mathrm{PS}}\right|} - 2{,}5\cdot\log k_1 - \frac{10}{3}\cdot\log P;
$$

$$
M_{\mathrm{S}} - 5^{\mathrm{m}}\log\frac{\left|d_{\mathrm{P,cef}}\right|}{\left|d_{\mathrm{PS}}\right|} - 2{,}5\cdot\log k_1 = -2{,}5\cdot\log k;
$$

$$
k = \mathrm{constant};
$$

$$
M_{\mathrm{cef}} = -2{,}5\cdot\log k - \frac{10}{3}\cdot\log P.
$$

**1p**


</Spoiler>
