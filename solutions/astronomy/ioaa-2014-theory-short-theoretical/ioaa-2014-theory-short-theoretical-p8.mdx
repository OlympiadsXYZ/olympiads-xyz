## Условие

A spherical space –ship orbits the Sun on a circular orbit, and spin around an axis of rotation that is perpendicular to the orbital plane of the space-ship. The temperature on the exterior surface of the ship is $T_N$. Assume the space -ship is a perfect black body and there is no activity inside it.

Find out the apparent magnitude of the Sun and the angular diameter of the Sun as seen by the astronaut on board of the space – ship. The following values are known:, $T_S$ - the effective temperature of the Sun; $R_S$ - the radius of the Sun; $d_0$ - the Earth –Sun distance; $m_0$ - apparent magnitude of Sun measured from Earth; $R_N$ - the radius of the space –ship.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- $\alpha=4(T_N/T_S)^2$, $m=m_0-5\lg(2d_0T_N^2/(R_ST_S^2))$.

  Result as printed in the supplied final official key.

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

***Problem 8. Marking scheme Space – ship orbiting the Sun***

According to the  Stefan – Boltzmann law,  the luminosity of the Sun is:

$$
L_{sun} = Q_{sun}\cdot 4\pi R_{\mathrm{Sun}}^2 = \sigma T_{\mathrm{Sun}}^4\cdot 4\pi R_{\mathrm{Sun}}^2,
$$

**1p**

At distance  $d$ from the Sun , where the space ship is the energy  which passes the unit of surface in an unit of time is:

$$
\phi_{\mathrm{Sun,d}} = \frac{L_{\mathrm{S}}}{4\pi d^2} = \frac{\sigma T_{\mathrm{S}}^4\cdot 4\pi R_{\mathrm{S}}^2}{4\pi d^2}.
$$

**1p**

The space ship receive through its entire surface, in the unit of time,  the energy:

$$
P_{received} = \frac{\sigma T_{\mathrm{Sun}}^4\cdot 4\pi R_{\mathrm{Sun}}^2}{4\pi d^2}\cdot\pi R_{ship}^2.
$$

Corresponding to its temperature, $T_{\mathrm{N}}$, according the Stefan -  Boltzmann law, the emitted energy by starship through its hole surface in the unit of time :

$$
P_{\mathrm{emis,N}} = \sigma T_{\mathrm{N}}^4\cdot 4\pi R_{\mathrm{N}}^2.
$$

**1p**

When the temperature stabilized at thermic equilibrium :

$$
P_{received,\mathrm{N}} = P_{\mathrm{emis,N}}
$$

$$
\frac{\sigma T_{\mathrm{S}}^4\cdot 4\pi R_{\mathrm{S}}^2}{4\pi d^2}\cdot\pi R_{\mathrm{N}}^2 = \sigma T_{\mathrm{N}}^4\cdot 4\pi R_{\mathrm{N}}^2
$$

**1p**

the distance of orbiting the Sun of the space ship is:

$$
d = \frac{T_{\mathrm{S}}^2R_{\mathrm{S}}}{2T_{\mathrm{N}}^2},
$$

**1p**

The angular diameter of the Sun as seen from the space ship :

$$
\alpha/2 = \frac{R_{\mathrm{S}}}{d}
$$

**1p**

$$
\alpha = \frac{2R_{\mathrm{S}}}{d} = 4\left(\frac{T_{\mathrm{N}}}{T_{\mathrm{S}}}\right)^2
$$

**1p**

According the Pogson formula written for Sun seen from Earth and space ship the following relation occurs:

$$
\lg\frac{E_{\mathrm{S,Nava}}}{E_{\mathrm{S,P}}} = -0{,}4\left(m - m_0\right)
$$

**1p**

$$
2\cdot\lg\left(\frac{d_0}{d}\right) = -0{,}4\left(m - m_0\right)
$$

**1p**

The apparent magnitude of the Sun as seen from the space ship

$$
m = m_0 - 5\cdot\lg\frac{2d_0T_{\mathrm{N}}^2}{R_{\mathrm{S}}T_{\mathrm{S}}^2}
$$

**1p**


</Spoiler>
