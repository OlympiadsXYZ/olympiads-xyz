## Условие

The *Lagrange* points are the five positions in an orbital configuration (assume circular orbits), where a small object is stationary relative to two big bodies, only gravitationally interacting with them- for example, an artificial satellite relative to Earth and Moon, or relative to Earth and Sun. In the **Figure 1** are sketched two possible locations of Lagrange points $L_3$ relative to relative to the Earth – Sun system. Find out which of the two locations $L_3^1$ and $L_3^2$ could be the real Lagrange point relative to the system Earth – Sun; show the reason for your answer with appropriate equations and calculate the difference between one AU and Sun - $L_3$ distance. You know the following data: the Earth - Sun distance $d_{ES}=14.96\cdot10^7\ \mathrm{km}$ and the Earth – Sun mass ratio $M_E/M_S=1/332946$.

\vec{F}_S + \vec{F}_P = \vec{F}_{cp} = m\vec{a}_{cp}

$$
F_S + F_P = ma_{cp} = m\omega^2\left(r_{PS} \pm w\right);
$$

2 points

The sign “+” for position $L_3'$ and „– “for $L_3''$

$$
K\frac{mM_S}{\left(r_{PS} \pm w\right)^2} + K\frac{mM_P}{\left(2r_{PS} \pm w\right)^2} = m\omega^2\left(r_{PS} \pm w\right);
$$

Using the assumption that $w \ll r_{PS}$

$$
\left(1 \pm \frac{w}{r_{PS}}\right)^{-2} \approx 1 \mp 2\frac{w}{r_{PS}} \qquad \left(1 \pm \frac{w}{2r_{PS}}\right)^{-2} \approx 1 \mp \frac{w}{r_{PS}};
$$

2points

The rotation speed

$$
\omega^2 = \frac{KM_S}{r_{PS}^3}
$$

The final relation

$$
w = \mp\frac{M_P r_{PS}}{\left(12M_S + M_P\right)}
$$

The value has to be positive, thus the $L_3''$ is the position of one Lagrange point ……2p
