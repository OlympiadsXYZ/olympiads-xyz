## Условие

The plot of the function $\log(L/L_S)=f(\log(M/M_S))$ for data collected from a number of stars is represented in figure 2. L and M are the luminosity and the mass of a star respectively and $L_S$ and $M_S$ the luminosity and the mass of the Sun respectively.

*Find an expression for the main sequence life-time* for a main sequence star from Hertzprung – Russell diagram, as a function of mass fraction converted to energy $\eta$ and mass ratio to the solar mass n, Use the following assumptions: the time spent by Sun in the same Main Sequence is $\tau_S$, for each star the mass fraction which changed into energy is $\eta$, the percent of the mass of Sun which changes into energy is $\eta_S$, the mass of each star is expressed as $n=M/M_S$ and assume that luminosity of the star remains constant, during its main sequence life time.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

***Problem 5. Marking scheme The life –time of a star from the main sequence***

**A.** The analysis of the graph :

The graph is linear:

$$
y = ax + b = ax;
$$

**1p**

From the graph it can be obtain the following data:

$$
\log\frac{L}{L_S} = a\cdot\log\frac{M}{M_S};
$$

**1p**

$$
a = \tan\alpha = \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{3{,}5}{1} = 3{,}5;
$$

**1p**

$$
L \sim M^{3{,}5}.
$$

**1p**

**1p**

The total energy of the star is:

$$
E = Mc^2,
$$

So the emitted energy due to the mass variation of the star is:

$$
\Delta E = c^2\Delta M,
$$

**1p**

According to the text

$$
\Delta M = \eta M;
$$

$$
\Delta E = c^2\eta M.
$$

**1p**

By using the definition of the luminosity :

$$
\frac{\Delta E}{\Delta t} = L;
$$

**1p**

$$
\Delta t = \tau;
$$

$$
\frac{c^2\eta M}{\tau} = L;
$$

$$
\tau = \frac{c^2\eta M}{L},\ \textbf{(2)}
$$

Which represents the life-time of the star.

**1p**

By using the results from the graph analysis

$$
L = \frac{L_S}{M_S^{3{,}5}}\cdot M^{3{,}5},
$$

Thus :

$$
\tau = \frac{c^2\eta M_S^{3{,}5}}{L_S}\cdot M^{-2{,}5}.
$$

**1p**

If use the same calculations for the Sun it can be obtain

$$
E_S = M_Sc^2;
$$

$$
\tau_S = \frac{c^2\eta_SM_S}{L_S},
$$

Which is the life-time of the Sun

$$
\tau = \frac{\eta}{\eta_S}\cdot\tau_S\cdot M_S^{2{,}5}\cdot M^{-2{,}5};
$$

$$
\frac{\tau}{\tau_S} = \frac{\eta}{\eta_S}\cdot\left(\frac{M}{M_S}\right)^{-2{,}5};\ M = nM_S;
$$

$$
\tau = \frac{\eta}{\eta_S}\left(n\right)^{-2{,}5}\tau_S.
$$
