## Условие

For an observer on Earth the pressure of the radiation emitted by Sun is $p_{\mathrm{rad},S}$ and the pressure of the radiations emitted by a star $\Sigma$ is $p_{\mathrm{rad},\Sigma}$.

Calculate the visual apparent magnitude of the star $\Sigma$ if the apparent visual magnitude of the Sun is $m_S$.

The following assumption may be useful for solving the problem:

Generally, the pressure of the electromagnetic radiation in vacuum is equal to the volume energy density of the electromagnetic radiation $\left(p_{\mathrm{rad}}=\frac{\Delta E}{\Delta V}\right)$.

The following data are known: $M_S$ - the mass of the Sun, $R_S$ - the radius of Sun, G - universal gravitational constant; $\sigma$ Stefan - Boltzmann’s constant; c – speed of light in vacuum.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- $m_\Sigma=m_S-2.5\log(p_{star}/p_{sun})$.

  Result as printed in the supplied final official key.

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

***Problem 7. Marking scheme. Pressure of light***

**2p**

Pogson law

$$
\log\frac{\Phi_{\Sigma,L_{E\Sigma}}}{\Phi_{S,L_{ES}}}=-0.4(m_\Sigma-m_S)
$$

$$
\Phi=\frac W{S\cdot t}.
$$

**2p**

$$
\left.\begin{aligned}Q_{planet}&=\frac{E_e}{S_{star}\cdot t}=\sigma T^4\\E_e&=P_{emis\,star}=L_{star}\end{aligned}\right\}\Rightarrow L_{star}=\sigma T^4\,4\pi R_{star}^2
$$

**2p**

At distance L the energy flux of the star

$$
\Phi_{star,L}=\frac{E_{star}}{S\cdot t}=2\frac{\sigma T^4R_{star}^2}{L^2}
$$

**2p** the pressure emitted by star

$$
p=\frac FS=\frac{E_e}{\Delta V}=\frac{\sigma T^4R_{star}^2}{cL^2}
$$

**2p**

$$
\log\frac{p_{star}}{p_{SUN}}=-0.4(m_\Sigma-m_S);
$$

$$
m_\Sigma=m_S-2.5\cdot\log\frac{p_{star}}{p_{sun}}.
$$


</Spoiler>
