## Условие

Figure 1 is a whole sky star chart of Yanqing, Beijing at 20:30 tonight (UTC+8) with the limit magnitude = $5^{\mathrm{m}}$ (m = magnitude). Four stars (about $1^{\mathrm{m}}$ - $3^{\mathrm{m}}$) and one planet (brighter than $2^{\mathrm{m}}$) are missing in this chart. In the chart, the distance from the centre is in proportion to zenith distance

**(1)** Draw a cross (X) on the location of each missing star and mark “T” on the chart, and draw a cross (X) on the location of the missing planet and mark “P” on the chart. **[20 т.]**

**(2)** Please mark the orientation of the star chart with “N” “E” “S” “W” at the edge of the star chart. **[5 т.]**

**(3)** On the chart, the celestial equator passes through many constellations. Please write down the name of any five of these constellations (IAU codes). **[10 т.]**

**(4)** Using the star chart, estimate the altitude of Aldebaran (α Tau), to the nearest degree. **[5 т.]**
