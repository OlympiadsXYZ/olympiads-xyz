<details>
<summary>Instructions</summary>

1. This part of the contest consists of 4 problems, is 1 hour long and is worth a total of 100 points.

2. Only blue pen should be used to fill in the answer boxes, draw and mark on the star chart.

3. You are not allowed to leave your working desk without permission. If you need any assistance (malfunctioning calculator, need to visit a restroom, etc.), please put up your hand to signal the supervisor.

4. The beginning and end of the competition will be indicated by a long sound signal.

5. Wait at your table until your envelope is collected. Once all envelopes are collected, your student guide will escort you out of the competition area.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Instructions</summary>

1. This part of the competition consists of 2 problems (O5 and O6), and is worth a total of 50 points.

2. You have 10 minutes to complete these problems.

3. Stop working once the timer expires.

4. At the end of the examination, hand over the test envelope to the supervisor at the station.

5. You will use a telescope to observe five red LED screens at a distance. The telescope is equipped with one eyepiece, but no finderscope. Some of these screens display the names of planets, some show Messier numbers, and some show equatorial coordinates.

</details>
