## Условие

**Day — Instructions**

1. This part of the contest consists of 4 problems, is 1 hour long and is worth a total of 100 points.

2. Only blue pen should be used to fill in the answer boxes, draw and mark on the star chart.

3. You are not allowed to leave your working desk without permission. If you need any assistance (malfunctioning calculator, need to visit a restroom, etc.), please put up your hand to signal the supervisor.

4. The beginning and end of the competition will be indicated by a long sound signal.

5. Wait at your table until your envelope is collected. Once all envelopes are collected, your student guide will escort you out of the competition area.

Figures 4.1 – 4.4 are four photos of Messier objects. For each of them, please write down the Messier catalogue number and name the constellation where it is located.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

<ProblemSection id="solution-official-key">

### Official day solution

M1 - Taurus (Tau), M104 - Virgo(Vir) or Corvus(Crv), M27 – Vulpecula (Vul), M42 (M43 is also acceptable) - Orion (Ori).

2.5 points for each Messier catalogue number, 2.5 points for each constellation name.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-suggested-correction">

### Suggestion by KOR — Day correction

M1 - Taurus (Tau), M104 - Virgo(Vir) not Corvus(Crv), M27 – Vulpecula (Vul), M42 (M43 is also acceptable) - Orion (Ori).

2.5 points for each Messier catalogue number, 2.5 points for each constellation name.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-correction-readme">

### Suggestion by KOR — Readme

There are two suggested correction for the Observation Problems.

1. Day – O4: M104 is in Virgo(Vir) not Corvus(Crv)
