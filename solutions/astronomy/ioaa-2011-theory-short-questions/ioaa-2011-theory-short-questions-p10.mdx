## Условие

10. Estimate the minimum energy a proton would need to penetrate the Earth's magnetosphere.
Assume that the initial penetration is perpendicular to a belt of constant magnetic field 30 $\mu$T  
and thickness 1.0 x $10^4$ km. Prepare the sketch of the particle trajectory. (Note that at such
high energies the momentum can be replaced by the expression $E/c$. Ignore any radiative
effects).
