## Условие

12. Due to the precession of the Earth's axis, the region of sky visible from a location with fixed
geographical coordinates changes with time. Is it possible that, at some point in time, Sirius
will not rise as seen from Krakow, while Canopus will rise and set? Assume that the Earth's
axis traces out a cone of angle $47^\circ$. Krakow is at latitude $50.1^\circ$ N; the current equatorial
coordinates (right ascension and declination) of these stars are:

Sirius ($\alpha$ CMa):  $6^{\mathrm h}\ 45^{\mathrm m},\ -16^\circ\ 43'$

Canopus ($\alpha$ Car):  $6^{\mathrm h}\ 24^{\mathrm m},\ -52^\circ\ 42'$
