*XI Asian-Pacific Astronomy Olympiad — Observational round. Clear sky · 13 ноември 2015 г.*

<details>
<summary>Observational round. Questions — Clear sky</summary>

During the tour you will approach to the examiner with “examination cards”. You will take randomly one examination card and show by hand the answers for questions of the problem 13 to the examiner. Be ready to answer. Other time is given for solution of the problem 14.

</details>

## Условие

<nav className="problem-sections-toc" aria-label="Части на задачата">

**В тази задача**

- [Examination card](#examination-card)

</nav>

Show by hand the directions and objects included in the examination card. You should show the directions to the objects in any case, even when they are located below the horizon or hidden from the observer by buildings, trees, etc.

***1/3 of a minute*** *is done to show each object or direction.*

<ProblemSection id="examination-card">

### Examination card

*[Transcription note: the supplied card prints the same four items side by side in English and Russian. Both columns are reproduced below.]*

| No. | English | Русский |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **North-West** direction | Направление на **северо-запад** |
| 2 | **Deneb** (α Cyg) | **Денеб** (α Cyg) |
| 3 | **Aldebaran** (α Tau) | **Альдебаран** (α Tau) |
| 4 | **Sirius** (α CMa) | **Сириус** (α CMa) |

</ProblemSection>
