## Условие

TRAPPIST-1d is a temperate exoplanet that orbits the ultra-cool M dwarf star TRAPPIST-1 with a semi-major axis of 0.022 AU. TRAPPIST-1 has a mass of 0.089 Solar masses and an effective temperature of 2511 K. Through transit timing variations induced by other planets in the TRAPPIST-1 system, TRAPPIST-1d is estimated to have a mass of 0.420 Earth masses. Assuming that TRAPPIST-1d has a circular orbit (which is a good approximation because the measured eccentricity is only 0.008), what is the radial velocity semi-amplitude of TRAPPIST-1 due to the orbital motion of TRAPPIST-1d, in m/s?

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 0.847 m/s

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Solution:** $M_p/M_\star = V_\star/V_p \rightarrow V_\star = M_pV_p/M_\star$. $V_p = 2\pi a/T$.  
$T = ([a(AU)]^3/[M_\star(M_{\mathrm{Sun}})])^{1/2} = (0.022AU^3/0.089M_{\mathrm{Sun}})^{1/2} \times 365.25\,days/year = 4.00$ days.

$$
V_p = \frac{2\pi(0.022AU \times 1.496 \times 10^{11}m}{4.00days \times 86400sec/day} = 59.8\mathrm{km/s}
$$

.

$$
V_\star = \frac{0.420 \times 5.97 \times 10^{24}kg \times 59.8 \times 10^{3}m/s}{0.089 \times 1.99 \times 10^{30}kg} = 0.847\ \mathrm{m/s}
$$

.

The radial velocity semi-amplitude of TRAPPIST-1 due to TRAPPIST-1d is 0.847 m/s.


</Spoiler>
