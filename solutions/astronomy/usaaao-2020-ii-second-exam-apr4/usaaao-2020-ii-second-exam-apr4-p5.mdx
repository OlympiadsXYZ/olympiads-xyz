## Условие

HD 209458b is a hot Jupiter exoplanet with a mass of 0.69 Jupiter masses. However, HD 209458b has an anomalous radius of 1.38 Jupiter radii that is inflated relative to Jupiter. Jupiter has an interior that is comprised of metallic hydrogen at pressures greater than 1 Mbar. Estimate the pressure, in Mbar, at the center of HD 209458b, and determine whether or not the interior of HD 209458b will also be comprised of metallic hydrogen.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- P<sub>c</sub> ≈ 1.57 Mbar (the estimate gives 3.15 Mbar; answers between 1 and 5 Mbar accepted); yes, the interior is metallic hydrogen

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Solution:** Estimate: $P_c \sim \rho gR \sim 3GM^2/(4\pi R^4)$. The actual solution (if a student knows the equation or can derive it) is $P_c = 3GM^2/(8\pi R^4)$.  
Plugging in, $P_c = 3 \times 6.67 \times 10^{-11}m^3/kg/s^2 \times (0.69 * 1.898 \times 10^{27}kg)^2/(8\pi(1.38 \times 69.91 \times 10^{6}\ \mathrm{m})^4 =$ 1.57 Mbar. Estimate would give twice the value (because of ignoring the integral, which is acceptable because no calculus is required), which is 3.15 Mbar.  
Accept answers between 1 and 5 Mbar. Yes, the interior of HD 209458b will be composed of metallic hydrogen.


</Spoiler>
