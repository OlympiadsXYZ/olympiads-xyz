## Условие

Solar wind consists of protons that fly with the speed of 300 km/s and they fill the space of interplanetary matter around Earth with 10 particles/cm$^{3}$. With what force is this “wind” hitting the Moon? Recall that mass of a proton is $m_p = 1.610^{-24}$ g. Radius of the Moon is $R_m$=1737 km.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 1.4 tons (force)

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: $F = 1.4$ tons.

From $2^{nd}$ Newton’s Law $F = a \cdot m = \frac{\Delta V}{\Delta t}m = \frac{\Delta(Vm)}{\Delta t}$, i.e. equals change of impulse per unit of time. We assume that the protons reaching to the Moon give its impulse without changing its mass. Let V be the wind speed and $\rho$ the density of particles. Then $\rho V$ particles hit per unit time per unit area of the Moon, giving impulse $m_p V \rho V$. Over the whole surface of the Moon we get $F = \pi R_m^2 \rho m_p V^2 = 1.4$ tons.


</Spoiler>
