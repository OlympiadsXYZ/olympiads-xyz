## Условие

On March 21st at true noon, length of the shadow of a vertical rod was equal to its height. On which geographic latitude did this happen?

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- φ = ±45° (both north and south count).

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: $\phi = \pm 45$.

$21^{st}$ of March is the day of spring equinox. Thus the Sun can have a height of $h = 90 - \phi$. Since the length of the shadow is equal to its height it means that height of the Sun was $45^{\circ}$. Thus geographic latitude of this place was $\phi = \pm 45^{\circ}$(both north and south count).


</Spoiler>
