## Условие

The Very Large Array radio interferometer ($\lambda$=1 m) has maximum baseline of D = 36.4 km. How large will an optical telescope have to be to achieve a similar angular resolution in visible light ($\lambda$=5500 A$^{\circ}$)?

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 20 mm

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: $20mm$

$$
\Theta[rad] = 1.22\frac{\lambda}{Diameter} = 1.22\frac{1}{36.4 \cdot 10^{3}} = 0.0000335rad
$$

$$
Diameter = 1.22\frac{\lambda}{\Theta} = 1.22\frac{550 \cdot 10^{-9}}{0.0000335} = 0.020m = 20mm
$$


</Spoiler>
