## Условие

In stars like the Sun, helium nuclei are formed by fusing hydrogen nuclei together in a process known as the proton-proton chain. One step of the proton-proton chain consists of a deuterium nucleus ($m_d = 2.01410$ u) fusing together with a hydrogen nucleus ($m_H = 1.00783$ u) to form a helium-3 nucleus ($m_{He} = 3.01603$ u), where $u = 1.6605 \times 10^{-27}$ kg. How much energy is released during this fusion reaction?

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 8.817e-13 J

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

It is necessary to calculate the mass defect. The missing mass has been converted into energy. From conservation of mass,

$$m_d + m_H = m_{He} + \Delta m.$$

Rearranging,

$$\Delta m = m_d + m_H - m_{He},$$

and plugging in the given masses,

$$\Delta m = (2.01410 + 1.00783 - 3.01603)\ \mathrm{u},$$

$$\Delta m = 0.0059\ \mathrm{u},$$

$$\Delta m = (0.0059\ \mathrm{u})\left(1.6605 \times 10^{-27}\ \frac{\mathrm{kg}}{\mathrm{u}}\right),$$

$$\Delta m = 9.797 \times 10^-30\ \mathrm{kg}.$$

Then to find the energy,

$$E = mc^2,$$

$$E = (9.797 \times 10^-30\ \mathrm{kg})\left(3.0 \times 10^{8}\ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}\right)^2,$$

$$E = 8.817 \times 10^{-13}\ \mathrm{J}.$$


</Spoiler>
