## Условие

The star Sualocin (RA: 20$^{\mathrm{h}}$ 39.6$^{\mathrm{m}}$, Dec: 15$^{\circ}$ 54.7’, absolute magnitude: -0.4) is about 78 pc away from our solar system, and the star Rotanev (RA: 20$^{\mathrm{h}}$ 37.5$^{\mathrm{m}}$, Dec: 14$^{\circ}$ 35.7’, absolute magnitude: 1.6) is about 31 pc away. An alien astronomer is on a planet with Earth’s mass and radius orbiting Rotanev. The planet has a uniform albedo of 0.3.

**(a)** What is the angular distance between Sualocin and Rotanev? **[4 т.]**

**(b)** What is the distance between these stars in parsecs? **[3 т.]**

**(c)** On the alien’s planet, what is the angular separation in the sky between Sualocin and our Sun? **[3 т.]**

**(d)** How much greater is the flux received by the planet from Sualocin than that received from our Sun? **[5 т.]**
