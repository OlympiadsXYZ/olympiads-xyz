## Условие

The “Iridium” satellites give brilliant flares due to reflection of sunlight from their highly polished main mission antennas which are highly reflective plates, each of size 188 × 86 cm. A satellite was overhead at the time of the flare and the apparent magnitude of the flare was $-8^{\mathrm{m}}$ at the centre.

**a)** Draw a ray diagram and make a rough estimate of the area on ground covered by the beams from any one of the antennas.

**b)** Find the height of the satellite above the surface of the earth.
