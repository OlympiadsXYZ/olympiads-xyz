## Условие

A *UBV* photometric (*UBV* Johnson’s) observation of a star gives $U = 8.15$, $B = 8.50$, and $V = 8.14$. Based on the spectral class, one gets the intrinsic color $(U - B)_{\mathrm{o}} = -0.45$. If the star is known to have radius of $2.3\ R_{\odot}$, absolute bolometric magnitude of -0.25, and bolometric correction (*BC*) of -0.15, determine:

**a.** the intrinsic magnitudes $U$, $B$, and $V$ of the star (take, for the typical interstellar matters, the ratio of total to selective extinction (color excess) $R_V = 3.2$),

**b.** the effective temperature of the star,

**c.** the distance to the star in pc.

Note: The relation between color excess of $U$ - $B$ and of $B - V$ is $E(U - B) = 0.72\ E(B - V)$.

Let $A_v$ be the interstellar extinction and $R = 3.2$, then $A_v = 3.2\ E(B\text{-}V)$.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

$U=8.15$, $B=8.50$, and $V=8.14$.

$(U-B)_o=-0.45$, $R=2.3R_\odot=1.60\times10^{11}$ cm,

$M_{bol}=-0.25$, $BC=-0.15$

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-1">

### a)

$U-B=8.15-8.50=-0.35$

The color excess for U-B:

$$
E(U-B)=(U-B)-(U-B)_o=-0.35-(-0.45)=0.10
$$

The relation between color excess of U-B and of B-V:

$$
E(U-B)=0.72E(B-V)\Longrightarrow E(B-V)=0.10/0.72=0.14\qquad(15\%)
$$

Let $A_v$ be the interstellar extinction and $R=3.2$, then

$$
A_v=3.2E(B-V)=3.2(0.14)=0.45
$$

$$
V-V_o=A_v\Longrightarrow V_o=V-A_v=8.14-0.45=7.69
$$

$$
E(B-V)=(B-V)-(B-V)_o\Longrightarrow(B-V)_o=(B-V)-E(B-V)
$$

$$
=(8.50-8.14)-0.14=0.22\qquad(15\%)
$$

$$
(B-V)_o=B_o-V_o=0{,}22\Longrightarrow B_o=0.22+V_o=0.22+7.69=7.91\qquad(10\%)
$$

$$
(U-B)_o=U_o-B_o=-0{,}45\Longrightarrow U_o=B_o-0.45=7.91-0.45=7.46\qquad(10\%)
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-2">

### b)

Luminosity - absolute magnitude relation:

$$
M_{bol}-M_{bol\odot}=-2.5\log L/L_\odot\Longrightarrow L/L_\odot=10^{-(M_{bol}-M_{bol\odot})/2.5}
$$

$$
=10^{-(-0.25-4.75)/2.5}=100\qquad(10\%)
$$

$$
L=100L_\odot=3.90\times10^{35}\text{ erg/det}
$$

$$
L=4\pi\sigma R^2T_{ef}^4
$$

$$
T_{ef}=\left(\frac L{4\pi\sigma R^2}\right)^{1/4}=\left(\frac{3.90\times10^{35}}{4\pi(5.67\times10^{-5})(1.60\times10^{11})^2}\right)^{1/4}=12092\text{ K}\qquad(15\%)
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-3">

### c)

$$
M_v-M_{bol}=BC\Longrightarrow M_v=M_{bol}+BC=-0.25-0.15=-0.40\qquad(10\%)
$$

$$
m_v-M_v=-5+5\log d+A_v\Longrightarrow5\log d=m_v-M_v+5-A_v
$$

$$
=8.14+0.40+5-0.45=13.09
$$

$$
d=10^{13.09/5}=10^{2.62}=414.9\text{ pc}\qquad(15\%)
$$

</ProblemSection>


</Spoiler>
