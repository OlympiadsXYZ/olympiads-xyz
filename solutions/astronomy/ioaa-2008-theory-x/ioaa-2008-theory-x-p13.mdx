## Условие

In the journey of a space craft, scientists make a close encounter with an object and they would like to investigate the object more carefully using their on-board telescope. For simplicity, we assume this to be a two-dimensional problem and that the position of the space craft is stationary in (0,0). The shape of the object is a disk and the boundary has the equation

$$
x^{2} + y^{2} - 10x - 8y + 40 = 0.
$$

Find the exact values of maximum and minimum of tan $\varphi$ where $\varphi$ is the angle of the telescope with respect to the $x$ direction during investigation from one edge to the other edge.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

<ProblemSection id="solution-method-1">

### I. Algebraic Solution

Let $y=mx$ be the line of sight of the telescope. **(20%)**

Then, the intersection of the line of sight and the circle is

$$
x^2+(mx)^2-10x-8(mx)+40=0
$$

$$
(1+m^2)x^2-(10+8m)x+40=0\qquad(20\%)
$$

The equation will have solution if its discriminant

$$
D=b^2-4ac\geq0,
$$

$$
=(-(10+8m))^2-4(1+m^2)40\geq0.
$$

The solution of the inequality is

$$
\frac56-\frac{\sqrt{10}}{12}\leq m\leq\frac56+\frac{\sqrt{10}}{12}\qquad(20\%)
$$

Therefore the exact value of the maximum value of $\tan\varphi$ is $\frac56+\frac{\sqrt{10}}{12}$ **(20%)**

and the minimum value is $\frac56-\frac{\sqrt{10}}{12}$ **(20%)**

### II. Geometric Solution

Let us write the equation of the circle in the following form **(20%)**

$$
(x-5)^2+(y-4)^2=-40+25+16=1
$$

The centre of the circle is (5,4) and the radius is 1

First we obtain $\tan\alpha=\frac45$ and $\tan\beta=\frac1{\sqrt{40}}$ **(20%)**

The minimum value of tan of elevation angle is **(30%)**

$$
\tan(\alpha-\beta)=\frac{\tan\alpha-\tan\beta}{1+\tan\alpha\tan\beta}=\frac{\frac45-\frac1{\sqrt{40}}}{1+\frac4{5\sqrt{40}}}=\frac56-\frac{\sqrt{10}}{12}
$$

The maximum value of tan of elevation angle is

$$
\tan(\alpha+\beta)=\frac{\tan\alpha+\tan\beta}{1-\tan\alpha\tan\beta}=\frac{\frac45+\frac1{\sqrt{40}}}{1-\frac4{5\sqrt{40}}}=\frac56+\frac{\sqrt{10}}{12}\qquad(30\%)
$$
