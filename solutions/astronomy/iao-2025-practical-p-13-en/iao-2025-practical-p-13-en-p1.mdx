## Условие

Cepheids. The distance to the Large Magellanic Cloud is 50 kpc, and its diameter is 10 kpc. During the research of this Galaxy, 12 Cepheids were discovered, most of which belong to the LMO, but some stars are only projected onto the galaxy. The data of the observations presented in Table 13 (see separate sheet).

| 2 | 1.51 | 16.64 |
| 3 | 1.92 | 16.30 |
| 4 | 2.48 | 15.14 |
| 5 | 2.71 | 15.89 |
| 6 | 3.74 | 15.61 |
| 7 | 3.94 | 15.54 |
| 8 | 5.10 | 15.19 |
| 9 | 7.08 | 15.72 |

| 11 | 9.14 | 14.52 |
| 12 | 12.32 | 14.13 |

**13.1.** Plot graph of the dependence of the apparent magnitude on the period for these stars. It is known that the magnitude of the Cepheids depends almost linearly on the logarithm of the period, so the X-axis should be made logarithmic (the decimal logarithms of the periods along the axis).

**13.2.** Identify which of these stars belong to the LMO and which do not.

**13.3.** Calculate the distances to those stars that are outside of the BMO.

**13.4.** Based on the graph, derive the formula for the dependence for Cepheids of the absolute magnitude (M) on the period (P, in days), calculate the necessary coefficients in this formula.
