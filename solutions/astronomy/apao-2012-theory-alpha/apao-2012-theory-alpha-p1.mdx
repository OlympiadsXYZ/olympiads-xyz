## Условие

Canopus was a very popular star in problems set of the XVII IAO in Gwangju, Korea (October 16-24, 2012). But nobody had success seeing it during that Olympiad. Maybe here you will be lucky? Calculate time for the best conditions for observation of Canopus during the next 24 hours. Bangladesh time should be used.
