## Условие

Four bright stars were erased from the sky map below. All of these stars have magnitudes lower than 3.0. Select the alternative with the four stars that were erased from the sky map.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '91.3%', '--fig-max': '584px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usaaao-2026-i-frst-exam/p11-fig1.png" alt="Circular sky map with a thick black border: black dots of different sizes for stars, no labels and no constellation lines." width="1826" height="1823" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

(a) $\alpha$ And, $\alpha$ CrB, $\beta$ Her, and $\alpha$ PsA.

(b) $\alpha$ And, $\alpha$ Aql, $\beta$ Cet, and $\alpha$ Lyr.

(c) $\alpha$ Aql, $\alpha$ CrB, $\beta$ Her, and $\alpha$ Peg.

(d) $\beta$ Cet, $\beta$ Her, $\alpha$ Peg, and $\alpha$ Oph.

(e) $\alpha$ Aqr, $\alpha$ CrB, $\alpha$ Cyg, and $\alpha$ Her.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- A

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Solution:** The four stars removed from the sky map are $\alpha$ And, $\alpha$ CrB, $\beta$ Her, and $\alpha$ PsA.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '84%', '--fig-max': '538px'}}>

**Answer: A**
