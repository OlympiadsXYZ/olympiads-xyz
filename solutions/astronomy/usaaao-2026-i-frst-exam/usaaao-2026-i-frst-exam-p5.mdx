You are stranded on a remote island somewhere on Earth, at an unknown date and time (in the $21^{\mathrm{st}}$ century). You look up and see the night sky.

(For avoidance of doubt, the illuminated side of the Moon is on the left.)

Which of the following is the best estimate of the month of the year?

(a) January

(b) April

(c) July

(d) September

(e) November

## Отговори

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Solution:** The moon is roughly $\frac{1}{3}$ illuminated with the illuminated side facing east (waning), which means that the Sun is about $60^{\circ}$ east along the ecliptic from the moon.

Since the moon is in Leo - where the Sun would be in September - this suggests the Sun is $60^{\circ}$, or two months, east of this point. So the current month is November.

**Answer: E**


</Spoiler>
