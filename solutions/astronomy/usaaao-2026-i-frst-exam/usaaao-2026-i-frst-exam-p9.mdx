## Условие

The asteroid Pallas orbits the Sun with a semi-major axis of 2.77 AU. What is its orbital period around the Sun in years?

(a) $(2.77)^{1/2}$

(b) $(2.77)^{3/2}$

(c) $(2.77)^{5/2}$

(d) $(2.77)^{2/3}$

(e) $(2.77)^{2/5}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- B

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Solution:** When solving for the solar system, Kepler’s Third law simplifies when semi-major axis ($a$) is expressed in AU and orbital period ($P$) is expressed in years, which becomes $P^2 = a^3$. Solving for $P$, we have $P = a^{3/2}$.

**Answer: B**


</Spoiler>
