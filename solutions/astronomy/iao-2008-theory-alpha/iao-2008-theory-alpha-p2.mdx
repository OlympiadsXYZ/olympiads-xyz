*XIII International Astronomy Olympiad · Theoretical round. Problems to solve · 13 октомври 2008 г.*

## Условие

Maybe you paid attention to that there are group of cats living in Grignano, to the right from the ICTP guesthouse, including four of them looking like absolutely black ones. Estimate the absolute bolometric stellar magnitude $M_{abc}$ of an absolutely black cat (abc) as it is an absolutely black body.
