*XIII International Astronomy Olympiad · Theoretical round. Problems to solve · 13 октомври 2008 г.*

## Условие

You know that there are Great oppositions of Mars for citizens of Earth. The magnitude of Mars were even $-2^{\mathrm m}.9$ during some of the very famous of these oppositions (like in August 27, 2003). But there are also Great oppositions of Venus for citizens of some planet(s). At what planet(s) is this possible? Find the apparent stellar magnitude of Venus visible from every such planet (or this planet) at such Great oppositions.
