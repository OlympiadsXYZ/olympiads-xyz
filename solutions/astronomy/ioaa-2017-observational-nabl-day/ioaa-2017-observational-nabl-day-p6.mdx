## Условие

**Instruction:** Without altering the polar alignment, find the observer’s latitude based on a previously polar-aligned equatorial mount.

**Included:**

- An Equatorial Mount Telescope that has already been **properly** aligned to a location in the northern celestial pole.
- Bubble Level.
- Latitude dial on the mount is covered (you may not use it).
