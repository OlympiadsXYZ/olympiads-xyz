## Условие

Which of the following is closest to the ratio of the farthest distance to the horizon that can be seen by an observer standing top of the Mount Everest on Earth (height = 8.8 km) and Olympus Mons on Mars (height = 25 km)?

A. 0.1

B. 1

C. 5

D. 10

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 1

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

*Solution:* **B: 1**

$D = \frac{R}{R + h} \times \sqrt{2hR + h^2}$ , and since $h \ll R$, we can approximate $D$ such that $D \propto \sqrt{hR}$ (and this proportionality relationship is all that matters)
