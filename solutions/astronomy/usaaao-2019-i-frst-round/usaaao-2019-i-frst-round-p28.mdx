## Условие

The New Horizons spacecraft completed a flyby of 2014 MU69 on New Year’s day of this year. 2014 MU69 is a Kuiper Belt Object with a semi-major axis of 44.58 AU. Estimate the maximum temperature at the surface of 2014 MU69, in Kelvin, assuming the object has zero albedo.

A. 41.7 Kelvin

B. 58.9 Kelvin

C. 83.3 Kelvin

D. 117.9 Kelvin

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 58.9 Kelvin

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

*Solution:* **B: 58.9 Kelvin**

$T_{\mathrm{irr}} = T_{\mathrm{star}} \times \sqrt{\frac{R_{\mathrm{star}}}{2A}} * 4^{1/4}$, where $T_{\mathrm{star}}$ is the temperature of the Sun (5770 K), R is the radius of the sun ($6.95 \times 10^8$ m), and A is the distance from the object and star (44.58 AU).

This is because the expression WITHOUT the factor of $4^{1/4}$ gives the equilibrium temperature assuming that the energy is fully distributed across the entire planet. However, since we want the maximum temperature here, we assume that only the daytime side re-emits, which means that the surface area in question doesn’t have the 4 factor (in the $4\pi r^2$), and that therefore $T_{\mathrm{irr}}$ requires an extra $4^{1/4}$ factor (see the following Wikipedia page for the full explanation: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planetary_equilibrium_temperature)


</Spoiler>
