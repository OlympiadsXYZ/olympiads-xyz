## Условие

Imagine a small space station with a total mass of $M=50$ ton placed far away from any celestial body. A cosmonaut with a total mass (with ammunitions) of $m=100$ kg appears $L=80$ m away from the station without any fuel. Calculate (estimate) the time $\tau$ after which the cosmonaut reaches the station due to gravitational attraction. Consider the initial velocity of the cosmonaut to be zero.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

For estimation we may consider the space station and cosmonaut as pointlike particles. The time $\tau_{1/2}$ after which these particles collide due to gravitational attraction is approximately equal to half of period of revolution of mass $m$ around mass $M$ on the orbit with the major semiaxis $L/2$. And the time $\tau$ that we should find approximately equals $\tau_{1/2}$ (neglecting the last part of orbit comparable with the sizes of station and cosmonaut). According to general III Kepler law,

$$
4\pi^2/GM=T^2/(L/2)^3,\qquad\text{so}\qquad T=2\pi(L/2)^{3/2}(GM)^{-1/2},\qquad\text{and}\qquad\tau_{1/2}=T/2=\pi(L^3/8GM)^{1/2}
$$

$$
\tau\approx\tau_{1/2}=4.35\cdot10^5\;\mathrm{s}\approx7250\;\mathrm{min}\approx121\;\mathrm{h}\approx5\;\text{days}.
$$

### Basic criteria. For work of Jury

*General note:*

*Only solutions using approximation of the limit for half of period of rotation on the elliptic orbits or using correct integral calculations $\tau$ may be evaluated in frames of “max 8 pt” criteria.*

*Solutions using formulae like $gt^2/2$ with using some average $g$* – not more than 2 pt in total.

*Solutions using formulae like $gt^2/2$ with taking $g$ as at the initial or final point* – not more than 1 pt in total.

Max 8 pt criteria for solutions using approach with period of rotation and III Kepler law:

- General understanding of the physical process (using approach with period of rotation) – 1 pt.
- $\tau\approx\tau_{1/2}$ – 1 pt.
- Algebra steps and correct finding $\tau_{1/2}=T/2=\pi(L^3/8GM)^{1/2}$ – 4 pt in total.
- Correct calculation of numerical answer – 2 pt (1 pt for more or less correct values of answer: in a range of “a few days”).
