## Условие

Two friends wanted to measure the height of the hill next to their village (latitude φ=40°). One of the friends climbed to the top of the hill and she agreed to send a light signal to her friend in the village as soon as she sees the sunset. On March 21, when they did this experiment, the friend in village received the light signal 4.1 minutes after the sunset from the village. Estimate the height of the hill and horizon distance for the person at the hill top. Ignore atmospheric refraction.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 602 m

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

First we should realise that the Sun doesn’t set vertically. It sets at an angle of $\theta = 90 - \varphi = 50^{\circ}$. Thus, the horizon depression *x* will be

$x = 15.0411' \times 4.1 \times sin\theta = 1.03^{o}sin50^{o} = 0.787^{o}.$ (4 points)

Let the height of the mountain be H. Let *C* be the centre of the Earth, point *T* - hill top and point *T’* is the point on the horizon as seen from T. Thus,

$$
cosx = \left[\frac{R_\oplus}{(R_\oplus + H)}\right]
$$

$H = \frac{R_\oplus(1-cosx)}{cosx} = 602$ metres (3 points)

The distance of horizon (from base of the mountain, along the surface of the earth) is found from the rectangular triangle *TCT*’ as

$D = (R_\oplus + H)sinx \approx R_\oplus sinx = 6.38 \times 10^3 sin0.787^{o} = 87.6$ km

Alternatively, $D = R_\oplus x = 6.38 \times 10^3 \times 0.787^{o} \times \pi \div 180^{o} = 87.6$ km (3 points)
