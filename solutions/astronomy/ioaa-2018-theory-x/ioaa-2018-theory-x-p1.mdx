## Условие

Read the statements given below and state if they are true or false:

**(a)** For some galaxies the apparent recession speed exceeds the speed of light. **[2 т.]**

**(b)** The velocity – Distance relation as given by Hubble cannot allow recession velocities to exceed the speed of light. **[2 т.]**

**(c)** Hubble-Lemaitre’s law (formerly known as Hubble’s Law) does not violate special relativity. **[2 т.]**

**(d)** If some galaxies would have an apparent recession speed exceeding the speed of light, then the photons from those galaxies can never reach us. **[2 т.]**

**(e)** As the expansion of Universe is accelerating, photons emitted right now from galaxies which have apparent recession speed equal to the speed of light will never reach us. **[2 т.]**
