## Условие

It is very interesting to observe that on one particular calendar day each year, the mean sidereal time will twice be 00:00:00.

**(a)** What will be the approximate R.A. of the Sun when this event happens?

**(b)** Estimate the exact date in 2018 for this event.

You may assume that at the Royal Greenwich Observatory, the mean sidereal time (GMST$_0$) was 6.706h at 0h, 1st January, 2018 (JD2458119.5).

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **(a)** 18 h
- **(b)** 21 September 2018 (GMST = 00:00:00 at 00:00:48 and again at 23:56:52)

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

(1) This can happen if the sidereal time is 00:00:00 just after the midnight and just before the midnight on the same day. It means that the sun should be near the Autumn (September) Equinox. Approximate R.A. of the Sun is 18$^{\mathrm{h}}$. (3 points)

(2) At 0$^{\mathrm{h}}$, 1$^{\mathrm{st}}$ January, 2018 (JD2458119.5), the mean sidereal time GMST$_0$ was 6.706$^{\mathrm{h}}$, i.e. GMST$_0$=100.59°. Since one mean solar day is longer than one mean sidereal day with 0.9856º (about 4 minutes), the GMST will increase $\Delta = 0.9856^{\circ}$at next midnight at 2$^{\mathrm{st}}$ January, 2018. Therefore, we can first calculate how many days it will take for GMST to reach 360°, i.e.

$T = \frac{360^{\circ}-GMST_0}{\Delta} = \frac{360^{\circ}-100.59^{\circ}}{0.9856^{\circ}} = 263.20$ (3 points)

i.e. after completion of 263 days, the GMST at midnight will be 359.80º.

Thus, GMST will be 00:00:00 on 264$^{\mathrm{th}}$ day at 00:00:48 and then again at 23:56:52 on the same day.

(2 points)

The 264$^{\mathrm{th}}$ day in the calendar of 2018 is 21$^{\mathrm{st}}$ September 2018. (2 points)


</Spoiler>
