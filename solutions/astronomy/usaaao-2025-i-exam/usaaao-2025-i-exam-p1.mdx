## Условие

Imagine you are on Deimos right now and you want to escape Mars because you are bored. If Deimos is currently at 23,460 km away from the center of Mars and its speed is currently around 1.35 km/s, how much more speed do you and Deimos need to escape Mars? *The mass of Mars is $6.39\times10^{23}$ kg.*

(a) 557 m/s

(b) 427 m/s

(c) 377 m/s

(d) 207 m/s

(e) None

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- A

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

To escape Mars, you would need to know the escape velocity of Deimos at its current position.

$$
v_{escape}=\sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R}}=\sqrt{\frac{2\times6.673\times10^{-11}\times6.39\times10^{23}}{23460\times10^3}}\approx1.907\ \mathrm{km/s}
$$

Hence, you would need

$$
\Delta v\approx1{,}907-1{,}35\approx557\ \mathrm{m/s}
$$

**Answer: A**

> **Source note:** The source prints comma decimals in the displayed subtraction; they are retained.


</Spoiler>
