*Theory — Q9-1 English (Official) · 15 т.*

## Условие



**9.1** The mass of Charon, the biggest satellite of Pluto, is 1/8th the mass of Pluto. Both bodies move in a circular orbit around a common center of mass. In addition, they both are tidally-locked.  
The distance between the center of the planet and the center of the satellite is $R = 19\,640\ \mathrm{km}$ and radius of the satellite is $r = 593\ \mathrm{km}$.

Let $g_0$ be the gravitational acceleration on the surface of Charon due only to its mass.Let A be the point on Charon surface directly facing Pluto, and B the point diametrically opposite. Compute the percentage difference between gravitational acceleration at A and B respect to $g_0$. **[15 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **9.1** 0.004 %

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">


<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '83.2%', '--fig-max': '533px'}}>

Let P and C be the centers of Pluto and Charon respectively, and CM their center of mass. Clearly $\overline{CM-P}=\frac19R$ and $\overline{CM-C}=\frac89R$. The effective gravitational field at points A and B are given by:

[By recognizing the vectorial nature of gravities/forces/fields] [4 points]

$$
\vec g_A=\frac{GM_P}{(R-r)^2}\hat r-\frac{GM_C}{r^2}\hat r-\omega^2\left(\frac89R-r\right)\hat r
$$

[1 point]

$$
\vec g_B=\frac{GM_P}{(R+r)^2}\hat r+\frac{GM_C}{r^2}\hat r-\omega^2\left(\frac89R+r\right)\hat r
$$

[1 point]

where $\omega^2=9\frac{GM_C}{R^3}$ is the angular speed common to both Pluto and Charon. Thus, the gravitational accelerations at these points are:

[by finding angular speed] [2 points]

$$
g_A=\frac{GM_P}{(R-r)^2}-\frac{GM_C}{r^2}-\frac{GM_C}{R^2}\left(8-9\frac rR\right)
$$

$$
g_B=\frac{GM_P}{(R+r)^2}+\frac{GM_C}{r^2}-\frac{GM_C}{R^2}\left(8+9\frac rR\right)
$$

Factorizing the expression to obtain formulas proportional to Charon’s mass [1 point]:

$$
g_A=-\frac{GM_C}{r^2}\left[1-\frac{M_P}{M_C}\left(\frac rR\right)^2\frac1{(1-\frac rR)^2}+\left(\frac rR\right)^2\left(8-9\frac rR\right)\right]
$$

$$
g_B=\frac{GM_C}{r^2}\left[1+\frac{M_P}{M_C}\left(\frac rR\right)^2\frac1{(1+\frac rR)^2}-\left(\frac rR\right)^2\left(8+9\frac rR\right)\right]
$$

Replacing the numerical value of the ratio of the two masses:

$$
g_A=-\frac{GM_C}{r^2}\left[1-\left(\frac rR\right)^2\frac8{(1-\frac rR)^2}+\left(\frac rR\right)^2\left(8-9\frac rR\right)\right]
$$

$$
g_B=\frac{GM_C}{r^2}\left[1+\left(\frac rR\right)^2\frac8{(1+\frac rR)^2}-\left(\frac rR\right)^2\left(8+9\frac rR\right)\right]
$$

Noting that the expressions in square brackets are dimensionless, it is helpful to replace the given values of $r$ and $R$, resulting [2 points by expressions of $g_A$ and $g_B$]:

$$
g_A=-\frac{GM_C}{r^2}(0{,}99929)
$$

$$
g_B=\frac{GM_C}{r^2}(0{,}99933)
$$

The percentage difference with respect to Charon’s normal gravity $g_0=\frac{GM_C}{r^2}$ is

$$
\frac{\left||g_A|-|g_B|\right|}{g_0}\cdot100\%=0{,}004\%=4\times10^{-3}\%
$$

[1 point]


</Spoiler>
