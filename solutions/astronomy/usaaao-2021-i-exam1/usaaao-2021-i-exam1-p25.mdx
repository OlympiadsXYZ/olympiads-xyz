## Условие

Suppose a spaceship is attempting a slingshot maneuver on a gas giant with mass 100 times that of the spaceship. Because the spaceship somewhat entered the planet’s atmosphere, kinetic energy was not conserved—only momentum. What is the ratio of the spaceship’s change in velocity to the planet’s change in velocity, $\frac{\Delta v_s}{\Delta v_p}$?

(a) 10

(b) 100

(c) -10

(d) -100

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- D

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

$m_1u_1 + m_2u_2 = m_1v_1 + m_2v_2$

$m_1v_1 = m_1u_1 + m_2u_2 - m_2v_2$

$v_1 - u_1 = \frac{m_2}{m_1}(u_2 - v_2)$

$\Delta v_1 = -100\Delta v_2$

Where body 1 is the spaceship, and body 2 is the planet.

**Answer: D**


</Spoiler>
