## Условие

Assume that a bright supernova appears in the sky on September 24, 2015 and you watch it at 22.00 (UTC+7). The planetarium will now show the sky for this date with the supernova. Mark the position of the supernova by a cross (x) in Answer Sheet 2. You can mark other stars by circle (o) for guidance. (Duration: 3 minutes)(50 points)
