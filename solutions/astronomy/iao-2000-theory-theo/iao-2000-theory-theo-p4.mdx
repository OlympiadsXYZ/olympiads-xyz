## Условие

Young scientists from the Komi-Republic territory (in the Russian Federation) registered a few days ago a new object looking like an eclipsing binary star. But the the period of this star was not stable: the stellar magnitude of the object is usually usually equal to $24.32^{\mathrm{m}}$. Once every 7-11 seconds it is rising to $24.52^{\mathrm{m}}$ for 0.2-0.3 0.3 seconds. After investigations it was clear that the shining object is eyes of a group group of absolutely black cats sitting on a small absolutely black body in our Solar Solar System and looking towards the Sun! And one of the cats is blinking! Calculate Calculate the number of cats in the group sitting on the small body and looking to the to the Sun. Draw a picture explaining your solution. Consider that all the cats are equal are equal in size.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Пусть общее число котов равно $N$. То есть, один кот моргает и $N-1$ — смотрят на Солнце постоянно. Таким образом $24{,}32^m$ соответствует $2N$ кошачьим глазам и $24{,}52^m$ — $(2N-2)$ глазам.

$$
m_{2N-2}-m_{2N}=-2{,}5\lg(I_{2N-2}/I_{2N})=-2{,}5\lg[(N-1)/N]
$$
$$
-2{,}5\lg[(N-1)/N]=24{,}52-24{,}32
$$
$$
\lg[N/(N-1)]=0{,}08
$$
$$
N/(N-1)=10^{0{,}08}=1{,}203\approx1{,}2.
$$

То есть $N=6$.

(Общая формула: $N=10^{0{,}4(m_2-m_1)}/(10^{0{,}4(m_2-m_1)}-1)=5{,}944\approx6$.)


</Spoiler>
