## Условие

There is a radio source placed on a satellite of some planet named "Olympia". The radio source is working all the time but an observer does not register the signal all the time due to eclipses. The figure shows the level of the receiving signal by the observer vs time. Find from these data the average density of the planet. Take into account that the orbit of the satellite is circular, the observer is in the plane of the satellite's orbit and "Olympia" is far away from the observer.
