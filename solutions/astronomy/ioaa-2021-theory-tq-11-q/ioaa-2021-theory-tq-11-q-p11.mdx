**11.1** What is the minimum speed with which a projectile must be launched from the Earth's surface at the equator such that the projectile reaches the north pole? **[12 т.]**

**11.2** Find the eccentricity of the trajectory described by the projectile **[3 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **11.1** 7195 m/s
- **11.2** $e=1/(1+\sqrt2)=0.414$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

### 11.1

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '35.3%', '--fig-max': '227px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ioaa-2021-theory-tq-11-q/p11-sol-fig1.png" alt="Projectile path from equator A to pole B around Earth O." width="708" height="640" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


[1 point]

$$
EM=-\frac{GMm}{R}+\frac12mv^2
$$

[1 point]

At points A and B, the $v$ is the same; so, if $v$ is minimum, EM is minimum:

We know that the mechanical energy for an elliptical orbit is given by:

$$
EM=-\frac{GMm}{2a}
$$

[1 point]

where 2a is the length of the major axis.

Since EM must be minimal, then 2a must be minimal [1 point]

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '35.6%', '--fig-max': '228px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ioaa-2021-theory-tq-11-q/p11-sol-fig2.png" alt="Ellipse with foci O and F, equal surface radii OA and OB, and minimum launch speed." width="712" height="672" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


[1 point]

**O:** Center of the Earth and one of the focus of the ellipse.

**F:** the other focus of the ellipse

$$
OB+BF=2a
$$

[1 point]

$OB+BF=2a$, must be minimal.

F takes an arbitrary position, so to minimize, BF must be perpendicular to OF:

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '42.4%', '--fig-max': '272px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ioaa-2021-theory-tq-11-q/p11-sol-fig3.png" alt="Choice of second focus F and the minimum right triangle with sides R and R divided by square root of two." width="849" height="1141" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


[2 points]

[1 point]

$$
OB+BF=R+\frac R{\sqrt2}
$$

[1 point]

$$
2a=R+\frac R{\sqrt2}
$$

This is the minimum value it can take

Correspondingly, the expression for the minimum velocity is

$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{2GM}{(1+\sqrt2)R}}
$$

[1 point]

Using the M and R values for the Earth, the minimum velocity is

$$
v=7195\ \frac{m}{s}
$$

[1 point]

### 11.2

Furthermore, $OF=2e\cdot a$, where $e$ is the eccentricity

$$
\frac R{\sqrt2}=2e\frac R2\left(1+\frac1{\sqrt2}\right)
$$

[2 points]

$$
e=\frac1{1+\sqrt2}=0.414
$$

[1 point]


</Spoiler>
