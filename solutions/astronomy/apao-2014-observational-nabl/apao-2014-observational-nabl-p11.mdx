*X Asian-Pacific Astronomy Olympiad — Observational round. Cloudy sky · 27 ноември 2014 г.*

## Условие

*Observations by the naked eye*

On the waterfront area of Lake Baikal, which can be seen from the observation place, you can see three lights, the location of which remind of Orion's Belt. (See below a negative photo of the lights of this area, “Orion's Belt” is in the upper right corner). Estimate the angular distance between the two lights to the right of the “Belt”.
