## Условие

*It is given:* $V_0 \approx 30\,\dfrac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{s}}$, the speed of the Earth in its circular orbit around the Sun; $v_0 \approx 7.9\ \frac{\mathrm{km}}{s}$, the speed of a terrestrial satellite orbiting the Earth in a very low circular orbit (the first cosmic speed).

*It is known that*: $\dfrac{M_{\mathrm{Earth}}}{R_{\mathrm{Earth}}} \ll \dfrac{M_{\mathrm{Sun}}}{R_{\mathrm{Earth-Sun}}}$.

The variation of the kinetic energy of the body, in relation to the Sun, is neglected during the motion of the body from the surface of the Earth to the limit of the gravitational attraction of the Earth.  
The approximate minimum escape velocity of a body B, relative to the Sun, launched at a distance from the Sun equal to *1 AU*, so that it leaves the Solar System forever, is:

a) $v_B \approx 45.52\ \frac{\mathrm{km}}{s}$

b) $v_B \approx 32.32\ \frac{\mathrm{km}}{s}$

c) $v_B \approx 22.22\ \frac{\mathrm{km}}{s}$

d) $v_B \approx 42.42\ \frac{\mathrm{km}}{s}$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- d)

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Let $\vec v_B$ the speed of body B at the time of its launch from Earth, in relation to the Sun, so that the body reaches the limit of the gravitational attraction of the Sun and there it is at rest in relation to the Sun. Using the details in Figure 1, in accordance with the law of conservation of mechanical energy, it follows:

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '64%', '--fig-max': '410px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ioaa-jr-2022-theory-multiple-choice/p3-sol-fig1.png" alt="Parabolic escape trajectory from Earth around the Sun." width="1280" height="1063" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<figcaption>Fig. 1</figcaption>
</figure>


$$
R_E\ll R_{ES};
$$

$$
\frac{mv_B^2}2-K\frac{mM_E}{R_E}-K\frac{mM_S}{R_{ES}}=0;
$$

$$
\frac{M_E}{R_E}\ll\frac{M_S}{R_{ES}};
$$

$$
\frac{mv_B^2}2-K\frac{mM_S}{R_{ES}}=0;
$$

$$
v_B=\sqrt2\sqrt{K\frac{M_S}{R_{ES}}};
$$

$$
\sqrt{K\frac{M_S}{R_{ES}}}=v_{ES}=V_{\mathrm{orbital}}=V_0,
$$

representing the orbital speed of the Earth on the circular trajectory around the Sun;

$$
v_B=\sqrt2\,V_0,
$$

representing the second cosmic speed in relation to the Sun (parabolic speed);

$$
R_{ES}\approx1.5\cdot10^8\ \mathrm{km};\quad T_{ES}=1\ \text{year};
$$

$$
V_0=\frac{2\pi R_{ES}}{T_S}\approx30\ \mathrm{km/s};\qquad v_B\approx42.42\ \mathrm{km/s}.
$$

*Conclusion:* the body launched from the Earth, reaches the limit of the gravitational attraction of the Sun, in relation to the Sun, on a parabolic trajectory, with the Sun in its focus.


</Spoiler>
