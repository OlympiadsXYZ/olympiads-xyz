## Условие

*It is given:* $V_0 \approx 30\,\dfrac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{s}}$, the speed of the Earth in its circular orbit around the Sun; $v_0 \approx 7.9\ \frac{\mathrm{km}}{s}$, the speed of a terrestrial satellite orbiting the Earth in a very low circular orbit (the first cosmic speed).

*It is known that*: $\dfrac{M_{\mathrm{Earth}}}{R_{\mathrm{Earth}}} \ll \dfrac{M_{\mathrm{Sun}}}{R_{\mathrm{Earth-Sun}}}$.

The variation of the kinetic energy of the body, in relation to the Sun, is neglected during the motion of the body from the surface of the Earth to the limit of the gravitational attraction of the Earth.  
The approximate minimum escape velocity of a body B, relative to the Sun, launched at a distance from the Sun equal to *1 AU*, so that it leaves the Solar System forever, is:

a) $v_B \approx 45.52\ \frac{\mathrm{km}}{s}$

b) $v_B \approx 32.32\ \frac{\mathrm{km}}{s}$

c) $v_B \approx 22.22\ \frac{\mathrm{km}}{s}$

d) $v_B \approx 42.42\ \frac{\mathrm{km}}{s}$.
