## Условие

The luminosity of a star $\Sigma$, is $L_\Sigma = 100 \cdot L_S$, where $L_S$ is the luminosity of the Sun, and the star’s surface temperature is $T_\Sigma = \frac{1}{2} \cdot T_S$, where $T_S$ is the Sun’s surface temperature.

Ratio between the radius of the star and the radius of the Sun is:

a) $\dfrac{R_\Sigma}{R_{\mathrm{S}}} = 40$;

b) $\dfrac{R_\Sigma}{R_{\mathrm{S}}} = 30$;

c) $\dfrac{R_\Sigma}{R_{\mathrm{S}}} = 60$;

d) $\dfrac{R_\Sigma}{R_{\mathrm{S}}} = 50$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- a)

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The luminosity of a star, meaning the energy of the total radiation emitted by that star, in the unit of time, through its entire surface, at all wavelengths, in accordance with the Stefan-Boltzmann law, results:

$$
L_\Sigma=100\cdot L_S;
$$

$$
L_\Sigma=4\pi\cdot R_\Sigma^2\cdot\sigma\cdot T_\Sigma^4;\qquad L_S=4\pi\cdot R_S^2\cdot\sigma\cdot T_S^4;
$$

$$
4\pi\cdot R_\Sigma^2\cdot\sigma\cdot T_\Sigma^4=10^2\cdot4\pi\cdot R_S^2\cdot\sigma\cdot T_S^4;\qquad R_\Sigma^2\cdot T_\Sigma^4=10^2\cdot R_S^2\cdot T_S^4;
$$

$$
\frac{R_\Sigma^2}{R_S^2}=10^2\cdot\frac{T_S^4}{T_\Sigma^4}=10^2\cdot\left(\frac{T_S}{T_\Sigma}\right)^4=10^2\cdot2^4;
$$

$$
\frac{R_\Sigma}{R_S}=40.
$$


</Spoiler>
