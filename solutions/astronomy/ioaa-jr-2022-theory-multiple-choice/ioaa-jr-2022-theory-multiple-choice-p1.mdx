## Условие

The diameter of the objective lens of a telescope, represented in the drawing in figure 1, is $D_{\mathrm{objective\ telescope}} = 300\ \mathrm{mm}$, and the diameter of the pupil of the observer's eye is $D_{\mathrm{pupil\ eye}} = 6\ \mathrm{mm}$.

The light gathering power, coming from a star, due to this telescope, is:

a) $g_{\mathrm{c}} = 2500$;

b) $g_{\mathrm{c}} = 1500$;

c) $g_{\mathrm{c}} = 1000$;

d) $g_{\mathrm{c}} = 3000$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- a)

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The degree of collection of light from a star, $g_c$, by a telescop, used as shown in the drawing in Figure 1, is defined as the ratio of the area of the circle's surface, whose diameter is equal to the diameter of the lens of the telescope, and the area of the circle, whose diameter is equal to the diameter of the pupil of the observer's eye:

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '78.7%', '--fig-max': '504px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ioaa-jr-2022-theory-multiple-choice/p1-sol-fig1.png" alt="Objective and ocular lens ray diagram with observer eye." width="1576" height="705" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<figcaption>Fig. 1</figcaption>
</figure>


$$
g_c=\frac{\frac\pi4D_{\text{objectiv telescope}}^2}{\frac\pi4D_{\text{pupil eye}}^2}=\left(\frac{D_{\text{objectiv telescope}}}{D_{\text{pupil eye}}}\right)^2;\qquad D_{\text{objectiv telescope}}>D_{\text{pupil eye}};\quad g_c>1.
$$


</Spoiler>
