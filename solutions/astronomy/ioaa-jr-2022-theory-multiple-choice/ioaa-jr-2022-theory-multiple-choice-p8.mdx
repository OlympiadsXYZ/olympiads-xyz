## Условие

In a future program, NASA plans to launch a spacecraft, aimed directly at the Sun, without a human crew, to gather information, on its way to the Sun, both about all the inner planets and, in particular, about the Sun.  
They are known: the distance Earth - Sun, $r_{\mathrm{ES}} = 1.5 \cdot 10^{11}\ \mathrm{m}$; the period of the Earth's rotation around the Sun, $T_{\mathrm{E}} = 3.15 \cdot 10^{7}\ \mathrm{s}$; the radius of the Sun, $R_{\mathrm{S}} \approx \dfrac{r_{\mathrm{ES}}}{200}$.  
If the launch of the spacecraft will be done in such a way that its motion relative to the Sun is a free fall, then the approximate duration of the Earth-Sun flight is:

a) $t \approx 3.6 \cdot 10^{5}\ s$

b) $t \approx 6.6 \cdot 10^{6}\ s$

c) $t \approx 5.6 \cdot 10^{6}\ s$

d) $t \approx 4.6 \cdot 10^{6}\ s$
