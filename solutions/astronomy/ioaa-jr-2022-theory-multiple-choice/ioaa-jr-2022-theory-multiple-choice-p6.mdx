## Условие

*It is known that*: the distance Saturn - Sun is 9.54 times greater than the distance Earth - Sun; the angular diameter of the Sun's disk, seen from Earth, is 32'.  
The angular diameter of the Sun seen from Saturn, is:

a) $\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Saturn}} \approx 0.001$ radian

b) $\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Saturn}} \approx 0.002$ radian

c) $\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Saturn}} \approx 0.003$ radian

d) $\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Saturn}} \approx 0.004$ radian
