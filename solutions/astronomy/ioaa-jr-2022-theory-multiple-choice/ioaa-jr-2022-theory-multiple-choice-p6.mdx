## Условие

*It is known that*: the distance Saturn - Sun is 9.54 times greater than the distance Earth - Sun; the angular diameter of the Sun's disk, seen from Earth, is 32'.  
The angular diameter of the Sun seen from Saturn, is:

a) $\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Saturn}} \approx 0.001$ radian

b) $\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Saturn}} \approx 0.002$ radian

c) $\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Saturn}} \approx 0.003$ radian

d) $\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Saturn}} \approx 0.004$ radian

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- a)

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Since the distance between Saturn and the Sun is 9.54 times greater than the distance from Earth to the Sun, it turns out that the angular diameter of the Sun's disk, observed from Saturn, is 9.54 times smaller than the angular diameter of the Sun, observed from Earth. so that, using the drawing in Figure 1, it results:

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '64%', '--fig-max': '410px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ioaa-jr-2022-theory-multiple-choice/p6-sol-fig1-v2.png" alt="Comparison of the angular diameters of the Sun seen from Saturn and Earth." width="1280" height="593" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<figcaption>Fig. 1</figcaption>
</figure>


$$
d_{\mathrm{Sun}}=r_{\mathrm{Saturn-Sun}}\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Saturn}};
$$

$$
d_{\mathrm{Sun}}=r_{\mathrm{Earth-Sun}}\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Earth}};
$$

$$
r_{\mathrm{Saturn-Sun}}=9.54r_{\mathrm{Earth-Sun}};
$$

$$
\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Saturn}}=\frac1{9.54}\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Earth}}=\frac{32'}{9.54}\approx3.35'\approx0.00097\ \text{radian};
$$

$$
\alpha_{\mathrm{Sun-Saturn}}\approx0.001\ \text{radian},
$$

representing the angular diameter of the Sun seen from Saturn.


</Spoiler>
