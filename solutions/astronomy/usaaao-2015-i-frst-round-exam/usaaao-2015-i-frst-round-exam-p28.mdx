*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

Suppose at the equator, a star passes through the zenith at local noon on the summer solstice. What is the right ascension and declination of the star?
a. 0h 0º
b. 0h 90º
c. 6h 0º
d. 12h 0º
e. 12h 90º

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- c

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: c


</Spoiler>
