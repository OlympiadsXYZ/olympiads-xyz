*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

A binary star system has two components: Star A and Star B. Star A has a mass of 5 solar masses, and Star B has the same mass as our Sun. Assuming circular orbits, how many times closer to the center of mass of the system is Star A than Star B?  
a. 1  
b. 3  
c. 5  
d. 10  
e. 25

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- c

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: c


</Spoiler>
