*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

40 light years away, an exoplanet orbits a star of 5 solar masses every 14 years. Assuming this system has an inclination of 90˚ as viewed from Earth, what is the projected diameter of the exoplanet's orbit as viewed from Earth?  
a. 0.3"  
b. 0.8"  
c. 1.6"  
d. 2.5"  
e. 1.2"

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- c

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: c


</Spoiler>
