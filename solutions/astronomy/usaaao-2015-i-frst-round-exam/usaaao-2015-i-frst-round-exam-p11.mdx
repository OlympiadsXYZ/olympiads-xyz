*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

What is, approximately, the peak wavelength of electromagnetic radiation emitted by a star at a temperature of 5,000 K?
a. 580 Angstroms
b. 5,800 Angstroms
c. 4,600 Angstroms
d. 2,900 Angstroms
e. 58,000 Angstroms

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- b

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: b


</Spoiler>
