*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

A synodic day on Mars is 24 hours and 40 minutes. If one Martian year is 687 earth-days, which of the following is closest to a sidereal day on Mars?
a. 23h 56m
b. 24h 15m
c. 24h 37m
d. 24h 40m
e. 24h 42m

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- c

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: c


</Spoiler>
