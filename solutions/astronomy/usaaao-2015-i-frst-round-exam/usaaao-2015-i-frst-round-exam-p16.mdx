*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

A and B, two main sequence stars of the same spectral class, have apparent magnitudes of 17 and 12, respectively. If star A is 1 kpc away, what is the distance to star B?  
a. 10 pc.  
b. 100 pc.  
c. 10 kpc.  
d. 50 pc.  
e. 100 kpc.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- b

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: b


</Spoiler>
