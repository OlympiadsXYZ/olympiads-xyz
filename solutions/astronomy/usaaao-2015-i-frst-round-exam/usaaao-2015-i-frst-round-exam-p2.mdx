*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

To have a lunar eclipse, the line of nodes must be pointing at the sun. The moon must also be in what phase?
a. New
b. First Quarter
c. Waxing Gibbous
d. Full
e. Waning Crescent

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- d

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: d


</Spoiler>
