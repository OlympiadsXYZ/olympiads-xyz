*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

To calculate the field of view of a telescope, you measure the time it takes Capella (RA:5.27h, dec:45.98º) to pass across the eyepiece. If the measured time is 2 minutes and 30 seconds, what is the field of view in arcseconds?  
a. 11.6'  
b. 26.5'  
c. 37.5'  
d. 52.5'  
e. 66.8

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- b

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: b


</Spoiler>
