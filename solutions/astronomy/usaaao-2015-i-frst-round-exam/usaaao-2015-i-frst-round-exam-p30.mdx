*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

A planet orbits a star with a projected semimajor axis of 0.24". What is the necessary aperture size of a telescope than can resolve this orbit using 1000 nm light?  
a. 0.13 m  
b. 0.52 m  
c. 1.05 m  
d. 3.10 m  
e. 2.04 m

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- b

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: b


</Spoiler>
