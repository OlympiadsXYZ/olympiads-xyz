*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

If a 1.2 solar mass star shows a radial velocity variation with a period of 9.2 days and amplitude of 32 m/s, estimate the minimum mass of the companion:  
a. $7.5*10^{26}$ kg  
b. $1.2*10^{26}$ kg  
c. $6.9*10^{27}$ kg  
d. $5.1*10^{15}$ kg  
e. $3.3*10^{27}$ kg

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- a

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: a


</Spoiler>
