*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

A type Ia supernova was observed in a galaxy with a redshift of 0.03. The supernova was determined to be $1.3 \times 10^8$ pc away from Earth. Determine the Hubble time using this observation.  
a. $1.41 \times 10^{10}$ years  
b. $1.41 \times 10^{10}$ seconds  
c. $1.33 \times 10^{9}$ years  
d. 47.1 years  
e. $1.33 \times 10^{9}$ seconds

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- a

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: a


</Spoiler>
