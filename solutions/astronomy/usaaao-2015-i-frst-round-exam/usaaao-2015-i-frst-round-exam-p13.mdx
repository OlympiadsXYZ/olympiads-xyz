*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

Suppose that you measure the parallax angle for a particular star to be 0.25 arcsecond. The distance to this star is
a. 2 pc
b. 0.5 ly
c. 2 ly
d. 4 pc
e. 0.5 pc

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- d

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: d


</Spoiler>
