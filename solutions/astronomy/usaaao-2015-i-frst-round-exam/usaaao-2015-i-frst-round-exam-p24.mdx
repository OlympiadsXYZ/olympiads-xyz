*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

Calculate the blackbody equilibrium temperature of Mars. Take Mars's albedo to be 0.25 and semimajor axis to be 1.5 AU  
a. 300 K  
b. 212 K  
c. 161 K  
d. 228 K  
e. 260 K

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- b

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: b


</Spoiler>
