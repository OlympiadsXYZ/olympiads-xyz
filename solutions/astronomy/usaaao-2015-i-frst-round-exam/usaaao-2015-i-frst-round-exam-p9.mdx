*USAAAO First Round 2015*

## Условие

Which two properties of galaxies does the Tully-Fisher relation utilize a correlation between?  
a. Luminosity and velocity dispersion  
b. Luminosity and rotational velocity  
c. Radius and metallicity  
d. Luminosity and metallicity  
e. Mass and surface brightness

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- b

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: b


</Spoiler>
