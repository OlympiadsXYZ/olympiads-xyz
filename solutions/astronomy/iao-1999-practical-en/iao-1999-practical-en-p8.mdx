*IV International Astronomy Olympiad*

<Warning title="Бележка към темата">
The English question8 says that the smaller component is fainter. The Russian/Bulgarian proceedings use the stronger approximation that it is dark. The English wording is retained. The exact observing night is not dated on the supplied practical sheet.
</Warning>

## Условие
