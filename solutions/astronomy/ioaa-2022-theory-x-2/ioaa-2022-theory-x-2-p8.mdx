*International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics 2022 Georgia — Theoretical Competition · 16 август 2022 г. · 20 т.*

## Условие

We are given a flat disk of mass M with inner radius $r$ and the outer radius $R$.

**(a)** A point mass $m$ is located on the symmetry axis of the disk at a distance $x$ from the plane of the disk (We assume throughout that mass $m$ stays on the symmetry axis, and is free to move in the vertical direction.). What is the gravitational force exerted on the point mass?

**(b)** what will be the frequency of the small oscillations of the system ($x \ll r$). **[10 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **(a)** $F_{\perp} = \frac{2GMmx}{R^2 - r^2}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 + r^2}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 + R^2}}\right)$
- **(b)** $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{Rr(R + r)}{2G(M + m)}}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

(a) Consider small surface element ($\Delta S$) of the disk. The gravitational field ($\Delta E$) generated by this surface element at at a point $O$ located at a distance $a$ from $\Delta S$ (see the figure below):

$$
\Delta E = G\frac{\sigma\Delta S}{a^2}
$$

where $\sigma$ is a surface density of the mass.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '43.2%', '--fig-max': '276px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ioaa-2022-theory-x-2/p8-sol-fig1.png" alt="An irregular grey surface labelled σ with a small element dS; a dashed normal rises from dS, and a thin cone dΩ goes from dS to a point O above right; the angle α is marked between the normal and the direction to O." width="864" height="565" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

where $\alpha$ is the angle between surface normal and the vector $\vec{r}$ and $\Delta\Omega$ is infinitesimal solid angle corresponding to the area $\Delta S$.

For a ring, the surface density will be given by $\sigma = M/\pi(R^2 - r^2)$.

As the horizontal component will get cancelled due to symmetry considerations, we only consider the normal component:

$$
\Delta E_{\perp} = \Delta E cos(\alpha) = \frac{G\sigma}{a^2}\Delta S cos(\alpha) = \frac{G\sigma}{a^2}\Delta S_{\perp} = G\sigma\Delta\Omega \qquad \mathbf{5.0}
$$

By Summation, we can obtain total normal field of the surface:

$$
E_{\perp} = G\sigma\Omega \qquad \mathbf{1.0}
$$

here $\Omega$ is the total solid angle subtended by the disk. In our case, when we observe disk from the point of view of the test particle:

$$
\begin{aligned} \Omega &= 2\pi\left(1 - \cos\theta_{out}\right) - 2\pi\left(1 - \cos\theta_{in}\right) \\ &= 2\pi\left(1 - \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + R^2}}\right) - 2\pi\left(1 - \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + r^2}}\right) \\ \Omega &= 2\pi x\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 + r^2}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 + R^2}}\right) && \mathbf{3.0} \\ \therefore F_{\perp} &= G\sigma\Omega m \\ F_{\perp} &= \frac{2GMmx}{R^2 - r^2}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 + r^2}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 + R^2}}\right) && \mathbf{1.0} \end{aligned}
$$

(b) In small displacement approximation:

$$
F_{\perp} \approx \frac{2GMmx}{R^2 - r^2}\left(\frac{1}{r} - \frac{1}{R}\right) = \left(\frac{2GMm}{Rr(R + r)}\right)x = kx \qquad \mathbf{3.0}
$$

when the distance between the disk and the point mass is $x$, the distance of the center of mass of the system from the center of the disk will be:

$$
x_1 = \frac{mx}{m + M} \qquad \mathbf{2.5}
$$

If we choose the center of mass of the system as an origin of our coordinate system, then equation of motion of the disk will have a form:

$$
\begin{aligned} Ma_1 &= -kx \\ Ma_1 + k\frac{m + M}{m}x_1 &= 0 && \mathbf{2.5} \end{aligned}
$$

For this simple harmonic oscillator, the period of small oscillations will be:

$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{Rr(R + r)}{2G(M + m)}} \qquad \mathbf{2.0}
$$


</Spoiler>
