*International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics 2022 Georgia — Theoretical Competition · 16 август 2022 г. · 10 т.*

## Условие

A planetary nebula, located $100\,\mathrm{pc}$ from the Earth, has the shape of a perfect circular ring with an inner radius of $7.0'$ and an outer radius of $8.0'$. Its luminosity is powered by the UV radiation from the white dwarf remnant at the center of the nebula. From other observations, we know that 2000 years ago the inner radius of the nebula was $3.5'$ and outer radius was $4.0'$. We believe that throughout these 2000 years the evolution of the nebula obeys a free expansion scenario, so gravity is negligible and the expansion velocity remains constant with time. Assume that all material in the planetary nebula was ejected at the same instant, but different gas particles have different velocities.

**(a)** Estimate the range of velocities of the gas particles **[4 т.]**

**(b)** Is the assumption of free expansion justified? Write **YES** or **NO** alongside appropriate calculations. **[3 т.]**

**(c)** If this planetary nebula is bright enough, would an astronaut aboard the International Space Station be able to resolve the thickness of the shell clearly? Write **YES** or **NO** alongside with necessary calculations. **[3 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **(a)** from $50.0\,\mathrm{km/s}$ to $57.0\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- **(b)** YES
- **(c)** YES

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

(a) The distance traveled by the gas particles of inner and outer radius can be calculated as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned} S_{out} &= d_{\oplus}(\theta'_{\mathrm{out}} - \theta_{\mathrm{out}}) = 100pc \cdot (8.0' - 4.0') = 24 \times 10^3\,\mathrm{au} \\ S_{in} &= d_{\oplus}(\theta'_{\mathrm{in}} - \theta_{\mathrm{in}}) = 100pc \cdot (7.0' - 3.5') = 21 \times 10^3\,\mathrm{au} && \mathbf{2.0} \\ \therefore v_{max} &= \frac{S_{out}}{t_0} = 57.0\,\mathrm{km/s} \\ v_{min} &= \frac{S_{in}}{t_0} = 50.0\,\mathrm{km/s} && \mathbf{2.0} \end{aligned}
$$

(b) At the inner edge, escape velocity from the white dwarf can be maximal ($1.44M_{\odot}$- Chandrasekhar limit)

$$
v_{esc} = \sqrt{\frac{2G \cdot 1.44M_{\odot}}{S_{in}}} \approx 0.35\,\mathrm{km/s} \qquad \mathbf{2.0}
$$

Since $v_{esc} \ll v_{min}$, the no-gravity scenario can be applied. YES **1.0**

(c)

$$
\begin{aligned} \phi &= 8' - 7.5' = 0.5' = 30'' \geq \frac{1.22\lambda}{D} \\ \therefore D &\geq \frac{1.22\lambda}{\phi} = \frac{1.22 \times 5 \times 10^{-7} \times 206265}{30} = 4.19\,\mathrm{mm} && \mathbf{2.0} \end{aligned}
$$

Hence YES **1.0**


</Spoiler>
