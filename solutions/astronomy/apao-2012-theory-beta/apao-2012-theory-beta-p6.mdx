## Условие

Engineers of Bangladesh created a new planet Bangladeya from a part of the Earth substance. Total area of surface of Bangladeya equals to the area of Bangladesh country ($S=147\ 600\ \mathrm{km}^2$). The planet is rotating around the Sun by circular orbit so that the mean climate on Bangladeya is the same as in Bangladesh country (the average temperature is 25.6 °C that is 10.5 degrees higher than average on the Earth).

**6.1.** Estimate the acceleration of gravity at the planet Bangladeya.

**6.2.** Calculate the duration (in Earth days) of the year on the planet Bangladeya.
