## Условие

4.  **Constellation of White Leopard.** According to an ancient legend of Middle Volga there was a constellation called White Leopard (Белый Барс – Pardus Album) in the sky in the very past, in which the number of stars were exactly equal to the number of letters in the Greek alphabet, and the stars had magnitudes $\alpha$ PaA – $+0.10^{m}$, $\beta$ PaA – $+0.20^{m}$, $\gamma$ PaA – $+0.30^{m}$, $\delta$ PaA – $+0.40^{m}$, and so on with adding $0.10^{m}$ till $\omega$ PaA. Calculate the total magnitude of the stars of this constellation.
