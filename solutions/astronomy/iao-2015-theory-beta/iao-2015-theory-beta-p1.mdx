## Условие

1.  **Noon at the Olympiad.** Yesterday, on October 16, 2015, the upper culmination of the Sun at the venue of the Olympiad was at 11:29:43 local time. Calculate as accurate as possible at what time the upper culmination of the Sun will be (or was) today.

Estimate the difference $\Delta h$ in the height of the Sun at the culminations yesterday and today.
