*III International Astronomy Olympiad*

## Условие

Suppose that the Sun collapsed suddenly to a black hole. How would the Earth's orbital period be affected?

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

If the Sun does not eject mass and does not irradiate gravitational waves during the collapse, its mass doesn't change. So the Earth orbit doesn't change either.


</Spoiler>
