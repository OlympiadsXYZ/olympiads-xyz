*III International Astronomy Olympiad*

## Условие

It is known that the equatorial coordinates of the vernal equinox point are $0\,\mathrm{hr}$ and $0\,\mathrm{deg}$. What are the coordinates of the north ecliptic pole?

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Taking into account the value of inclination of the Earth rotation axis ($\varepsilon=23.5^\circ$) we can see that the North ecliptic pole coordinates are: right ascension of $18^{\mathrm h}$ and declination of $66.5^\circ$.


</Spoiler>
