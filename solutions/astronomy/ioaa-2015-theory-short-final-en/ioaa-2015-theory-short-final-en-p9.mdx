*SHORT PROBLEMS*

## Условие

Assume that the Sun is a perfect blackbody. Venus is also assumed to be a blackbody, with temperature $T_\mathrm{V}$, and it is in thermal equilibrium (i.e. it is radiating about as much energy as it receives from the Sun) at its orbital distance of 0.72 AU. Suppose that at closest approach to Earth, Venus has an angular diameter of about 66 arcsec. What is the flux density of Venus at the closest approach to Earth as observed by a radio telescope at an observing frequency of 5 GHz?

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 80.7 Jy

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**[Add the formula on the Table of Constants (also mentioning that the Rayleigh-Jeans formula is a derivation from Planck’s formula)]**

**Answer and Marking Scheme:**

**1** Solar luminosity is

$L_\odot = 4\pi R_\odot^2\sigma T_\odot^4$

$= 4\times3.14\times(6.96\times10^{10})^2\,(5.67\times10^{-5})(5780)^4$ ergs/sec

$= 3.9\times10^{33}$ ergs/sec

**[10]**

**2** Flux of the Sun intercepted by Venus is

$L_V = (A_{V(projected)}/A_{(sphere,0.72au)})\times L_\odot$

Also,

$L_V = 4\pi R_V^2\sigma T_V^4$

So,

$4\pi R_V^2\sigma T_V^4 = (\pi R_V^2/4\pi d^2)\times L_\odot = (\pi R_V^2/4\pi d^2)\times4\pi R_\odot^2\sigma T_\odot^4$

**[30]**

**3** Therefore,

$T_V^4 = (R_\odot^2/4d^2)\times T_\odot^4 = [(6.957\times10^{5}\ \mathrm{km})^2/4\times(0.72\times149.6\times10^{6})^2]\times(5780)^4$

$= (483998490000/46407499776000000)\times(5780)^4 = (4.84\times10^{11}/4.64\times10^{16})\times(5780)^4$

$= 1.0429316216908190111241385226915\mathrm{e}{-5}\times1114577187760656$

$= 1.04\times10^{-5}\times1.11\times10^{15}$

$= 11624277939.308134327737156481455 = 1.1624\times10^{10}$

$T_V = 328.5$ K

**[20]**

**4** The flux density of Venus at closest approach to Earth at an observing frequency of 5 GHz:

($1'' = 4.84\times10^{-6}$ rad)

$S_{5GHz} = B_{5GHz}\,\Omega = (2kT_b\nu^2/c^2)\,\Omega \qquad (\rightarrow T_b = T_V)$

$= (2\times1.38\times10^{-16}\times328.5\times(5\times10^{9})^2\times\pi\,(66\times4.84\times10^{-6})^2)/(3\times10^{10})^2$

$= 8.07\times10^{-22}$ ergs s$^{-1}$ cm$^{-2}$ Hz$^{-1}$

$= 80.7$ Jy

**[40]**


</Spoiler>

13. Two protons collide to produce deuteron, positron, and neutrino in the center of a star. Write down the reaction equation and calculate the minimum temperature needed for this reaction. Assume that the neutrino mass is negligible.

Answer

**1** $p + p + \Delta E \rightarrow D + e^{+} + \nu_e$ **[20]**

**2** $\Delta E = (m_e + m_D - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + (m_p + m_n) - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + m_n - m_p)c^2$ **[20]**

**3** $\Delta E = (9.109\times10^{-31} + 1.675\times10^{-27} - 1.673\times10^{-27})\mathrm{kg}\,(2.99792\times10^{8}\mathrm{ms^{-1}})^2 = 2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J$ **[20]**

**4** $\Delta E \sim k_BT \rightarrow T \sim \frac{\Delta E}{k_B}$ **[20]**

**5** $T = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J}{8.315\frac{J}{mol\ K}} = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}mol\ K}{8.315}\times6.02\times10^{23}\frac{1}{mol} = 1.894\times10^{10}\ K$ **[20]**

</details>
