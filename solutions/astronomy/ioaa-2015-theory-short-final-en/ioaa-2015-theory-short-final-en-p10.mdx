*SHORT PROBLEMS*

## Условие

A molecular hydrogen cloud is known to have a temperature *T* = 115 K. The hydrogen atoms (assumed spherical) have (covalent) radius $r_H = 0.37\ x\ 10^{-10} m$ and the separation centre-to-centre distance between the two atoms is $d_{H_2} = 0.74\ x\ 10^{-10} m$. Assume that the molecules are in thermal equilibrium. Estimate the frequency at which they will radiate due to molecular rotational excitation.

> A molecular hydrogen cloud is known to have a temperature *T* = 115 K. If the atoms (assumed spherical) in this cloud have masses of about $5\times10^{-23}$ *g* and radii of 3.5 Å, and the separation distance between the hydrogen atoms is [Placeholder]. Assume that the molecules are homogenous and in thermal equilibrium. Estimate the frequency at which they will radiate due to molecular rotational excitation.
>
> **[Add the separation distance between hydrogen atom.]**

**Answer and Marking Scheme:**

**1** The molecules rotate with angular velocity ω and for spherical molecules, their moment of inertia is $I = \frac{2}{5}mr^2$, i.e., **[The formula of the moment of inertia will be corrected accordingly]**

$I = \frac{2}{5}mr^2 = \frac{2}{5}(5\times10^{-23}\ \mathrm{g})(3.5\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{cm})^2 = 2.45\times10^{-38}\ \mathrm{g\ cm^{-2}}$

**[30]**

**2** In thermal equilibrium, we have: **[Change formula into: kT]**

$\frac{1}{2}I\omega^2 = 3/2\ kT$

**[30]**

**3** So we can estimate:

$\omega = (3kT/I)^{1/2} = (3\times1.38\times10^{-16}\ 115/2.45\times10^{-38})^{1/2} = 1.39\times10^{12}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$

$\nu = \omega/2\pi = 2.2\times10^{11}$ Hz

**[40]**

13. Two protons collide to produce deuteron, positron, and neutrino in the center of a star. Write down the reaction equation and calculate the minimum temperature needed for this reaction. Assume that the neutrino mass is negligible.

Answer

**1** $p + p + \Delta E \rightarrow D + e^{+} + \nu_e$ **[20]**

**2** $\Delta E = (m_e + m_D - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + (m_p + m_n) - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + m_n - m_p)c^2$ **[20]**

**3** $\Delta E = (9.109\times10^{-31} + 1.675\times10^{-27} - 1.673\times10^{-27})\mathrm{kg}\,(2.99792\times10^{8}\mathrm{ms^{-1}})^2 = 2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J$ **[20]**

**4** $\Delta E \sim k_BT \rightarrow T \sim \frac{\Delta E}{k_B}$ **[20]**

**5** $T = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J}{8.315\frac{J}{mol\ K}} = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}mol\ K}{8.315}\times6.02\times10^{23}\frac{1}{mol} = 1.894\times10^{10}\ K$ **[20]**

</details>
