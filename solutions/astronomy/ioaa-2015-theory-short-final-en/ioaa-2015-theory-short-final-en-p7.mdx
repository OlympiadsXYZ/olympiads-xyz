*SHORT PROBLEMS*

## Условие

A galaxy at the boundary of a galaxy cluster of radius 10 Mpc is expected to escape from the cluster if it has an initial velocity of at least 700 km/s relative to the center of the cluster. Calculate the density of the cluster.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- $9.20121 \times 10^{-27}$ kg/m³

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Answer and Marking Scheme:**

**1** We have the total energy of the escaping galaxy:

$E_T = E_K + E_P = \frac{mV^2}{2} - \frac{GMm}{R}$

**[15]**

**2** Where $V$ is velocity of galaxy and $R$ radius of galaxy cluster

Galaxy will escape from galaxy cluster if the total energy, $E_T = 0$

**[10]**

**3** $E_T = 0 \rightarrow \frac{mV^2}{2} - \frac{GMm}{R} = 0$

$V^2 = \frac{2GM}{R}$

**[15]**

**4** Escape velocity

$$
V^2 = \frac{2G\left(\frac{4\pi}{3}R^3\rho\right)}{R}
$$

**[20]**

**5** Density of cluster

$$
\rho = \frac{3V^2}{8\pi GR^2}
$$

**[15]**

**6** V=700 km/s

R=10 Mpc

$G=6.6378\times10^{-11}\mathrm{Nm^2kg}$

$\rho=9.20121\times10^{-27}\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

**[25]**


</Spoiler>

13. Two protons collide to produce deuteron, positron, and neutrino in the center of a star. Write down the reaction equation and calculate the minimum temperature needed for this reaction. Assume that the neutrino mass is negligible.

Answer

**1** $p + p + \Delta E \rightarrow D + e^{+} + \nu_e$ **[20]**

**2** $\Delta E = (m_e + m_D - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + (m_p + m_n) - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + m_n - m_p)c^2$ **[20]**

**3** $\Delta E = (9.109\times10^{-31} + 1.675\times10^{-27} - 1.673\times10^{-27})\mathrm{kg}\,(2.99792\times10^{8}\mathrm{ms^{-1}})^2 = 2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J$ **[20]**

**4** $\Delta E \sim k_BT \rightarrow T \sim \frac{\Delta E}{k_B}$ **[20]**

**5** $T = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J}{8.315\frac{J}{mol\ K}} = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}mol\ K}{8.315}\times6.02\times10^{23}\frac{1}{mol} = 1.894\times10^{10}\ K$ **[20]**
