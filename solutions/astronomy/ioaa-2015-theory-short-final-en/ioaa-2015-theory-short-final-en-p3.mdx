*SHORT PROBLEMS*

## Условие

On 27 May 2015 at 02:18:49, the occultation of the star HIP 89931 ($\delta$ −Sgr) by the asteroid 1285 Julietta was observed from Borobudur temple, which was located at the center of the asteroid shadow path. It lasted for only 6.201 s. Assume that Earth’s orbit is circular and the orbit of Julietta is on the ecliptic plane and revolves in the same direction as Earth. At the occultation, Julietta is near its aphelion. At the time of the occultation the distances of Julietta from the Sun and the Earth are 3.076 AU and 2.156 AU respectively. Find the approximate diameter of asteroid Julietta, if the semi major axis of Julietta is $a = 2.9914$ AU.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Answer and Marking Scheme:**

<ProblemSection id="solution-step-1">

### 1 Calculate the Earth revolution speed

**[20]**

Earth revolution speed is

$$
v_E = \frac{2\pi\bar{r}_E}{T_E} = \frac{2\pi a_E}{T_E} = 29805\ m/s
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-step-2">

### 2 Calculate Julietta revolution speed

**[25]**

Julietta revolution is (since the given data is semimajor axis, the orbit of Julietta is ellipse)

$$
v_J = \sqrt{GM\left(\frac{2}{r_J} - \frac{1}{a}\right)} = 16742{,}9\ m/s
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-step-3">

### 3 Calculate the approximate dimension of Julietta

From the geometry

$$
\cos\alpha = \frac{r_E^2 + r_J^2 - r_{J-E}^2}{2r_Er_J} = 0.995
$$

Thus, Julietta’s relative projected speed to Earth is

$$
v_{J,rel} = v_E - v_J\cos\alpha = 13984.16\ m/s
$$

**[40]**

Hence, the approximate dimension of Julietta is

$$
l_J = v_{J,rel}t_{occil} = 86701.8\ m
$$

**[15]**

13. Two protons collide to produce deuteron, positron, and neutrino in the center of a star. Write down the reaction equation and calculate the minimum temperature needed for this reaction. Assume that the neutrino mass is negligible.

Answer

**1** $p + p + \Delta E \rightarrow D + e^{+} + \nu_e$ **[20]**

**2** $\Delta E = (m_e + m_D - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + (m_p + m_n) - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + m_n - m_p)c^2$ **[20]**

**3** $\Delta E = (9.109\times10^{-31} + 1.675\times10^{-27} - 1.673\times10^{-27})\mathrm{kg}\,(2.99792\times10^{8}\mathrm{ms^{-1}})^2 = 2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J$ **[20]**

**4** $\Delta E \sim k_BT \rightarrow T \sim \frac{\Delta E}{k_B}$ **[20]**

**5** $T = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J}{8.315\frac{J}{mol\ K}} = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}mol\ K}{8.315}\times6.02\times10^{23}\frac{1}{mol} = 1.894\times10^{10}\ K$ **[20]**
