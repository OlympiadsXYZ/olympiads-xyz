*SHORT PROBLEMS*

## Условие

An observer is trying to determine an approximate value of the orbital eccentricity of a man-made satellite. When the satellite was at apogee, it was observed to have moved by $\Delta\theta_1 = 2'44''$ in a short time. When the radius vector connecting Earth and the satellite is perpendicular to the major axis (true anomaly is equal to $90^{\circ}$), within the same duration of time, it was observed to have moved by $\Delta\theta_2 = 21'17''$. Assume that the observer is located at the center of the Earth. Find an approximate value of the eccentricity of the satellite’s orbit.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 0.64

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Answer and Marking Scheme:**

**1 Identify expression for $r(\theta)$.** **[30]**

We know that $r(\theta) = \frac{a(1-e^2)}{1-e\cos\theta}$. Hence

$$
r(0) = \frac{a(1-e^2)}{1-e} = a(1+e)
$$

$$
r\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = \frac{a(1-e^2)}{1-e\times0} = a(1-e^2)
$$

**2 Estimate area of sectors using area of triangles.** **[20]**

Estimate the area of swept out sectors as the area of sectors of circles

$$
A(S_1) \approx 0.5\times\Delta\theta_1\times(r(0))^2 = \Delta\theta_1(a(1+e))^2
$$

$$
A(S_2) \approx 0.5\times\Delta\theta_2\times\left(r\left(\tfrac{\pi}{2}\right)\right)^2 = \Delta\theta_2(a(1-e^2))^2
$$

**3 Use Kepler’s second law to find a relation between the ratio $\frac{\Delta\theta_1}{\Delta\theta_2}$ and the eccentricity $e$.** **[30]**

Use Kepler’s second law to obtain that

$$
A(S_1) = A(S_2)
$$

$$
\Delta\theta_1\times(a(1+e))^2 = \Delta\theta_2\times(a(1-e^2))^2
$$

Thus,

$$
\frac{\Delta\theta_1}{\Delta\theta_2} = \left(\frac{1-e^2}{1+e}\right)^2 = (1-e)^2 = \frac{2'44''}{21'17''} \approx 0.12843
$$

**4 Obtain an estimate value of the eccentricity.** **[20]**

Thus, the eccentricity is $e \approx 1 - \sqrt{0.12843} = 0.64$

13. Two protons collide to produce deuteron, positron, and neutrino in the center of a star. Write down the reaction equation and calculate the minimum temperature needed for this reaction. Assume that the neutrino mass is negligible.

Answer

**1** $p + p + \Delta E \rightarrow D + e^{+} + \nu_e$ **[20]**

**2** $\Delta E = (m_e + m_D - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + (m_p + m_n) - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + m_n - m_p)c^2$ **[20]**

**3** $\Delta E = (9.109\times10^{-31} + 1.675\times10^{-27} - 1.673\times10^{-27})\mathrm{kg}\,(2.99792\times10^{8}\mathrm{ms^{-1}})^2 = 2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J$ **[20]**

**4** $\Delta E \sim k_BT \rightarrow T \sim \frac{\Delta E}{k_B}$ **[20]**

**5** $T = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J}{8.315\frac{J}{mol\ K}} = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}mol\ K}{8.315}\times6.02\times10^{23}\frac{1}{mol} = 1.894\times10^{10}\ K$ **[20]**
