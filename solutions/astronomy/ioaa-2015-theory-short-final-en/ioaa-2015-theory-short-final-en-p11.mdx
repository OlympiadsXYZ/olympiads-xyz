*SHORT PROBLEMS*

## Условие

The mass density of an object is inversely proportional to the radial distance from the center of the object with a factor of proportionality $\alpha = 5.0\times10^{13}\ kg/m^2$. If the escape velocity at the surface of the object is $v_0 = 1.5\times10^{4}\ m/s$ , calculate the total mass of the object.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- $9.1 \times 10^{21}$ kg

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Answer**

**1** $\frac{1}{2}mv_0^{\ 2} - \frac{GmM}{R} = 0 \rightarrow R = \frac{2GM}{v_0^{\ 2}}$

**[20]**

**2** $\Delta m(r) = \rho4\pi r^2\Delta r = 4\pi\alpha r\Delta r \rightarrow M = 2\pi\alpha R^2$

$(dm(r) = \rho4\pi r^2dr = 4\pi\alpha rdr \rightarrow M = 2\pi\alpha R^2)$

**[30]**

**3** $M = \alpha2\pi R^2 = \alpha2\pi\frac{4G^2M^2}{v_0^{\ 4}} \rightarrow M = \frac{v_0^{\ 4}}{8\pi\alpha G^2}$

**[30]**

**4** $M = \frac{v_0^{\ 4}}{8\pi\alpha G^2} = \frac{(1.5\times10^{4}\ ms^{-1})^4}{8\pi(5\times10^{13}\ kgm^{-2})(6.67\times10^{-11}\mathrm{kg^{-1}m^3s^{-2}})^2} = 9.1\times10^{21}\ kg$

**[20]**


</Spoiler>

13. Two protons collide to produce deuteron, positron, and neutrino in the center of a star. Write down the reaction equation and calculate the minimum temperature needed for this reaction. Assume that the neutrino mass is negligible.

Answer

**1** $p + p + \Delta E \rightarrow D + e^{+} + \nu_e$ **[20]**

**2** $\Delta E = (m_e + m_D - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + (m_p + m_n) - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + m_n - m_p)c^2$ **[20]**

**3** $\Delta E = (9.109\times10^{-31} + 1.675\times10^{-27} - 1.673\times10^{-27})\mathrm{kg}\,(2.99792\times10^{8}\mathrm{ms^{-1}})^2 = 2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J$ **[20]**

**4** $\Delta E \sim k_BT \rightarrow T \sim \frac{\Delta E}{k_B}$ **[20]**

**5** $T = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J}{8.315\frac{J}{mol\ K}} = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}mol\ K}{8.315}\times6.02\times10^{23}\frac{1}{mol} = 1.894\times10^{10}\ K$ **[20]**

</details>
