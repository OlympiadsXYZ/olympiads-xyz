*SHORT PROBLEMS*

## Условие

Several exoplanets have been observed in the Gliese 876 system $(\mathrm{M_G} = 0.33 \pm 0.03\mathrm{M_\odot})$ as given in the following table,

| Gliese System | Mass | Semi Major Axis (AU) |
|---|---|---|
| Gliese 876 b | $2.276\ \mathrm{M_J}$ | 0.2083 |
| Gliese 876 c | $0.714\ \mathrm{M_J}$ | 0.1296 |
| Gliese 876 d | $6.8\ \mathrm{M_\oplus}$ | 0.0208 |
| Gliese 876 e | $15\ \mathrm{M_\oplus}$ | 0.334 |

where $\mathrm{M_\odot}$ is mass of Sun, $\mathrm{M_J}$ is mass of Jupiter ($M_J = 1.89813\times10^{27}\ kg$), and $\mathrm{M_\oplus}$ is mass of Earth. Assume that all these planets revolve around Gliese 876 in the same direction. Two planets are said to be in resonant orbits if the synodic period of one planet with respect to the other planet is an integer multiple of the orbital period of the second planet.

Find if any of the exoplanets of Gliese 876 system may have resonant orbits.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- Gliese 876 c and Gliese 876 b have resonant orbit (m = 1.96 ≈ 2); Gliese 876 b and Gliese 876 e have resonant orbit (m = 1.9679 ≈ 2).

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Answer and Marking Scheme:**

**1 Determine planet periods** **[50]**

Synodic period of planet 1 and 2 (assume $P_1 < P_2$) is $\frac{1}{P_S} = \frac{1}{P_1} - \frac{1}{P_2} \;\rightarrow\; P_S = \frac{P_1P_2}{P_2 - P_1}$

Resonant orbit happens if

$$
P_S = mP_1 \;\rightarrow\; \frac{P_1P_2}{P_2 - P_1} = mP_1 \;\rightarrow\; m = \frac{P_2}{P_2 - P_1} > 1,\ \mathrm{integer}
$$

Using the data of the Gliesean system we have

| Gliese System | Mass (kg) | Semi Major Axis (m) | Period (Earth, days) $P = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{a^3}{GM_G}}$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gliese 876 star | $6.64359\times10^{29}$ | | |
| Gliese 876 b | $4.31938\times10^{27}$ | $3.1161\times10^{10}$ | 60.07568 |
| Gliese 876 c | $1.35564\times10^{27}$ | $1.9388\times10^{10}$ | 29.48304 |
| Gliese 876 d | $4.079\times10^{25}$ | $3.1116\times10^{9}$ | 1.8956 |
| Gliese 876 e | $8.7194\times10^{25}$ | $5.0011\times10^{10}$ | 122.1432 |

**2 Calculate resonant orbit for each planet**

From the results above we see that

- Gliese 876 c and Gliese 876 b have resonant orbit

$$
m = \frac{P_b}{P_b - P_c} = 1.96 \approx 2
$$

**[25]**

- Gliese 876 b and Gliese 876 e have resonant orbit

$$
m = \frac{P_e}{P_e - P_b} = 1.9679 \approx 2
$$

13. Two protons collide to produce deuteron, positron, and neutrino in the center of a star. Write down the reaction equation and calculate the minimum temperature needed for this reaction. Assume that the neutrino mass is negligible.

Answer

**1** $p + p + \Delta E \rightarrow D + e^{+} + \nu_e$ **[20]**

**2** $\Delta E = (m_e + m_D - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + (m_p + m_n) - 2m_p)c^2 = (m_e + m_n - m_p)c^2$ **[20]**

**3** $\Delta E = (9.109\times10^{-31} + 1.675\times10^{-27} - 1.673\times10^{-27})\mathrm{kg}\,(2.99792\times10^{8}\mathrm{ms^{-1}})^2 = 2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J$ **[20]**

**4** $\Delta E \sim k_BT \rightarrow T \sim \frac{\Delta E}{k_B}$ **[20]**

**5** $T = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}\ J}{8.315\frac{J}{mol\ K}} = \frac{2.6198\times10^{-13}mol\ K}{8.315}\times6.02\times10^{23}\frac{1}{mol} = 1.894\times10^{10}\ K$ **[20]**
