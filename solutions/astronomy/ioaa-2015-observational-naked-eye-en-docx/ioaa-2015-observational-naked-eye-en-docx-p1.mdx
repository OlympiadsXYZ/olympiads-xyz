*OBSERVATIONAL QUESTIONS — Naked Eye*

<Warning title="Бележка към темата">
The six numbered tasks share one answer chart and are preserved as one complete observing exercise. The printed task points total 100, but no overall total is printed. The 60-minute duration in the general instructions is shared by naked-eye and small-telescope observations. The Messier table prints M20 twice with different data, and the answer maps print Nova Sagitarii; both are retained. The official key numbers the five brightest stellar positions but supplies no explicit list of their names. For part 6 it gives angular tolerances only, without a numerical Nunki–Shaula separation.
</Warning>

Naked Eye

<ProblemSection id="part-1">

### 1. (10 т.)

In the blank square given in the answer sheet, you should assume that Nova Sagittarii 2 is in the center. (marked by green star)

RA(J2000) : 18h 36m 56.84s

Dec(J2000) : -28$^{\circ}$ 55’ 39.8”

Draw Declination and Right Ascension lines passing through Nova Sagittarii. Also draw parallel gridlines at convenient interval and label the accordingly. (The field of view is 45$^{\circ}$)

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="part-2">

### 2. (25 т.)

Mark with filled circle, the five brightest stars in the field of view on the answer sheet along with their names. You can add several other stars for your orientation.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="part-3">

### 3. (10 т.)

Draw an unfilled circle representing the Moon’s position tomorrow night (on 29 July 2015).

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="part-4">

### 4. (25 т.)

Mark the position of any five Messier Objects in the field of view by cross (x), and write down their names beside their marks.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="part-5">

### 5. (10 т.)

Draw a line representing the galactic plane.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="part-6">

### 6. (20 т.)

Mark the positions of Nunki ($\sigma$ Sgr) and Shaula ($\lambda$ Sco) by star (*). Draw a line connecting the two stars. Estimate the angular distance between these stars and write it on the line connecting both stars.

</ProblemSection>
