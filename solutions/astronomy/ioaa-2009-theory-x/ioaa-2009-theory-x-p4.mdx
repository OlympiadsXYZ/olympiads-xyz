## Условие

In a typical Persian architecture, on top of south side windows there is a structure called "Tabeshband" (shader), which controls sunlight in summer and winter. In summer when the Sun is high, Tabeshband prevents sunlight to enter rooms and keeps inside cooler. In the modern architecture it is verified that the Tabeshband saves about 20% of energy cost. Figure (1) shows a vertical section of this design at latitude of $36.0^\circ\mathrm N$ with window and Tabeshband.

Using the parameters given in the figure, calculate the maximum width of the Tabeshband, "$x$", and maximum height of the window, "$h$" in such a way that:

1. No direct sunlight can enter to the room in the summer solstice at noon.
2. The direct sunlight reaches the end of the room (indicated by the point A in the figure) in the winter solstice at noon.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Zenith angle of the Sun at noon in summer solstice is

$$
z_s=\varphi-23.5^\circ=12.5^\circ.
$$

(1.5 points)

Zenith angle of the Sun at noon in winter solstice is

$$
z_w=\varphi+23.5^\circ=59.5^\circ.
$$

(1.5 points)

In summer:

$$
\tan z_s=\frac{x}{h}=0.22.
$$

(1.5 points)

In winter:

$$
\tan z_w=\frac{D+x}{H}=1.70.
$$

(1.5 points)

$$
x=1.70H-D=0.60\ \mathrm m.
$$

(2 points)

$$
h=2.73\ \mathrm m.
$$

(2 points)
