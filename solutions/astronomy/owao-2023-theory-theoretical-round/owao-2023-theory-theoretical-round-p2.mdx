*ΩWAO 2023 ◇ Theoretical Round ◇ Problems*

## Условие

Currently, a star has a proper motion of $0.5''$/year with a parallax of $0.08''$. The hydrogen H$\alpha$ line in the stellar spectrum is observed at wavelength $\lambda = 6561.0$ Å. Assume the star’s velocity vector to be constant. Estimate the radial velocity of the star in 20 000 years.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- -81 km/s

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

First, we determine the current distance to the star from the known parallax $\varpi$:

$$
r = \frac{1}{\varpi\,['']} = 12.5\ \mathrm{pc}.
$$

Then, let us find the current tangential velocity of the star. It is quite convenient to use the ratio $1\ \mathrm{au} = 1\ \mathrm{pc} \times 1''$:

$$
V_{\tau,1} = \mu\,[''/\mathrm{yr}] \cdot r\,[\mathrm{pc}] = 0.5 \times \frac{1}{0.08}\,[\mathrm{au/yr}] = 6.25\ [\mathrm{au/yr}] = 29.6\ [\mathrm{km/s}]. \qquad {}^{2}
$$

Next, we estimate the current radial velocity. According to the Doppler effect formula

$$
\frac{V_{r,1}}{c} = \frac{\lambda_{\mathrm{obs}} - \lambda_0}{\lambda_0} \quad\Rightarrow\quad V_{r,1} = c \cdot \frac{\lambda_{\mathrm{obs}} - \lambda_0}{\lambda_0} = 3 \cdot 10^5 \times \frac{6561.0 - 6562.8}{6562.8} = -82.3\ \mathrm{km/s}.
$$

The total velocity is

$$
V = \sqrt{V_{\tau,1}^2 + V_{r,1}^2} = \sqrt{82.3^2 + 29.6^2} = 87\ \mathrm{km/s}.
$$

In 20 000 years, the star will cover a distance of

$$
|M_1M_2| = 87\ \mathrm{km/s} \times 20\,000 \cdot (365.25 \times 24 \times 60 \times 60)\ \mathrm{s} = 5.5 \cdot 10^{13}\ \mathrm{km} = 1.8\ \mathrm{pc}.
$$

$S$ denotes the Sun, $M_1$ is the current position of the star and $M_2$ is the position of the star in future. Let us write the law of cosines for $\triangle M_1M_2S$:

$$
|M_2S|^2 = |M_1M_2|^2 + |M_1S|^2 - 2 \cdot |M_1M_2| \cdot |M_1S| \cos\gamma
$$

where $|M_1S| = \dfrac{1}{\varpi\,['']} = 12.5$ pc, $\gamma = \arccos\dfrac{V_{r,1}}{V} = 19^{\circ}$.

Consequently,

$$
|M_2S|^2 = 1.8^2 + 12.5^2 - 2 \cdot 1.8 \cdot 12.5 \cos 19^{\circ} \Rightarrow |M_2S| = 10.8\ \mathrm{pc}.
$$

Next, we write the law of cosines for the same triangle:

$$
|M_1S|^2 = |M_1M_2|^2 + |M_2S|^2 - 2 \cdot |M_1M_2| \cdot |M_2S| \cos\theta;
$$

$$
\cos\theta = \frac{|M_1M_2|^2 + |M_2S|^2 - |M_1S|^2}{2 \cdot |M_1M_2| \cdot |M_2S|} = \frac{1.8^2 + 10.8^2 - 12.5^2}{2 \cdot 1.8 \cdot 10.8} = -0.935 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \theta = 159^{\circ}.
$$

The radial velocity is

$$
V_{r,2} = V\cos\theta = 87 \cdot \cos 159^{\circ} = -81\ \mathrm{km/s}.
$$
