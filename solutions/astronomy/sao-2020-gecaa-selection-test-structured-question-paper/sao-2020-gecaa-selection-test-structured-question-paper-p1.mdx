*GeCAA Selection Test Structured Question Paper · 4 т.*

## Условие

Suppose we have an equation of state for stars:

$$
P = A\rho^{\beta} \qquad (1)
$$

where $P$ is the pressure, $\rho$ is the density, and $A$ is a constant. Such a solution is called a *polytrope*, and one can **estimate** an approximate relationship between the mass $M$ and density $\rho$ of the form $M \propto R^{\alpha}$.

Find $\alpha$. State any assumptions you made.

*Hint: The hydrostatic equilibrium condition given by $\frac{dP}{dr} = -\frac{GM_r\rho_r}{r^2}$ may be useful.*

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- $\alpha = \frac{3\beta - 4}{\beta - 2}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

As a (rather) crude estimation, one may take

$$
\frac{dP}{dr} \sim \frac{\Delta P}{\Delta r} \sim \frac{-P}{R} = -\frac{GM_r\rho_r}{R^2}
$$

**M1**

We then obtain the scaling

$$
P = \frac{GM\rho}{R} \sim \rho^2 R^2 = A\rho^{\beta}
$$

$$
\rho = R^{\frac{2}{\beta - 2}}
$$

**M2**

$$
M \sim \rho R^3 \sim R^{\frac{3\beta - 4}{\beta - 2}}
$$

**A1**

$$
\alpha = \frac{3\beta - 4}{\beta - 2}
$$


</Spoiler>
