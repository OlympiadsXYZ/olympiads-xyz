*GeCAA Selection Test Structured Question Paper · 7 т.*

## Условие

In a flat, **matter-dominated** universe, the scale factor $a$ is described by the equation:

$$
a = \left(\frac{t}{t_0}\right)^{2/3} \qquad (2)
$$

where $t_0$ is the present age of the universe, at which $a = 1$. A spectroscopic study of uranium/thorium content revealed the age of the galaxy to be 12.3 Gyr.

**a** Given that the galaxy was formed 500 million years after the Big Bang, what value of the Hubble Constant $H_0$ would be consistent with this estimate? **[4 т.]**

**b** Determine the corresponding value of the critical density as well. **[3 т.]**

If you may require it, the Friedmann equations are given by:

$$
H^2 = \left(\frac{\dot{a}}{a}\right)^2 = \frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho - \frac{kc^2}{a^2} \qquad (3)
$$

You are also given that $\frac{d}{dx}(x^n) = nx^{n-1}$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a** 50.9 km s^-1 Mpc^-1
- **b** 4.87e-27 kg m^-3

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

$$
H = \frac{\dot{a}}{a} = \frac{2}{3}\frac{1}{t}
$$

**M2**

The age of the universe could be obtained as $12.3 + 0.5\mathrm{Gyr} = 12.8\mathrm{Gyr}$

**M1**

$$
\frac{2}{3}\frac{1}{12.8\mathrm{Gyr}} = 1.65 \times 10^{-18} s^{-1} = 50.9\mathrm{kms}^{-1}\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}
$$

**A1**

Setting $k$ to 0 in the Friedmann equations,

**M2**

$$
H^2 = \frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho
$$

$$
\rho = \frac{3H^2}{8\pi G} = 4.87 \times 10^{-27}\mathrm{kgm}^{-3}
$$

**A1**


</Spoiler>
