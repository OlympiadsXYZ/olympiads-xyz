## Условие

The energies of an electron in a hydrogen atom are given by

$$E_n = -\frac{13.606\ \mathrm{eV}}{n^2}$$

where $n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$ represents the principal quantum number of the shell in which the electron is located.

The Ly-$\alpha$ spectral line is produced when an electron transitions from the $n = 2$ to the $n = 1$ energy level. Astronomers observe that the wavelength of the Ly-$\alpha$ line in a distant receding galaxy’s emission spectrum is $\Delta\lambda = 7.13\ \mathrm{nm}$ greater than the value measured in a lab.

Calculate the object’s approximate distance from us in Mpc (assuming Hubble’s constant $H_0 = 70\ \mathrm{km/s/Mpc}$).

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 251 Mpc

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

To find the wavelength of the spectral line, we note that

$$\frac{hc}{\lambda} = E_2 - E_1$$

Then,

$$\lambda = \frac{hc}{E_2 - E_1} \approx 121.52\ \mathrm{nm}$$

Answers within $\pm$ 0.5 nm of this result should be accepted due to potential differences in rounding.

In order to calculate the recessional velocity, we use the fact that $\Delta\lambda$ results from the redshift due to motion away from us. Thus,

$$v_r = cz = \frac{\Delta\lambda}{\lambda} c \approx 17600\ \mathrm{km/s}$$

To calculate the distance to this object, we use Hubble’s Law:

$$d = \frac{v_r}{H_0} \approx \boxed{251\ \mathrm{Mpc}}$$


</Spoiler>
