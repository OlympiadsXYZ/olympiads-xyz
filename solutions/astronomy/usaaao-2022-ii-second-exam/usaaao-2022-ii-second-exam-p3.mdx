## Условие

Consider a star $A$ (apparent magnitude $m_A = 10.9$, radius $R_A = 0.42 R_{\odot}$). A periodic transiting event is observed to have a decrease the collected flux by 0.07 %. If this event was caused by a transiting exoplanet around star $A$, what would be the radius of that exoplanet in Earth radii?

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 1.212 R<sub>earth</sub>

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The depth of a transit $\delta$ equals the ratio of the squared planet to star radii $\frac{R_p^2}{R_*^2}$. So, $R_p = \sqrt{\delta} \times R_* = \sqrt{0.0007} \times 0.42 R_{\odot} = 1.212 R_{\oplus}$.


</Spoiler>
