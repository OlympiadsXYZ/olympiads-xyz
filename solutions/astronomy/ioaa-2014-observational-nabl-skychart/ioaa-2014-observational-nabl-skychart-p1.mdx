### Questions

Please write ONLY on this side of the paper

The map represents the sky in Suceava (Latitude $47^{0}$ 39’ North, Longitude $26^{0}$ 15’ East) at 19:00 UT on the day of test. The observer who made the sky-map was at a very high altitude above Suceava; the Zenith point is in the center of the chart. Solve question 1 to 4 on one copy of the map and questions 5 to 8 on the second copy of map.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="question-1">

### Question 1 (2 т.)

Draw on the map the horizon for an observer located on the ground in Suceava.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="question-2">

### Question 2 (8 т.)

Draw the celestial equator, the ecliptic, the galactic equator and the local meridian on the map with continuous lines.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="question-3">

### Question 3 (9 т.)

Mark the cardinal points (as N for north, E for east, S for south and W for west). Mark all the visible planets (except Uranus and Neptune) of the Solar System on the map and number them as 1, 2, …,6 in the order of increasing orbital radius (Skip number 3 for the Earth). Note that planets are <u>not</u> currently shown on the map.

</ProblemSection>
