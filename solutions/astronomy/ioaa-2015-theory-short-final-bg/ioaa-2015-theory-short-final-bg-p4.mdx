## Условие

Представете си, че наблюдател използва хипотетичен телескоп, работещ в далечната инфрачервена област (от 20 до 640 μm). Телескопът е с размерите на Земята. Телескопът е открил невъртяща се, електронеутрална, свръхмасивна черна дупка с маса $2.1\times10^{10}M_\odot$. Намерете максималното разстояние от което телескопът би могъл да наблюдава черната дупка като неточков обект.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Answer and Marking Scheme:**

<ProblemSection id="solution-step-1">

### 1 Identify the relationship between the telescope’s resolution and the angular size of black hole

**[25]**

This problem can be solved by using relationship between the telescope’s resolution and the angular size of black holes which can be seen in this equation:

$$
\phi_{telescope} \le \phi_{blackhole} \;\Rightarrow\; 1.2\frac{\lambda}{D_{telescope}} \le \frac{\text{Diameter of event horizon}}{\text{Observer Distance to galatic' s the center}}
$$

$$
1.2\frac{\lambda}{D_{telescope}} \le \frac{2R_{blackhole}}{d}
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-step-2">

### 2 Identify the relation for determining the distance

**[50]**

Then, it is assumed that the radius of black hole is the Schwarszchild radius; and the telescope is also Earth-sized telescope. Therefore:

$$
1.2\frac{\lambda}{2R_{Earth}} \le \frac{2R_{Schwarzchild,BH}}{d}
$$

$$
d \le \frac{4\times R_{Schwarzchild,BH}\times R_{Earth}}{1.2\times\lambda} \;\Rightarrow\; d \le \frac{4\times M_{Black\ Hole}\times R_{Schwarchild,Sun}\times R_{Earth}}{1.2\times M_{Sun}\times\lambda}
$$

$$
d \le \frac{4\times2.1\times10^{10}M_{Sun}\times R_{Schwarchild,Sun}\times R_{Earth}}{1.2\times M_{Sun}\times\lambda} \;\Rightarrow\; d \le \frac{8.4\times10^{10}\times R_{Schwarchild,Sun}\times R_{Earth}}{1.2\times\lambda}
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-step-3">

### 3 Calculating the maximum-observer distance

**[25]**

To find the maximum distance, then we should use the minimum λ.

Therefore:

$$
d_{\max} = \frac{8.4\times10^{10}\times R_{Schwarchild,Sun}\times R_{Earth}}{1.2\times\lambda_{\min}} = \frac{8.4\times10^{10}\times2.95\times10^{3}\times6.3708\times10^{6}}{1.2\times20\times10^{-6}}\ m = 6.6\times10^{25}\ m
$$

</ProblemSection>
