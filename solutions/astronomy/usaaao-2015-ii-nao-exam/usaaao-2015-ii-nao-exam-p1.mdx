## Условие

A blue star with effective temperature $T_{\mathrm{eff}} = 10{,}000\ \mathrm{K}$ and apparent magnitude $m = 5$ is located 150 pc from Earth. Find the radius of the star.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

$T = 10000$ K, $m = 5$, $d = 150$ pc  
$m - M = 5\log(d/10)$  
$M = m - 5\log(d/10) = 5 - 5\log(15) = -0.88$  
We compare this with the absolute magnitude of the sun, $4.83$.  
$\frac{L}{L_{solar}} = 100^{(4.83 - -0.88)/5} = 192 L_{solar}$  
From the Stefan-Boltzmann Law, $L = 4\pi R^2 \sigma T^4$  
Finding the ratio with the sun, we get:  
$\frac{L}{L_{solar}} = \left(\frac{R}{R_{solar}}\right)^2 \left(\frac{T}{T_{solar}}\right)^4$  
$\frac{R}{R_{solar}} = \left(\frac{L}{L_{solar}}\right)^{1/2} \left(\frac{T_{solar}}{T}\right)^2 = 4.6 R_{solar}$


</Spoiler>
