## Условие

The radial velocity curve of a nearby Solar-mass star shows that it has a planet orbiting it with a period of 3 days. This planet causes its host star to have a radial velocity semi-amplitude of 50 m/s. Assuming that this planet’s orbit is perfectly inclined to Earth, and has 0 eccentricity, what is the mass of this planet, in Jupiter masses?

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

$T = 3$ days, $K = 50$ m/s, $M = 1 M_{solar}$
$T^2 = \frac{a^3}{M}$
$\left(\frac{3}{365}\right)^{2/3} = 0.041\ \mathrm{AU}$ for the planet's orbit.
For the star's orbit:
$T = \frac{2\pi r}{v}$
$r = \frac{Tv}{2\pi} = 1.38 \cdot 10^{-5}\ \mathrm{AU}$
Relating the two, we have:
$m_* r_* = m_p r_p$, so we have $m_p = \frac{r_*}{r_p} m_*$
Plugging in, we find $m_p = 3.36 \cdot 10^{-4} M_{solar}$


</Spoiler>
