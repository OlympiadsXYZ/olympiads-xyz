## Условие

Assuming that the universe currently is well described by a density parameter $\Omega_0 = 1$, there is no dark energy and the current temperature of the universe is 2.73 K, compute how long from the present it will take for the universe to cool down by 0.2 K. Remember that the temperature of the universe is inversely proportional to its radius (the scale factor).
