**8.1.** Draw on the map as a dotted line the ecliptic and identify with "Ecl".

**8.2.** Draw on the map as a continuous line the celestial equator and identify with "Eqt".

**8.3.** Identify on the map the following 12 constellation, writing their three letter code nearby: Pup, Ant, Lup, Cen, Lyn, Dra, UMa, Pyx, LMi, Per, Vel, Cru.

**8.4.** Identify on the map the following 5 stars, writing the indicated number nearby: 1. Alnilam (ε Ori), 2. Alphard (α Hya), 3. Miram (η Per), 4. Eltanin (γ Dra), 5. Kochab (β UMi).
