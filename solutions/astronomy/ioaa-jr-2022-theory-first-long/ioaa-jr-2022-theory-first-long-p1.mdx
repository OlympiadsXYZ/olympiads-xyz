*Problem 1. Zodiac IOAA – J - 2022*

## Условие

In their apparent motions, the Moon and the other large planets in our solar system do not stray far from the plane of the ecliptic. Their apparent trajectories described on the celestial sphere remain contained in a region that extends symmetrically on both sides of the ecliptic, having a total width of about 18º. In the drawing in figure 1, in the plane of the ecliptic, the heliocentric orbit of the Earth is shown, and in the drawing in figure 2, the equivalent apparent geocentric orbit of the Sun, in relation to the Earth, is shown.

The time intervals of the evolution of the true Sun in each of the 12 constellations of the Zodiac are as follows: **1)** Aries, 21 III - 20 IV; **2)** Taurus, 21 IV - 20 V; **3)** Gemini, 21 V - 20 VI; **4)** Cancer, 21 VI - 22 VII; **5)** Leo, 23 VII - 22 VIII; **6)** Virgo, 23 VIII - 22 IX; **7)** Libra, 23 IX - 22 X; **8)** Scorpio, 23 X - 21 XI; **9)** Sagittarius, 22 XI - 21 XII; **10)** Capricorn, 22 XII - 21 I; **11)** Aquarius, 22 I - 19 II; **12)** Pisces, 20 II - 21 III.

The International Olympics of Astronomy and Astrophysics for Juniors, Edition I, was to take place in SUCEAVA, in ROMANIA, on March 28 - April 3, 2020. The event, proposed by ROMANIA, was established at the International Olympics of Astronomy and Astrophysics, 13th Edition, held in Hungary, on August 2-10, 2019.

But, given the known conditions, OIAA for Juniors, Edition I, is taking place in Romania, only now, in November 2022!

**a)** *Identify*:

**1.** the sign of the OIAA development, 13th Edition, Hungary, August 2 - 10, 2019;

**2.** the sign when the OIAA - J was to take place - Edition I, March 28 - April 3, 2020, Romania (Edition I, proposal);

**3**. the sign of the OIAA - J - Edition I, November, 2022.

**b)** Each of the 3 specified Olympic events can be considered to have taken place on the day when the Sun, viewed from Earth, was in the middle of the angular range corresponding to each of the 3 signs.

**1.** *Determine* the time interval between any two of these three events:

$$
\Delta t_{\mathrm{H-R,1}};\ \Delta t_{\mathrm{R,1-R,2}};\ \Delta t_{\mathrm{H-R,2}}.
$$

**2.** *Estimate*, by direct measurements on the drawing, by means of a protractor, the value of the angle between the position vector of the Earth, in relation to the Sun, corresponding to each of the three specified moments, ie the directions of the vectors $\vec{r}_{\mathrm{H}}$, $\vec{r}_{\mathrm{R,1}}$ and respectively $\vec{r}_{\mathrm{R,2}}$, and the direction of the apse line, Aph – Ph, ie the angles $\alpha_{\mathrm{H}}$, $\alpha_{\mathrm{R,1}}$ and respectively $\alpha_{\mathrm{R,2}}$.

**c)** *Determine* the area of the surface described by the position vector of the center of the Earth, $\vec{r}$, in relation to the center of the Sun:

**1.** from position $\vec{r}_{\mathrm{Hungry}}$ to position $\vec{r}_{\mathrm{Romania,1}}$;

**2.** from position $\vec{r}_{\mathrm{Romania,1}}$ to position $\vec{r}_{\mathrm{Romania,2}}$;

**3.** from position $\vec{r}_{\mathrm{Hungry}}$ to position $\vec{r}_{\mathrm{Romania,2}}$.

**d)** *Determine*:

**1.** the distance between the Earth and the Sun, on the day when in Suceava, in 2020, the IOAA for Juniors, Edition I should have taken place, $r_{\mathrm{R,1}}$, if, for the ellipse representing the Earth's orbit around the Sun, the following are known: large semiaxis, $a = 149\,597\,500\ \mathrm{km}$ and small semiaxis, $b = 149\,580\,670\ \mathrm{km}$;

**2.** the acceleration of the center of the Earth, on the day when in Suceava, in 2020, the IOAA for Juniors should have taken place, $a_{\mathrm{E,1}}$, and compared with the gravitational acceleration in the gravitational field of the Sun, corresponding to the distance $r_{\mathrm{R,1}}$ from the center of the Sun, $g_{\mathrm{S,1}}$;

**3.** the components of the speed of the center of the Earth, $\vec{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{R}}$, parallel to the major axis of the ellipse and respectively perpendicular to the major axis of the ellipse, in the days of the International Olympics in Romania, $\mathrm{v}_{//}$ and respectively $\mathrm{v}_{\perp}$.

*It is known* that the kinetic moment of the Earth in relation to the center of the Sun and the total mechanical energy of the Earth-Sun system are given by the expressions:

$$
L = M_{\mathrm{E}}b\cdot\sqrt{\frac{KM_{\mathrm{S}}}{a}};\ E = -K\frac{M_{\mathrm{E}}M_{\mathrm{S}}}{2a}.
$$

*Given*: gravitational attraction constant, $K = 6.67\cdot 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{Nm^{2}kg^{-2}}$; mass of the Sun, $M_{\mathrm{S}} = 1.989\cdot 10^{30}\ \mathrm{kg}$.

**1.** for IOAA – 2019, Hungary: $\vec{r}_{\mathrm{H}};\ t_{\mathrm{H}} =$ 6 August, 2019;

**2.** for IOAA – J – 2020, Romania - proposal: $\vec{r}_{\mathrm{R,1}};\ t_{\mathrm{R,1}} =$ 4 April, 2020,

**c)**

**1.** It is known that in the evolution of the Earth in the elliptical orbit around the Sun, according to Keppler's third law, the areolar velocity of the Earth is constant, so that:

$$
\Omega = \frac{\Delta A}{\Delta t} = \mathrm{constant};
$$

$$
\frac{A_{\mathrm{ellipse}}}{T} = \frac{\Delta A_{\mathrm{H-R,1}}}{\Delta t_{\mathrm{H-R,1}}};
$$

$$
\Delta A_{\mathrm{H-R,1}} = \frac{\Delta t_{\mathrm{H-R,1}}}{T}A_{\mathrm{ellipse}} = \frac{\Delta t_{\mathrm{H-R,1}}}{T}\cdot\pi\cdot a\cdot b;
$$

$$
\Delta t_{\mathrm{H-R,1}} = 223\ \mathrm{days};\ T = 365.256\ \mathrm{days};
$$

$$
a = 149\,597\,500\ \mathrm{km};\ b = 149\,580\,670\ \mathrm{km};
$$

$$
\Delta A_{\mathrm{H-R,1}} = \frac{223\ \mathrm{days}}{365.256\ \mathrm{days}}\cdot 3.14\cdot 149\,597\,500\ \mathrm{km}\cdot 149\,580\,670\ \mathrm{km};
$$

$$
\Delta A_{\mathrm{H-R,1}} = \frac{223}{365.256}\cdot 3.14\cdot 149\,597\,500\cdot 149\,580\,670\ \mathrm{km^{2}};
$$

$$
\Delta A_{\mathrm{H-R,1}} = 4.2897\cdot 10^{16}\ \mathrm{km^{2}}.
$$

**2.**

$$
\frac{A_{\mathrm{ellipse}}}{T} = \frac{\Delta A_{\mathrm{R,1-R,2}}}{\Delta t_{\mathrm{R,1-R,2}}};
$$

$$
\Delta A_{\mathrm{R,1-R,2}} = \frac{\Delta t_{\mathrm{R,1-R,2}}}{T}A_{\mathrm{ellipse}} = \frac{\Delta t_{\mathrm{R,1-R,2}}}{T}\cdot\pi\cdot a\cdot b;
$$

$$
\Delta A_{\mathrm{R,1-R,2}} = \frac{886\ \mathrm{days}}{365.256\ \mathrm{days}}\cdot 3.14\cdot 149\,597\,500\ \mathrm{km}\cdot 149\,580\,670\ \mathrm{km};
$$

$$
\Delta A_{\mathrm{R,1-R,2}} = \frac{886}{365.256}\cdot 3.14\cdot 149\,597\,500\cdot 149\,580\,670\ \mathrm{km^{2}};
$$

$$
\Delta A_{\mathrm{R,1-R,2}} = 1.704\cdot 10^{17}\ \mathrm{km^{2}};
$$

**3.**

$$
\frac{A_{\mathrm{ellipse}}}{T} = \frac{\Delta A_{\mathrm{H-R,2}}}{\Delta t_{\mathrm{H-R,2}}};\ \frac{A_{\mathrm{ellipse}}}{T} = \frac{\Delta A_{\mathrm{H-R,1}}}{\Delta t_{\mathrm{H-R,1}}};

$$
\Delta A_{\mathrm{H-R,2}} = \frac{1109\ \mathrm{days}}{365.256\ \mathrm{days}}\cdot 3.14\cdot 149\,597\,500\ \mathrm{km}\cdot 149\,580\,670\ \mathrm{km};

**1.** According to the notations in figure 5, representing the position of the Earth, in relation to the Sun, on the day of, when, as we have shown, $\vec{r}_{\mathrm{R,1}} \perp \left(\mathrm{Aph - Ph}\right)$, it results:

\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} + \frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}} = 1;\ x = c;\ y = r_{\mathrm{R,1}};

c^{2} = a^{2} - b^{2};

\frac{c^{2}}{a^{2}} + \frac{r_{\mathrm{R,1}}^{2}}{b^{2}} = 1;\ \frac{a^{2} - b^{2}}{a^{2}} + \frac{r_{\mathrm{R,1}}^{2}}{b^{2}} = 1;

**2.**

$$
K\frac{M_{\mathrm{E}}M_{\mathrm{S}}}{r_{\mathrm{R,1}}^{2}} = M_{\mathrm{E}}a_{\mathrm{E,1}};\ a_{\mathrm{E,1}} = K\frac{M_{\mathrm{S}}}{r_{\mathrm{R,1}}^{2}};

$$

$$
a_{\mathrm{E,1}} = \frac{6.67\cdot 10^{-11}\cdot 1.989\cdot 10^{30}}{\left(149\,563\,841.9\right)^{2}\cdot 10^{6}}\ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s^{2}}};

$$
a_{\mathrm{E,1}} \approx 0.006\ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s^{2}}}.

$$
g_{\mathrm{S,1}} = K\cdot\frac{M_{\mathrm{S}}}{r_{\mathrm{R,1}}^{2}} = a_{\mathrm{E,1}}.

$$
\vec{L}_{\mathrm{R,1}} = \vec{r}_{\mathrm{R,1}}\ \mathrm{x}\ M_{\mathrm{E}}\,\vec{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{R,1}};\ L_{\mathrm{R,1}} = r_{\mathrm{R,1}}M_{\mathrm{E}}\,\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{R,1}}\cdot\sin\left(\vec{r}_{\mathrm{R,1}};\vec{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{R,1}}\right) = r_{\mathrm{R,1}}M_{\mathrm{E}}\,\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{R,1}}\cdot\sin\alpha,
$$

according to the law of conservation of kinetic momentum, it follows:

$$
\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{R,1}}\cdot\sin\alpha = \mathrm{v}_{//};
$$

$$
L_{\mathrm{R,1}} = r_{\mathrm{R,1}}M_{\mathrm{E}}\,\mathrm{v}_{//};\ L = M_{\mathrm{E}}b\cdot\sqrt{\frac{KM_{\mathrm{S}}}{a}};
$$

$$
M_{\mathrm{E}}b\cdot\sqrt{\frac{KM_{\mathrm{S}}}{a}} = r_{\mathrm{R,1}}M_{\mathrm{E}}\,\mathrm{v}_{//};\ r_{\mathrm{R}} = \frac{b^{2}}{a};
$$

$$
\mathrm{v}_{//} = \frac{a}{b}\cdot\sqrt{\frac{KM_{\mathrm{S}}}{a}};
$$

$$
K = 6.67\cdot 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{Nm^{2}kg^{-2}};\ M_{\mathrm{S}} = 1.989\cdot 10^{30}\ \mathrm{kg};
$$

$$
a = 149\,597\,500\ \mathrm{km};\ b = 149\,580\,670\ \mathrm{km};
$$

$$
\mathrm{v}_{//} = \frac{149\,597\,500\ \mathrm{km}}{149\,580\,670\ \mathrm{km}}\cdot\sqrt{\frac{6.67\cdot 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{Nm^{2}kg^{-2}}\cdot 1.989\cdot 10^{30}\ \mathrm{kg}}{149\,597\,500\cdot 10^{3}\ \mathrm{m}}};

$$
\mathrm{v}_{//} = \frac{149\,597\,500}{149\,580\,670}\cdot\sqrt{\frac{6.67\cdot 10^{-11}\cdot 1.989\cdot 10^{30}}{149\,597\,500\cdot 10^{3}}}\ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}};
$$

$$
\mathrm{v}_{//} = \frac{149\,597\,500}{149\,580\,670}\cdot\sqrt{\frac{6.67\cdot 1.989}{149\,597\,500}}\cdot 10^{8}\ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}};
$$

$$
\mathrm{v}_{//} = \frac{\sqrt{149\,597\,500\cdot 6.67\cdot 1.989}}{149\,580\,670}\cdot 10^{8}\ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}};
$$

$$
\mathrm{v}_{//} = 29\,782.9\ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}};\ \mathrm{v}_{//} = 29\,782.9\cdot 10^{-3}\ \frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{s}};
$$

$$
\mathrm{v}_{//} = 29.7829\ \frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{s}}.
$$

According to the law of conservation of mechanical energy it results:

$$
E = -K\frac{M_{\mathrm{E}}M_{\mathrm{S}}}{2a};
$$

$$
E_{\mathrm{R,1}} = \frac{M_{\mathrm{E}}\,\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{R}}^{2}}{2} - K\frac{M_{\mathrm{E}}M_{\mathrm{S}}}{r_{\mathrm{R,1}}};
$$

$$
\frac{M_{\mathrm{E}}\,\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{R,1}}^{2}}{2} - K\frac{M_{\mathrm{E}}M_{\mathrm{S}}}{r_{\mathrm{R,1}}} = -K\frac{M_{\mathrm{E}}M_{\mathrm{S}}}{2a};
$$

$$
\frac{\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{R,1}}^{2}}{2} - K\frac{M_{\mathrm{S}}}{r_{\mathrm{R,1}}} = -K\frac{M_{\mathrm{S}}}{2a};
$$

$$
\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{R,1}} = \sqrt{KM_{\mathrm{S}}\left(\frac{2}{r_{\mathrm{R,1}}} - \frac{1}{a}\right)} = \sqrt{KM_{\mathrm{S}}\cdot\frac{2a - r_{\mathrm{R,1}}}{r_{\mathrm{R,1}}a}};
$$

$$
K = 6.67\cdot 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{Nm^{2}kg^{-2}};\ M_{\mathrm{S}} = 1.989\cdot 10^{30}\ \mathrm{kg};\ r_{\mathrm{R,1}} = 149\,563\,841{,}9\ \mathrm{km};\ a = 149\,597\,500\ \mathrm{km};
$$

$$
\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{R,1}} = \sqrt{6.67\cdot 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{Nm^{2}kg^{-2}}\cdot 1.989\cdot 10^{30}\ \mathrm{kg}\cdot\frac{2\cdot 149\,597\,500\ \mathrm{km} - 149\,563\,841.9\ \mathrm{km}}{149\,563\,841.9\ \mathrm{km}\cdot 149\,597\,500\ \mathrm{km}}};
$$
