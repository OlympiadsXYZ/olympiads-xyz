*Observational round. Problems to solve · 22 октомври 2002 г.*

## Условие

Now, you shall visit 3 observational places.

From each place, you shall see small sky area.

Write down in a table the following items:

- No more than 2 visible (by yourself) constellations (you can write also 1–2 stars names);
- Direction of your observations, relatively the Galactic plane, (e.g. toward its center, to North from its center, to North from its plane an so on).

Working time at each place is not larger than 3 minutes.
