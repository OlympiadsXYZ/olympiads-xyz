*7th International Olympiad on Astronomy & Astrophysics, 27 July – 5 August 2013, Volos Greece — Observational test · 27 юли 2013 г.*

<details>
<summary>Указания</summary>

When you arrive at the telescope, you will be provided with suitable equipment and shown how to use it. You will also be given the answer form. Fill in your student code number before starting the questions.

</details>

## Условие

Without using the setting circles of the telescope, complete the following tasks:

<figure>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ioaa-2013-observational-nabl-night/p2-fig1.png" alt="Star chart of the region around Sagitta with neighbouring constellations Cygnus, Lyra, Vulpecula, Delphinus, Aquila, Peg and Equ; constellation lines in green, boundaries dashed in yellow; marked objects M27, M71, M56, M57, M29, NGC 6992, NGC 6960 and stars labelled α, β, γ, ε, ζ." />
<figcaption>The field in the vicinity of the Dumbell Nebula</figcaption>
</figure>

**(a)** bring the bright star (m = 3.51 mag) *γ Sagitta* (*RA* = $19^{\mathrm{h}}\,58^{\mathrm{m}}\,45.39^{\mathrm{s}}$, *Dec* = +19° 29′ 31.5″) to the centre of view of the telescope. Ask the attendant to verify the observation. **[3 т.]**

**(b)** bring the famous *Dumbell Nebula*, M27 (*RA* = $19^{\mathrm{h}}\,59^{\mathrm{m}}\,36.34^{\mathrm{s}}$, +22° 43′ 16.09″) to the center of the field of view. Ask the attendant to verify the observation. **[5 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

***Answer:*** (1) The student should recognize the constellation of Sagitta and point the telescope at *γ Sagitta*, which is the brightest star of the constellation. **(3 point)**

(2) The students should notice that the two targets have very similar *Right Ascension*. Therefore given the *RA* and *Dec* of the bright star *γ Sagitta* (m = 3.51 mag), should be able to quickly locate *M27* as the equatorial mounting is already aligned. Then should keep the *RA* axis locked, release the Dec knob and turn the telescope by about 3.25 degrees towards Polaris. The Dumbell Nebula will appear in the field of view. **(5 point)**

[Marking scheme: Points given according to time spent. Total time required: ≤4 min: 100%, ≤5 min: 80%, ≤6 min: 50%.

[Maximum allowed time **6 minutes**]

(Note: If the student fails to locate *M27* and complains, the attendant does it 30 s and he notes this down)


</Spoiler>
