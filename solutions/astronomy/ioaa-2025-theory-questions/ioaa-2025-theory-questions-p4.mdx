## Условие

Consider a main sequence star surrounded by a nebula. The observed V-band magnitude of the star is 11.315 mag. The ionised region of nebula close to the star emits H$\alpha$ and H$\beta$ lines; their wavelengths are $0.6563\,\mu\mathrm m$ and $0.4861\,\mu\mathrm m$, respectively. The theoretically predicted ratio of fluxes in H$\alpha$ to H$\beta$ lines is $f_{H\alpha}/f_{H\beta}=2.86$. However, when this radiation passes through the outer portion of the cold dusty nebula, the observed emission fluxes of H$\alpha$ and H$\beta$ lines are $6.80\times10^{-15}\,\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$ and $1.06\times10^{-15}\,\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$, respectively.

The extinction $A_\lambda$ is a function of wavelength and is expressed as

$$
A_\lambda=\kappa(\lambda)E(B-V).
$$

Here, $\kappa(\lambda)$ is the extinction curve and $E(B-V)$ denotes the colour excess in the filter bands B and V. The extinction curve (with $\lambda$ in $\mu\mathrm m$) is given as follows.

$$
\kappa(\lambda)=\begin{cases}
2.659\times\left(-1.857+\dfrac{1.040}{\lambda}\right)+R_V,&0.63\leq\lambda\leq2.20\\
2.659\times\left(-2.156+\dfrac{1.509}{\lambda}-\dfrac{0.198}{\lambda^2}+\dfrac{0.011}{\lambda^3}\right)+R_V,&0.12\leq\lambda\leq0.63
\end{cases}
$$

where, $R_V=A_V/E(B-V)=3.1$ is the ratio of total-to-selective extinction.

**(T04.1)** Find the values of $\kappa(\mathrm H\alpha)$ and $\kappa(\mathrm H\beta)$. **[3 т.]**

**(T04.2)** Find the value of the ratio of colour excess $\dfrac{E(\mathrm H\beta-\mathrm H\alpha)}{E(B-V)}$. **[4 т.]**

**(T04.3)** Estimate the extinction due to nebula, $A_{H\alpha}$ and $A_{H\beta}$, at H$\alpha$ and H$\beta$ wavelengths, respectively. **[6 т.]**

**(T04.4)** Estimate the extinction of the nebula ($A_V$) and the apparent magnitude of the star in the V band, $m_{V0}$, in the absence of the nebula. **[2 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

### (T04.1)

For H$\alpha$ substituting in equation for $\kappa(\lambda)$, we get

$$
\kappa(\mathrm{H}\alpha)=2.659\times\left(-1.857+\frac{1.040}{0.6563}\right)+3.1,
$$
$$
\boxed{\kappa(\mathrm{H}\alpha)=2.4}\qquad\text{(1.5)}.
$$

Similarly, for H$\beta$ we have

$$
\kappa(\mathrm{H}\beta)=2.659\times\left(-2.156+\frac{1.509}{0.4861}-\frac{0.198}{0.4861^2}+\frac{0.011}{0.4861^3}\right)+3.1,
$$
$$
\boxed{\kappa(\mathrm{H}\beta)=3.6}\qquad\text{(1.5)}.
$$

For $\kappa(\mathrm{H}\beta)$:

| Half credit lower limit | Full credit range | Half credit upper limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| | 3.6 to 3.7 | |

### (T04.2)

$$
E(\mathrm{H}\beta-\mathrm{H}\alpha)=A_{\mathrm{H}\beta}-A_{\mathrm{H}\alpha}\qquad\text{(2.0)}
$$
$$
=[\kappa(\mathrm{H}\beta)-\kappa(\mathrm{H}\alpha)]E(B-V)=1.270E(B-V),
$$
$$
\boxed{\frac{E(\mathrm{H}\beta-\mathrm{H}\alpha)}{E(B-V)}=1.2}\qquad\text{(2.0)}.
$$

Credit of only 2.0 marks if answer is given without showing earlier expression relating color excess $E(\mathrm{H}\beta-\mathrm{H}\alpha)$ to extinction $A$.

| Half credit lower limit | Full credit range | Half credit upper limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| | 1.2 to 1.3 | |

### (T04.3)

If $f_{\lambda,\mathrm{obs}}$ and $f_{\lambda,\mathrm{em}}$ represent the observed and emitted fluxes at wavelength $\lambda$, respectively, then the extinction is given by the equation

$$
A_\lambda=-2.5\log\left(\frac{f_{\lambda,\mathrm{obs}}}{f_{\lambda,\mathrm{em}}}\right)\qquad\text{(1.0)}.
$$

Given, $f_{\mathrm{H}\alpha,\mathrm{em}}/f_{\mathrm{H}\beta,\mathrm{em}}=2.86$,

$$
E(\mathrm{H}\beta-\mathrm{H}\alpha)=A_{\mathrm{H}\beta}-A_{\mathrm{H}\alpha}
$$
$$
=-2.5\log\left(\frac{f_{\mathrm{H}\beta,\mathrm{obs}}}{f_{\mathrm{H}\alpha,\mathrm{obs}}}\times\frac{f_{\mathrm{H}\alpha,\mathrm{em}}}{f_{\mathrm{H}\beta,\mathrm{em}}}\right)
$$
$$
=-2.5\log\left(\frac{1.06\times10^{-15}}{6.80\times10^{-15}}\times2.86\right)=0.88\qquad\text{(1.5)}.
$$

From extinction curve, we have $E(\mathrm{H}\beta-\mathrm{H}\alpha)=1.2E(B-V)$. Equating these two, we obtain

$$
E(B-V)=\frac{0.88}{1.2}=0.73\qquad\text{(0.5)}.
$$

This gives,

$$
A_{\mathrm{H}\alpha}=\kappa(\mathrm{H}\alpha)E(B-V)=2.4\times0.73,
$$
$$
\boxed{A_{\mathrm{H}\alpha}=1.8\,\mathrm{mag}}\qquad\text{(1.5)},
$$
$$
A_{\mathrm{H}\beta}=\kappa(\mathrm{H}\beta)E(B-V)=3.6\times0.73,
$$
$$
\boxed{A_{\mathrm{H}\beta}=2.6\,\mathrm{mag}}\qquad\text{(1.5)}.
$$

Full credit for $E(B-V)$ between 0.69–0.73, no credit outside this range. Other values do not change when rounded to 2 significant digits.

For $A_{\mathrm{H}\alpha}$:

| Half credit lower limit | Full credit range | Half credit upper limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| | 1.6 to 1.8 | |

For $A_{\mathrm{H}\beta}$:

| Half credit lower limit | Full credit range | Half credit upper limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| | 2.5 to 2.6 | |

### (T04.4)

$$
A_V=3.1\times E(B-V)=3.1\times0.69,
$$
$$
\boxed{A_V=2.3\,\mathrm{mag}}\qquad\text{(1.0)},
$$
$$
m_{V0}=m_V-A_V=11.315-2.3,
$$
$$
\boxed{m_{V0}=9.0\,\mathrm{mag}}\qquad\text{(1.0)}.
$$
