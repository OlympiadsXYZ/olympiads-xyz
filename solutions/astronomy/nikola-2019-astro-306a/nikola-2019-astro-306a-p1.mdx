## Условие

There are several exoplanets observed in the Gliese 876 system ($M_G = 0.334 \pm 0.03 M_\odot$) as given in the following table,

| Gliese System | Mass | Semi Major Axis (au) |
|---|---|---|
| Gliese 876 b | $2.2756 \pm 0.0045\mathrm{M_J}$ | $0.2083 \pm 0.000020$ |
| Gliese 876 c | $0.7142 \pm 0.0039\ \mathrm{M_J}$ | $0.1296 \pm 0.000024$ |
| Gliese 876 d | $6.83 \pm 0.40\ \mathrm{M_\oplus}$ | $0.0208 \pm 0.00000015$ |
| Gliese 876 e | $14.6 \pm 1.7\ \mathrm{M_\oplus}$ | $0.3343 \pm 0.0013$ |

where $\mathrm{M_\odot}$ is mass of Sun, $\mathrm{M_J}$ is mass of Jupiter ($M_J = 1.89813 \times 10^{27}\ kg$), and $\mathrm{M_\oplus}$ is mass of Earth.

Find, if any of the exoplanets of Gliese 876 system have resonant orbits (their synodic period is a multiple integer of one of the periods).
