## Условие

At November 7, 2005, the Mars opposition will occur. During this event Mars will be situated at $0.47\ \mathrm{a.u.}$ from the Earth, being quite close to the ecliptic line ($27'$ south of it) as observed from the Earth. For this event the Martian Space Agency is planning the near-Mars space mission with astronauts onboard purposed to observe the Earth transit the centre of the solar disk. To diminish expenses the space mission will follow the optimal way – to observe the transit from the minimum possible distance from Mars. In what constellation will Mars be observed from the station in the middle of the Earth transit?

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- near the South Ecliptic pole in the Doradus constellation, near to Large Magellanic Cloud.

</Spoiler>
