## Условие

Astronomers find a "star" which position in the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram is about $8^{\mathrm m}$ higher than the corresponding stars of the main sequence. Assume that the "star" is a cluster of similar stars and estimate the number of stars in the cluster.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- about 1600 stars

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Let us consider that the absolute stellar magnitude and lightness of the cluster are $M(\text{cluster})$ and $I(\text{cluster})$, of a star in the cluster is $M(\text{star})$ and $I(\text{star})$. Initial relationship is:

$$
I(\text{cluster})=I(\text{N-stars})=N\cdot I(\text{one-star}).
$$

So

$$
\lg I(\text{N-stars})=\lg N+\lg I(\text{one-star}),
$$

$$
\lg N=\lg I(\text{N-stars})-\lg I(\text{one-star}),
$$

$$
5/2\lg N=5/2\lg I(\text{N-stars})-5/2\lg I(\text{one-star})=\Delta M,
$$

$$
\lg N=2/5\Delta M,
$$

$$
N=10^{0.4\cdot\Delta M}.
$$

As it done

$$
\Delta M=M(\text{cluster})-M(\text{one-star})=M(\text{N-stars})-M(\text{one-star})=8^{\mathrm m}.
$$

So

$$
N=10^{0.4\cdot8}=10^{3.2}=1585\ \text{stars}\approx1600\ \text{stars}.
$$

**Answer:** about 1600 stars.

Note: Answer "1585 stars" is not correct! There is data "about $8^{\mathrm m}$", so it is approximate value. For example, by varying this value only by only $0.1^{\mathrm m}$ we will get range of formal answers from 1445 to 1738, and by $0.5^{\mathrm m}$: from 1000 to 2512.


</Spoiler>
