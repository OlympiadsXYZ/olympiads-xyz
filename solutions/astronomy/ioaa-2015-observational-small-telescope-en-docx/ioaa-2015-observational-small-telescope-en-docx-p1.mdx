*OBSERVATIONAL QUESTIONS — Small Telescope · 100 т.*

## Условие

In this round you are going to observe the Moon using the telescope you have already made. Set up your telescope on the table provided for you. You have been given a map of the Moon. Please answer these questions on the given map.

<figure>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ioaa-2015-observational-small-telescope-en-docx/p1-fig1.png" alt="Map of the full Moon (drawn lunar chart) on the answer sheet, showing the maria and craters without any labels or directions." />
<figcaption>SMALL TELESCOPE OBSERVATION ANSWER SHEET</figcaption>
</figure>

**1.** Sketch the terminator of the Moon. **[25 т.]**

**2.** Write down the celestial cardinal directions on the map (N,S,E,W) **[25 т.]**

**3.** Mark the position of the south pole of the Moon with a cross (x). **[25 т.]**

**4.** Mark the position of the famous Tycho crater with a circle (the size of the circle should be comparable to the size of the crater). **[25 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

1. Termination line inside the white area : Full Score
Termination line crosses the white area : Half Score

2. Cardinal Points (Inside the white line, Full Score)

3. South Pole (Smallest Circle, Full Score; Medium Circle, Half Score; Largest Circle, Quarter Score)

4. Tycho Crater
