## Условие

*Observations with telescope*

There are 3 binary stars on the given sky chart: β Cyg, δ Lyr, and ε Lyr.

For each of the binaries do the following:

Point the telescope to the binary. Compare the star field seen in telescope’s field of view with three star charts given on a separate sheet. Write down the designations of the binary stars in each blank box under appropriate star chart. Mark North direction on every star chart.
