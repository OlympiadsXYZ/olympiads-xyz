*XVIII International Astronomy Olympiad. Observational round. Questions. Clear sky · 6 септември 2013 г.*

## Условие

*Observations by the naked eye*

Find the object corresponding to the following criteria:

- The object is the second brightest star in its constellation.
- The object is visible approximately 28 degrees from α UMi.
- The equatorial coordinates of the object are: **RA $11^{\mathrm{h}}$**, **dec $+62^{\circ}$**.

Answer the questions:

**a)** What is the Bayer designation (e.g. β Ori) of the identified object?

**b)** Write the name of the constellation in Latin, which the object is visible in.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a)** α UMa
- **b)** Ursa Major

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

a) α UMa.

b) Ursa Major.

*Source note: the official key gives α UMa; the question’s wording “second brightest star” is retained as printed.*


</Spoiler>
