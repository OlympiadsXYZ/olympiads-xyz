*XX International Astronomy Olympiad, Kazan, Tatarstan, Russia, 15–23.X.2015 · 15 октомври 2015 г.*

## Условие

You have 2.5 minutes to read the problem.  
Then an accelerated animation of the Sun's and planets's motion relative to stars will be shown twice during 5 minutes. Moreover at the 2nd time the animation will be paused at the beginning and at the end.  
After that you will have 2 minutes to write the answers.

**9.1.** Estimate dates (+/- 1 week) of the beginning and of the end of the animation.

**9.2.** Write, which constellations (their standard three-letter designation) the Mars and the Venus had passed.
