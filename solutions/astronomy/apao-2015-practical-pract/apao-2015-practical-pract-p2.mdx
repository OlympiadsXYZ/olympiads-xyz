## Условие

You are given the data for the mass (M, in masses of Jupiter) and surface gravity (g) of some exoplanets (see “sheet of data”).

**9.1.** Plot the $\log(M/M_J)$ against $\log(g)$.

**9.2.** It is given that the density ($\rho$) of these exoplanets varies approximately as a function of their mass (M) in following fashion:

$$
\rho=\alpha\cdot M^{\beta},
$$

where $\beta$ is an integer. Determine values of $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

**9.3.** What will be the surface gravity of Earth if the plot in part 9.2. is extended to it.

**9.4.** Is the value calculated in part 9.4. of the same order of magnitude as the actual value? Write in English “Yes” or “No”.

$M_{\mathrm{Jupiter}}=318\ M_{\mathrm{Earth}}$
