## Условие

The binary star YY Gem is also known as Castor Ca & Castor Cb. It is known from preliminary observations that the period of this binary star is more than 8 hours but less than 12 hours. O. Struve & V. Zebergs (1959, ApJ, 130, 783) measured radial velocities of the two components and get the results presented at the enclosed “sheet of data”.  
Velocities are given in km/s and time given is UT in the table.

**8.1.** Plot graph of the velocities of components vs time.

**8.2. (α)** Find exact orbital period of the components.

**8.2. (β)** Find exact orbital period, orbital radius and masses of the two components.
