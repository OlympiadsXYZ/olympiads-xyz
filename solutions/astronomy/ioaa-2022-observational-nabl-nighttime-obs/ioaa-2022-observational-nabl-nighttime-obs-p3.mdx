## Условие

**Time available: 2 min**

Using the result from problem O2, determine the focal length of the telescope. Assume the apparent field of view of the 25 mm eyepiece is 45 degrees.

Show and explain your calculations.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 940-1875 mm

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

$F = f \cdot \frac{AFOV}{FOV}$

Full markings will be given for the range $f =$ **940-1875mm**, but only if calculated using a correct formula and a value of $FOV$ from previous question.


</Spoiler>
