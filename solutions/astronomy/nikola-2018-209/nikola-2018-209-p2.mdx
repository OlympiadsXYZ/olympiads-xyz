## Условие

8) The Galactic Center is believed to contain a super-massive black hole with a mass $M = 4 \times 10^{6}\ M_{\odot}$. The astronomy community is trying to resolve its event horizon, which is a challenging task. For a non-rotating black hole, this is the Schwarzschild radius, $R_s = 3(M/M_{\odot})$ km. Assume that we have an Earth-sized telescope (using Very Long Baseline Interferometry). What wavelengths should we adopt in order to resolve the event horizon of the black hole? The Sun is located at 8.5 kpc from the Galactic Center.
