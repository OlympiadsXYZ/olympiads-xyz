## Условие

Приемаме, че понастоящем Вселената се описва добре от параметър на плътността $\Omega_0 = 1$. Приемете, че няма тъмна енергия и температурата на Вселената в настоящия момент е 2.73 К. Като знаете, че температурата на Вселената е обратнопропорционална на нейния радиус (т.нар. мащабен фактор), пресметнете след колко време Вселената ще се охлади с 0.1 К.

### English archive solution

The differential equation may be solved using an approximation (Way 1) or calculus (Way 2).

**Way 1.** By definition of the Hubble constant, $da/dt=Ha$, where $a$ is the scale factor. Approximate $da/dt\approx\Delta a/\Delta t$, suppose the variations of scale factor and Hubble constant are small compared with their values, and use $aT=\text{constant}$:

$$
\frac{\Delta a}{\Delta t}=H_i a_i\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\frac{\Delta T}{\Delta t}=-H_iT_i\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Delta t=-\frac1{H_i}\frac{\Delta T}{T_i}=5.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{years}.
$$

**Way 2.** In a matter-dominated universe, $\rho\propto a^{-3}$, with no dark energy and $\Omega_0=1$:

$$
\Omega_0=\frac{8\pi G\rho}{3H^2}=\frac{8\pi G\zeta}{3H^2a^3}=1,
$$

$$
\left(\frac{da}{dt}\right)^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\frac\zeta a\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\frac{da}{dt}=\sqrt{\frac{8\pi G}{3}\frac\zeta a}.
$$

Integrating,

$$
\frac23\left[a_f^{3/2}-a_i^{3/2}\right]=\sqrt{\frac{8\pi G\zeta}{3}}\,\Delta t,
$$

$$
\Delta t=\frac{\frac23a_i^{3/2}\left[(a_f/a_i)^{3/2}-1\right]}{\sqrt{8\pi G\zeta/3}}=\frac2{3H_i}\left[\left(\frac{a_f}{a_i}\right)^{3/2}-1\right].
$$

Using $aT=\text{constant}$, $a_iT_i=a_fT_f$ and $a_f/a_i=T_i/T_f=2.73/2.63=1.038$. The time is $\Delta t=5.2\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{years}$.

**Marking:** common, finding final scale factor 2 pt. Way 1, using approximation correctly 7 pt and final answer 1 pt. Way 2, finding differential equation 4 pt, integrating 3 pt, final answer 1 pt. Observation: 7 pt for correctly using $a\propto t^{2/3}$.
