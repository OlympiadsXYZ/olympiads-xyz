## Условие

**4. Hydroplanet.** Hydroplanet consists of a rocky “core” of radius $R$ and a thick layer of water surrounding it from all sides. Local humans live at the bottom of this world’s ocean (i.e., on the surface of the “core”), and hydrosphere is an analogue of our atmosphere for them. Local scientists observe astronomical objects from the bottom of the ocean. Like on our Earth, the duration of day-night period on Hydroplanet is $T = 24$ hours.

**4.1.** Find the minimum depth $H$ of the ocean, for which celestial bodies will be visible at the horizon.

**4.2.** What will be the duration of the day for inhabitants of the planet’s equator? The disk of the central star can be considered as a point source of light.

**4.3.** Calculate the value of “atmoshperical” refraction at the horizon on such a planet.

Points of your solution have to include drawings with all necessary sizes or angular sizes. The outer surface of the ocean is smooth, no waves or ripples.
