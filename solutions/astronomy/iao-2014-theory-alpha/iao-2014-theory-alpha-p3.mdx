## Условие

Can the star Proxima Centauri be visible from the vicinity of Alpha Centauri by naked eye? («да-yes» or «нет-no»). Justify your solution by calculations of the corresponding apparent magnitude of Proxima Centauri.

### Solution

Let us find the distance $d$ between Proxima and Alpha Centauri.

$$
d=(x^2+y^2+z^2)^{1/2},
$$

where x, y and z are projections of the distance on the axes of our coordinate system. Necessary data we should take from the “Data on some stars”. Approximately

$$
x=|\alpha_{\rm PC}-\alpha_{\alpha C}|\cos\delta_{P\alpha C}\,L_{P\alpha C}=\frac{|\alpha_{\rm PC}-\alpha_{\alpha C}|\cos\delta_{P\alpha C}}{p_{P\alpha C}},
$$

$$
y=|\delta_{\rm PC}-\delta_{\alpha C}|L_{P\alpha C}=\frac{|\delta_{\rm PC}-\delta_{\alpha C}|}{p_{P\alpha C}},
$$

$$
z=|L_{\rm PC}-L_{\alpha C}|=|1/p_{\rm PC}-1/p_{\alpha C}|,
$$

where $\alpha,\delta,L,p$ are right ascension, declination, distance from the Earth, parallax, and values with sub-index PC are related to Proxima Centauri; those with sub-index αC, to Alpha Centauri; and PαC, to the middle point of Proxima and Alpha Centauri. So, from the “Data on some stars”, we should take $\alpha,\delta,p$ of Proxima and Alpha Centauri. Calculations give us:

$$
x=0.0269\ \mathrm{pc},\quad y=0.0425\ \mathrm{pc},\quad z=0.0383\ \mathrm{pc},\quad d=0.0632\ \mathrm{pc}.
$$

Thus, in comparison with observation from the Earth, the magnitude of Proxima Centauri while observed from the vicinity of Alpha Centauri is smaller by

$$
\Delta m=5\log(L_{P\alpha C}/d)=-5\log(d\,p_{P\alpha C})\approx6.57
$$

than the magnitude while observed from the Earth.

$$
m=m_E-\Delta m=11.05-6.57\approx4.5.
$$

It is less than the limit value for naked eye, 6, so Proxima Centauri can be visible from the vicinity of Alpha Centauri by naked eye, «да-yes».

*Note: To prove the proposal «нет-no» of the first question, it would not be necessary to make so many calculations of x, y, z and d. It is evident that $d\ge z$, so if Proxima Centauri cannot be visible from the distance z, it would be also not visible from the distance d.*
