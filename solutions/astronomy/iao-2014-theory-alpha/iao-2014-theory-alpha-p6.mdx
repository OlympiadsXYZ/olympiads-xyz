## Условие

**6. Brightest stars.** Which four stars will be the brightest in the night sky of Cholpon-Ata in the CL (150th) century AD? Consider that ~5° of the sky above the horizon are blocked by mountains. Explain your answer and prove it by necessary drawings and calculations. Any names of stars write in Latin.
