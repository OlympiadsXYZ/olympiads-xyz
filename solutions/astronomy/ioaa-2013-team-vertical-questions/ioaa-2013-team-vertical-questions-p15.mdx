*13 т.*

## Условие



**i.** The maximum effective temperature of the surface of a Cepheid is $9,000K$ and the minimum is $7,000K$. The difference between its brightness maximum and minimum is $2.0$ Absolute Bolometric Magnitudes. How many times bigger is the maximum radius of the Cepheid than its minimum radius? (one decimal) **[2 т.]**

**ii.** In 2013, after how many days after the Summer Solstice did the Earth reach Aphelion? (integer) **[2 т.]**

**iii.** The Sun belongs to the Population \_\_ stars. **[1 т.]**

**iv.** The comet LINEAR is a periodic comet with aphelion distance of 5.29 A.U. And aphelion velocity as 10.45km/s. What is the semi-major axis of its orbit in A.U.? (up to 1$^{st}$ decimal) **[2 т.]**

**v.** Identify the following planets by recognizing their internal structure: Jupiter, Neptune, Mercury **[3 т.]**
