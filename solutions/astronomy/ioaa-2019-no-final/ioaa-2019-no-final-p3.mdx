## Условие

The star chart in the answer sheet shows a part of constellation Lyra. In this chart, **draw the FOV circle around M57 as accurately as possible.**

If you cannot find M57, the assistant will help you, but only after 5 minutes. In this case you will lose the marks for pointing to the object.
