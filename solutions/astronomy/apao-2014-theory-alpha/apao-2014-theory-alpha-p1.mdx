## Условие

Babr is a local representative of the biological family of cats (felidae) depicted on the coat of arms of Irkutsk, and the emblem of the X APAO (with a telescope in his mouth). On June 6, 2012, Irkutsk astronomers have photographed the transit of the planet Venus passing across the solar disk (fig. 1). And on November 19, 2014, they saw a sunspot, like a paw print of babr, and named this spot “footstep of babr” (fig. 2). The surface of the Sun is shown in the same scale in both images.

**1.1** Find which is larger and how much larger: the area of a sunspot “footstep of babr” or the area of the Earth's surface.

**1.2** Estimate, what mass would a felid have, which may print footsteps of such size on the solar surface. Consider the structure of the space felid to be similar to one of the Siberian felidae. With the mass of what cosmic body can the result be compared? Recollect for yourself the required information about the felidae.
