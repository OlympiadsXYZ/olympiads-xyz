## Условие

The Babr decided to join company of astronomy animals-observers and came to Listvyanka to the X APAO to take photos of stars of Orion’s Belt near the horizon. Will this be possible? (**«да-yes»** or **«нет-no»**). When is it better to take the photos: on rising, culmination or setting of Orion? At approximately what time (use time of Irkutsk) should the photos be taken? The solution has to include an artistic picture with an image of the Babr-observer taking photos of Orion’s Belt.
