## Условие

Galaxies in rich clusters have a higher tendency to merge with other galaxies, and thus tend to be poor standard candles. The *Coma* cluster contains 1000 galaxies within its half-mass radius $r_h = 1.5Mpc$.

**(a)** Determine the mean number density of galaxies within $r_h$. **[2 т.]**

**(b)** Given that the typical cross-sectional area of a galaxy is $A = 10^{-3}\mathrm{MPc}^2$ and that the velocity dispersion of the Coma cluster is $v = 880\mathrm{km/s}$, on average how far will a galaxy in the Coma cluster travel before colliding with another galaxy? Determine the average merger rate (per year). **[4 т.]**

**(c)** *NGC 4874* in the Coma cluster, has an apparent magnitude of 12.7. Considering it to be approximately circular with angular diameter 1.26’, determine its average surface brightness, in $mag/arcsec^2$. **[3 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **(a)** $7.07 \times 10^{-17}$ pc⁻³
- **(b)** l = 14.1 Mpc; t = 4.96 × 10¹⁷ s = 15.7 billion years; merger rate N/t = 6.4 × 10⁻⁸ /year
- **(c)** 21.8 mag/arcsec²

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**3a**

$$
n = \frac{1000}{\frac{4}{3}\pi r_h^3} = 7.07 \times 10^{-17}\mathrm{Pc}^{-3}
$$

$$
= 2.41 \times 10^{-66}m^{-3}
$$

**M1** **A1**

**3b**

The mean free path $l$ is given by

$$
l = (\sigma n)^{-1}
$$

$$
= 14.1\mathrm{MPc}
$$

**M2**

$$
t = \frac{l}{v} = 4.96 \times 10^{17}s = 15.7\ \text{billion years}
$$

**M1**

This is larger than the age of the universe! **A1**

Within the half mass of the galaxy, the total average merger rate is

$$
\frac{N}{t} = 6.37 \times 10^{-8}/\text{year}
$$

$$
= 6.4 \times 10^{-8}/\text{year}
$$

**3c**

$$
\Omega = \frac{\pi\theta_D^2}{4}
$$

$$
S = m + 2.5\log(\Omega) = 21.8\ \mathrm{mag/arcsec^2}
$$

**M2** **A1**


</Spoiler>
