## Условие

Answer **ALL** questions in this section.

You’re suddenly teleported to the antipode of Point Nemo (41.123° N, 56.607° E). Even though you’re at the opposite end of the “loneliest place on Earth”, you’re still stuck in the middle of a desolate desert in Kazakhstan-Uzbekistan border, and you’ve resigned to your fate. To kill time, you’ve decided to do some night sky observation.

Luckily for you, you have 2 watches! But you remember that’s because one of them is faulty… You read the watches and they read as follows:

Watch 1: **20th March 2021 07:20 a.m. (GMT+8)**

Watch 2: **9h 28m 25.68s**

Where both watches read the local time and local sidereal time respectively.

Thankfully, your obsession with the constellation Boötes resulted in you being able to precisely remember the equatorial coordinates of Arcturus (14h 15m 38.22s, +19° 10’ 10.0”).

**1.** For starters, let’s get your bearings straight. Mark on the star chart the cardinal directions (North, South, East and West) right outside the boundaries of the star chart with ‘N’ and ‘S’ respectively. The size of the letters should be similar to this font size. **[1 т.]**

**2.** Trace and label **BOTH** the ecliptic and meridian. **[2 т.]**

**3.** You remember a village elder from a nearby village said that today is the Vernal Equinox. Knowing this,

**3.(a)** Mark with an ‘X’ on the boundary of the star chart where the Sun would rise. **[1 т.]**

**3.(b)** You suddenly remember that watch 2 is the faulty watch. Provide an estimate to what a correct reading should be. **[2 т.]**

**4.** Now, for observation,

**4.(a)** Is the Summer Triangle visible? What are its 3 constituent stars? Clearly mark it on the star chart by connecting the 3 stars and label it. **[2 т.]**

**4.(b)** Is the Keystone in Hercules visible? Clearly mark it on the star chart by connecting the stars that comprise this asterism and label it. **[1 т.]**

**4.(c)** Clearly mark and label **ANY 2** of the following constellations (They must be fully visible):

1. Aquila
2. Scorpius
3. Sagittarius
4. Ophiuchus
5. Perseus
6. Lupus
7. Cepheus
8. Orion

Note: As long as all of the lines fall within the constellation boundaries, the answer will be accepted **[4 т.]**

**4.(d)** Circle clearly and label **ANY 3** of the following Deep Sky Objects (They must be visible):

1. M4 Crab Globular Cluster
2. M16 Eagle Nebula
3. M41
4. M29 Cooling Tower
5. C94 Jewel Box
6. M44 Praesepe
7. C80 Omega Centauri Cluster
8. M45 Pleiades
9. C102 Southern Pleiades
10. Small Magellanic Cloud **[6 т.]**

**4.(e)** A prominent star has been omitted from the star chart this whole time.

**4.(e) i.** Circle the approximate location of the star and identify the star. **[2 т.]**

**4.(e) ii.** Name **ONE** Deep Sky Object that lies within the constellation boundaries this star is in. **[1 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**3. (a)** ‘X’ should be marked on East. **A1**

**(b)** LST = 14h 57m 19.2s ± 5m **A1**

Given that the RA of Arcturus is 14h 15m 38.22s, we can estimate the LST of the observer by considering the distance between Arcturus and the meridian. **M1**

**4. (a)** **A1**
