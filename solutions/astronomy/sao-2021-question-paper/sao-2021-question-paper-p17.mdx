- **1.** Constellation: Vulpecula, Sagitta, Lyra, Lacerta or Cygnus; any visible stars and DSOs within the frame.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**1.**

a. Vulpecula, Sagitta, Lyra, Lacerta, Cygnus

b. Any visible stars within the frame

c. Any visible DSOs within the frame

**2.**

a. Carina Vela

b. Any visible stars within the frame

c. Any visible DSOs within the frame
