## Условие

The star α-Centauri (Rigel Kentaurus) is a triple star which consists of two main-sequence stars α-Centauri A and α-Centauri B representing visual binary system, and the third star, called Proxima Centauri, which is smaller and fainter than the other two stars. The angular distance between α-Centauri A and α-Centauri B is 17.59”. The binary system has an orbital period of 79.24 years. The visual magnitudes of α-Centauri A and α-Centauri B are -0.01 and 1.34 respectively. Their color indices are 0.65 and 0.85 respectively. Use the data below to answer the following questions.

$BC$=Bolometric Correction, $(B-V)_0$=Intrinsic Color

Questions:

**1.** Plot the curve $BC$ versus $(B-V)_0$.

**2.** Determine the apparent bolometric magnitudes of α-Centauri A and α-Centauri B using the corresponding curve.

**3.** Calculate the mass of each star.

*Notes*:

1. **Bolometric correction** (BC) is a correction that must be made to the apparent magnitude of an object in order to convert an object's visible magnitude to its bolometric magnitude:

$$
BC = m_v - m_{bol} \quad \text{or} \quad BC = M_v - M_{bol}
$$

2. **Luminosity mass relation** : $M_{bol} = -10.2\log\left(\frac{\mathrm{M}}{\mathrm{M}_{\odot}}\right) + 4.9$

- **3.** M<sub>A</sub> = 1.1518 M<sub>sun</sub>, M<sub>B</sub> = 0.8785 M<sub>sun</sub>

**(20%)**

2.

⊙ For α Cen. A. , its colour index is given by $B - V = 0.65$. Then, by taking the line that intersecting the above curve we find $BC = 0.10$. **(10%)**

⊙ For α Cen. B. , its colour index is given by $B - V = 0.85$. Then, by taking the line that intersecting the above curve we find $BC = 0.25$. **(10%)**

From the relation $m_{\mathrm{v}} - m_{\mathrm{bol}} = BC$ it is then obtained,

⊙ For α Cen. A, $m_{\mathrm{v}} = -0.01$, we get $m_{\mathrm{bol}} = -0.01 - 0.10 = -0.11$ **(10%)**

⊙ For α Cen. B, $m_{\mathrm{v}} = 1.34$, we get $m_{\mathrm{bol}} = 1.34 - 0.25 = 1.09$ **(10%)**

3. Periodic orbit of α Centauri star is $P = 79.24$ years and its angle of semi major axis is $\alpha = 17.59''$. From the result 2a we find that the apparent bolometric magnitude of α Centauri A is $m_{\mathrm{bolA}} = -0.11$, while the apparent bolometric magnitude of α Centauri B is $m_{\mathrm{bolB}} = 1.09$. (two decimal places)

*1st iteration :*

Step 1 : Let $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{A}}$ and $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{B}}$ be the mass of Centauri A and Centauri B stars, respectively. Then, we firstly take $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{A}} + \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{B}} = 2$

Step 2 : Inserting the values of $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{A}} + \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{B}}$, $P$, and $\alpha$ to the third Kepler equation namely,

$$
\left(\frac{\alpha}{p}\right)^3 = \left(\mathcal{M}_A + \mathcal{M}_B\right)P^2
$$

One gets $p$, that is

$$
p = \left[\frac{\alpha^3}{\left(\mathcal{M}_A + \mathcal{M}_B\right)P^2}\right]^{1/3} = \left[\frac{(17.59)^3}{2(79.24)^2}\right]^{1/3} = \left[0.4334\right]^{1/3} = 0.7568''
$$

Step 3 : We determine the magnitude of absolute bolometric using Pogson equation,

$$
m_{bol} - M_{bol} = -5 - 5\log p \Longrightarrow M_{bol} = m_{bol} + 5 + 5\log p
$$

For α Centauri A:

$$
M_{bolA} = m_{bolA} + 5 + 5\log p = -0.11 + 5 + 5\log(0.7568) = -0.11 + 5 - 0.6051 = 4.2849
$$

For α Centauri B :

$$
M_{bolB} = m_{bolB} + 5 + 5\log p = 1.09 + 5 + 5\log(0.7568) = 1.09 + 5 - 0.6051 = 5.4849
$$

Step 4 : We determine the mass of both stars using luminosity mass relation,

$$
M_{bol} = -10.2\log\left(\frac{\mathcal{M}}{\mathcal{M}_{\odot}}\right) + 4.9 \Longrightarrow \mathcal{M} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - M_{bol}}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot}
$$

For α Centauri A:

$$
\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{A}} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - M_{bolA}}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - 4.2849}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 1.1490\mathcal{M}_{\odot}
$$

For α Centauri B:

$$
\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{B}} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - M_{bolB}}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - 5.4949}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 0.8763\mathcal{M}_{\odot}
$$

**(30%)**

*2nd iteration* Repeating the step 1 until 4 and using the previous resulting mass in the first iteration:

Step 1 : $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{A}} + \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{B}} = 1.1490 + 0.8763 = 2.0253$

Step 2 :
$$
p = \left[\frac{\alpha^3}{\left(\mathcal{M}_A + \mathcal{M}_B\right)P^2}\right]^{1/3} = \left[\frac{(17.59)^3}{(2.0253)(79.24)^2}\right]^{1/3} = 0.7536
$$

Step 3 : For α Centauri A:

$$
M_{bolA} = m_{bolA} + 5 + 5\log p = -0.11 + 5 + 5\log(0.7536) = 4.2757
$$

For α Centauri B :

$$
M_{bolB} = m_{bolB} + 5 + 5\log p = 1.09 + 5 + 5\log(0.7536) = 5.4757
$$

Step 4 : For α Centauri A:

$$
\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{A}} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - M_{bolA}}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - 4.2757}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 1.1513\mathcal{M}_{\odot}
$$

For α Centauri B:

$$
\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{B}} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - M_{bolB}}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - 5.4757}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 0.8781\mathcal{M}_{\odot}
$$

**(10%)**

*3 rd Iteration* Repeating the step 1 until 4 and using the previous resulting mass in the second iteration:

Step 1 : $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{A}} + \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{B}} = 1.1513 + 0.8781 = 2.0294$

Step 2 :
$$
p = \left[\frac{\alpha^3}{\left(\mathcal{M}_A + \mathcal{M}_B\right)P^2}\right]^{1/3} = \left[\frac{(17.59)^3}{(2.0294)(79.24)^2}\right]^{1/3} = 0.75314
$$

Step 3 : For α Centauri A:

$$
M_{bolA} = m_{bolA} + 5 + 5\log p = -0.11 + 5 + 5\log(0.7531) = 4.2751
$$

For α Centauri B :

$$
M_{bolB} = m_{bolB} + 5 + 5\log p = 1.09 + 5 + 5\log(0.7531) = 5.4751
$$

Step 4 : For α Centauri A:

$$
\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{A}} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - M_{bolA}}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - 4.2751}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 1.1515\mathcal{M}_{\odot}
$$

For α Centauri B:

$$
\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{B}} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - M_{bolB}}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - 5.4751}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 0.8782\mathcal{M}_{\odot}
$$

**(0%)**

*4 th Iteration* Repeating the step 1 until 4 and using the previous resulting mass in the third iteration:

Step 1 : $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{A}} + \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{B}} = 1.1515 + 0.8782 = 2.0297$

Step 2 :
$$
p = \left[\frac{\alpha^3}{\left(\mathcal{M}_A + \mathcal{M}_B\right)P^2}\right]^{1/3} = \left[\frac{(17.59)^3}{(2.0297)(79.24)^2}\right]^{1/3} = 0.7531
$$

Step 3 : For α Centauri A:

$$
M_{bolA} = m_{bolA} + 5 + 5\log p = -0.11 + 5 + 5\log(0.7531) = 4.2753
$$

For α Centauri B :

$$
M_{bolB} = m_{bolB} + 5 + 5\log p = 1.09 + 5 + 5\log(0.7531) = 5.4743
$$

Step 4 : For α Centauri A:

$$
\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{A}} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - M_{bolA}}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - 4.2753}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 1.1517\mathcal{M}_{\odot}
$$

For α Centauri B:

$$
\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{B}} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - M_{bolB}}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - 5.4743}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 0.8784\mathcal{M}_{\odot}
$$

**(0%)**

*5 th Iteration* Repeating the step 1 until 4 and using the previous resulting mass in the fourth iteration:

Step 1 : $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{A}} + \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{B}} = 1.1517 + 0.8784 = 2.0301$

Step 2 :
$$
p = \left[\frac{\alpha^3}{\left(\mathcal{M}_A + \mathcal{M}_B\right)P^2}\right]^{1/3} = \left[\frac{(17.59)^3}{(2.0301)(79.24)^2}\right]^{1/3} = 0.7530''
$$

Step 3 : For α Centauri A:

$$
M_{bolA} = m_{bolA} + 5 + 5\log p = -0.11 + 5 + 5\log(0.7530) = 4.2740
$$

For α Centauri B :

$$
M_{bolB} = m_{bolB} + 5 + 5\log p = 1.10 + 5 + 5\log(0.7530) = 5.4740
$$

Step 4 : For α Centauri A:

$$
\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{A}} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - M_{bolA}}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - 4.2740}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 1.1518\mathcal{M}_{\odot}
$$

For α Centauri B:

$$
\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{B}} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - M_{bolB}}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 10^{\left(\frac{4.9 - 5.4740}{10.2}\right)}\mathcal{M}_{\odot} = 0.8785\mathcal{M}_{\odot}
$$

**(0%)**

The results of the fifth iteration equal the results of the fourth iteration, so

The mass of α Centauri A : $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{A}} = 1.1518\ \mathcal{M}_{\odot}$

The mass of α Centauri B : $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{B}} = 0.8785\ \mathcal{M}_{\odot}$
