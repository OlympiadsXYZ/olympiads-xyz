*OBSERVATIONAL EXAM – 1ST NIGHT · 4 август 2012 г.*

## Условие

Use ***star chart-2*** to estimate the magnitude of the missing star, shown as a cross, inside NGC 6231. Use magnitude of other stars as reference.

**Note**: To avoid confusion between decimal dot and real stars, dots are suppressed. So, magnitude 60 correspond to magnitude 6.0. Give your answer using one decimal scale and 0.1 precession. (Duration: 5 min)
