*OBSERVATIONAL EXAM – 1ST NIGHT · 4 август 2012 г.*

<details>
<summary>General instructions — Observational Exam</summary>

- The duration of the exam is 20 min for each student.
- There will be a copy of these instructions inside the envelope with the exam that will be given to you.
- The exam is individual, and **no conversation** with anybody except with the guide who will be helping you with the telescope is allowed.
- The necessary sky charts are included in the exam that will be handed to you.
- In questions that involve pointing the telescope, the student can ask help of assistant at any time, at the cost of not getting the full marks for that question.
- **Material provided for each student:** red flashlight, laser pointer, pencil, pen, eraser and clipboard.
- **WRITE YOUR ANSWERS IN INK.** The organization will give you pens.

</details>

## Условие

Estimate the diameter of the field of view of this telescope, using 10mm Plössl eyepiece and ***star chart-1***, showing nearby region of open cluster NGC 6231. On ***star chart 1*** there are two angular distances shown. Use them as reference. Express your answer in arc minutes. (Duration: 5 min)

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 24,0 arc minutes (0.4°)

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Official key item 1:** Telescope already pointed to that region (or not?) (5 minutes).

**Answer: 24,0 arc minutes (0.4°).** We will tolerate up to 10% error as correct answer. Error between 10 to 20% will be considered 70% correct.


</Spoiler>
