## Условие

Calculate, which star emits more energy: the Sun or Alpha Centauri A+B.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- Alpha Centauri A+B emits more energy than the Sun.

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

<ProblemSection id="solution-official-solution">

### α-4. Alpha Centauri.

The magnitude of the Sun is $M_{\mathrm{Sun}} = -26.8^{\mathrm{m}}$, the magnitude of Alpha Centauri A – $m_{\alpha\mathrm{CenA}} = -0{,}01^{\mathrm{m}}$, Alpha Centauri B – $m_{\alpha\mathrm{CenB}} = 1{,}33^{\mathrm{m}}$. Comparing these values one can calculate, from which star more light comes to us, and how many times more. But we can not know, which of the objects emits more, because they are at different distances.

Let us calculate the apparent magnitude of Alpha Centauri A, if it was located at the place of the Sun,

$$
M_{\alpha\mathrm{CenA}} = m_{\alpha\mathrm{CenA}} + 5\cdot\log((\mathrm{pc/au})''/p_{\alpha\mathrm{Cen}}) = -0{,}01^{\mathrm{m}} + 5^{\mathrm{m}}\cdot\log(206265''/0.747'') \approx -0{,}01^{\mathrm{m}} - 27.21^{\mathrm{m}} = -27.22^{\mathrm{m}}
$$

This value is smaller than that of the Sun. Then Alpha Centauri A radiates more energy. Adding radiation Alpha Centauri B (not even calculating the value), gets more. Thus, Alpha Centauri A+B emits more energy than the Sun.

</ProblemSection>
