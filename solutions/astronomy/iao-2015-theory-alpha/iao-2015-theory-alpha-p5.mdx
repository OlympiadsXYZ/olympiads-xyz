## Условие

**5. Motion of a satellite.** An artificial satellite, moving in equatorial, slightly elliptical orbit passed the perigee point at the height of $H_P = 428.0$ km from the sea level; and its speed at the point was 0.6% higher than the circumferential velocity of the given point. Find the time after which the satellite will reach the height $H_1 = 498.0$ km?
