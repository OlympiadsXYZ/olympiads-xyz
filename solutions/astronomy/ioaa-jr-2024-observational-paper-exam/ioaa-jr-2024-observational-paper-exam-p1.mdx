*IOAA jr. 2024 — Observation Exam, English (Official) · 43,5 т.*

## Условие

The map in the answersheet represents the sky somewhere in Nepal. The Zenith is at the center of the chart.

**1.** Estimate the latitude of the location from where this sky map is captured. [2.5 points] **[2,5 т.]**

**2.** Label the cardinal points(N,E,S,W) on the map and spot three planets from the Solar System in the sky map.[1x7 = 7 points] **[7 т.]**

**3.** Draw on the map with continuous lines the celestial equator (EQ), the ecliptic (EC), and the local meridian (ME) and label them.[1.5x3 = 4.5 points] **[4,5 т.]**

**4.** Trace 10 constellations in the sky map and label them with numbers and write the corresponding latin name of the constellations or IAU code in the table below. [1x10 = 10 points] **[10 т.]**

**5.** Identify and mark the 4 brightest stars in the sky map below. Number the star from 1 – the brightest, and continue with the fainter ones till number 4 for the faintest. Fill in the following table the Bayer designation or the official IAU name of the four identified stars. [1.5x4 = 6 points] **[6 т.]**

**6.** Mark (with a small circle) positions of the following objects and label them. [1.5x7 = 10.5 points]

- M42
- M81
- M41
- M31
- $\gamma$ Ori
- $\alpha$ Per
- $\alpha$ Cyg **[10,5 т.]**

**7.** Estimate the equatorial coordinates of the star Aldebaran ($\alpha$ Tau). [3 points] **[3 т.]**
