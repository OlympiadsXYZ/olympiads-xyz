<details>
<summary>Indications — READ CAREFULY</summary>

**Outside the dome:**

1) The observational round in the field should take a maximum of 30 minutes;
2) Please pay attention to the instructions by assistants.
3) You will be directed to a designated telescope. Here you will find attached to the clipboard the answersheet.
4) For the observational test outdoor, we are using a Newtonian telescope on equatorial mount EQ5 (D=200 mm, F=1000 mm).

   Note: the telescope is already aligned, but not necessarily calibrated – do not change the position of the tripod!

5) Fill your student ID in the box.
6) **Please write the time at the start of the observation test on the top of next page!**
7) PLEASE WRITE ONLY ON THE PRINTED SIDE OF THE ANSWER SHEET.  DON’T USE THE REVERSE SIDE. The evaluator will not take into account what is written on the reverse of the answersheet.

**GOOD LUCK!**
