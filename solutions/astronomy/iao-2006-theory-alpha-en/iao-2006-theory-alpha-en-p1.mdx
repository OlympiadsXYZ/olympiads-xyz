## Условие

On the observational round you will use a simple Newton-system telescope with the mirror diameter $D=125$ mm, focal length $F=1025$ mm and 3 eye-pieces with focal lengths $f_1=12$ mm, $f_2=25$ mm and $f_3=38$ mm. Find on what distances ($\Delta x_1=$, $\Delta x_2=$, $\Delta x_3=$ in vertical direction on the middle figure) it is necessary to move the corresponding eye-pieces (1,2,3) to readjust the telescope from observation of the sky objects to observation of a monkey sitting at a distance $L=50$ m from you. (Your answer should have both formula and numerical values.) What eyepiece is the most suitable for observation of the monkey? Why?

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

$$
\frac1L+\frac1x=\frac1F,\qquad x=\frac{FL}{L-F}.
$$

Sky objects: $L=\infty$, $x_0=F$.

Monkey: $L=L_m$, $x_m=FL_m/(L_m-F)$.

$$
\Delta x_m=\Delta x_m-x_0=\frac{FL_m}{L_m-F}-F=\frac{F^2}{L_m-F}.
$$

The answer does not depend on the focal lengths of the eye-piece, i.e.

$$
\Delta x_1=\Delta x_2=\Delta x_3=\frac{F^2}{L_m-F}=\frac{1025^2}{50000-1025}=21.45\ \mathrm{mm}.
$$

The eye-piece with the largest focal length is the most suitable for observation of the monkey. The magnification factors are $F/f$, for $f_1=12$ mm, $f_2=25$ mm and $f_3=38$ mm, which means that $\Gamma=85.4$, 41 and 26.9 respectively. So the effective distance of observing the monkey is $50\ \mathrm m/\Gamma$, 59 cm, 122 cm and 186 cm respectively. If you remember the field of view of any usual telescope and the size of a monkey, it would be evident that even for $f_3=38$ mm it is too difficult to observe the whole monkey to say nothing about $f_2=25$ mm and $f_1=12$ mm. So $f_3=38$ mm is the most suitable eye-piece among those that we have.


</Spoiler>
