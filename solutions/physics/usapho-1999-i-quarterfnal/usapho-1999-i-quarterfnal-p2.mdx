## Условие

A cube of mass M starts at rest at point A at a height of 4R, where R is the radius of the circular part of the track. The cube slides down the track and around the loop. The cube is very small compared to the size of the track.

<figure>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-1999-i-quarterfnal/p2-fig1.png" alt="Track diagram: a small square cube at point A at the top of a vertical track that curves down and into a circular loop of radius R; point B (with a small square) is at the top of the loop. A grey double-headed vertical arrow labelled 4R marks the height of A, and a grey horizontal arrow labelled R marks the loop radius." />
</figure>

**a.** Assuming the track is frictionless, find the force the track exerts on the cube at point B. Express your answer as a function of Mg. **[15 т.]**

**b.** The cube is replaced by a sphere with mass M and radius r ($r \ll R$). Assuming that the sphere starts at the same height 4R and rolls without slipping, find the force the track exerts on the sphere at point B. Express your answer as a function of Mg. **[10 т.]**

(contributed by Mary Mogge)

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a.** $N = 3Mg$
- **b.** $N = \tfrac{13}{7}Mg$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

2. a. Apply energy conservation to equate total energy at point A and point B. **[2]**

$4MgR = 2MgR + \tfrac{1}{2}Mv^2$ **[3]**

$Mv^2 = 4MgR$ **[1]**

Applying Newton's 2$^{\mathrm{nd}}$ law to the forces at point B **[2]**

$N + Mg = M\dfrac{v^2}{R}$ **[4]**

$N + Mg = \dfrac{(4MgR)}{R}$ **[1]**

or $N = 3Mg$ downward **[2]**

b. Since it is rolling at point B, it also has rotational kinetic energy there

$4MgR = 2MgR + \tfrac{1}{2}Mv^2 + \tfrac{2}{5}MR^2\omega^2$ **[3]**

With $\omega = v/R$ **[3]**

$2MgR = \left(\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{2}{5}\right)Mv^2 = \tfrac{7}{10}Mv^2$ **[1]**

$N + Mg = \dfrac{\left(\tfrac{20}{7}MgR\right)}{R}$ **[1]**

Thus $N = \tfrac{13}{7}Mg$ downward **[2]**


</Spoiler>
