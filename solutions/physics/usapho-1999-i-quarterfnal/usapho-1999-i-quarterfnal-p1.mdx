## Условие

A tennis ball launcher is placed on the floor at the front end of a train that has a uniform forward acceleration of $2.00\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$. The launcher projects a ball at an initial speed of $25.0\ \mathrm{m/s}$ with respect to the train toward its rear. The ball achieves a maximum height of $10.0\ \mathrm{m}$. Ignore air resistance.

**a.** Find $\theta_o$, the angle of launch with respect to the floor. **[10 т.]**

**b.** Find how far from the front end of the train the ball lands. **[15 т.]**

(contributed by Leaf Turner)

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a.** 34.1 °
- **b.** 67.3 m

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

1. $y_{\max} = 10\ \mathrm{m}$ $\quad v_o = 25\ \mathrm{m/s}$ $\quad a = 2\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$

a. The train's acceleration does not affect the vertical motion.

At the highest point, $v_y = 0$ **[2]**

$v_y^2 = v_{oy}^2 - 2gy$ **[2]**

$v_{oy} = \sqrt{2gy_{\max}} = \sqrt{2(9.8\ \mathrm{m/s^2})(10\ \mathrm{m})} = 14\ \mathrm{m/s}$ **[2]**

Since $v_{oy} = v_o \sin\theta$ **[2]**

$\theta = \sin^{-1}\left(v_{oy}/v_o\right) = \sin^{-1}\left(14\ \mathrm{m/s} / 25\ \mathrm{m/s}\right) = 34.1^{\circ}$ **[2]**

b. The time to reach $y_{\max}$ is obtained from $v_y = v_{oy} - gt$ at the highest point

$t = \dfrac{v_{oy}}{g} = \dfrac{14\ \mathrm{m/s}}{9.8\ \mathrm{m/s^2}} = 1.43\ \mathrm{s}$ **[5]**

The time to land is twice that, or $t = 2.86$ s. **[2]**

During that time, the ball moves a distance $x_b = v_{oc}t - v_o\cos\theta t$ **[2]**

where $v_{oc}$ is the velocity of the train with respect to the Earth when the ball is launched.

And the front end of the train moves $x_p = v_{oc}t + \tfrac{1}{2}at^2$ **[2]**

The difference of those displacements is the ball's displacement with respect to the train.

$\Delta x = x_b - x_p = -v_o\cos\theta t - \tfrac{1}{2}at^2$ **[2]**

$\Delta x = -25\ \mathrm{m/s}\left(\cos 34.1^{\circ}\right)2.86\ \mathrm{s} - \tfrac{1}{2}\left(2\ \mathrm{m/s^2}\right)\left(2.86\ \mathrm{s}\right)^2 = -67.3\ \mathrm{m}$ **[2]**

(Either sign okay. 67.4 m also okay. Part b may also be solved in the train's accelerating frame or in an inertial frame with the same velocity with respect to the Earth that the train had when the ball was launched. Any correct method may be used.)


</Spoiler>
