*Olympiad of Metropolises, Moscow. Blitz Contest, September, 5 · 5 септември 2016 г.*

## Условие

Given a cube, consider diagonals of two faces such that the two diagonals share a vertex (figure).

Find the angle between diagonals.

Give the answer in degrees.
