*Olympiad of Metropolises, Moscow. Blitz Contest, September, 5 · 5 септември 2016 г.*

## Условие

Let $\mathcal{R}$ be the set of all triples $(x, y, z)$ of non-negative integers such that $x + y + z = 100$ and at least one of two conditions holds: $x \ge 28$ or $z \ge 42$.

For example, one such triple is $(27, 12, 61)$.

How many elements does set $\mathcal{R}$ have?
