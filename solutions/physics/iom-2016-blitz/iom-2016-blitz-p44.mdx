*Olympiad of Metropolises, Moscow. Blitz Contest, September, 5 · 5 септември 2016 г.*

## Условие

Array A has 15 positive integers indicating the price for visiting a station on a linear road:

$\{10, 5, 20, 10, 30, 40, 30, 30, 30, 50, 100, 2, 10, 3, 20\}$

Jerry starts at the first station and he wants to get to the last station in the most inexpensive route. When Jerry is in station $i$ he has two options: to move to station $i+1$ or skip it and getting straight to station $i+2$.

Jerry has to pay for each station he visits. What is the minimal cost for Jerry’s trip including start and finish values? (For example, for array 10 2 9 5 the minimal cost is 17 = 10 + 2 + 5)
