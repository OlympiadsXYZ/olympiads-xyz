*Olympiad of Metropolises, Moscow. Blitz Contest, September, 5 · 5 септември 2016 г.*

## Условие

See figure.

Point $M$ is the center of the circle, $BA$ equals to radius of the circle, $\angle CMD = 78^{\circ}$.

Find $\angle BFC$.

Give the answer in degrees.
