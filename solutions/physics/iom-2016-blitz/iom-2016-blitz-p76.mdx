*Olympiad of Metropolises, Moscow. Blitz Contest, September, 5 · 5 септември 2016 г.*

## Условие

A box contains 501 pieces of identical coins. Each coin weighs 4.13 grams and is composed of gold and silver. We dissolve one of the coins in 12.6 g of 50% (in mass fraction) nitric acid and observe liberation of a brown gas. 0.06 moles of nitric acid remains after the reaction is complete. How many rubles does the box cost in total after the experiment if 1 mol of gold costs 5 rubles and 1 mol of silver costs 2 rubles? Give the integer value.
