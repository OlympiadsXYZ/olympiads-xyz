## Условие

A 20.0 g sample of mercury(II) oxide (M = 216.6) is heated to high temperature, causing it to decompose to metallic Hg and O$_2$ gas. What volume of O$_2$ is produced (measured at 0 $^{o}$C, 1 atm)?

1. 1.03 L
2. 2.07 L
3. 4.14 L
4. 14.0 L
