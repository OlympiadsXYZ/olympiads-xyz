*Olympiad of Metropolises, Moscow. Blitz Contest, September, 5 · 5 септември 2016 г.*

## Условие

Let’s call a number “increasing” if every digit in it from left to right is less than the next digit, for example 1234 or 24789.

Find out how many of these numbers exists that consist of 4 adjacent prime numbers, for example 2357 (2, 3, 5, 7) or 2345789 (2, 3, 457, 89).
