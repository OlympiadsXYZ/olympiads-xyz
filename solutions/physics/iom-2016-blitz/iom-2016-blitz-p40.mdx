## Условие

26.4 g of a mixture of FeS and FeS$_2$ reacts with an excess of oxygen giving 20.8 g of the solid product (Fe$_2$O$_3$). What is the mass percentage (%) of FeS in this mixture? Give the integer answer without units.

(Use atomic masses: Fe – 56, S – 32.)
