*Olympiad of Metropolises, Moscow. Blitz Contest, September, 5 · 5 септември 2016 г.*

## Условие

A voltaic cell is made of Pb and Fe as well as of 1 M solutions of lead(II) nitrate and iron(II) sulfate. Using only the data given below

| Reaction | E$^{o}$ |
|---|---|
| Pb$^{4+}$(aq) + 4e → Pb(s) | 0.837 V |
| Pb$^{4+}$(aq) + 2e → Pb$^{2+}$(aq) | 1.800 V |
| Fe$^{2+}$(aq) + 2e → Fe(s) | –0.439 V |

determine the standard emf of the voltaic cell, assuming that most stable species are formed in the solution at each electrode. In the answer, give the integer value of emf*1000 (without units) (for example, 1800 instead of 1.800).
