*Olympiad of Metropolises, Moscow. Blitz Contest, September, 5 · 5 септември 2016 г.*

## Условие

The answer should be given in SI-units and rounded to the nearest integer, unless otherwise is stated. $g = 10$ m/s$^2$.

A point-like object is placed on the optical axis of a thin converging lens. At which distance from the lens, in the units of its focal length, should the object be placed in order to be closest to its real image?
