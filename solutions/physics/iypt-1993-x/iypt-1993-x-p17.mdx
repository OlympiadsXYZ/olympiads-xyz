Karate is power, speed, force and beauty! [16] Develop objective quantitative criteria making it possible to confer a “black belt” to a karate fighter. Maybe, you’ll become the inventor of a BB (black belt) device badly needed by referees or you’ll create a KM (karate meter) complex which is even more necessary for karate fighters for improving their skill.

<ProblemSection id="note-16">

### Note [16]

There is no introduction Karate is power, speed, force and beauty!” in the Slovak text. The Hungarian text reads: “The karate is talent, speed, strength and beauty!”
