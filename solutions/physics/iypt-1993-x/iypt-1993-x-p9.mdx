(S, C, K) is a power supply consisting of

S, a source of constant voltage in the range 10‒300 V, [8]

C, a capacitor with $C=1000\,\mu\mathrm F$,

K, a switch;

L is an induction coil;

F is a ferromagnetic core;

R is a metal ring projectile with mass from 1 to 100 g.

$C_n$ is a converter (some device that converts the energy passing from the capacitor to inductance L in a way you need.) This element does not contain energy sources. It may be completely absent from your gun.

You are to construct, make and demonstrate the electromagnetic gun. It is worth mentioning that the demonstration of your gun will take place with the power supply (elements S, C and K) presented by the Organizing Committee of the YPT. [9] Develop two variants of the gun: [9]

**9.** A long-range gun is to be constructed to shoot a ring to a maximum altitude. [10] The control parameter is the quantity $H=kh/U^2$, where $k=10000\,\mathrm{V^2}$, [11] h is the height of the projectile, U is the voltage to which the capacitor is charged.

**10.** A gun-lift is to be constructed to achieve the maximum work of lifting [12] a weight (ring). The control parameter is $W=mgh$, where m is the mass of the ring, $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
