*Problem 1: Resistor circuit · Problem 2: The descent of a skier · Problem 3: “The bead on the spring” · Problem 4: Galileo Galilei – “The minimum descent time” · 25 т.*

## Условие

A constant voltage $U_0$ is applied to the *ab* ends of the circuit in figure. The resistors have resistances $R_1=R_2=R_5=R$, $R_3=2R$, $R_4=4R$. The voltmeter and the ammeter are considered ideal.

**A.** (**9 points**) With the **switch S open** determine:

**A1.** The equivalent resistance, $R_{ab}$, between the *ab* ends of the circuit. **[4 т.]**

**A2.** The values measured by the ammeter and the voltmeter. **[3 т.]**

**A3.** The power dissipated on the resistor $R_4$. **[2 т.]**

**B.** (**13 points**) With the **switch S closed** determine:

**B1.** The equivalent resistance, $R_{ab}$, between the *ab* ends of the circuit. **[9 т.]**

**B2.** The values measured by the ammeter and the voltmeter. **[2 т.]**

**B3.** The power dissipated on the resistor $R_4$. **[2 т.]**

**C.** The switch S is closed and opened periodically, so that in a time period *T*, *2T/3* of the time is closed and *T/3* of the time is opened. Determine the average power dissipated on the resistor $R_4$. **[3 т.]**

Assume that all current values are reached instantaneously.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **A1.** $R_{ab} = 1{,}5R$
- **A2.** $I_A = 0,\quad U_V = \frac{U_0}{6}$
- **A3.** $P_4 = \frac{U_0^2}{9R}$
- **B1.** $R_{ab} = \frac{34}{23}R$
- **B2.** $I_5 = \frac{1}{17}\frac{U_0}{R},\quad U_5 = \frac{1}{17}U_0$
- **B3.** $P_4 = \frac{25U_0^2}{289R}$
- **C.** $P_{aver} \approx 0{,}09\frac{U_0^2}{R}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**A1**. **(4 points)**

$$
R_s = R_A + R_B \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
R_p = \frac{R_A R_B}{R_A + R_B} \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
R_{12} = R_1 + R_2 = 2R \qquad \textbf{(0.5p)}
$$

$$
R_{34} = R_3 + R_4 = 6R \qquad \textbf{(0.5p)}
$$

$$
R_{ab} = \frac{R_{12}R_{34}}{R_{12} + R_{34}} = 1{,}5R \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

**A2. (3points)**

$$
I_A = 0 \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
I_{12} = \frac{U_0}{R_{12}} = \frac{U_0}{2R} \qquad \textbf{(0.5p)}
$$

$$
I_{34} = \frac{U_0}{R_{34}} = \frac{U_0}{6R} \qquad \textbf{(0.5p)}
$$

$$
U_V = \left|I_{34}R_3 - I_{12}R_1\right| = \frac{U_0}{6} \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

**A3. (2 points)**

$$
P_4 = I_{34}^2 R_4 \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
P_4 = \frac{U_0^2}{9R} \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
**B1. (9 points)**\_
$$

$$
I_1R - I_5R - 2I_3R = 0 \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '40.4%', '--fig-max': '259px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/balkan-2022-x/p1-sol-fig1.png" alt="The circuit redrawn with switch closed: current I enters at a and splits into I1 through R (upper left) and I3 through 2R (lower left); I2 flows through R (upper right), I4 through 4R (lower right); the middle vertical resistor R carries I5 upwards from the lower node to the upper node; terminal b on the right." width="809" height="536" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

$$
I_2R - 4I_4R + I_5R = 0 \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
I_1 + I_5 = I_2 \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
I_3 = I_4 + I_5 \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
I_1R + I_2R = U_0 \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
I_1 = \frac{16}{34}\frac{U_0}{R} \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
I_3 = \frac{7}{34}\frac{U_0}{R} \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
\left(I_1 + I_3\right)R_{ab} = \frac{23}{34}\frac{U_0}{R} \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
R_{ab} = \frac{34}{23}R \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

**B2. (2 point)**

$$
I_5 = \frac{1}{17}\frac{U_0}{R} \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
U_5 = \frac{1}{17}U_0 \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

**B3. (2 points)**

$$
I_4 = \frac{5}{34}\frac{U_0}{R} \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
P_4 = 4I_4^2R = \frac{25U_0^2}{289R} \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

**C. (3 point)**

$$
P_{med} = \frac{L}{T} \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
L = P_1\Delta t_1 + P_2\Delta t_2 \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$

$$
P_{aver} \approx 0{,}09\frac{U_0^2}{R} \qquad \textbf{(1p)}
$$


</Spoiler>
