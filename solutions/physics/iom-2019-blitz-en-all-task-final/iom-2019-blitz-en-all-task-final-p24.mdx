## Условие

Andrew and Bob are given a number $n$. Andrew and Bob take turns, Andrew takes the first turn. On his turn a player chooses $d$ such that $d$ is a natural number ranging from 1 to $n-1$ and d is a divisor of $n$. Then $d$ is subtracted from $n$. The player who can’t make a move loses. For which values of n Andrew can win no matter how Bob plays? (Multiple choice)

1) 2;

2) 3;

3) 2018;

4) 2019;

5) 2020.
