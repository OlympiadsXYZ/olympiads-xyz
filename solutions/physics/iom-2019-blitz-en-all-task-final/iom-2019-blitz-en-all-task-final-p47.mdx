*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES, Moscow, September 2, 2019 · 2 септември 2019 г.*

## Условие

In hydrogen atom, the electronic energy levels depend on the principal quantum number $n$ as follows: $E_n \sim -1/n^2$. Which of the following transitions corresponds to the largest wavelength in the emission spectrum of hydrogen atom?

1) $n = 2 \to n = 1$

2) $n = 3 \to n = 2$

3) $n = 4 \to n = 2$

4) $n = 6 \to n = 3$
