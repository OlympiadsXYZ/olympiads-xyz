*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES, Moscow, September 2, 2019 · 2 септември 2019 г.*

## Условие

Assume $\lim\limits_{n\to\infty} a_n = A$, $b_n = |a_n - A|$. Which of the following statements are correct?

1) $\{b_n\}$ is a non-increasing sequence.

2) There exists a positive integer $N$ such that $\{b_N, b_{N+1}, \ldots\}$ is a non-increasing sequence.

3) There exists a positive integer $N$ such that $b_n = 0$ for any $n \ge N$.

4) There exists a sequence of positive integers $k_1 < k_2 < \ldots$ such that $\{b_{k_1}, b_{k_2}, \ldots\}$ is a non-increasing sequence.
