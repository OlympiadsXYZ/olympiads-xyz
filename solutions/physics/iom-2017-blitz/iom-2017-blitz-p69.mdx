*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

Turan and Samir exercised on the same route. Samir jogged the first half of the route at 6 km/h and ran the other half at 12 km/h, taking a total time of $x$. Turan walked the first third of the route at 5 km/h and ran the rest of the route at 15 km/h, taking a total time of $y$. Find $x : y$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 1.125

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 1.125
