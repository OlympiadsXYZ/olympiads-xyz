*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

Each edge of a regular square pyramid $SABCD$ (with apex $S$) is of length 5. Points $P$, $Q$, $R$ are taken on the edges $SA$, $AB$, $BC$ respectively such that $PA = AQ = RC = 2$. Find the distance from the vertex $D$ to the plane $PQR$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 3.5

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 3.5


</Spoiler>
