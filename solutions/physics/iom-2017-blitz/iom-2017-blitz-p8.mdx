*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

How many asymmetric carbon atoms (chiral carbons, chirality centers) are found in  
1,3-dichloro-4-methyl-2-pentanol?

Give the integer number.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 2

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 2


</Spoiler>
