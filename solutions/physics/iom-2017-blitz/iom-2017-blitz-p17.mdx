*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

At the extensions of the sides $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DA$ of a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ beyond the points $B$, $C$, $D$, $A$ respectively points $M$, $N$, $P$, $Q$ are marked such that $BM = AB$, $CN = 2BC$, $DP = CD$, $AQ = 2AD$. Find the area of the quadrilateral $MNPQ$ given that the area of $ABCD$ is 4.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 32

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 32


</Spoiler>
