*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

Which of the following compounds give alkaline solutions?

1. sodium sulfate
2. sodium sulfide
3. sodium carbonate
4. sodium hydrogen carbonate
5. sodium hydrogen sulfate

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 2; 3; 4

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 2; 3; 4


</Spoiler>
