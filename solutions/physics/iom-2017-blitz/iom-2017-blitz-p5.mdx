*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

The diagonals $AC$ and $CE$ of a regular hexagon $ABCDEF$ are divided by inner points $M$ and $N$ respectively, so that $\frac{AM}{AC} = \frac{CN}{CE} = r$ . What is the value of $21r^2$ if $B$, $M$, and $N$ are collinear?

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 7

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 7


</Spoiler>
