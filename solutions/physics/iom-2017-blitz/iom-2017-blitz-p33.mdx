*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

Choose the process(es) decreasing the system entropy.

1. Evaporation of an alcohol
2. Dissolution of hydrogen chloride in water
3. Condensation of water vapor
4. Iron melting
5. Ammonia synthesis from elements

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 2; 3; 5

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 2; 3; 5


</Spoiler>
