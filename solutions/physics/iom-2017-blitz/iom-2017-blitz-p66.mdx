*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

Determine which crystal has a higher index of refraction if angles of incidence and angles of refraction of a laser beam that goes through three crystals are:

1) $50^{\circ}$ and $30^{\circ}$,
2) $30^{\circ}$ and $18^{\circ}$,
3) $60^{\circ}$ and $38^{\circ}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 2

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 2


</Spoiler>
