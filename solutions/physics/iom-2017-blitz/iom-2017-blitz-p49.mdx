*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

Four solutions are given below:

1. 0.1 M acetic acid ($pK_a = 4.75$)
2. 0.1 M formic acid ($pK_a = 3.75$)
3. 1 M formic acid ($pK_a = 3.75$)
4. 0.00066 M ammonia ($pK_b = 4.75$)

Choose the solution with the highest degree of ionization and calculate its pH. In the answer, give only pH rounded to the integer value.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 10

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 10


</Spoiler>
