*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

A friction force of 1 N acts for 2 s on a 500 g hockey puck which initial speed is 10 m/s. Determine the distance traveled by the puck during this period.

The answer should be given in the SI units unless otherwise stated. If the numerical result turns out to be an integer, it should be given as the answer. Otherwise round the result to the first decimal.  
$g = 10\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 16 m

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 16


</Spoiler>
