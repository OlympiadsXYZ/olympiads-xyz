*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

For a triangle $ABC$ with incenter $I$, denote by $D$ the midpoint of the minor arc $BC$ on the circumcircle. Let $\angle BIC = 120^{\circ}$, $ID = 7/\sqrt{3}$ and $BC = AB + 2$. Find the value of $AI \cdot \sqrt{3}$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 6

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 6


</Spoiler>
