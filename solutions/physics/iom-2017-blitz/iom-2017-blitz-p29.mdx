*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

How many moles of propanoyl-chloride have reacted with an excess of ethanol if the product contains $1.2 * 10^{23}$ oxygen atoms?

1. 0.05 mol
2. 0.1 mol
3. 0.2 mol
4. 0.4 mol

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 2

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 2


</Spoiler>
