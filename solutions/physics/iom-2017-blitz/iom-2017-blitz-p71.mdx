*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

Which of the statements are true for the hereunder octahedral complexes?

1. There are two geometric isomers for $[Fe(en)_2Cl_2]^+$
($en = NH_2CH_2CH_2NH_2$ is a bidentate ligand)
2. The oxidation state if iron is $+2$ in $[Fe(en)_2Cl_2]^+$
3. $[Fe(en)_2Cl_2]^+$ is diamagnetic, because en is a strong field ligand
4. There are 6 isomers for $[Pt(CN)_2(H_2O)_2Br_2]$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 1; 4

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 1; 4


</Spoiler>
