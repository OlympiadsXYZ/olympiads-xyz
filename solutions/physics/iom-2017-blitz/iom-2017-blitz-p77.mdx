*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

An ionic compound is composed of three elements and contains 61.3 mass.% of fluorine. Determine the compound formula. Give the answer as its molar mass rounded to the nearest integer, without units.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 62

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 62


</Spoiler>
