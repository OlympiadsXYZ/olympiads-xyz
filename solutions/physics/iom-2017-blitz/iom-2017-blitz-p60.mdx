*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

It is given that 67 in base-x is equal to 51 in base-y, what are the smallest $x$ and $y$ for which the equality is verified? Give the answer as a single number concatenating $x$ and $y$ (e.g. if $x$=10 and $y$=8 write the answer as 108).

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 912

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 912


</Spoiler>
