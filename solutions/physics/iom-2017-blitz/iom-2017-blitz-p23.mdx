*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 4 · 4 септември 2017 г.*

## Условие

When two resistors are connected in parallel their total resistance is 6.25 times less than that of their connection in series. What is the ratio of the resistor values?

The answer should be given in the SI units unless otherwise stated. If the numerical result turns out to be an integer, it should be given as the answer. Otherwise round the result to the first decimal.  
$g = 10\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 4

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 4


</Spoiler>
