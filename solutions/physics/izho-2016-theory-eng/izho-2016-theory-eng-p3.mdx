## Условие

The electrical circuit contains the voltage source $U_0$ connected in series with the resistor $R = 1{,}00\ k\Omega$ and the nonlinear capacitor, whose capacitance depends on the voltage across it and is graphically shown in the figure below.

**1.** Assume that $U_0 = 5{,}0\ V$. Find the charge of the capacitor, which appears on its plates after a sufficiently long period of time. **[0,75 т.]**

Suppose that at the initial time moment the charge of the capacitor is zero, and the voltage source provides $U_0 = 10$ V. It is seen from the graph that the capacitance of the capacitor at this voltage goes to infinity, i.e. $C(10\ V) = \infty$.

**2.** How long does it take for a capacitor to be charged to the voltage $U_0 = 10$ V? **[0,25 т.]**

**3.** Find the time moment $t$, when the charge of the capacitor is equal to $q = 4{,}0\ \mu Cl$. **[3 т.]**

**4.** Find the time interval $\Delta t$ at which the charge of the capacitor grows from $q_0 = 4{,}0\ \mu Cl$ to $q = 8{,}0\ \mu Cl$. **[0,5 т.]**

**5.** Find the charge of the capacitor at the time moment $t_0 = 3{,}0\ ms$. **[0,5 т.]**

Let a source provide a constant voltage with a small portion of alternating voltage such that $U_0 = U + \delta U\sin\omega t = [5{,}000 + 0{,}100\sin\omega t\ ]$ V, where $\omega = 2500\ rad/s$. After a sufficiently long period of time electrical oscillations of voltage and current are set up in the circuit.

**6.** What is the phase difference $\varphi$ between the voltage oscillations across the capacitor and the resistor? **[0,5 т.]**

**7.** Find the dependence of the electric current in the circuit $I(t)$ as a function of time. **[4 т.]**

**8.** Find the voltage across the capacitor $U_C(t)$ as a function of time. **[0,5 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **1.** 0.5 μC
- **2.** $t = \infty$
- **3.** 2 ms
- **4.** 5.2 ms
- **5.** 5 μC
- **6.** $\varphi = -\frac{\pi}{2}$
- **7.** $I(t) = [44{,}7\sin(2500t + 1{,}1)]\ \mu A$
- **8.** $U_C(t) = [5{,}000 + 0{,}089\sin(2500t - 0{,}464)]\ V$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

1. **[0.75 points]** After a long period of time the electric current in the circuit turns zero and the capacitor will be fully charged, i.e.

$$
I = 0. \qquad (1)
$$

The voltage provided by the source is thus drop on the capacitor whose capacitance at $U_0 = 5$ V is obtained from the graph

$$
C = 0{,}10\ \mu F. \qquad (2)
$$

The charge of the capacitor is therefore found as

$$
q = CU_0 = 0.50\ \mu Cl. \qquad (3)
$$

2. **[0.25 points]** Since the electric current in the circuit is finite and charging the capacitor to the voltage drop of $U_0 = 10$ V requires an infinite charge, the corresponding time is obtained as.

$$
t = \infty. \qquad (4)
$$

3. **[3.0 points]** Let the capacitor be charged with the charge $q$ and its capacitance is equal to $C$, then, since all elements are connected in series, one gets

$$
U_0 = \frac{q}{C} + IR, \qquad (5)
$$

where the electric current is obtained as

$$
I = \frac{dq}{dt}. \qquad (6)
$$

Substituting (6) into (5) yields

$$
dt = \frac{R}{U_0 - \frac{q}{C(q)}}dq = f(q)dq, \qquad (7)
$$

where $f(q) = R/\left(U_0 - \frac{q}{C(q)}\right)$ is a function of the charge of the capacitor.

The function $f(q)$ is easily retrieved from the provided graph of $C = C(U)$ and turns out linear as shown in the figure below.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '52.2%', '--fig-max': '335px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/izho-2016-theory-eng/p3-sol-fig1.png" alt="Straight-line graph of f(q), ms/μCl (vertical axis, 0.0 to 2.0) against q, μCl (horizontal axis, 0 to 10): the line starts at about 0.1 at q = 0 and reaches 2.0 near q = 9.5." width="1046" height="776" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

The linear equation has the form

$$
f(q) = a + bq, \qquad (8)
$$

$$
a = 0.10\ \frac{ms}{\mu Cl}, \qquad (9)
$$

$$
b = 0.20\frac{ms}{(\mu Cl)^2}. \qquad (10)
$$

The time needed for a capacitor to be charge to $q = 4$ мкКл, is derived from equations (7) and (8) as

$$
t = aq + \frac{1}{2}bq^2 = 2.0\ ms \qquad (11)
$$

4. **[0.5 points]** The time interval $\Delta t$, needed for the capacitor to increase its charge from $q_0 = 4$ μCl till $q = 8$ μCl is found as

$$
t = (q - q_0)\left(a + \frac{1}{2}b(q + q_0)\right) = 5.2ms. \qquad (12)
$$

5. **[0.5 points]** Solving equation (11) one obtains that

$$
q_{1/2} = \frac{-a \pm \sqrt{a^2 + 2bt}}{b}. \qquad (13)
$$

It is obvious that at the initial time moment $q(0) = 0$, that is why the plus sign must be taken in formula (13) and one finally gets that

$$
q = \frac{\sqrt{a^2 + 2bt} - a}{b} = 5{,}0\ \mu Cl. \qquad (14)
$$

6. **[0.5 points]** For an ordinary capacitor its charge is proportional to the voltage drop across it, i.e.

$$
q = CU, \qquad (15)
$$

and the electric current in the circuit is derived as

$$
I = \frac{dq}{dt} = C\frac{dU}{dt} \sim \frac{dU}{dt}. \qquad (16)
$$

Since the capacitor and the resistor are connected in a series, then the electric current passing through them is the same, and, thus, the oscillation of voltage on the resistor is in phase with the oscillation of the current. Substituting $U \sim \sin\omega t$ gives rise to $I \sim \cos\omega t = \sin\left(\omega t - \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$, i.e. the phase difference between the oscillations of the voltage across the capacitor and the resistor is $\varphi = -\frac{\pi}{2}$.

The circuit contains the nonlinear capacitor but the proportionality in equation (16) stays the same, since the oscillation of the voltage is small compared with the constant voltage provided by the source, i.e.

$$
\varphi = -\frac{\pi}{2}. \qquad (17)
$$

7. **[4.0 points]** The voltage drop has constant and alternating components. After a long period of time the constant component of the voltage will be dropped across the capacitor only, i.e.

$$
U_C = 5{,}000\ V, \qquad (18)
$$

and the constant component of the voltage drop across the resistor will be equal to zero, i.e.

$$
U_R = 0. \qquad (19)
$$

In our case the capacitance is voltage dependent, that is why equation (16) is rewritten in the form

$$
I = \frac{dq}{dt} = C(U)\frac{dU}{dt} + U\frac{dC(U)}{dU}\frac{dU}{dt} = C_{\mathrm{eff}}\frac{dU}{dt}, \qquad (20)
$$

where the effective capacitance is found as

$$
C_{\mathrm{eff}} = C(U) + U\frac{dC(U)}{dU} = 0.200\ \mu F. \qquad (21)
$$

It is well known that the reactive resistance of the capacitor is equal to

$$
X_{\mathrm{C}} = \frac{1}{\omega C_{\mathrm{eff}}}. \qquad (22)
$$

To calculate the electric current for the resistor and the capacitor connected in a series let us use the vector diagram shown in the figure below.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '44.2%', '--fig-max': '283px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/izho-2016-theory-eng/p3-sol-fig2.png" alt="Vector diagram: U_R along the current direction I (horizontal arrow), U_C pointing vertically down, and the resultant δU along the diagonal of the rectangle; the angle α is between U_R and δU." width="884" height="566" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

From this diagram the amplitude of the electric current is easily found as

$$
I = \frac{\delta U}{\sqrt{R^2 + \frac{1}{\omega^2C_{\mathrm{eff}}^2}}} = 44.7\ \mu A. \qquad (23)
$$

and the corresponding phase shift $\alpha$ between the current and the voltage is obtained as

$$
\alpha = \mathrm{arctg}\left(\frac{U_C}{U_R}\right) = \mathrm{arctg}\left(\frac{1}{\omega C_{\mathrm{eff}}R}\right) = 1{,}11\ rad = 63{,}4^{\circ}. \qquad (24)
$$

Finally, the dependence of the electric current on the time is derived as

$$
I(t) = [44{,}7\sin(2500t + 1{,}1)]\ \mu A. \qquad (25)
$$

8. **[0.5 points]** The amplitude of the voltage oscillation on the capacitor is obtained from the vector diagram as

$$
U_C = \delta U\sin\alpha. \qquad (26)
$$

Finally, taking into account the constant component of the voltage drop on the capacitor one gets

$$
U_{\mathrm{C}}(t) = U_{\mathrm{C}} + \delta U\sin\alpha\sin\left(\omega t - \frac{\pi}{2} + \alpha\right) = [5{,}000 + 0{,}089\sin(2500t - 0{,}464)]\ V. \qquad (27)
$$
