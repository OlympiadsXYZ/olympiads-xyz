## Условие

A fireworks rocket explodes in the night sky at a height $h$ in an isotrope manner.

**(a)** Show that the consisting parts of the rocket form a sphere in every instant of their falling.

**(b)** Calculate the radius of the sphere created by these particles when they are all on the ground.
