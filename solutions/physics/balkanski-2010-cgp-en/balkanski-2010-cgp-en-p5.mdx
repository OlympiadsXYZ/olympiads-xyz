## Условие

A vertical ladder falls down without initial speed. The contact with the ground is frictionless.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '59.3%', '--fig-max': '380px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/balkanski-2010-cgp-en/p5-fig1.png" alt="A ladder drawn as a thick blue line: vertically on the horizontal ground, labelled “t=0”, and inclined in a position labelled “intermediate position”, its lower end sliding on the ground to the right; the angle θ between the inclined ladder and the ground is marked." width="1187" height="793" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

**(a)** Find the speed $V_G$ (of the center of masses) as a function of $\Omega$ (the angular speed) and $\theta$. Calculate the speed $V_G$ when the ladder touches the ground entirely.

**(b)** Write down the equation of movement of the center of masses in the very first moment of the falling. We will note $\alpha = \pi/2 - \theta$. Show that the angle $\alpha$ is increasing under an exponential law with a characteristic time $\tau = \sqrt{\frac{L}{6g}}$.
