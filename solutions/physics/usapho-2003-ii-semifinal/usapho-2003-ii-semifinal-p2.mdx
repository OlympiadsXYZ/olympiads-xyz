All of the numbered boxes represent resistors. Each one has resistance $R$.

**a.** What is the equivalent resistance between points A and C? **[10 т.]**

**b.** What is the equivalent resistance between points A and B? **[15 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a.** $R_{eq} = \frac{2}{3}R$
- **b.** $R_{eq} = \frac{8}{15}R$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

a. By symmetry the current through resistor 1 and 3 is the same. No current branches off through resistor 2. Similarly no current flows through resistor 7. The circuit reduces to that shown to the right. Each branch has two resistors in series for a total series resistance of
$$R_t = 2R.$$
The three branches are in parallel, so the equivalent resistance is
$$\frac{1}{R_{eq}} = \frac{1}{2R} + \frac{1}{2R} + \frac{1}{2R} = \frac{3}{2R}.$$
Therefore
$$R_{eq} = \frac{2}{3}R$$

b. By symmetry the current through resistor 2 and 4 is the same. The current through resistor 5 is the same as that through resistor 7. The circuit reduces to that shown below. The series combination 5 and 7 has resistance
$$R_{57} = 2R.$$
This combination is in parallel with 8.
$$\frac{1}{R_{857}} = \frac{1}{R} + \frac{1}{2R} = \frac{3}{2R}.$$
This combination is in series with 3 and 6.
$$R_{368573} = R + R + \tfrac{2}{3}R = \tfrac{8}{3}R$$
This combination is in parallel with the two other branches.
$$\frac{1}{R_{eq}} = \frac{1}{R} + \frac{1}{2R} + \frac{3}{8R} = \frac{15}{8R}$$
$$R_{eq} = \frac{8}{15}R$$
