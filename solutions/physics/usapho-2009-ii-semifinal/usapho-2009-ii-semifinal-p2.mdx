## Условие

A mixture of $^{32}$P and $^{35}$S (two beta emitters widely used in biochemical research) is placed next to a detector and allowed to decay, resulting in the data below. The detector has equal sensitivity to the beta particles emitted by each isotope, and both isotopes decay into stable daughters.

You should analyze the data graphically. Error estimates are not required.

| Day | Activity | Day | Activity | Day | Activity |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 64557 | 40 | 12441 | 200 | 1121 |
| 5 | 51714 | 60 | 6385 | 250 | 673 |
| 10 | 41444 | 80 | 3855 | 300 | 467 |
| 20 | 27020 | 100 | 2734 | | |
| 30 | 18003 | 150 | 1626 | | |

**a.** Determine the half-life of each isotope. $^{35}$S has a significantly longer half-life than $^{32}$P.

**b.** Determine the ratio of the number of $^{32}$P atoms to the number of $^{35}$S atoms in the original sample.
