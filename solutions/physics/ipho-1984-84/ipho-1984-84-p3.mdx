*Problems of the XV International Physics Olympiad (Sigtuna, 1984)*

## Условие

An electronic frequency filter consists of four components coupled as in the upper figure. The impedance of the source can be neglected and the impedance of the load can be taken as infinite. The filter should be such that the voltage ratio $U_{out}/U_{in}$ has a frequency dependence shown in the lower where $U_{in}$ is the input voltage and $U_{out}$ is the output voltage. At frequency $f_0$ the phase lag between the two voltages is zero.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '33.5%', '--fig-max': '214px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ipho-1984-84/p3-fig1.png" alt="Circuit: input terminals U_in on the left, output terminals U_out on the right; in the upper line two impedance boxes in series, bridged by a third box above them; from the node between the two series boxes a fourth box goes down to the lower common line." width="670" height="373" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

In order to build the filter you can choose from the following components:

2 resistors, 10 kΩ  
2 capacitors, 10 nF  
2 solenoids, 160 mH (iron-free and with negligible resistance)

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '27.7%', '--fig-max': '177px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ipho-1984-84/p3-fig2.png" alt="Graph with vertical axis labelled U_in / U_out, a value 1 marked on it, and horizontal axis f: the curve starts near 1, dips to a minimum at the frequency f_0 (dashed vertical line) and rises again." width="554" height="422" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

Construct, by combining four of these components, a filter that fulfils the stated conditions. Determine the frequency $f_0$ and the ratio $U_{out}/U_{in}$ at this frequency for as many component combinations as possible.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- f<sub>0</sub> = 1/(2πRC) with U<sub>out</sub>/U<sub>in</sub> = 2/3 (also with resistors and capacitors switched); f<sub>0</sub> = R/(2πL) with solenoids instead of capacitors

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The conditions at very high and very low frequencies can be satisfied with for example the following circuit

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '32.3%', '--fig-max': '207px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ipho-1984-84/p3-sol-fig1.png" alt="Twin circuit: two capacitors C in series in the upper line, bridged above by a resistor R; from the node between the capacitors a resistor R goes down to the lower line." width="646" height="380" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

Using either the graphic vector method or the analytic jω method we can show that the minimum occurs for a frequency $f_0 = \frac{1}{2\pi RC}$ when the ratio between the output and input voltages is 2/3. Switching the resistors and the capacitors gives a new circuit with the same frequency $f_0$. Another two possibilities is to exchange the capacitors for solenoids where we get $f_0 = \frac{R}{2\pi L}$.  
There are further eight solutions with unsymmetric patterns of the electronic components.


</Spoiler>
