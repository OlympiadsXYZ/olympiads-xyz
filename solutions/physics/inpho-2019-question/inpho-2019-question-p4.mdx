## Условие

Consider a long narrow cylinder of cross section $A$ filled with a compressible liquid up to height $h$ whose density $\rho$ is a function of the pressure $P(z)$ as $\rho(z)=\frac{\rho_0}{2}\left(1+\frac{P(z)}{P_0}\right)$ where $P_0$ and $\rho_0$ are constants. The depth $z$ is measured from the free surface of the liquid where the pressure is equal to the atmospheric pressure ($P_{\mathrm{atm}}$).

**a)** Obtain the pressure ($P(z)$) as a function of $z$. Obtain the mass ($M$) of liquid in the tube.

**Summary answer boxes:** $P(z)=$ [blank]; $M=$ [blank]. **[5 т.]**

**b)** Let $P_i(z)$ be the pressure at $z$, if the liquid were incompressible with density $\rho_0/2$. Assuming that $P_0\gg\rho_0gz$ obtain an approximated expression for $\Delta P=P(z)-P_i(z)$.

**Summary answer box:** $\Delta P\approx$ [blank].

Detailed answers can be found on page numbers: [blank]. **[2 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

### (a)

$$
P(z)=\int\rho(z)g\,dz+P_{\mathrm{atm}}\qquad (4.1)
$$

$$
=\int_0^z\frac{\rho_0}2\left(1+\frac{P(z)}{P_0}\right)g\,dz+P_{\mathrm{atm}}\qquad (4.2)
$$

$$
\frac{dP(z)}{dz}=\frac{\rho_0}2\left(1+\frac{P(z)}{P_0}\right)g\qquad (4.3)
$$

$$
\int_{P_{\mathrm{atm}}}^{P(z)}\frac{dP(z)}{P_0+P(z)}=\int_0^z\frac{\rho_0g\,dz}{2P_0}\qquad (4.4)
$$

$$
\log\frac{P_0+P(z)}{P_0+P_{\mathrm{atm}}}=\frac{\rho_0gz}{2P_0}\qquad (4.5)
$$

$$
P(z)=\left[(P_0+P_{\mathrm{atm}})e^{\rho_0gz/2P_0}-P_0\right]\qquad (4.6)
$$

$$
\rho(z)=\frac{\rho_0(P_0+P_{\mathrm{atm}})}{2P_0}e^{\rho_0gz/2P_0}\qquad (4.7)
$$

$$
M=\int_0^h\rho(z)A\,dz\qquad (4.8)
$$

$$
=\frac Ag(P_0+P_{\mathrm{atm}})(e^{\rho_0gh/2P_0}-1)\qquad (4.9)
$$

### (b)

$$
P_i(z)=P_{\mathrm{atm}}+\frac{\rho_0}2gz\qquad (4.10)
$$

$$
P(z)=(P_0+P_{\mathrm{atm}})e^{\rho_0gz/2P_0}-P_0\qquad (4.11)
$$

$$
\approx P_0\left(1+\frac{P_{\mathrm{atm}}}{P_0}\right)\left[1+\frac{\rho_0gz}{2P_0}+\left(\frac{\rho_0gz}{2P_0}\right)^2\frac12\right]-P_0\qquad (4.12)
$$

$$
\Delta P=P(z)-P_i(z)\qquad (4.13)
$$

$$
=\frac{(\rho_0gz)^2}{8P_0}+\frac{P_{\mathrm{atm}}}{P_0}\frac{\rho_0gz}2+\frac{P_{\mathrm{atm}}}2\left(\frac{\rho_0gz}{2P_0}\right)^2\qquad (4.14)
$$

which is correct upto second order in $\rho_0gz/2P_0$.

$$
\Delta P\approx\frac{P_{\mathrm{atm}}}{P_0}\frac{\rho_0gz}2\qquad (4.15)
$$

which is correct upto first order in $\rho_0gz/2P_0$.

**Both Eqs. (4.14) and (4.15) (mentioning order of approximation) gain full credit.**
