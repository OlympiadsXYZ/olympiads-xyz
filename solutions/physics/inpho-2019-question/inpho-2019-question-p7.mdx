## Условие

As shown, a magnet of mass $m=19.00\ \mathrm g$ slides on a rough non-magnetic metallic inclined plane which makes an angle $\theta$ with the horizontal. One can change the angle of inclination of the plane. Due to relative motion between the magnet and the plane, eddy currents are generated inside the metal which retard motion of the magnet.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

### (a)

$$
V_T=\frac{mg(\sin\theta-\mu\cos\theta)}b\qquad (7.1)
$$

$$
S(t)=V_T\left[t-\frac mb\left(1-e^{-tb/m}\right)\right]\qquad (7.2)
$$

### (b)

$V_T=$ slope of the graph $=0.315\ \mathrm{m/s}$.

Accepted range $0.301\ \mathrm{m/s}\le V_T\le0.329\ \mathrm{m/s}$.

### (c)

$$
\frac{V_T}{\cos\theta}=\frac{mg}b\tan\theta-\frac{\mu mg}b\qquad (7.3)
$$

Slope of the graph $=0.91\ \mathrm{m/s}=\frac{mg}b\Rightarrow b=0.21\ \mathrm{N\cdot s/m}$.

Intercept $=0.16=\frac{\mu mg}b\Rightarrow\mu=0.18$.

Accepted range of slope $=0.86$–$0.96\ \mathrm{m/s}$.

Accepted range of intercept $=0.12$–$0.19\ \mathrm{m/s}$.

$$
0.19\le b\le0.22\ \mathrm{m/s}\quad\text{and}\quad0.14\le\mu\le0.20
$$
