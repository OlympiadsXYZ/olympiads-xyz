## Условие

Consider $n$ moles of a monoatomic non-ideal (realistic) gas. Its equation of state may be described by the van der Waal’s equation

$$
\left(P+\frac{an^2}{V^2}\right)\left(\frac Vn-b\right)=RT
$$

where $a$ and $b$ are positive constants and other symbols have their usual meanings. The internal energy change of a realistic gas can be given by

$$
dU=C_VdT+\left\{T\left(\frac{dP}{dT}\right)_V-P\right\}dV
$$

As indicated in the above expression, the derivative of pressure is taken at constant volume. We take one mole of the gas ($n=1$) through a Diesel cycle (ABCDA) as shown in the following $P$-$V$ diagram (diagram is not to scale). During the whole cycle assume that the molar heat capacity at constant volume ($C_V$) remains constant at $3R/2$. Path AB and CD are reversible adiabats.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

### (a)

$$
dU=C_VdT+\left\{\frac{RT}{V-b}-P\right\}dV
$$

AB and CD are reversible adiabats, hence entropy change during these processes are zero.

$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{AB}}=\Delta S_{\mathrm{CD}}=\int_A^B\frac{dQ}T=\int_C^D\frac{dQ}T=0\qquad (6.1)
$$

$$
\int_A^B\frac{dQ}T=\int\frac{C_V}T\,dT+\int\frac R{V-b}\,dV=0\qquad (6.2)
$$

$$
C_V\ln\frac{T_B}{T_A}=-R\ln\left(\frac{V_B-b}{V_A-b}\right)\qquad (6.3)
$$

$$
T_B=T_A\left(\frac{V_B-b}{V_A-b}\right)^{-R/C_V}=T_A\left(\frac{V_B-b}{V_A-b}\right)^{-2/3}\qquad (6.4)
$$

### (b)

Highest temperature during cycle is at $T_C$.

$$
T_B=407.5\ \mathrm K\qquad (6.5)
$$

which gives, from van der Waal equation

$$
P_B=33.18\ \mathrm{atm}=P_C\qquad (6.6)
$$

$$
\Rightarrow T_C=803.76\ \mathrm K\approx804\ \mathrm K\qquad (6.7)
$$

Different value of $T_C$ (within a range) obtained due to reasonable roundoff in previous step(s) will be credited.

### (c)

$$
dQ_{\mathrm{in}}=dQ_{\mathrm{BC}}=dU+P_BdV\qquad (6.8)
$$

$$
=C_VdT+\frac{RT}{V-b}\,dV\qquad (6.9)
$$

$$
=C_VdT+\left(P+\frac a{V^2}\right)dV\qquad (6.10)
$$

$$
Q_{\mathrm{in}}=C_V(T_C-T_B)+P_B(V_C-V_B)-\left.\frac aV\right|_{V_B}^{V_C}\qquad (6.11)
$$

$$
Q_{\mathrm{out}}=Q_{\mathrm{DA}}=C_V(T_A-T_D)\qquad (6.12)
$$

$$
\eta=1-\frac{Q_{\mathrm{out}}}{Q_{\mathrm{in}}}=67.7\%\approx68\%\qquad (6.13)
$$

Different value of $\eta$ (within a range) obtained due to reasonable roundoff in previous step(s) will be credited.

### (d)

$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{BC}}=\int\frac{dQ}{dT}=\int\frac{C_VdT}T+\int\frac R{V-b}\,dV\qquad (6.14)
$$

$$
=C_V\ln\frac{T_C}{T_B}+R\ln\frac{V_C-b}{V_B-b}\qquad (6.15)
$$

$$
=1.73R=\Delta S_{\mathrm{DA}}\qquad (6.16)
$$
