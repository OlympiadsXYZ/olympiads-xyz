Two triangular prisms, with angles $30^{\circ}$ and $45^{\circ}$ are disposed as in the figure above. A ray of light, that is parallel to the prisms basis, is entering the system, and is leaving in the same direction. What is the refractive index $n_2$ of the $45^{\circ}$ prism if the refractive index of the $30^{\circ}$ prism is equal to $n_1 = 2.1$ ? We recall the trigonometric identity :

$$
\sin(\alpha - \beta) = \sin(\alpha)\cos(\beta) - \sin(\beta)\cos(\alpha) \qquad (5)
$$
