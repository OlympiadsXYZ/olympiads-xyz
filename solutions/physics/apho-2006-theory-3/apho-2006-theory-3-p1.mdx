*7th Asian Physics Olympiad — Theoretical Question 3 · 2,5 т.*

## Условие

This problem consists of four not related parts.

**A. [2.5 points]** The Mariana Abyss in the Pacific Ocean has a depth of $H = 10920\ m$. Density of salted water at the surface of the ocean is $\rho_0 = 1025\ kg/m^3$, bulk modulus is $K = 2{,}1\cdot 10^9\ Pa$, acceleration of gravity is $g=9.81 m/s^2$. Neglect the change in the temperature and in the acceleration of gravity with the depth, and also neglect the atmospheric pressure.

**A1)** Find the relation between the density $\rho(x)$ and pressure $P(x)$ at the depth of $x$.

**A2)** Find the numerical value of the pressure $P(H)$ at the bottom of the Mariana Abyss. You may use iterative methods to solve this part.

***Note*:** The fluids have very small compressibility. Compressibility coefficient is defined as

$$
\kappa = -\frac{1}{V}\left(\frac{dV}{dP}\right)_{T=const}
$$

Bulk modulus $K$ is the inverse of $\kappa$: $K=1/\kappa$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **A1)** $\rho(x) = \rho_0\left(1 + \frac{P(x)}{K}\right)$
- **A2)** $1.13 \times 10^{8}$ Pa

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**A**

The change of the pressure is related to the change in the density via

$$
\Delta P = -K\frac{\Delta V}{V} = K\frac{\Delta\rho}{\rho_0}, \qquad (1)
$$

where $\rho_0$ is the density of water at the surface.

$$
\rho = \rho_0 + \Delta\rho = \rho_0\left(1+\frac{\Delta\rho}{\rho_0}\right) = \rho_0\left(1+\frac{\Delta P}{K}\right), \qquad (2)
$$

where $\Delta P \approx P$ (we neglect the atmospheric pressure). Then

(A1)
$$
\rho(x) = \rho_0\left(1+\frac{P(x)}{K}\right). \qquad (3)
$$

The change of the hydrostatic pressure with the depth equals

$$
dP = g\cdot\rho(x)dx, \quad \frac{dP}{dx} = g\rho(x) = g\rho_0 + g\rho_0\frac{P(x)}{K}, \qquad (4)
$$

$$
\frac{dP(x)}{dx} - \frac{g\rho_0}{K}P(x) = g\rho_0. \qquad (5)
$$

The solution of this differential equation with boundary condition $P(0) = 0$ is

$$
P(x) = K\left(\exp\frac{g\rho_0}{K}x - 1\right). \qquad (6)
$$

Since $\frac{g\rho_0}{K}H \ll 1$, we can use the expansion

$$
\exp z \approx 1 + z + \frac{z^2}{2!} + \ldots, \qquad (7)
$$

thus

$$
P(x) \cong g\rho_0 x + \frac{1}{2K}\left(g\rho_0 x\right)^2 \qquad (8)
$$

The last formula can be simply derived using the method of successive iterations. First, the pressure can be estimated without compressibility taken into account:

$$
P_0(x) = g\rho_0 x. \qquad (9)
$$

Correction to the density in the first approximation can be obtained using $P_0(x)$:

$$
\rho_1(x) = \rho_o\left(1 + \frac{g\rho_0 x}{K}\right). \qquad (10)
$$

Now, correction to pressure can be obtained using $\rho_1(x)$:

$$
P_1(H) = \int_0^H \rho_1(x)g\,dx = g\rho_0 x + \frac{1}{2K}\left(g\rho_0 x\right)^2, \qquad (11)
$$

as obtained earlier.

Putting in the numerical values, we get

(A2)
$$
P(H) = (1098\cdot 10^5 + 28{,}7\cdot 10^5)\,\text{Па} \approx 1{,}13\cdot 10^8\ \text{Па}. \qquad (12)
$$
