*7th Asian Physics Olympiad — Theoretical Question 3 · 2,5 т.*

## Условие

**B. [2.5 points]** Light mobile piston separates the vessel into two parts. The vessel is isolated from the environment. One part of the vessel contains $m_1 = 3g$ of hydrogen at the temperature of $T_{10} = 300\ \mathrm{K}$, and the other part contains $m_2 = 16\ g$ of oxygen at the temperature of $T_{20} = 400\ \mathrm{K}$. Molar masses of hydrogen and oxygen are $\mu_1 = 2\ g/mole$ and $\mu_2 = 32\ g/mole$ respectively, and $R = 8.31 J/(K\cdot mole)$. The piston weakly conducts heat between oxygen and hydrogen, and eventually the temperature in the system equilibrates. All the processes are quasi stationary.

**B1)** What is the final temperature of the system $T$?

**B2)** What is the ratio between final pressure $P_f$ and initial pressure $P_i$?

**B3)** What is the total amount of heat $Q$, transferred from oxygen to hydrogen?

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **B1)** 325 K
- **B2)** 1
- **B3)** 1091 J

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**B**

The total work done by the gases is zero. Thus at any moment the total internal energy equals the original value:

$$
\frac{m_1}{\mu_1}C_V T_1 + \frac{m_2}{\mu_2}C_V T_2 = \frac{m_1}{\mu_1}C_V T_{10} + \frac{m_2}{\mu_2}C_V T_{20}, \qquad (13)
$$

where $\mu_1 = 2\ g/mole$ and $\mu_2 = 32\ g/mole$ are molar masses of hydrogen and oxygen, and $C_V=5R/2$ is the molar heat capacity of diatomic gas. The final temperature of the system is

(B1)
$$
T = \frac{\frac{m_1}{\mu_1}T_{10} + \frac{m_2}{\mu_2}T_{20}}{\frac{m_1}{\mu_1} + \frac{m_2}{\mu_2}} = 325\ K. \qquad (14)
$$

The temperature of oxygen decreases, and the amount of heat Q is transferred to hydrogen by heat conduction. The piston will move in the direction of the oxygen, thus the hydrogen does a positive work $A>0$, and the change of the internal energy of oxygen is $\Delta U = A - Q$. On the other hand,

$$
\Delta U = \frac{m_2}{\mu_2}\frac{5}{2}R(T - T_{20}) = -779\ J. \qquad (15)
$$

To find $A$, let us prove that the pressure $P$ doesn’t change. Differentiating the equations of the state for each gas, we get

$$
\Delta T_1 = \frac{\mu_1}{m_1 R}\left(P\Delta V + V_1\Delta P\right), \quad \Delta T_2 = \frac{\mu_2}{m_2 R}\left(-P\Delta V + V_2\Delta P\right). \qquad (16)
$$

where $V_i$ are the gas volumes, and $\Delta V = \Delta V_1 = -\Delta V_2$ is the change of the volume of the hydrogen. Differentiating (1), we get

$$
\frac{m_1}{\mu_1}\Delta T_1 + \frac{m_2}{\mu_2}\Delta T_2 = 0. \qquad (17)
$$

Substituting (2) into (3), we obtain $(V_1+V_2)\cdot\Delta P = 0$, thus

(B2)
$$
P_f/P_i = 1. \qquad (18)
$$

Then the work done by the hydrogen is

$$
A = P\cdot\Delta V = -\frac{m_2}{\mu_2}R\cdot\Delta T_2 = \frac{m_2}{\mu_2}R(T_{20} - T) = 312\ J. \qquad (19)
$$

The total amount of heat transferred to hydrogen is

(B3)
$$
Q = A - \Delta U = 1091 J. \qquad (20)
$$
