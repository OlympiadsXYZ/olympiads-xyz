## Условие

The circuit diagrammed at the right consists of a 6.0-V power supply and eight 12.0-$\Omega$ resistors, labeled 1–8.

**a.** What is the equivalent resistance of the circuit? **[5 т.]**

**b.** What is the current in each resistor? Please use $I_j$ to denote the current in resistor $j$. **[5 т.]**

**c.** Resistors 1, 5, and 7 are replaced with 4.0-$\mu$F capacitors. What is the charge on each capacitor after the circuit has been connected for a very long time? Please use $Q_j$ to denote the charge on the capacitor in location $j$. **[5 т.]**

**d.** The capacitor in location 5 is replaced with a second 6.0-V power supply (positive end closest to resistor 3 and negative end closest to resistor 6). What is the current through each resistor after the circuit has been connected for a very long time? Please use $I_j$ to denote the current in resistor $j$. In this part 2, 3, 4, 6, and 8 denote 12.0-$\Omega$ resistors, 1 and 7 denote 4.0-$\mu$F capacitors, and 5 denotes a 6.0-V power supply. **[5 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-a">

### a. (5 т.)

Because of symmetry, $I_1=I_2$ and $I_4=I_5$ and $I_7=I_8$.

No current flows through 3 or 6. $I_3=0$ and $I_6=0$.

1, 4, and 7 are effectively in series with an equivalent resistance of $R_s=R+R+R=3R$.

Similarly for the right branch containing 2, 5, and 8.

The left and right branches are in parallel so, the equivalent resistance is given by

$$
\frac1{R_{eq}}=\frac1{3R}+\frac1{3R}=\frac2{3R}.
$$

$$
R_{eq}=\frac{3R}{2}=\frac{3(12\,\Omega)}2=18\,\Omega.
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-b">

### b. (5 т.)

As stated in the argument in Part a. $I_3=0$ and $I_6=0$. All the other currents are equal $I_1=I_2=I_4=I_5=I_7=I_8=I$.

Applying Kirchhoff’s loop rule to the left loop

$$
0=6.0\text{ V}-I(3R),\qquad I=\frac{6.0\text{ V}}{3R}=\frac{6.0\text{ V}}{36\,\Omega}=\frac16\text{ A}.
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-c">

### c. (5 т.)

When the circuit has been connected for a very long time, the capacitors are fully charged. No current flows through 1, 5, and 7. There is only one path for the current, through 2, 3, 4, 6, and 8. These five resistors are effectively in series with $V_R=(1/5)6.0\text{ V}=1.2\text{ V}$ across each. Applying Kirchhoff’s loop rule to the upper loop

$$
0=V_R+V_R-V_{C1},\qquad Q_1=CV_{C1}=C(2V_R)=(4.0\,\mu\text{F})(2.4\text{ V})=9.6\,\mu\text{C}.
$$

Similarly, $Q_7=9.6\,\mu\text{C}$. For the middle loop,

$$
0=V_R+V_R+V_R-V_{C5},\qquad Q_5=C(3V_R)=(4.0\,\mu\text{F})(3.6.0\text{ V})=14.4\,\mu\text{C}.
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-d">

### d. (5 т.)

When the circuit has been connected for a very long time, no current flows through 1 or 7. The same current flows through 3, 4 and 6.

$$
0=6.0\text{ V}-3RI,\qquad I_3=I_4=I_6=\frac{6.0\text{ V}}{36\,\Omega}=\frac16\text{ A}.
$$

There is 2.0 V across each of the resistors 3, 4, and 6 and capacitors 1 and 7. Hence the voltage across 2 and 8 is zero and $I_2=I_8=0$.

</ProblemSection>


</Spoiler>
