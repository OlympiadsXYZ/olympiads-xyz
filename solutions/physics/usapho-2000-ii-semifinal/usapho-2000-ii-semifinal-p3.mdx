## Условие

A relativistic particle decays into two photons. One of the photons is traveling to the right along the x-axis with frequency $\nu_1$. The second photon is traveling to the left with frequency $\nu_2$, where $\nu_1>\nu_2$. Express your answers to the following in terms of given quantities and known constants.

**a.** Find the velocity $V$ of the original particle. **[5 т.]**

**b.** Find the rest mass of the original particle. **[5 т.]**

**c.** What are the frequencies of the photons in the rest frame of the original particle? What are their directions of travel in this rest frame? **[5 т.]**

**d.** You are given the following formula:

$$
p'_x=F_1p_x+F_2\frac{E_\gamma}{c}
$$

where $p'_x$ is the observed x-component of the momentum of either photon, whereas $E_\gamma$ and $p_x$ are the energy and x-component of the momentum of the photon in the rest frame of the particle. Using your results determine the functions $F_1$ and $F_2$ in terms of $V/c$, where $V$ is the speed of the original particle. **[5 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-a">

### a. (5 т.)

The relativistic energy and momentum of a particle are given by

$$
E=\frac{mc^2}{\sqrt{1-(V/c)^2}},\qquad p=\frac{mV}{\sqrt{1-(V/c)^2}}
$$

where $m$ is the particle mass and $V$ is the particle speed. The energy and momentum of a photon of frequency $\nu$ are given by

$$
E_\gamma=h\nu,\qquad p_\gamma=\frac{h\nu}{c}.
$$

Applying energy conservation, we have

$$
\frac{mc^2}{\sqrt{1-(V/c)^2}}=h\nu_1+h\nu_2\qquad (A3.1)
$$

while momentum conservation gives

$$
\frac{mV}{\sqrt{1-(V/c)^2}}=\frac{h\nu_1}{c}-\frac{h\nu_2}{c}.\qquad (A3.2)
$$

Dividing (A3.2) by (A3.1), we have

$$
\frac{mV}{mc^2}=\frac{h(\nu_1-\nu_2)}{ch(\nu_1+\nu_2)}.
$$

Canceling common factors and solving for $V$

$$
V=c\frac{\nu_1-\nu_2}{\nu_1+\nu_2}\quad\text{to the right}.\qquad (A3.3)
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-b">

### b. (5 т.)

Solving (A3.1) for $m$

$$
m=\frac h{c^2}(\nu_1+\nu_2)\sqrt{1-(V/c)^2}.
$$

and substituting in (A3.3)

$$
m=\frac h{c^2}(\nu_1+\nu_2)\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{\nu_1-\nu_2}{\nu_1+\nu_2}\right)^2}=\frac h{c^2}\sqrt{(\nu_1+\nu_2)^2-(\nu_1-\nu_2)^2}.
$$

$$
m=\frac{2h}{c^2}\sqrt{\nu_1\nu_2}.\qquad (A3.4)
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-c">

### c. (5 т.)

In the rest frame of the particle the two photons must have equal and opposite momenta. 1 travels to the right with frequency $\nu$ and 2 travels to the left with frequency $\nu$. In its rest frame the particle’s energy is $mc^2$. Applying energy conservation we have

$$
mc^2=h\nu+h\nu=2h\nu.
$$

Combining this with (A3.4)

$$
mc^2=2h\sqrt{\nu_1\nu_2}=2h\nu.
$$

Or

$$
\nu=\sqrt{\nu_1\nu_2}=\frac{mc^2}{2h}
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-d">

### d. (5 т.)

Writing the given equation for photon 1

$$
\frac{h\nu_1}{c}=F_1\frac{h\nu}{c}+F_2\frac{h\nu}{c}=(F_1+F_2)\frac{h\nu}{c}
$$

Or

$$
(F_1+F_2)=\frac{2h\nu_1}{mc^2}.\qquad (A3.5)
$$

While for photon 2

$$
-\frac{h\nu_2}{c}=-F_1\frac{h\sqrt{\nu_1\nu_2}}{c}+F_2\frac{h\sqrt{\nu_1\nu_2}}{c}
$$

or

$$
(F_1-F_2)=\frac{2h\nu_2}{mc^2}\qquad (A3.6)
$$

Adding (A3.5) and (A3.6)

$$
2F_1=\frac{2h}{mc^2}(\nu_1+\nu_2)
$$

and comparing to (A3.1)

$$
F_1=\frac1{\sqrt{1-(V/c)^2}}.
$$

Subtracting (A3.6) from (A3.5) we have

$$
2F_2=\frac{2h}{mc^2}(\nu_1-\nu_2)
$$

and comparing to (A3.2)

$$
F_2=\frac{(V/c)}{\sqrt{1-(V/c)^2}}
$$

</ProblemSection>
