## Условие

An empty 4.2-g balloon is filled with air to a pressure of 2.0 atm at the ambient temperature of $20.0^\circ$ C. When filled it is spherical with a radius of 15.0 cm. The mean molecular mass of air is 28.8 g/mole and the atmospheric pressure is $1.01\times10^5$ Pa.

**a.** The filled balloon is weighed by placing it on a scale. What is the scale reading? **[15 т.]**

**b.** The filled balloon is now gently heated until its radius increases by 10%. What is the scale reading now? **[5 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Let $R=0.15\text{ m}=$ the radius of the balloon

$p=2\times1.01\times10^5\text{ Pa}=$ The pressure inside the balloon

$m_b=0.0042\text{ kg}=$ The mass of the empty balloon

$T=(20+273)\text{ K}=$ The temperature of the air

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-a">

### a. (15 т.)

The volume of the balloon is $V=\frac43\pi R^3=\frac43\pi(0.15\text{ m})^3=0.0141\text{ m}^3$.

Using the ideal gas law

$$
n=\frac{pV}{RT}=\frac{(2.02\times10^5\text{ Pa})(0.0141\text{ m}^3)}{(8.31\text{ J/mole}\cdot\text{K})(293\text{ K})}=1.17\text{ moles}.
$$

Therefore the mass of the air inside the balloon is

$$
m=nM=(1.17\text{ moles})(28.8\text{ g/mole})=33.7\text{ g}=0.0337\text{ kg}.
$$

The scale reading will be equal to the force of gravitational attraction $(m+m_b)g$ on the balloon and the enclosed air minus the buoyant force $B=m_{dis}g=\rho gV$ acting on the balloon, where $m_{dis}$ is the mass of the air displaced by the balloon.

$$
F_s=(m+m_b)g-m_{dis}g=(m+m_b-m_{dis})g.
$$

The temperature and volume of the air displaced is the same as that of the air within the balloon but the pressure is only half as much.

$$
m_{dis}=n_{dis}M=\tfrac12nM=\tfrac12m=0.0168\text{ kg}.
$$

$$
F_s=(0.0337\text{ kg}+0.0042\text{ kg}-0.0168\text{ kg})(9.8\text{ m/s}^2)=0.21\text{ N}.
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-b">

### b. (5 т.)

The amount of air inside the balloon and its mass do not change. The volume of air displaced and its mass both change by the amount

$$
m'_{dis}=\rho V'=\rho\tfrac43\pi(R')^3=\rho\tfrac43\pi(1.1R)^3=(1.1)^3\rho\tfrac43\pi R^3=(1.1)^3m_{dis}=(1.1)^3(0.0168\text{ kg})=0.0224\text{ kg}.
$$

$$
F_s=(m+m_b-m'_{dis})g=(0.0337\text{ kg}+0.0042\text{ kg}-0.0224\text{ kg})(9.8\text{ m/s}^2)=0.15\text{ N}.
$$

</ProblemSection>


</Spoiler>
