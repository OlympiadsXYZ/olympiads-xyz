## Условие

Four massless rigid rods are connected into a quadrilateral by four hinges. The hinges have mass $m$, and allow the rods to freely rotate. A spring of spring constant $k$ is connected across each of the diagonals, so that the springs are at their relaxed length when the rods form a square.

Assume the springs do not interfere with each other. If the square is slightly compressed along one of its diagonals, its shape will oscillate over time. What is the period of these oscillations?

(A) $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{4k}}$

(B) $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{2k}}$

(C) $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$

(D) $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{2m}{k}}$

(E) $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{4m}{k}}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 2\pi\sqrt\{\dfrac\{m\}\{2k\}\}

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Suppose that one of the masses has moved a distance $x$ from its original position, and it currently has speed $v$. By symmetry, all of the other masses have also moved a distance $x$, and also have a speed $v$, so the total kinetic energy is $K = (4m)v^2/2$. Each spring is either stretched or compressed by a distance $2x$, so the total elastic potential energy is $U = (8k)x^2/2$. Thus, the kinetic and potential energy are the same as that of a system of mass $m_{\mathrm{eff}} = 4m$ on a spring of spring constant $k_{\mathrm{eff}} = 8k$, so the angular frequency is $\sqrt{k_{\mathrm{eff}}/m_{\mathrm{eff}}} = \sqrt{2k/m}$.


</Spoiler>
