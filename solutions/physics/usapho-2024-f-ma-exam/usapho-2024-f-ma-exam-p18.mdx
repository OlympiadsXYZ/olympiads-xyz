## Условие

A satellite is initially in a circular orbit of radius $R$ around a planet of mass $M$. It fires its rockets to instantaneously increase its speed by $\Delta v$, keeping the direction of its velocity the same, so that it enters an elliptical orbit whose maximum distance from the planet is $2R$.

<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2024-f-ma-exam/p18-fig1-v2.png" alt="A planet at the centre of a circle of radius R and a larger circle of radius 2R; an ellipse starts tangent to the inner circle at the point marked start and reaches the outer circle at the diametrically opposite point marked end; a dotted line joins start and end through the planet." width="639" height="651" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

What is the value of $\Delta v$? (Hint: when the satellite is in an elliptical orbit with semimajor axis $a$, its total energy per unit mass is $-GM/2a$.)

(A) $0.08\sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{R}}$

(B) $0.15\sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{R}}$

(C) $0.22\sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{R}}$

(D) $0.29\sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{R}}$

(E) $0.41\sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{R}}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 0.15\sqrt\{\dfrac\{GM\}\{R\}\}

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The initial speed in the circular orbit is $v_i = \sqrt{GM/R}$. In the elliptical orbit, the semimajor axis is $a = 3R/2$, so the total energy per satellite mass is

$$
-\frac{GM}{3R} = -\frac{GM}{R} + \frac{1}{2}v_f^2
$$

which implies

$$
v_f^2 = \frac{4}{3}\frac{GM}{R}.
$$

Computing $v_f - v_i$ gives the answer.


</Spoiler>
