## Условие

A wheel of radius $R$ has a thin rim and four spokes, each of which have uniform density.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '25.1%', '--fig-max': '161px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2024-f-ma-exam/p19-fig1-v3.png" alt="A wheel with a rim labelled m and four perpendicular spokes: the upper, left and right spokes are labelled m, the thick lower spoke is labelled 3m; a right-angle mark at the centre." width="503" height="512" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

The entire rim has mass $m$, three of the spokes each have mass $m$, and the fourth spoke has mass $3m$. The wheel is suspended on a horizontal frictionless axle passing through its center. If the wheel is slightly rotated from its equilibrium position, what is the angular frequency of small oscillations?

(A) $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{3R}}$

(B) $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{2R}}$

(C) $\sqrt{\dfrac{2g}{3R}}$

(D) $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{R}}$

(E) $\sqrt{\dfrac{7g}{6R}}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- \sqrt\{\dfrac\{g\}\{3R\}\}

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

This system is a physical pendulum. The moment of inertia about the center is

$$
I = mR^2 + \frac{1}{3}\left(m + m + m + 3m\right)R^2 = 3mR^2.
$$

When the system is rotated a small angle $\theta$ from equilibrium, the restoring torque comes from the difference in masses of the top and bottom spokes, so

$$
\tau = -(3m - m)g\frac{R}{2}\sin\theta \approx -mgR\theta.
$$

As a result, we have $\alpha = -(g/3R)\theta$ from which we read off $\omega = \sqrt{g/3R}$.


</Spoiler>
