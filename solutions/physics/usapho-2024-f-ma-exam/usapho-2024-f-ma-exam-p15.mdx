## Условие

The viscous force between two plates of area $A$, with relative speed $v$ and separation $d$, is $F = \eta Av/d$, where $\eta$ is the viscosity. In fluid mechanics, the Ohnesorge number is a dimensionless number proportional to $\eta$ which characterizes the importance of viscous forces, in a drop of fluid of density $\rho$, surface tension $\gamma$, and length scale $\ell$. Which of the following could be the definition of the Ohnesorge number?

(A) $\dfrac{\eta\ell}{\sqrt{\rho\gamma}}$

(B) $\eta\ell\sqrt{\dfrac{\rho}{\gamma}}$

(C) $\eta\sqrt{\dfrac{\rho}{\gamma\ell}}$

(D) $\eta\sqrt{\dfrac{\rho\ell}{\gamma}}$

(E) $\dfrac{\eta}{\sqrt{\rho\gamma\ell}}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- \dfrac\{\eta\}\{\sqrt\{\rho\gamma\ell\}\}

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

From the definition of viscosity, the units of $\eta$ are $\mathrm{kg\,m/s^2}$. In addition, the units of $\rho$ are $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and the units of $\ell$ are m. As for the surface tension, it is a force per length, so its units are $\mathrm{kg/s^2}$. We thus conclude that $\sqrt{\rho\gamma\ell}$ has the same units as $\eta$, so that choice (E) is the only possibility proportional to $\eta$ that is properly dimensionless.


</Spoiler>
