## Условие

A space probe with mass $m$ at point $P$ traverses through a cluster of three asteroids, at points $A$, $B$, and $C$. The masses and locations of the asteroids are shown below.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '27.9%', '--fig-max': '179px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2024-f-ma-exam/p12-fig1-v3.png" alt="Asteroid C of mass 3M at the top; the probe P of mass m a distance d below it; a further distance d below P is the midpoint of the line joining asteroid A (mass M) on the left and asteroid B (mass 2M) on the right, each a distance d from that midpoint, with a right-angle mark." width="560" height="611" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

What is the torque on the probe about point $C$?

(A) $\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\dfrac{GMm}{d}$

(B) $\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{GMm}{d}$

(C) $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\dfrac{GMm}{d}$

(D) $\dfrac{GMm}{d}$

(E) $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}\,GMm}{d}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- \dfrac\{1\}\{2\sqrt\{2\}\}\dfrac\{GMm\}\{d\}

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Only asteroids $A$ and $B$ contribute to the torque. Using the standard formula for torque, we have

$$
\tau = \frac{GMm}{(\sqrt{2}\,d)^2}\left(2\,\frac{d}{\sqrt{2}} - \frac{d}{\sqrt{2}}\right) = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\frac{GMm}{d}.
$$


</Spoiler>
