## Условие

A bead of mass $m$ can slide frictionlessly on a vertical circular wire hoop of radius 20 cm.

</figure>

The hoop is attached to a stand of mass $m$, which can slide frictionlessly on the ground. Initially, the bead is at the bottom of the hoop, the stand is at rest, and the bead has velocity 2 m/s to the right. At some point, the bead will stop moving with respect to the hoop. At that moment, through what angle along the hoop has the bead traveled?

(A) $30^{\circ}$

(B) $45^{\circ}$

(C) $60^{\circ}$

(D) $90^{\circ}$

(E) $120^{\circ}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 60°

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

When the bead stops moving with respect to the hoop, they must have the same velocity. By conservation of horizontal momentum, that velocity must be half the initial velocity. Thus, by conservation of energy,

$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}(2m)(v/2)^2 + mg\Delta h
$$

where $\Delta h$ is the change in height of the bead. Solving gives $\Delta h = 10\ \mathrm{cm}$, which implies the bead slides by $60^{\circ}$ along the hoop.


</Spoiler>
