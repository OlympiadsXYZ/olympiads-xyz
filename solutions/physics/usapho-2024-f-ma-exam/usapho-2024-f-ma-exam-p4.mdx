## Условие

A bouncy ball is thrown vertically upward from the ground. Air resistance is negligible, and the ball’s collisions with the ground are perfectly elastic. Which of the following shows the kinetic energy of the ball as a function of time? Assume the collisions are too quick for their duration to be seen in the plot.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

When the ball is in the air, its velocity is $v(t) = v_0 - gt$, so its kinetic energy is proportional to $v^2 = (v_0 - gt)^2$, which is a concave up parabola. When the ball bounces, its speed stays the same, so the kinetic energy stays the same and there’s no discontinuity.


</Spoiler>
