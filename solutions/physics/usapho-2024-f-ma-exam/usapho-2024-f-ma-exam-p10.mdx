## Условие

A block of mass $m$ is connected to the walls of a frictionless box by two massless springs with relaxed lengths $\ell$ and $2\ell$, and spring constants $k$ and $2k$ respectively. The length of the box is $3\ell$. The system rotates with a constant angular velocity $\omega$ about one of its walls.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '39.4%', '--fig-max': '252px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2024-f-ma-exam/p10-fig1.png" alt="An open box rotating with angular velocity ω about a vertical axis through its left wall; inside, a block m is attached to the left wall by a spring labelled ℓ, k and to the right wall by a spring labelled 2ℓ, 2k." width="789" height="420" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

Suppose the block stays at a constant distance $r$ from the axis of rotation, without touching either of the walls. What is the value of $r$?

(A) $\dfrac{2k\ell}{2k - m\omega^2}$

(B) $\dfrac{2k\ell}{2k + m\omega^2}$

(C) $\dfrac{2k\ell}{3k + m\omega^2}$

(D) $\dfrac{3k\ell}{3k - m\omega^2}$

(E) $\dfrac{3k\ell}{3k + m\omega^2}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- \dfrac\{3k\ell\}\{3k - m\omega²

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

We balance forces on the block in the reference frame rotating with the box, to give $m\omega^2 r = k(r - \ell) + 2k(r - \ell)$. Solving for $r$ gives the answer.


</Spoiler>
