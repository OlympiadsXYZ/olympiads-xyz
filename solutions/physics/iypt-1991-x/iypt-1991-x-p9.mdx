An apple dropped [15] from a balcony of a multi-storey building will calmly descend into the hands of your friend, if you attach to the apple a propeller cut out of dense paper [16]. Explain the principle of work for such a parachute and study the dependence of the drag force on the descent rate and on the sizes of the propeller’s blades.

<ProblemSection id="note-15">

### Note [15]

The original Russian text is ambiguous, as the word «брошенный» can imply both the release without initial speed and the launch in the air under any angle

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="note-16">

### Note [16]

The 1992 Czech and the Slovak texts read, “rigid paper”, and specify that the propeller “is attached to this apple with a match”

</ProblemSection>
