A small knitting needle, [18] with two rounded pieces of polyurethane foam [19] strung onto it, is shot [20] out of a blowgun. Find the optimal blowpipe size to shoot such a projectile. What maximum projectile speed did you succeed to achieve?

<ProblemSection id="note-17">

### Note [17]

“Hunter’s gun”, according to the 1991 English text

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="note-18">

### Note [18]

Wording about the needle and its size varies across the translations.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="note-19">

### Note [19]

“Porolon”, a transliterated Russian name for a particular foamy material («поролон»), as it reads in the 1991 English, “paralon” in the 1996 Czech and 2006 English texts. It reads “polystyrene” in the 1992 Czech text, and “foam rubber” in the French text

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="note-20">

### Note [20]

“Is shot centrally”, in the Slovak text

</ProblemSection>
