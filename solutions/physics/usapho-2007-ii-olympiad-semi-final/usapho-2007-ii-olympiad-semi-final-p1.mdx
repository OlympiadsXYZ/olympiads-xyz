## Условие

A group of 12 resistors is arranged along the edges of a cube as shown in the diagram below. The vertices of the cube are labeled $a$-$h$.

**a.** The resistance between each pair of vertices is as follows:

$R_{ab} = R_{ac} = R_{ae} = 3.0\ \Omega$

$R_{cg} = R_{ef} = R_{bd} = 8.0\ \Omega$

$R_{cd} = R_{bf} = R_{eg} = 12.0\ \Omega$

$R_{dh} = R_{fh} = R_{gh} = 1.0\ \Omega$

What is the equivalent resistance between points $a$ and $h$? **[13 т.]**

**b.** The three $12.0\ \Omega$ resistors are replaced by identical capacitors. $C_{cd} = C_{bf} = C_{eg} = 15.0\ \mu\mathrm{F}$. A $12.0\ \mathrm{V}$ battery is attached across points $a$ and $h$ and the circuit is allowed to operate for a long period of time. What is the charge ($Q_{cd}$, $Q_{bf}$, $Q_{eg}$) on each capacitor after this long period of time? **[12 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a.** $\frac{44}{15}\ \Omega$ ≈ $2.933$ Ω
- **b.** 120 μC

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

a. There is a high degree of symmetry present. Points $b$, $c$, and $e$ are at the same potential; similarly, points $d$, $f$, and $g$ are at the same potential. The circuit then reduces to a series connection of three parallel resistor clusters.

The three parallel clusters have effective resistances of $1\ \Omega$, $8/5\ \Omega$, and $1/3\ \Omega$. The effective resistance of the circuit is then $44/15\ \Omega$.

b. After a long time no current will flow through the branches of the circuits containing capacitors. The circuit then reduces to a parallel connection of three series resistor clusters.

The effective resistance of the circuit is $4\ \Omega$, the current through the circuit is then

$$
(12\ \mathrm{V})/(4\ \Omega) = 3\ \mathrm{A}. \qquad (A1-1)
$$

The three branches are identical; each then carries $1\ \mathrm{A}$.

The potential drop across each capacitor is the same as the potential drop across the $8\ \Omega$ resistors, so

$$
V_C = (1\ \mathrm{A})(8\ \Omega) = 8\ \mathrm{V}. \qquad (A1-2)
$$

Finally, the charge on each capacitor is

$$
Q = (15\ \mu\mathrm{F})(8\ \mathrm{V}) = 120\ \mu\mathrm{C}. \qquad (A1-3)
$$


</Spoiler>
