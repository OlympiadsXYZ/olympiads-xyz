## Условие

**В тази задача**

- [b.](#b)

</nav>

A simple gun can be made from a uniform cylinder of length $L_0$ and inside radius $r_c$. One end of the cylinder is sealed with a moveable plunger and the other end is plugged with a cylindrical cork bullet. The bullet is held in place by friction with the walls of the cylinder. The pressure outside the cylinder is atmospheric pressure, $P_0$. The bullet will just start to slide out of the cylinder if the pressure inside the cylinder exceeds $P_{cr}$.

<ProblemSection id="a">

### a. (16 т.)

There are two ways to launch the bullet: either by heating the gas inside the cylinder and keeping the plunger fixed, or by suddenly pushing the plunger into the cylinder. In either case, assume that an ideal monatomic gas is inside the cylinder, and that originally the gas is at temperature $T_0$, the pressure inside the cylinder is $P_0$, and the length of the cylinder is $L_0$.

**i.** Assume that we launch the bullet by heating the gas without moving the plunger. Find the minimum temperature of the gas necessary to launch the bullet. Express your answer in terms of any or all of the variables: $r_c, T_0, L_0, P_0$, and $P_{cr}$. **[8 т.]**

**ii.** Assume, instead that we launch the bullet by pushing in the plunger, and that we do so quickly enough so that no heat is transferred into or out of the gas. Find the length of the gas column inside the cylinder when the bullet just starts to move. Express your answer in terms of any or all of the variables: $r_c, T_0, L_0, P_0$, and $P_{cr}$. **[8 т.]**

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="b">

### b. (9 т.)

It is necessary to squeeze the bullet to get it into the cylinder in the first place. The bullet normally has a radius $r_b$ that is slightly larger than the inside radius of the cylinder; $r_b - r_c = \Delta r$, is small compared to $r_c$. The bullet has a length $h \ll L_0$. The walls of the cylinder apply a pressure to the cork bullet. When a pressure $P$ is applied to the bullet along a given direction, the bullet’s dimensions in that direction change by

$$
\frac{\Delta x}{x} = \frac{-P}{E}
$$

for a constant $E$ known as Young’s modulus. You may assume that compression along one direction does not cause expansion in any other direction. (This is true if the so-called Poisson ratio is close to zero, which is the case for cork.)

If the coefficient of static friction between the cork and the cylinder is $\mu$, find an expression for $P_{cr}$. Express your answer in terms of any or all of the variables: $P_0, \mu, h, E, \Delta r$, and $r_c$.

</ProblemSection>

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- $P_{cr} = P_0 + \frac{2\mu E h}{r_c^2}\delta r$
- **a., i.** $T = \frac{P_{cr}}{P_0}T_0$
- **a., ii.** $L = L_0\left(\frac{P_0}{P_{cr}}\right)^{5/3}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

<ProblemSection id="solution-a">

### a.

Two parts, solved individually.

i. Isochoric compression:

$$
\frac{P_f}{P_i} = \frac{T_f}{T_i}, \qquad (A2-1)
$$

so

$$
T = \frac{P_{cr}}{P_0}T_0. \qquad (A2-2)
$$

ii. Adiabatic compression:

$$
PV^{\gamma} = \mathrm{const}, \qquad (A2-3)
$$

where $\gamma = C_P/C_V = (C_V + 1)/C_V = 5/3$ for a monatomic gas. Consequently

$$
P_0 L_0^{\gamma} = P_{cr} L^{\gamma}, \qquad (A2-4)
$$

and then

$$
L = L_0\left(\frac{P_0}{P_{cr}}\right)^{5/3}. \qquad (A2-5)
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-b">

### b.

The normal pressure on the bullet comes from

$$
P = \frac{\delta r}{r_c}E. \qquad (A2-6)
$$

Therefore, the normal force on the bullet is

$$
F_N = \frac{\delta r}{r_c}E \times (2\pi r_c h), \qquad (A2-7)
$$

and finally the force of friction is $\mu F_N$. The force due to the pressure difference between the inside of the barrel and the outside must equal the normal force, so

$$
(\pi r_c^2)(P_{cr} - P_0) = 2\pi h\mu\ \delta r\ E, \qquad (A2-8)
$$

and then

$$
P_{cr} = P_0 + \frac{2\mu E h}{r_c^2}\delta r. \qquad (A2-9)
$$

</ProblemSection>


</Spoiler>
