## Условие

D.1  A solid wooden ball of radius $r_0$ is floating in the water. Ignoring frictional effects, the frequency of small oscillations would be $\omega_0$, but because of viscous friction, after being displaced vertically, the frequency of decaying oscillations is actually $0.99\,\omega_0$. What is the minimum radius $r_{\min}$ of a wooden ball floating in water that undergos small oscillations when displaced? Hint: the viscous drag force acting on a given body is proportional to its speed relative to the bulk of the fluid, and to the viscosity $\eta$ of the fluid it is moving in. The unit of the viscosity is $\mathrm{kg/(m \cdot s)}$.
