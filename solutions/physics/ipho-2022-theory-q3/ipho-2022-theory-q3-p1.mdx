## Условие

Part A. Spaghetto (2.0 points)

A.1  A spaghetto straw of diameter $d$ is being balanced horizontally from its middle. If $d = 1\ \mathrm{mm}$, the straw breaks under its own weight once its length reaches $l = 50\ \mathrm{cm}$. What is the maximum length $l'$ of the straw of diameter $d' = 1\ \mathrm{cm}$ before it breaks under its own weight?
