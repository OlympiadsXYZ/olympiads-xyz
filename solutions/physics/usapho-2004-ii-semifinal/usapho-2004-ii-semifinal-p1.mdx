## Условие

The capacitors in the circuit diagrammed to the right are initially uncharged.

**a.** What is the current in each resistor immediately after the switch labeled S is closed? **[10 т.]**

After a very long time, the capacitors are fully charged.

**b.** What is the current in each resistor at this time? **[5 т.]**

**c.** What is the charge on each capacitor? **[10 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a.** I₁₀ = 0.273 A, I₂₀ = 0.164 A, I₃₀ = 0.109 A
- **b.** I₃₀ = 0, I₂₀ = I₁₀ = 0.200 A
- **c.** 5.33 μC

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

a. At the instant the switch is closed, there is no charge on the capacitor and no voltage across it. Applying Kirchhoff’s junction rule $I_{10} = I_{20} + I_{30}$.

Applying Kirchhoff’s loop rule to the right loop $0 = (20.0\ \Omega)I_{20} - (30.0\ \Omega)I_{30}$.

Solving for $I_{20}$ and then $I_{10}$, $I_{20} = 1.5I_{30}$ $\quad I_{10} = 1.5I_{30} + I_{30} = 2.5I_{30}$

Applying Kirchhoff’s loop rule to the left loop

$$
0 = 6.00\ \mathrm{V} - (20.0\ \Omega)I_{20} - (10.0\ \Omega)I_{10} = 6.00\ \mathrm{V} - (20.0\ \Omega)(1.5I_{30}) - (10.0\ \Omega)(2.5I_{30})
$$

$$
0 = 6.00\ \mathrm{V} - (30.0\ \Omega)I_{30} - (25.0\ \Omega)I_{30} = 6.00\ \mathrm{V} - (55.0\ \Omega)I_{30}
$$

Solving for the currents $I_{30} = \dfrac{6.00\ \mathrm{V}}{55.0\ \Omega} = 0.109\ \mathrm{A}$. $\quad I_{20} = 1.5(0.109\ \mathrm{A}) = 0.164\ \mathrm{A}$

and $I_{10} = 2.5(0.109\ \mathrm{A}) = 0.273\ \mathrm{A}$

*$\{$A solution using parallel and series resistor combinations is equally valid.$\}$*

b. When the switch has been closed for a very long time, the capacitors are fully charged. Current no longer flows in the capacitor branch and $I_{30} = 0$

Applying Kirchhoff’s loop rule to the left loop

$$
0 = 6.00\ \mathrm{V} - (20.0\ \Omega)I_{10} - (10.0\ \Omega)I_{10} = 6.00\ \mathrm{V} - (30.0\ \Omega)I_{10}
$$

$$
I_{20} = I_{10} = \frac{6.00\ \mathrm{V}}{30.0\ \Omega} = 0.200\ \mathrm{A}
$$

c. The two capacitors are in series. Both have the same charge. The equivalent capacitance is

$$
\frac{1}{C_{eq}} = \frac{1}{C_2} + \frac{1}{C_4} = \frac{1}{2.00\ \mu\mathrm{F}} + \frac{1}{4.00\ \mu\mathrm{F}} = \frac{3}{4.00\ \mu\mathrm{F}}
$$

or
$$
C_{eq} = 1.33\ \mu\mathrm{F}.
$$

The voltage across the equivalent capacitance is the same as that across the 20.0 Ω resistor.

$$
V_c = V_{20} = (20.0\ \Omega)I_{20} = (20.0\ \Omega)(0.200\ \mathrm{A}) = 4.00\ \mathrm{V}.
$$

So the charge
$$
Q_2 = Q_4 = Q_{eq} = C_{eq}V_c = (1.33\ \mu\mathrm{F})(4.00\ \mathrm{V}) = 5.33\ \mu\mathrm{C}
$$


</Spoiler>
