## Условие

A ball is thrown so that it hits a stationary ramp and then bounces up the ramp.  Immediately before the first collision between the ball and the ramp, the ball has a velocity $v_\mathrm{o}$ that is horizontal as shown in the accompanying diagram.  Assume that all collisions are perfectly elastic, that there is no friction between the ramp and the ball, and that air resistance is negligible.  At the instant that the ball makes its $N$+1$^{\mathrm{st}}$ collision with the ramp, the ball is moving perpendicular to the ramp.  Express your answers in terms of $N$, $v_\mathrm{o}$, and $g$.

**a.** Find the angle that the ramp makes with the horizontal. **[20 т.]**

**b.** What is the distance, measured along the ramp, between the point at which the first collision occurs and the point at which the $N$+1$^{\mathrm{st}}$ collision occurs? **[5 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a.** $\tan\theta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2N}}$
- **b.** $x = \frac{v_0^2}{g}\frac{N}{\sqrt{2N + 1}}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

A4. Selecting the $y$-axis perpendicular to the ramp and the $x$-axis parallel to the ramp in the upward direction, the components of the gravitational acceleration are

$a_x = -g\sin\theta$ and $a_y = -g\cos\theta$,

where $\theta$ is the angle the ramp makes with the horizontal. The components of the initial velocity are $v_{0x} = v_0\cos\theta$ and $v_{0y} = -v_0\sin\theta$.

a. At each collision with the plane, the $x$-component of velocity does not change while the $y$-component reverses sign. At the start of the first bounce $v_{0y} = +v_0\sin\theta$.

The $y$-displacement is given by $$y = y_0 + v_{0y}t + \tfrac{1}{2}a_y t^2 \qquad (\mathrm{A4-1})$$

Let $y_0 = 0$ at $t = 0$, the start of the first bounce. Let $t = t_1$, the time when the ball returns to the ramp $y = 0$, at the end of the first bounce. Substituting these values into (A4 – 1)

$$
0 = v_0\sin\theta t_1 - \tfrac{1}{2}g\cos\theta t_1^2.
$$

Solving for $t_1$ and eliminating the initial time $t_1 = 0$, $$t_1 = \frac{2v_0\sin\theta}{g\cos\theta} = \frac{2v_0}{g}\tan\theta.$$

The velocity at the end of the first bounce, as the ball is about to impact the ramp again, is

$$
v_y = v_{0y} + a_y t = v_0\sin\theta - g\cos\theta t = v_0\sin\theta - g\cos\theta\frac{2v_0\sin\theta}{g\cos\theta} = -v_0\sin\theta.
$$

Upon hitting the ramp the velocity reverses to become $v_{0y} = +v_0\sin\theta$.

Each bounce has the same $v_{0y}$ and $a_y$, so each bounce takes the same amount of time $t_1$.

Therefore the time for $N$ bounces is $$t_N = Nt = N\frac{2v_0\sin\theta}{g\cos\theta} = \frac{2Nv_0}{g}\tan\theta. \qquad (\mathrm{A4-2})$$

There is no impulse in the $x$-direction, so the $x$-equations hold continuously. At the end of the Nth bounce, the ball’s velocity is perpendicular to the ramp, $v_x = 0$. Substituting this into the equation for the $x$-component of velocity,

$$
v_x = v_{0x} + a_x t
$$

$$
0 = v_0\cos\theta - g\sin\theta t_N = v_0\cos\theta - g\sin\theta\left(N\frac{2v_0\sin\theta}{g\cos\theta}\right).
$$

Dividing by $v_0\cos\theta$, $$0 = 1 - \frac{2N\sin^2\theta}{\cos^2\theta} = 1 - 2N\tan^2\theta.$$

Solving for $\tan\theta$ $$\tan\theta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2N}} \qquad (\mathrm{A4-3})$$

b. The x-displacement is given by $x = v_{0x}t + \frac{1}{2}a_x t^2$.

The maximum displacement occurs at the end of the Nth bounce, time $t_N$. Combining (A4 – 2) and (A4 – 3) $$t_N = \frac{2Nv_0}{g}\tan\theta = \frac{2Nv_0}{g}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2N}} = \frac{v_0}{g}\sqrt{2N}.$$

Substituting this into the x-equation,

$$
x = (v_0\cos\theta)\left(\frac{v_0}{g}\sqrt{2N}\right) + \tfrac{1}{2}(-g\sin\theta)\left(\frac{v_0}{g}\sqrt{2N}\right)^2 = \frac{v_0^2}{g}\cos\theta\sqrt{2N} - \frac{v_0^2}{2g}2N\sin\theta
$$

Using the triangle to the right to determine $\cos\theta$ and $\sin\theta$

$$
\cos\theta = \sqrt{\frac{2N}{2n + 1}} \qquad \sin\theta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2N - 1}}
$$

$$
x = \frac{v_0^2}{g}\sqrt{\frac{2N}{2N + 1}}\sqrt{2N} - \frac{v_0^2}{2g}2N\frac{1}{\sqrt{2N + 1}} = \frac{v_0^2}{g}\frac{N}{\sqrt{2N + 1}}
$$
