## Условие

Sound sources S$_1$ and S$_2$ are located at $+x_0$ and $–x_0$, respectively.  The sources produce tones with the same phase and amplitude.  The frequency can be varied from 175 Hz to 625 Hz, but both speakers have the same frequency.  Use 340 m/s for the velocity of sound in air and $x_0 = 0.850$ m.

<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2004-ii-semifinal/p3-fig1.png" alt="An x-axis with a y-axis arrow at the origin; source S2 marked by a dot at –x0 and source S1 at +x0." />
</figure>

**a.** For which frequency or frequencies in the above range is minimum sound intensity heard at all points along the $x$-axis with $x > x_0$. **[8 т.]**

**b.** Next consider the region on the $x$-axis between the two sound sources S$_1$ and S$_2$.  Assuming that the amplitude $A$ does not decrease with distance from the source, write an equation that gives the resultant sound wavefunction in the form $\Psi(x,t) = 2A\psi(x)\phi(t)$, where $\psi$ and $\phi$ are functions of $x$ and $t$, respectively.  The equation may also depend on the wavelength $\lambda$ and frequency $f$ of the sound waves and should be valid for all points on the $x$-axis with $-x_0 < x < +x_0$. **[10 т.]**

**c.** For the frequency or frequencies found in Part (a), at which points between $x = –\ 0.85$ m and $x = +0.85$ m does minimum sound intensity occur? **[7 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a.** 300 Hz and 500 Hz
- **b.** $\Psi = 2A\cos(kx)\sin(\omega t - kx_0)$
- **c.** For f = 300 Hz: x = ±0.283 m; for f = 500 Hz: x = ±0.170 m, ±0.510 m

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

A3. a. In order for minimum sound intensity to be heard in the region along the $x$-axis with $x > x_0$, the distance between sources must be an odd half-integer multiple of the wavelength $\lambda$.

$$
2x_0 = (2n - 1)\frac{\lambda}{2} = (2n - 1)\frac{v}{2f} \qquad n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots
$$

where $v = 340\ \mathrm{m/s}$, the velocity of sound in air, and $175\ \mathrm{Hz} \le f \le 625\ \mathrm{Hz}$, the frequency of the sound that produces minimum intensity. Solving for the frequency

$$
f_n = (2n - 1)\frac{v}{4x_0} = (2n - 1)\frac{(340\ \mathrm{m/s})}{4(0.85\ \mathrm{m})} = (2n - 1)(100\ \mathrm{Hz}).
$$

The frequencies in the possible range are

$f_2 = 300\ \mathrm{Hz}$ and $f_3 = 500\ \mathrm{Hz}$

b. In the region between the sources, source S$_1$ emits a wave $\Psi_1$ that travels to the left and source S$_2$ emits a wave $\Psi_2$ that travels to the right.

$$
\Psi_1 = A\sin(\omega t + k(x - x_0)) \qquad \Psi_2 = A\sin(\omega t - k(x + x_0))
$$

where $k = \dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda}$ and $\omega = 2\pi f$.

Adding the waves to determine the resultant wave

$$
\Psi = \Psi_1 + \Psi_2 = A\sin(\omega t + k(x - x_0)) + A\sin(\omega t - k(x + x_0))
$$

$$
\Psi = A\sin(\omega t - kx_0)\cos(kx) + A\cos(\omega t - kx_0)\sin(kx)
$$

$$
+ A\sin(\omega t - kx_0)\cos(kx) - A\cos(\omega t - kx_0)\sin(kx)
$$

$$
\Psi = 2A\cos(kx)\sin(\omega t - kx_0)
$$

Note: This equation has the correct $x$-dependence. The waves travel the same distance to reach $x = 0$. This point is an interference maximum. Any expression with

$$
\Psi = 2A\cos(kx)\sin(\omega t - kx_0 + \delta)
$$

where $\delta$ is a phase constant is valid.

c. Minimum sound intensity occurs when $\cos(kx) = 0$, i.e., $kx = \pm(2n + 1)\pi/2$. Solving for $x$,

$$
x = \pm\frac{(2n + 1)\pi}{2k} = \pm\frac{(2n + 1)\pi}{2(2\pi/\lambda)} = \pm\frac{(2n + 1)\lambda}{4} = \pm\frac{(2n + 1)v}{4f}
$$

For $f = 300$ Hz:
$$
x = \pm\frac{(2n + 1)(340\ \mathrm{m/s})}{4(300\ \mathrm{Hz})} = \pm(2n + 1)(0.283\ \mathrm{m}) = \pm 0.283\ \mathrm{m} \qquad \text{with } n = 0.
$$

For $f = 500$ Hz:
$$
x = \pm\frac{(2n + 1)(340\ \mathrm{m/s})}{4(500\ \mathrm{Hz})} = \pm(2n + 1)(0.170\ \mathrm{m}) = \pm 0.170\ \mathrm{m},\ \pm 0.510\ \mathrm{m}
$$

with $n = 0, 1$.
