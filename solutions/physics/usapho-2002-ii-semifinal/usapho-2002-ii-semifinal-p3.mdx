## Условие

Fundamental quanta of length, time, and mass are the Planck length $\lambda_{\mathrm{P}}$, the Planck time $t_{\mathrm{P}}$, and the Planck mass $m_{\mathrm{P}}$. Use the fact that they depend only on the Newton’s gravitational constant $G$, Planck’s constant $h$, and speed of light in a vacuum $c$ and no other constant to derive expressions for them and then calculate their numerical values.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- λ_P = (Gh/c³)^(1/2) = 4.05 × 10⁻³⁵ m; t_P = (Gh/c⁵)^(1/2) = 1.35 × 10⁻⁴³ s; m_P = (hc/G)^(1/2) = 5.46 × 10⁻⁸ kg

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The Planck length $\lambda_{\mathrm{P}}$, the Planck time $t_{\mathrm{P}}$, and the Planck mass $m_{\mathrm{P}}$ depend only on the Newton’s gravitational constant $G$, Planck’s constant $h$, and speed of light in a vacuum $c$ and no other constant. Use dimensional analysis to obtain the equations. Let

[T] represent the dimension of time

[L] represent the dimension of length

[M] represent the dimension of mass

Since $G$ has units $\mathrm{N{\bullet}m^2/kg^2 = (kg{\bullet}m/s^2)\ m^2/kg^2 = m^3kg^{-1}s^{-2}}$ its dimensions are $[L]^3[M]^{-1}[T]^{-2}$ for $G$

$h$ has units $\mathrm{J\ s = (kg{\bullet}m^2/s^2)s = m^2kg^1s^{-1}}$ its dimensions are $[L]^2[M]^1[T]^{-1}$ for $h$

$c$ has units $\mathrm{m/s = m^1s^{-1}}$ its dimensions are $[L]^1[M]^0[T]^{-1}$ for $c$

Let

$$
\lambda_p = G^ah^bc^d.
$$

Then analyzing the dimensions

$$
[L]^1[M]^0[T]^0 = [L]^{3a}[M]^{-a}[T]^{-2a}[L]^{2b}[M]^{b}[T]^{-b}[L]^{d}[M]^0[T]^{-d} = [L]^{3a+2b+d}[M]^{-a+b}[T]^{-2a-b-d}.
$$

Equating exponents of [L] $\quad 1 = 3a+2b+d$

Equating exponents of [M] $\quad 0 = -a+b$

Equating exponents of [T] $\quad 0 = -2a-b-d$

Solving these equations $\quad b = a$ and $d = -3a$ then $1 = 3a + 2a - 3a = 2a$

So $a = 1/2$, $b = 1/2$, and $d = -3/2$ and

$$
\lambda_p = G^{1/2}h^{1/2}c^{-3/2} = \left(\frac{Gh}{c^3}\right)^{1/2} = \left(\frac{\left(6.67\times10^{-11}\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}\right)\left(6.63\times10^{-34}\mathrm{J\cdot s}\right)}{\left(3.0\times10^{8}\mathrm{m/s}\right)^3}\right)^{1/2} = 4.05\times10^{-35}\mathrm{m}
$$

Let

$$
t_p = G^ah^bc^d.
$$

Then analyzing the dimensions

$$
[L]^0[M]^0[T]^1 = [L]^{3a+2b+d}[M]^{-a+b}[T]^{-2a-b-d}.
$$

Equating exponents of [L] $\quad 0 = 3a+2b+d$

Equating exponents of [M] $\quad 0 = -a+b$

Equating exponents of [T] $\quad 1 = -2a-b-d$

Solving these equations $\quad b = a$, $d = -5a$, and $1 = -2a - a + 5a = 2a$

So $a = 1/2$, $b = 1/2m$ and $d = -5/2$

$$
t_p = G^{1/2}h^{1/2}c^{-5/2} = \left(\frac{Gh}{c^5}\right)^{1/2} = \left(\frac{\left(6.67\times10^{-11}\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}\right)\left(6.63\times10^{-34}\mathrm{J\cdot s}\right)}{\left(3.0\times10^{8}\mathrm{m/s}\right)^5}\right)^{1/2} = 1.35\times10^{-43}\mathrm{s}
$$

Let

$$
m_p = G^ah^bc^d.
$$

Then analyzing the dimensions

$$
[L]^0[M]^1[T]^0 = [L]^{3a+2b+d}[M]^{-a+b}[T]^{-2a-b-d}.
$$

Equating exponents of [L] $\quad 0 = 3a+2b+d$

Equating exponents of [M] $\quad 1 = -a+b$

Equating exponents of [T] $\quad 0 = -2a-b-d$

Solving these equations $\quad a = -1/2$, $b = 1/2$, and $d = 1/2$

$$
m_p = G^{-1/2}h^{1/2}c^{1/2} = \left(\frac{hc}{G}\right)^{1/2} = \left(\frac{\left(6.63\times10^{-34}\mathrm{J\cdot s}\right)\left(3.0\times10^{8}\mathrm{m/s}\right)}{\left(6.67\times10^{-11}\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}\right)}\right)^{1/2} = 5.46\times10^{-8}\mathrm{kg}
$$


</Spoiler>
