## Условие

A pendulum consists of a small metal sphere of mass $M$ attached to a 0.60-m massless string. It hangs from a support that is located 0.30 m to the right of and 0.40 m above a fixed small rod oriented perpendicular to the plane of swing. Assume that there is no friction between the rod and the string and ignore air resistance. The pendulum is pulled to the right and released from rest when the bob is located 0.15 m higher than the rod (See Fig. 1). As the pendulum swings to the left, the string contacts the rod and the sphere begins to loop around it (See Fig. 2). Ignore the diameter of the rod and take $y$ to be the height of the sphere above the rod. Express your answers to the following in terms of $y,M$, and $g$.

**a.** Find an expression for the tension $T$ in the string when the string is in contact with the rod. **[15 т.]**

**b.** Find the x- and y-components of the force that the rod exerts on the string. **[10 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a.** $T=3Mg(1-10y)$
- **b.** $F_x=-3Mg(1-10y)[0.60-\sqrt{1-(10y)^2}],\quad F_y=-3Mg(1-10y)(0.80+10y)$

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">


<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '61.7%', '--fig-max': '395px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2000-i-quarterfnal/p4-sol-fig1-astra-r6-20260925.png" alt="Pendulum contacting rod: tension forces, weight, radius0.10m and geometry." width="1235" height="509" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

**a.** Applying energy conservation between the initial point $h_0=0.15\ m$ and the point $y$

$K_0+U_0=K_y+U_y$ **[2]**

Initially $K_0=0$, $U_0=Mgh_0$ **[1+1]**

At point $y$ $K_y=(\frac12)Mv^2$, $U_y=Mgy$ **[1+1]**

$Mgh_0=(\frac12)Mv^2+Mgy$ (4.1) **[1]**

The sphere is moving in a circle of radius

$R=0.60\ m-\sqrt{(0.40\ m)^2+(0.30\ m)^2}=0.10\ m$. **[1]**

The total centripetal force has two contributions, the tension in the string $T$ and the radial component of the sphere’s weight, $Mg\cos\theta$. Setting the total centripetal force equal to the mass times the centripetal acceleration

$T+Mg\cos\theta=M\frac{v^2}{R}$ (4.2) **[4]**

Solving (4.1) for $Mv^2$ and substituting into (4.2)

$T+Mg\cos\theta=\frac{2Mg(h_0-y)}{R}$, (4.3) **[1]**

From the geometry $\cos\theta=\frac yR$. **[1]**

Combining with (4.3) and solving for $T$

$$
T=\frac{2Mg(h_0-y)}R-Mg\frac yR=\frac{Mg(2h_0-3y)}R=\frac{Mg(0.30m-3y)}{0.10m}
$$

$T=3Mg(1-10y)$ where y is in meters. **[1]**

**b.** By Newton’s third law, $F$, the force the rod exerts on the string, is equal and opposite to the force the string exerts on the rod. If there is no friction between the rod and the string, then the tension in both string segments is the same $T$.

$F_x=-\sum T_{sx}=-(T\sin36.9^\circ-T\sin\theta)$ **[2]**

and $F_y=-\sum T_{sy}=-(T\cos36.9^\circ+T\cos\theta)$. **[2]**

Since $\cos\theta=\frac yR$, $\sin\theta=\sqrt{1-(\frac yR)^2}$ **[1+1]**

$F_x=-T\left(0.60-\sqrt{1-(\frac yR)^2}\right)$ **[1]**

$F_x=-3Mg(1-10y)\left[0.60-\sqrt{1-(10y)^2}\right]$ y in meters. **[1]**

$F_y=-T\left(0.80+\frac yR\right)$ **[1]**

$F_y=-3Mg(1-10y)(0.80+10y)$ y in meters. **[1]**


</Spoiler>
