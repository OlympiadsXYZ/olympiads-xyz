## Условие

An accelerometer consists of a ball of mass $m$ attached to one end of a string. The other end of the string is attached to a cart of mass $M$. When the cart rolls down an inclined plane of angle $\theta$ with respect to the horizontal, the string makes a constant angle of $2\theta/3$ with respect to the vertical. See diagram at the right. Express your answers to the following in terms of known and given quantities. Assume air resistance is negligible.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '55.8%', '--fig-max': '358px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2000-i-quarterfnal/p2-fig1-astra-r6-20260925.png" alt="Cart M on slope theta carrying hanging ball m; string angle2theta/3." width="1118" height="595" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

**a.** What is the acceleration of the cart? **[13 т.]**

**b.** What is the coefficient of friction between the cart and the plane? **[12 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a.** $a=2g\sin(\theta/3)$
- **b.** $\mu=\tan(\theta/3)$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**a.** Resolving the string tension $T$ and the ball weight $mg$ into components parallel and perpendicular to the plane as shown in the diagram at the right.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '44%', '--fig-max': '282px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2000-i-quarterfnal/p2-sol-fig1-astra-r6-20260925-v2.png" alt="Forces on the accelerometer ball resolved parallel and perpendicular to the slope." width="881" height="1113" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


[1 point for each correct force/component] **[6]**

we have for the perpendicular component: $\sum F_\perp=0$ **[1]**

$T\cos(\theta/3)=mg\cos\theta$ (2.1) **[1]**

And for the parallel component: $\sum F_\parallel=ma$ **[1]**

$mg\sin\theta-T\sin(\theta/3)=ma$ (2.2) **[1]**

Solving (2.1) for $T$ $T=mg\frac{\cos\theta}{\cos(\theta/3)}$ **[1]**

and substituting into (2.2):

$mg\sin\theta-mg\frac{\cos\theta\sin(\theta/3)}{\cos(\theta/3)}=ma$ **[1]**

$a=g\left(\sin\theta-\cos\theta\frac{\sin(\theta/3)}{\cos(\theta/3)}\right)$ (2.3) **[1]**

(While it is not required to simplify the above to obtain full credit, it equals $a=2g\sin(\theta/3)$.)

**b.** The diagram to the right shows the forces acting on the cart-accelerometer system.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '36.9%', '--fig-max': '236px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2000-i-quarterfnal/p2-sol-fig2-astra-r6-20260925.png" alt="Forces on cart and accelerometer: normal, friction, weight and components." width="738" height="680" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


[1 point for each correct force/component] **[5]**

For the perpendicular component: $\sum F_\perp=0$ **[1]**

$F_n-(m+M)g\cos\theta=0$ (2.4) **[1]**

And for the parallel component:

$\sum F_\parallel=(m+M)a$ **[1]**

$(m+M)g\sin\theta-\mu F_n=(m+M)a$ (2.5) **[1]**

Combining equations (2.3), (2.4), and (2.5)

$$
(m+M)g\sin\theta-\mu(m+M)g\cos\theta=(m+M)g\left[\sin\theta-\cos\theta\frac{\sin(\theta/3)}{\cos(\theta/3)}\right]
$$

**[1]**

Dividing through by $(m+M)g$

$\sin\theta-\mu\cos\theta=\sin\theta-\cos\theta\frac{\sin(\theta/3)}{\cos(\theta/3)}$ **[1]**

Solving for the coefficient of friction $\mu$:

$\mu=\frac{\sin(\theta/3)}{\cos(\theta/3)}=\tan(\theta/3)$

(Or any equivalent answer.) **[1]**


</Spoiler>
