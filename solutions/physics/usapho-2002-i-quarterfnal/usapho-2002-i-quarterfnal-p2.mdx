## Условие

A particle of mass $m$ is constrained to move in one dimension. A force $\mathbf{F}$ acts on the particle. $\mathbf{F}$ always points toward the position labeled $E$. For example, when the particle is to the left of $E$, $\mathbf{F}$ points to the right. The magnitude of $\mathbf{F}$ is a constant $F$ except at point $E$ where it is zero.

</figure>

**a.** The system is horizontal. $\mathbf{F}$ is the net force acting on the particle. The particle is displaced a distance $A$ from the equilibrium position $E$ and released from rest. What is the period of the motion? **[10 т.]**

**b.** The system is vertical. Both the force $\mathbf{F}$ described above and gravity act on the system. The particle is displaced a distance $A$ above the position $E$ and released from rest. $F > mg$. What is the period of the motion? **[15 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a.** $T = 4\sqrt{\frac{2Am}{F}}$
- **b.** $T = \left(\frac{4F}{F - mg}\right)\sqrt{\frac{2Am}{F + mg}}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**2. a.** When the particle is released, it begins to move with an acceleration which has magnitude

$$
a = \frac{F}{m}
$$
**[2]**

and is directed toward position $E$. Since it starts from rest $\quad A = \tfrac{1}{2}at^2$ **[2]**

It reaches $E$ in time $t$

$$
t = \sqrt{\frac{2A}{a}} = \sqrt{\frac{2Am}{F}}
$$
**[1]**

The particle oscillates back and forth between amplitude A on the right and A on the left. **[2]**

The time for one complete cycle is four times this $\quad T = 4t$ **[2]**

$$
T = 4\sqrt{\frac{2Am}{F}}
$$
**[1]**

**b.** The particle oscillates between amplitude $A$ above point $E$ and $A'$ below. $A \ne A'$ **[1]**

Let $t_1$ = time it takes the particle to go from amplitude $A$ above to point $E$ and from $E$ up to $A$.

Let $t_2$ = time it takes the particle to go from point $E$ to amplitude $A'$ below and from $A'$ up to $E$.

The period is $\quad T = 2t_1 + 2t_2$ **[2]**

When the particle is above point E, the net force acting on it is

$$
ma = \sum F = F + mg \quad \text{downward.}
$$
**[1]**

The magnitude of the acceleration is $\quad a = \dfrac{F + mg}{m}$ **[1]**

Starting from rest the particle travels a distance $A$ given by $\quad A = \tfrac{1}{2}at_1^{\,2}$ **[1]**

Solving for $t_1$

$$
t_1 = \sqrt{\frac{2A}{a}} = \sqrt{\frac{2Am}{F + mg}}
$$
**[1]**

The particle passes through E with velocity

$$
v_E = at_1 = \left(\frac{F + mg}{m}\right)\sqrt{\frac{2Am}{F + mg}}
$$
**[1]**

When the particle is below point E, the net force acting on it is

$$
ma = \sum F = F - mg \quad \text{upward}
$$
**[1]**

The magnitude of the acceleration is $\quad a = \dfrac{F - mg}{m}$ **[1]**

It reaches maximum downward displacement $A'$ when $v = 0$. **[1]**

$$
v = 0 = v_E - at_2
$$
**[1]**

Solving for $t_2$

$$
t_2 = \frac{v_E}{a} = \left(\frac{F + mg}{m}\right)\sqrt{\frac{2Am}{F + mg}}\left(\frac{m}{F - mg}\right) = \left(\frac{F + mg}{F - mg}\right)\sqrt{\frac{2Am}{F + mg}}
$$
**[1]**

$$
T = 2t_1 + 2t_2 = 2\sqrt{\frac{2Am}{F + mg}} + 2\left(\frac{F + mg}{F - mg}\right)\sqrt{\frac{2Am}{F + mg}}
$$
**[1]**

$$
T = 2\sqrt{\frac{2Am}{F + mg}}\left(1 + \frac{F + mg}{F - mg}\right) = \left(\frac{4F}{F - mg}\right)\sqrt{\frac{2Am}{F + mg}}
$$
**[1]**


</Spoiler>
