*BPO6 Problems 20-24 June 2024. Montenegro · 20 юни 2024 г. · 25 т.*

## Условие

A quarter of a circle connects the vertical and the horizontal parts of a track. The radius of circular arc is $R = 20\ \mathrm{cm}$. A ball of unknown mass $m$ slides on the track with no friction. The ball is connected (through a slit along the track) with a stretched spring, as is shown in the figure. The length of the unstretched spring is $L_0 = 0.20\ \mathrm{m}$, and the spring constant is $k = 100\ \mathrm{N/m}$.

The sliding ball starts with zero initial speed from a point that is $\alpha = 45^{\circ}$ above the horizontal line when viewed from the centre of the arc, i.e. starts from point A, as is shown in the figure. The ball reaches the maximum speed at the angle $\beta = 34^{\circ}$ below the horizontal line, at point B, as is shown in the figure ($g = 9.81\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$). Derive the mathematical expression and calculate the numerical value for:

**a.** the length of the spring when the ball is at the starting position, at point A; **[3 т.]**

**b.** the elongation of the spring when the ball is at the starting position at point A; **[1 т.]**

**c.** the length of the spring when the ball is at the position of maximal speed, at point B; **[4 т.]**

**d.** the elongation of the spring when the ball is at the of maximal speed, at point B; **[1 т.]**

**e.** the magnitude of the spring force when the ball has maximal speed, at point B; **[2 т.]**

**f.** the acceleration component of the ball, tangential to the track at point B; **[2 т.]**

**g.** the mass of the ball; **[5 т.]**

**h.** the maximum speed of the ball (at point B); **[4 т.]**

**i.** the speed of the ball at point C. **[3 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a.** 0.45 m
- **b.** 0.25 m
- **c.** 0.25 m
- **d.** 0.05 m
- **e.** 5 N
- **f.** 0 m/s^2
- **g.** 0.14 kg
- **h.** 6.9 m/s
- **i.** 4.8 m/s

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

1. Calculate the length of the spring when the ball is at the starting position at point A. **[3 points]**

$L_A^2 = R^2 + (R + x_A)^2$ **[1 point]**

$tg\alpha = \dfrac{x_A}{R}, \quad \alpha = 45^{0} \quad \Rightarrow \quad tg\alpha = 1 \quad \Rightarrow \quad x_A = R$ **[1 point]**

$\Rightarrow L_A^2 = R^2 + (2R)^2 = 5R^2$

$\Rightarrow L_A = R\sqrt{5} = 44.7cm \simeq 45cm.$ **[0.5 + 0.5 point]**

2. Calculate the elongation of the spring when the ball is at the starting position at point A. **[1 points]**

$\Delta L_A = L_A - L_0 = 24.7cm \simeq 25cm.$ **[0.5 + 0.5 point]**

3. Calculate the length of the spring when the ball is at the position of maximal velocity at point B. **[4 points]**

$L_B^2 = (2R - y_B)^2 + x_B^2$ **[1.5 point]**

$x_B = R - R\sin\beta = R(1 - \sin\beta)$ **[0.5 point]**

$y_B = R\cos\beta$ **[0.5 point]**

$\beta = 34^{0} \quad \Rightarrow \quad \sin\beta = 0.559, \quad \cos\beta = 0.829$

$\Rightarrow x_B = 8.82cm$

$\Rightarrow y_B = 16.6cm$

$$
\begin{aligned} \Rightarrow L_B^2 &= (2R - R\cos\beta)^2 + R^2(1 - \sin\beta)^2 = \\ &= R^2\left[(2 - \cos\beta)^2 + (1 - \sin\beta)^2\right] = \\ &= R^2(6 - 4\cos\beta - 2\sin\beta) = 1.566R^2 \end{aligned}
$$
**[1 point]**

$\Rightarrow L_B = R\sqrt{1.566} = 25.02cm \simeq 25cm.$ **[0.5 point]**

4. Calculate the elongation of the spring when the ball is at the of maximal velocity at point B. **[1 points]**

$\Delta L_B = L_B - L_0 = 5.02cm \simeq 5.0cm.$ **[0.5 + 0.5 point]**

5. Calculate the magnitude of the spring force when the ball has maximal velocity at point B. **[2 points]**

$F_k = k\Delta L_B = 5.02N \simeq 5.0N.$ **[1.5 + 0.5 point]**

6. Calculate the acceleration of the ball tangential to the track at point B. **[2 points]**

$v_B = v_{MAX} \quad \Rightarrow \quad a_{Bt} = 0.$

7. Calculate the mass of the ball. **[5 points]**

At point B, using $a_{Bt} = 0$ we can write

$F_k \cos\delta = mg\cos\beta$ **[2.5 points]**

$\Rightarrow m = \dfrac{F_k\cos\delta}{g\cos\beta}$ **[1 point]**

$\delta = 180^{0} - (\gamma + \beta)$ **[0.5 points]**

$\cos\gamma = \dfrac{x_B}{L_B} = \dfrac{8.82}{25.02} = 0.352 \quad \Rightarrow \quad \gamma = 69.3^{0}$

$\Rightarrow \delta = 180^{0} - (69.4^{0} + 34^{0}) = 76.7^{0}$ **[0.5 points]**

$\Rightarrow \cos\delta = 0.231^{0}$

$\Rightarrow m = \frac{5.02 \cdot 0.231}{9.81 \cdot 0.829} kg = 0.142kg \simeq 0.14kg.$ **[0.5 points]**

8. Calculate the maximum speed of the ball (at point B). **[4 points]**

We use work-kinetic energy theorem for positions A and B:

$mgh_A + \dfrac{1}{2}k\Delta L_A^2 = mgh_B + \dfrac{1}{2}k\Delta L_B^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}mv_{MAX}^2$ **[2 points]**

$\Rightarrow v_{MAX}^2 = 2g(h_A - h_B) + \dfrac{k}{m}(\Delta L_A^2 - \Delta L_B^2)$ **[0.5 points]**

$h_A = 2R, \quad h_B = x_B = R(1 - \sin\beta)$ **[0.5 + 0.5 points]**

$\Rightarrow v_{MAX}^2 = \ldots = 47.01\dfrac{m^2}{s^2}$

$\Rightarrow v_{MAX} = 6.88\frac{m}{s} \simeq 6.9\frac{m}{s}$ **[0.5 points]**

9. Calculate the speed of the ball at point C. **[3 points]**

We use again work-kinetic energy theorem for positions A and C:

$mgh_A + \dfrac{1}{2}k\Delta L_A^2 = mgh_C + \dfrac{1}{2}k\Delta L_C^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}mv_C^2$ **[0.5 points]**

$\Rightarrow v_C^2 = 2g(h_A - h_C) + \dfrac{k}{m}(\Delta L_A^2 - \Delta L_C^2)$ **[0.5 points]**

$h_A = 2R, \quad h_C = 0$ **[0.5 + 0.5 points]**

$\Delta L_C = L_C - L_0 = 2R - L_0 = 20cm$ **[0.5 points]**

$\Rightarrow v_C^2 = \ldots = 22.54\dfrac{m^2}{s^2}$

$\Rightarrow v_C = 4.76\frac{m}{s} \simeq 4.8\frac{m}{s}.$ **[0.5 points]**
