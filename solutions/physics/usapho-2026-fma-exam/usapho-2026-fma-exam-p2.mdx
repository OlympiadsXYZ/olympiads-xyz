## Условие

A series of dominoes are stood upright in a line. The dominoes have mass density $\rho$, height $h$, and spacing $d$ between them. When the first domino is knocked over, a wave of falling dominoes propagates down the line with speed $v$.

Now, a second set of dominoes is set up identically, but with all dimensions ($h$ and $d$) scaled by $\lambda$. The mass density is the same. Let $v'$ denote the wave speed of the scaled system. Which of the following best describes how $v'$ depends on $\lambda$?

(A) $v' \sim \frac{1}{\sqrt{\lambda}}$

(B) $v' \sim \lambda$

(C) $v' \sim \frac{1}{\lambda}$

(D) $v' \sim \sqrt{\lambda}$

(E) $v'$ is independent of $\lambda$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- D

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The wave speed should go like the ratio of spacing $d$ to the timescale $\tau$ of a domino falling. Clearly $\tau$ must go like $\sqrt{\frac{h}{g}}$ by dimensional analysis. So $v \sim d\sqrt{\frac{g}{h}}$. After scaling, the new wave speed must go like: $v_\lambda \sim \lambda d\sqrt{\frac{g}{\lambda h}} \implies v' \sim \lambda^{1/2}$


</Spoiler>
