## Условие

Two small balls are launched simultaneously from the same point at some height above horizontal ground. One ball is launched vertically upward with speed $3\ \mathrm{m/s}$, while the other is launched horizontally with speed $4\ \mathrm{m/s}$ and lands on the ground at a horizontal distance of $20\ \mathrm{m}$ from the launch point. Neglect air resistance. At the moment the second ball lands, how far is it from the first ball?

(A) 25 m

(B) 36 m

(C) 60 m

(D) 80 m

(E) 240 m

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Their relative velocity magnitude is 5 m/s and it takes 5 seconds to cover 20 m with horizontal component of velocity of 4 m/s
