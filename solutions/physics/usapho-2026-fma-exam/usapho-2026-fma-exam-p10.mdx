## Условие

Consider the following three solid objects, each of mass $M$ and uniform density:

(i) a half-ball of radius $a$;

(ii) a cylinder of radius $a$ and height $a$;

(iii) a cylinder of radius $a$ and height $2a$.

For each object, consider the gravitational acceleration at the center of its flat circular face. Let $g_{\mathrm{ball}}$, $g_a$, and $g_{2a}$ denote these accelerations for the half-ball, the cylinder of height $a$, and the cylinder of height $2a$, respectively. Which of the following is correct?

(A) $g_a > g_{\mathrm{ball}} > g_{2a}$

(B) $g_{\mathrm{ball}} > g_{2a} > g_a$

(C) $g_{\mathrm{ball}} > g_a > g_{2a}$

(D) $g_{2a} > g_{\mathrm{ball}} > g_a$

(E) $g_{2a} > g_a > g_{\mathrm{ball}}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- C

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

In all three cases, symmetry ensures that the gravitational acceleration at the specified point is perpendicular to the flat circular face.

Starting from the half-ball, imagine continuously deforming it into a cylinder of radius $a$ and height $a$ while keeping the total mass fixed. During this deformation, mass elements move farther from the observation point and spread farther from the symmetry axis, reducing the perpendicular component of the gravitational field. Thus,

$$
g_{\mathrm{ball}} > g_a.
$$

Next, deform the cylinder of height $a$ into a cylinder of the same radius but height $2a$. This moves mass farther away along the symmetry axis and further reduces the gravitational acceleration at the center of the base:

$$
g_a > g_{2a}.
$$

Therefore,

$$
g_{\mathrm{ball}} > g_a > g_{2a}.
$$


</Spoiler>
