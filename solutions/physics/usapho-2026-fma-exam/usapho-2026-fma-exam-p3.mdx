## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

If we start from top, then on first segment $v_x > 0$ and $v_y = 0$, next $v_x < 0$ and $v_y < 0$ and on the last segment $v_x = 0$ and $v_y < 0$.
