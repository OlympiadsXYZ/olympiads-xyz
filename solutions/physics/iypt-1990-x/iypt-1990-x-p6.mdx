## Условие

A П-shaped soldered glass tube contains some water (Fig. 2).

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '19.7%', '--fig-max': '126px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/iypt-1990-x/water-tube-astra-root15-20260925.png" alt="Original sealed inverted-U glass tube with different water levels separated by height H." width="395" height="429" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

If there is an initial difference of water levels $H$, then the water levels will become equal after some time. Estimate the rate of this equalization for a given $H$ and $T=\mathrm{const}$,

**a.** if there is no air in the tube

**b.** if there is some air in the tube, at normal atmospheric pressure.
