## Условие

A cylinder is moving towards the closed end in a long tube filled with water (Fig. 3).

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '40.3%', '--fig-max': '258px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/iypt-1990-x/cylinder-astra-root15-20260925.png" alt="Original cylinder moving at speed v toward the closed left end of a water-filled tube." width="807" height="180" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


The inner diameter of the tube is D, diameter of the cylinder is d, the cylinder length is L, $D-d=h$, $L>D$, $h\ll D$. How does the resistance force depend on the speed of cylinder? Compare the theoretical estimations with the experimental results.
