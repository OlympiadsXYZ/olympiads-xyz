## Условие

Consider the Bohr model of the hydrogen atom. Let $m_e$ and $e$ be the mass and magnitude of the charge of the electron respectively. Let $a_0$ be the ground state radius (Bohr radius).

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

### (a)

Centripetal acceleration is given by Coulomb force.

$$
\frac{m_ev^2}{a_0}=\frac{Ke^2}{a_0^2};\quad K=\frac1{4\pi\epsilon_0};\quad m_evr=\hbar\qquad (3.1)
$$

$$
a_0=\frac{\hbar^2}{Km_ee^2}\qquad (3.2)
$$

$$
\text{Total energy}=\frac{m_ev^2}{2}-\frac{Ke^2}{a_0}=-\frac{Ke^2}{2a_0}\qquad (3.3)
$$

$$
I_{\mathrm H}=\frac{Ke^2}{2a_0}\qquad (3.4)
$$

### (b)

$$
\frac{m_ev^2}{r}=\frac{2Ke^2}{r^2}\text{ and }m_evr=\hbar\qquad (3.5)
$$

$$
r=\frac{\hbar^2}{2Km_ee^2}=\frac{a_0}{2}\qquad (3.6)
$$

$$
\text{Total Energy (T.E.)}=\frac12m_ev^2-\frac{2Ke^2}{r}=-\frac{Ke^2}{r}=\frac{2Ke^2}{a_0}=-4I_{\mathrm H}\qquad (3.7)
$$

$$
I_{\mathrm{He}^+}=4I_{\mathrm H}\qquad (3.8)
$$

### (c)

$$
\frac{m_ev^2}{r}=\frac{KZe^2}{r^2}-\frac{Ke^2}{(2r)^2}\text{ and }m_evr=\hbar\qquad (3.9)
$$

$$
m_ev^2r=Ke^2\left(Z-\frac14\right)\text{ and }m_ev^2r=\frac{\hbar^2}{m_er}\qquad (3.10)
$$

$$
r=\frac{\hbar^2}{Km_ee^2\left(Z-\frac14\right)}=\frac{a_0}{\left(Z-\frac14\right)}\qquad (3.11)
$$

### (d)

$$
\text{Kinetic Energy (K.E.)}=m_ev^2=\frac{Ke^2\left(Z-\frac14\right)}{r}\qquad (3.12)
$$

$$
=\frac{Ke^2\left(Z-\frac14\right)^2}{a_0}\qquad (3.13)
$$

$$
=2\left(Z-\frac14\right)^2I_{\mathrm H}\qquad (3.14)
$$

$$
\text{Potential Energy (P.E.)}=-\frac{2KZe^2}{r}+\frac{Ke^2}{2r}\qquad (3.15)
$$

$$
=\frac{-2Ke^2}{r}\left(Z-\frac14\right)\qquad (3.16)
$$

$$
=\frac{-2Ke^2}{a_0}\left(Z-\frac14\right)^2\qquad (3.17)
$$

$$
=-4\left(Z-\frac14\right)^2I_{\mathrm H}\qquad (3.18)
$$

$$
\mathrm{(T.E.)}_i=-2\left(Z-\frac14\right)^2I_{\mathrm H}\qquad (3.19)
$$

$$
\mathrm{(T.E.)}_f=-Z^2I_{\mathrm H}\qquad (3.20)
$$

$$
I_Z^{\mathrm{th}}=\mathrm{(T.E.)}_f-\mathrm{(T.E.)}_i=2\left(Z-\frac14\right)^2I_{\mathrm H}-Z^2I_{\mathrm H}\qquad (3.21)
$$

$$
I_Z^{\mathrm{th}}=\left(Z^2-Z+\frac18\right)I_{\mathrm H}\qquad (3.22)
$$

### (e)

$$
\Delta I_Z=I_Z^{\mathrm{th}}-I_Z^{\mathrm{expt}}=I_Z^{\mathrm{th}}-I_{Z^*}^{\mathrm{th}}=\left\{\left[Z^2-Z+\frac18\right]-\left[(Z-\alpha)^2-(Z-\alpha)+\frac18\right]\right\}I_{\mathrm H}\qquad (3.23)
$$

$$
\Delta I_Z=\left[-\alpha^2+2Z\alpha-\alpha\right]I_{\mathrm H}\qquad (3.24)
$$

|  | $Z$ | $(I_Z^{\mathrm{expt}})$ | $I_Z^{\mathrm{th}}$ | $(I_Z^{\mathrm{th}}-I_Z^{\mathrm{expt}})$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mathrm H^-$ | 1 | 0.055 | 0.125 | 0.07 |
| He | 2 | 1.81 | 2.13 | 0.32 |
| $\mathrm{Li}^+$ | 3 | 5.56 | 6.13 | 0.57 |
| $\mathrm{Be}^{++}$ | 4 | 11.32 | 12.13 | 0.81 |
| $\mathrm B^{3+}$ | 5 | 19.07 | 20.13 | 1.06 |
| $\mathrm C^{4+}$ | 6 | 28.83 | 30.13 | 1.30 |
| $\mathrm N^{5+}$ | 7 | 40.60 | 42.13 | 1.53 |
| $\mathrm O^{6+}$ | 8 | 54.37 | 56.13 | 1.76 |
| $\mathrm F^{7+}$ | 9 | 70.15 | 72.13 | 1.98 |

A plot of $\Delta I_Z$ vs $Z$ is a linear graph.

Slope of the graph $=2\alpha=0.24$

$\alpha=0.12$

One can also linearize in the following way:

$$
I_Z^{\mathrm{expt}}=I_{Z^*}^{\mathrm{th}}=\left(Z-\alpha-\frac12\right)^2-\frac18\qquad (3.25)
$$

$$
\sqrt{I_Z^{\mathrm{expt}}+\frac18}=Z-\alpha-\frac12\qquad (3.26)
$$

Plot of $\sqrt{I_Z^{\mathrm{expt}}+\frac18}$ vs $Z$ is a linear graph. This method gives $\alpha=0.10$. Both methods are acceptable.

Accepted range of $\alpha$ is : $0.10\leq\alpha\leq0.14$.

Graph is on the last page of this booklet.
