## Условие

An imperfect spring has a restoring force $F$ that depends on the displacement $x$ from equilibrium as in the graph shown below.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '26%', '--fig-max': '167px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2021-fma-exam/p23-fig1-v2.png" alt="Graph of F against x: F is zero between −d and d, and decreases linearly with slope −k outside, rising to the upper left for x < −d and falling to the lower right for x > d." width="521" height="357" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

The slope of the curve for $x < -d$ and $x > d$ is a constant $-k$. A mass $m$ is attached to the spring and released from rest at the position $x = A$, where $A > d$. What is the period of the subsequent motion?

(A) $T = \sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}\left(2\pi + \dfrac{2d}{A}\right)$

(B) $T = \sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}\left(2\pi + \dfrac{2d}{A - d}\right)$

(C) $T = \sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}\left(2\pi + \dfrac{4d}{A - d}\right)$

(D) $T = \sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}\left(2\pi + \dfrac{\pi d}{A - d}\right)$

(E) $T = \sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}\left(2\pi + \dfrac{2\pi d}{A - d}\right)$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- C

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The full motion is the combination of two parts: simple harmonic motion as if the spring were ideal (when the mass is at $|x| > d$), and motion with uniform velocity in the region $|x| < d$. By conservation of energy, the velocity in this region satisfies

$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}k(A - d)^2
$$

so the time spent in this region is

$$
\frac{4d}{v} = \frac{4d}{A - d}\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}.
$$

The time spent at $|x| > d$ is $2\pi\sqrt{m/k}$, and adding these two times gives the result.


</Spoiler>
