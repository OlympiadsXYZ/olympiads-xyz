## Условие

A point mass slides with speed $v$ on a frictionless horizontal surface between two fixed parallel walls, initially a distance $L$ apart. It bounces between the walls perfectly elastically. You move one of the walls towards the other by a distance $0.01L$, with speed $0.0001v$. What is the final speed of the point mass?

(A) $1.001v$

(B) $1.002v$

(C) $1.005v$

(D) $1.01v$

(E) $1.02v$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- D

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The total time is $100L/v$, during which 50 round-trips occur. Because the collisions are elastic, each collision with the moving wall increases the speed of the block by $0.0002v$. Thus, the final speed is increased by $(50)(0.0002v) = 0.01v$.


</Spoiler>
