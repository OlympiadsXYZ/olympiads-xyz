## Условие

A block is released from rest at the top of a fixed, frictionless ramp with horizontal length $L$ and inclination $\theta$.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '20.8%', '--fig-max': '133px'}}>

For a fixed value of $L$, which value of $\theta$ minimizes the time needed for the block to reach the bottom of the ramp?

(A) $30^{\circ}$

(B) $45^{\circ}$

(C) $60^{\circ}$

(D) $75^{\circ}$

(E) $80^{\circ}$

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The acceleration down the ramp is $a = g\sin\theta$, and $d = at^2/2$ where the distance $d = L/\cos\theta$. Hence $t^2 \propto d/a \propto 1/\sin\theta\cos\theta \propto 1/\sin(2\theta)$. The maximum of $\sin(2\theta)$, which minimizes $t$, is at $\theta = 45^{\circ}$.


</Spoiler>
