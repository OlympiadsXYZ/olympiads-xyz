## Условие

An object of mass $m = 1\,\mathrm{kg}$ is attached to a platform of mass $M = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ with a spring of spring constant $k = 400\,\mathrm{N/m}$. There is no friction between the object and the platform, and the coefficient of static friction between the platform and the ground is $\mu = 0.1$. The object is placed at its equilibrium position, and then given a horizontal velocity $v$.

<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2021-fma-exam/p12-fig1-v2.png" alt="An L-shaped platform M resting on the ground; a spring k attached to its raised left part holds a block m lying on the platform, with an arrow v pointing to the right above the block." width="564" height="312" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

For what $v$ will the platform never slip on the ground?

(A) $v \le 0.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

(B) $v \le 0.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

(C) $v \le 0.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$

(D) $v \le 0.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

(E) $v \le 0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- C

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The force of the spring on the platform is $F_s = kx$, where $x$ is the displacement of the object from equilibrium. The maximum value of $x$ can be found by conservation of energy,

$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}kx^2
$$

which gives a maximum spring force $F_s = v\sqrt{km}$ acting on the platform. The maximum possible friction force on the platform is $(M + m)\mu g$. Setting these two equal, the maximum velocity is

$$
v = \frac{(M + m)\mu g}{\sqrt{km}} = 0.25\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$


</Spoiler>
