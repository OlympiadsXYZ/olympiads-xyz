## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- D

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Because the car is speeding up, its acceleration has a tangential component, i.e. a component parallel to the racetrack. Because the car is turning, its acceleration also has a component $v^2/r$ directed towards the center of the track. Since $v$ increases through the turn, this inward radial acceleration increases through the turn. The only choice with all of these features is (D).


</Spoiler>
