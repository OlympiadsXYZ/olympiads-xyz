## Условие

Two massless rods are attached to frictionless pivots, with their ends touching. The distances between the pivot points and the endpoints of the rods are shown below.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '44.6%', '--fig-max': '286px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2021-fma-exam/p3-fig1-v2.png" alt="Two horizontal rods: the left rod has a downward force F at its left end and a pivot 2 m from that end, with 1 m from the pivot to its right end; the right rod rests on the right end of the left rod, with its pivot 0.5 m from its left end and 2 m from the pivot to its right end, where an upward force F′ acts." width="894" height="327" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

Neglecting friction between the rods, if a force $F$ is applied at the left end of the left rod, what force $F'$ must be applied at the right end of the right rod to keep the system in equilibrium?

(A) $F/8$

(B) $F/2$

(C) $4F/7$

(D) $6F/5$

(E) $2F$

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

By balancing torques on the first rod, we see that the normal force on the right end is twice the force exerted on the left end; that is, the first rod is acting as a lever with mechanical advantage 2. By similar logic, the second rod acts as a lever with mechanical advantage $1/4$. Thus, the combination of the two has mechanical advantage $2(1/4) = 1/2$, so $F' = F/2$.


</Spoiler>
