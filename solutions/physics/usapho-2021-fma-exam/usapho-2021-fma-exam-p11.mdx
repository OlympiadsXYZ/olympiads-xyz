## Условие

Projectiles $A$, $B$, and $C$ are simultaneously thrown off a cliff, and take the trajectories shown.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '33.7%', '--fig-max': '216px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2021-fma-exam/p11-fig1-v5.png" alt="Three parabolic trajectories launched from the top edge of a shaded cliff, landing on the ground at A (nearest, a narrow arc of medium height), B (the highest arc) and C (the farthest, the lowest and widest arc)." width="674" height="496" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

Neglecting air resistance, rank the times $t_A$, $t_B$, and $t_C$ they take to hit the ground.

(A) $t_A < t_B < t_C$

(B) $t_A < t_C < t_B$

(C) $t_C < t_B < t_A$

(D) $t_C < t_A < t_B$

(E) There is not enough information to decide.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- D

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Since the $x$ and $y$ directions are independent, the time to impact only depends on the initial $v_y$, which in turn determines the maximum height. The higher the maximum height, the longer the trajectory takes.


</Spoiler>
