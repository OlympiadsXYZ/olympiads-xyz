## Условие

A train starts from city $A$ and stops in city $B$. The distance between the cities is $s$. The train’s maximal acceleration is $a_1$ and its maximal deceleration is $a_2$ (in absolute value). What is the shortest time in which the train can travel between $A$ and $B$?

(A) $2\sqrt{\dfrac{s}{a_1 + a_2}}$

(B) $2\sqrt{\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{a_1 a_2}}}$

(C) $\sqrt{2\dfrac{s(a_1 + a_2)}{a_1 a_2}}$

(D) $\sqrt{\dfrac{2 s a_2}{a_1(a_1 + a_2)}}$

(E) $\sqrt{\dfrac{s\sqrt{a_1 a_2}}{(a_1 + a_2)^2}}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- C

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

When going from city to city as quickly as possible, the train accelerates for some time $t_1$, reaches a maximum speed $v$, then immediately decelerates for a time $t_2$, coming to rest right when it arrives at the new city. The speed $v$ obeys $v = a_1 t_1$ and $v = a_2 t_2$, and so

$$
a_1 t_1 = a_2 t_2 = v.
$$

The average speed of the train is $v/2$, so we also have

$$
(t_1 + t_2)\frac{v}{2} = s.
$$

If we solve these equations for $t_1$, we find

$$
t_1 = \sqrt{\frac{2 a_2 s}{a_1(a_1 + a_2)}}.
$$

Then we can find that

$$
t_2 = \frac{a_1 t_1}{a_2},
$$

and therefore

$$
t = t_1 + t_2 = t_1\left(\frac{a_1 + a_2}{a_2}\right).
$$

Substituting in our equation for $t_1$, we have

$$
t = \sqrt{\frac{2s(a_1 + a_2)}{a_1 a_2}}.
$$

Alternatively, we can consider the limit $a_2 \to \infty$. In this case, the train accelerates all the way to the next city, and the time is

$$
t_{a_2 \to \infty} = \sqrt{\frac{2s}{a_1}}.
$$

Only the correct answer simplifies appropriately.
