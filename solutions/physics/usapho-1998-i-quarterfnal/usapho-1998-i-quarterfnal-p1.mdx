## Условие

A disc of radius $R$ spins with angular velocity $\omega_0$ about its axis, which is held horizontally in frictionless bearings. The disc’s moment of inertia about the spin axis is $I_0$. At a certain instant, a small chip of mass $m$ breaks off the disc and flies away moving tangent to the wheel. The chip breaks off when it is at the top of the disc (i.e., at “12:00 o’clock”), which is point A in the accompanying diagram. Please specify all answers in terms of the given quantities and known constants.

**(a, 4 points)** What is the initial velocity $\mathbf v_0$ of the chip at point A when it separates from the disc? **[4 т.]**

**(b, 7)** How far is the chip from the axis of the disc when it arrives at the level of the axis (point B on the diagram)? **[7 т.]**

**(c, 7)** With what speed does the chip arrive at point B? **[7 т.]**

**(d, 7)** After the chip breaks off, what is the angular velocity of the disc? **[7 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **(a, 4 points)** $\mathbf v_0=R\omega_0\mathbf i$
- **(b, 7)** $x=R\omega_0\sqrt{2R/g}$
- **(c, 7)** $v_B=[(R\omega_0)^2+2gR]^{1/2}$
- **(d, 7)** $\omega\prime=\omega_0$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**(a, 4)** $\mathbf v_0=R\omega_0\mathbf i$ (in terms of the coordinate system shown) **(4 points)**

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '29.8%', '--fig-max': '191px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-1998-i-quarterfnal/p1-sol-fig1-astra-r6-20260925-v2.png" alt="Disc rotating clockwise with x axis rightwards and y axis upwards from its top." width="597" height="483" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


**(b, 7)** This is a projectile problem: with $t=0$ at the top of the disc, we have

$x=v_0t=R\omega_0t$ **(2)**

and $y=-\frac12gt^2$. **(2)**

At point B, $y=-R$, so that $t=\sqrt{2R/g}$ **(2)**

and therefore $x=R\omega_0\sqrt{2R/g}$ **(1)**

**(c, 7)** One may use either conservation of energy, or projectile kinematics.

Energy conservation approach:

$E_A=E_B$ (recognizing the principle) **(3)**

$\frac12m(R\omega_0)^2+mgR=\frac12mv_B^2+0$ (applying it) **(2)**

$v_B=[(R\omega_0)^2+2gR]^{1/2}$ (solving) **(2)**

Projectile kinematics approach:

$v_B^2=v_x^2+v_y^2$ **(2)**

where $v_x=R\omega_0$ **(2)**

and $v_y^2=2gR$ [from $v_y^2=v_{0y}^2+2a_y(y-y_0)$] **(2)**

and thus, $v_B=[(R\omega_0)^2+2gR]^{1/2}$ **(1)**

**(d, 7)** By conservation of angular momentum,

$L=L\prime$ (recognizing the principle) **(2)**

which becomes $I_0\omega_0=I\prime\omega\prime$ (applying it) **(1)**

Let’s calculate $I\prime$: it has two contributions, one for the damaged disc missing the chip, and one for the chip. When the chip comes loose,

$$
I\prime=[I_0-mR^2]\text{ (for the damaged disc)}+[mR^2]\text{ (for the chip)}
$$

$=I_0$. **(3)**

Hence, $\omega_0=\omega\prime$. **(1)**


</Spoiler>
