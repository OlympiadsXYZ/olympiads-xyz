This experimental problem propose an analysis for an electrical black box, which has three terminals - denoted by 1,2,3. To discover the elements that compose it, is provided a measuring device comprising a voltage source variable (denoted by $S$), connected in series with an ammeter (denoted by $A$); the two terminals of the device are denoted by $P$ and $Q$. To the same terminals $P$ and $Q$ is mounted a voltmeter (denoted by $V$). Ammeter and power supply have negligible internal resistance; internal resistance of the voltmeter can be considered as being infinite.

The terminals 1,2 and $P$ are placed in electrical contact. If terminals 3 and $Q$ are also in electrical contact and the tension furnished by the source varies, pairs of values (indicated by the measuring instruments) are obtained. These data are given in the table 1. Current intensity is positive when the current passes through the ammeter from $P$ to $Q$.

**Table 1**

| $U$(V) | −6 | −4 | −2 | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $I$(A) | −0.2 | 1.2 | 2.6 | 4.0 | 5.4 | 6.8 |

Then, terminals 2,3,$P$ are putted in electrical contact. If and terminals 1,$Q$ are also in electrical contact, the pairs of values indicated by the measuring instruments - where the voltage supplied by the source varies - are given in table 2.

**Table 2**

| $U$(V) | 0 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $I$(A) | −5.2 | −4.0 | −2.8 | −1.6 | −0.4 | +0.8 |

Finally, terminals 3,1,$P$ are putted in electrical contact. If and terminals 2,$Q$ are also in electrical contact, the pairs of values indicated by the measuring instruments - where the voltage supplied by the source varies - are given in table 3.

**Table 3**

| $U$(V) | −6 | −4 | −2 | 0 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $I$(A) | −0.6 | 0 | 0.6 | 1.2 | 1.8 |

Assume that all current-voltage dependencies are linear.

In this problem, calculation of errors is NOT requested.

$$

$$

Expresia pantei $m_{12}=\dfrac 1{R_{23}}+\dfrac 1{R_{31}}$; expresia intercepţiei $n_{12}=-\dfrac{E_{23}}{R_{23}}+\dfrac{E_{31}}{R_{31}}$ —0,40p.

$$
I_{\mathrm{sc},12}=-\frac{E_{23}}{R_{23}}+\frac{E_{31}}{R_{31}},\quad U_{\mathrm{cd},12}=\frac{\dfrac{E_{23}}{R_{23}}-\dfrac{E_{31}}{R_{31}}}{\dfrac 1{R_{23}}+\dfrac 1{R_{31}}}.

0,40p.

Expresia caracteristicii curent-tensiune a cutiei negre, pentru legarea $(2,3,P),(1,Q)$:

$$
i=U\left(\frac 1{R_{31}}+\frac 1{R_{12}}\right)+\left(-\frac{E_{31}}{R_{31}}+\frac{E_{12}}{R_{12}}\right).
$$

Expresia pantei $m_{23}=\dfrac 1{R_{31}}+\dfrac 1{R_{12}}$; expresia intercepţiei $n_{23}=-\dfrac{E_{31}}{R_{31}}+\dfrac{E_{12}}{R_{12}}$ —0,40p.

Expresiile pentru intersecţiile cu axele de coordonate:

$$

$$

0,40p.

Expresia caracteristicii curent-tensiune a cutiei negre, pentru legarea $(3,1,P),(2,Q)$:

$$
i=U\left(\frac 1{R_{12}}+\frac 1{R_{23}}\right)+\left(-\frac{E_{12}}{R_{12}}+\frac{E_{23}}{R_{23}}\right).
