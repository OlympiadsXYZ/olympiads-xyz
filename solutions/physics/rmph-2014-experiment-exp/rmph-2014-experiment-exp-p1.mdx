This experimental problem I propose that - using a chronometer and a piece of paper calibrated in arbitrary units a.u. - to determine the geometric dimensions of a can and express the results in meters. Also, the problem propose that - using experimental measurements you've done on the can - to determine its mass in kilograms, and its density in SI units. For this you will be provided with an experimental set, consisting of:

A. A cylindrical tin closed at one end (a can).

B. A sheet of paper marked with equidistant lines; distance between the lines is NOT known.

C. A chronometer.

D. A very strong thread, inextensible, perfectly deformable and of negligible mass.

E. A plastic template contour of Romanian borders allowing a proper suspension of can.

F. A tank vessel containing chemically pure water (water is assumed ideal fluid without viscosity).

G. A plastic syringe with unknown internal volume; on the syringe are marked equidistant divisions. The volume of liquid removed from the syringe when the piston travel a division is the fifth part of the volume of the syringe. Water will be added in the can in "portions" corresponding to the volume of a division - considered as "elementary volume".

H. Double-sided scotch tape for mounting the template on table.

I. Absorbent paper towels.

J. A toothpick (of negligible mass) serving to yield the initial rotation of the can and to observe its oscillations.

Study small rotating oscillations of empty can and of the can that have added one or more elementary volumes of water. Considers that the gravitational acceleration is $g=9.8\,\mathrm{m\cdot s^{-2}}$ and that the density of water is $\rho_0=1.0\times 10^3\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^{-3}}$. Write all data known or measured and all the results you get in different workloads using two significant figures.

Fill in the appropriate boxes in the answer sheet with your results.

In solving the problem uses the following notations:

| Notation | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| $L$ | For the length of each of the two suspension wires |
| $a=rL$ | For the radius of the cover of can |
| $b=pL$ | For the height of the can |
| $t=qL$ | For sheet thickness of which the can was made |
| $v=\dfrac{\pi a^2b}{N}=\dfrac{\pi r^2p}{N}L^3$ | For „elementary” volume corresponding to a division marked on the syringe. In expression $N$ is five time the number of syringe of water necessary to completely fill the can. |
| $\rho=\eta\rho_0$ | For unknown density of can material. |
| $m$ | For unknown mass of empty can. |
| $m_1$ | For the unknown mass of elementary volume of water $v$. |

Keep in mind that $t\ll a$.

Consider the coordinate system $Oxyz$ shown in the figure below. When the can is in balance, the center of the circular upper face of the box is the origin $O$ of the system; the $Ox$ system axis passing through the two mounting holes of the suspension wires. Axis $Oy$ is perpendicular to the axis. The $Oz$ axis of the coordinate system is the axis of symmetry of the box.

### Task no. 1 - Deduction of the expressions useful for solving experimental problem (4 т.)

Assume that the can rotates so that the centers of its circular faces remain on $Oz$ axis. In the new situation, the direction of each wires forms angle $\varphi$ with the vertical original direction. During rotation, the wires are always stretched; a diameter of circular face of can rotates with the angle $\alpha$, and the can rises with $h$. Consider that one of the suspension wires is trapped in $C$. In Figure 1 is represented the evolution of the radius $OC$ of the top face of the can.

In the figure 1, $OC$ is the radius of the top face of the can in the equilibrium position and $O'C''$ is the radius of the top face of the can when the box is rotated by the angle $\alpha$ around the axis of symmetry. $O'C'$ is a segment of length equal to the radius, parallel to $OC$ and at a distance $h$ of $OC$.

**1.a** Write the expression for the coordinates of $C''$ - the place where a suspension wire is caught - in the situation where the box is rotated by the angle $\alpha$ around the vertical axis of symmetry. Express your answer in terms of, $a,h$ and $\alpha$. **[0,2 т.]**

**1.b** Deduce the expression of angle $\varphi$ as function on the angle $\alpha$, length $L$ and radius $a$ of can cover. **[0,6 т.]**

**1.c** Deduce the expression of angle $\varphi$ and elevation $h$, when the angle $\alpha$ is small enough to admit that $\sin\alpha\simeq\alpha;\ \cos\alpha\simeq 1;\ \alpha^2=0$. Express the results as functions of, $L,a$ and $\alpha$. **[0,4 т.]**

**1.d** Deduce the expression of the interior volume $V$, as well as the expressions for the mass $m$ and moment of inertia $J$ of the empty can. Express results as appropriate functions of $L,r,p,q,\eta$ and $\rho_0$. **[0,6 т.]**

**1.e** Determine the expression of the mass $m_k$ and of moment of inertia $J_k$ of the can filed with $k$ “elementary volumes “of water. Express the results as appropriate functions of $L,r,p,q,\eta,\rho_0,N$ and $k$. **[0,4 т.]**

**1.f** Deduce the expression of the momentum $M_T$ of the forces acting on the can when it is rotated by a small angle $\alpha$. Express your answer in terms of $m_k,g,a,L$ and $\alpha$. **[0,8 т.]**

**1.g** Neglecting damping, determine the equation of motion describing the rotation of the can with a small angle $\alpha$ and show that it performs simple harmonic motion. . **[0,6 т.]**

**1.h** Period of oscillatory motion of the can has the expression $T_{\mathrm{oscil}}=\dfrac{2\pi}{\Omega}$ where

$$
\Omega^2=\frac{g}{L^w}\,r^2\,\frac{q\eta(r+2p)+pr\dfrac{k}{N}}{r^2q\left(\dfrac r 2+2p\right)\eta}.
$$

Find the unknown coefficient $w$ in the above expression. **[0,4 т.]**

**Problema experimentală nr. 1 – Determinarea lungimii, a masei şi a densităţii cu ajutorul cronometrului**

<ProblemSection id="solution-key-1a">

### 1.a

**Sarcina de lucru nr. 1 – Deducerea expresiilor utile pentru rezolvarea problemei experimentale**

Pentru expresia coordonatelor punctului $C''$ al locului în care este prins firul de suspensie:

$$
C''(a\cos\alpha,a\sin\alpha,h).
$$

0,20p; punctaj item: 0,20p.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-key-1b">

### 1.b

Pentru deducerea expresiei unghiului $\varphi$:

$$
\varphi=\arcsin\left(\frac{2a}{L}\sin\frac\alpha 2\right).
$$

0,60p. Observaţie: pentru rezolvarea parţială a acestei sarcini de lucru se acordă numai 0,2p. Punctaj item: 0,60p.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-key-1c">

### 1.c

Pentru determinarea expresiei unghiului $\varphi$, în cazul în care unghiul $\alpha$ este suficient de mic: $\varphi\cong r\alpha$ —0,20p.

Pentru determinarea expresiei pentru înălţimea $h$, în cazul în care unghiul $\alpha$ este suficient de mic:

$$
h\cong 2L\left(\frac\varphi 2\right)^2,\qquad h\cong\frac{a^2\alpha^2}{2L},\qquad h\cong 0.
$$

0,20p. Punctaj item: 0,40p.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-key-1d">

### 1.d

Pentru deducerea expresiei volumului interior al cutiei goale $V=\pi L^3r^2p$ —0,20p.

Pentru deducerea expresiei masei cutiei goale $m=\pi L^3\rho_0rq\eta(r+2p)$ —0,20p.

Pentru deducerea expresiei momentul de inerţie al cutiei goale:

$$
J=\pi a^2t\rho\frac{a^2}{2}+2\pi abt\rho a^2,\qquad J=\pi L^5r^3q\rho_0\left(\frac r 2+2p\right)\eta.
$$

0,20p. Punctaj item: 0,60p.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-key-1e">

### 1.e

Pentru determinarea expresiei masei cutiei în care s-au turnat $k$ volume elementare de apă:

$$
m_k=m+\frac{\pi a^2b}{N}\rho_0k,\qquad m_k=\pi L^3\rho_0r\left[q\eta(r+2p)+pr\frac kN\right].
$$

0,20p.

Pentru determinarea expresiei pentru momentul de inerţie $J_k$ al cutiei care conţine un număr $k$ întreg de volume elementare de apă: $J_k=J$ pentru oricare $k$ —0,20p.

Observaţie: Apa este considerată lichid ideal cu viscozitate nulă; ea nu se roteşte atunci când vasul care o conţine este în mişcare de rotaţie. Momentul de inerţie al cutiei rămâne $J$, oricare ar fi cantitatea de apă din interiorul său. Punctaj item: 0,40p.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-key-1f">

### 1.f


<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '29.6%', '--fig-max': '189px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/rmph-2014-experiment-exp/p1-sol-fig1-v2.png" alt="Complete force and torque geometry with all original tensions/components/arrows/phi/alpha and can." width="592" height="643" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


$$
2T\cos\varphi=m_kg,\qquad T\cong\frac{m_kg}{2},\quad\text{în cazul în care }\cos\varphi\cong 1.
$$

0,20p.

Expresia modulului componentei din planul orizontal pentru fiecare dintre tensiuni:

$$
T\sin\varphi\cong T\varphi,\qquad T\sin\varphi\cong\frac{m_kg\varphi}{2}.
$$

0,20p.

Expresia momentului fiecărei componente a tensiunii din fire conţinută în planul orizontal:

$$
M\cong\frac{m_kg\varphi}{2}a\sin\left(\frac\pi 2-\frac\alpha 2\right),\qquad M\cong\frac{m_kg}{2}a\varphi.
$$

0,20p.

Expresia momentului total determinat de componentele orizontale ale tensiunilor din fire $M_T=2M$:

$$
M_T\cong m_kga\varphi,\qquad M_T\cong m_kg\frac{a^2}{L}\alpha.
$$

0,20p. Punctaj item: 0,80p.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-key-1g">

### 1.g

Pentru ecuaţia mişcării de rotaţie a cutiei:

$$
J_k\ddot\alpha=-M_T,\qquad J_k\ddot\alpha+m_kg\frac{a^2}{L}\alpha=0.
$$

0,20p.

$$
J_k\ddot\alpha+\Omega^2\alpha=0.
$$

Precizarea că ecuaţia descrie o oscilaţie armonică a mărimii unghiului $\alpha$, cu pulsaţia

$$
\Omega^2=\frac gL\frac{q\eta(r+2p)+pr\dfrac kN}{q\left(\dfrac r 2+2p\right)\eta}.
$$

0,40p. Punctaj item: 0,60p.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-key-1h">

### 1.h

Pentru determinarea puterii la care apare $L$ în expresia perioadei, $w=1$:

$$
T_{\mathrm{oscil}}=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{Lq\left(\dfrac r 2+2p\right)\eta}{g\left[q\eta(r+2p)+pr\dfrac kN\right]}}.
$$

0,40p; punctaj item: 0,40p.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-key-2a">

### 2.a

**Sarcina de lucru nr. 2 – Măsurări**

Pentru — exemplu de răspuns:

$$
a=32\,\mathrm{u.a.},\quad b=100\,\mathrm{u.a.},\quad t=0.3\,\mathrm{u.a.},\quad L=440\,\mathrm{u.a.}
$$

0,30p. Observaţie: Dacă rezultatele indicate de concurent nu sunt determinate din cel puţin trei măsurări se acordă doar 0,10p.

$$
r=\frac aL=7.0\cdot 10^{-2},\quad p=\frac bL=2.3\cdot 10^{-1},\quad q=\frac tL=6.8\cdot 10^{-4}.
$$

0,30p.

Mărimea volumului materialului metalic al cutiei în unităţi arbitrare. Exemplu de răspuns:

$$
V_m=\pi at(a+2b)=\pi(32\,\mathrm{u.a.})(0.3\,\mathrm{u.a.})(32\,\mathrm{u.a.}+2\cdot 100\,\mathrm{u.a.})=2.3\cdot 10^4(\mathrm{u.a.})^3.
$$

0,20p. Observaţie: Dacă rezultatul nu se încadrează în intervalul $2.1\cdot 10^4(\mathrm{u.a.})^3\le V_m\le 2.5\cdot 10^4(\mathrm{u.a.})^3$, atunci se acordă zero puncte. Punctaj item: 0,80p.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-key-2b">

### 2.b

Pentru mărimea volumului cutiei în unităţi arbitrare. Exemplu de răspuns:

$$
