## Условие

Let $a,b,c,d > 0$ and $abcd = 1$. Prove that
$$
\frac{(a-1)(c+1)}{1+bc+c} + \frac{(b-1)(d+1)}{1+cd+d} + \frac{(c-1)(a+1)}{1+da+a} + \frac{(d-1)(b+1)}{1+ab+b} \ge 0.
$$
