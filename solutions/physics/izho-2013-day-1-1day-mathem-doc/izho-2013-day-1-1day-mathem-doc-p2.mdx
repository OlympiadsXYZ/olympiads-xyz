## Условие

Find all odd positive integers $n > 1$ such that there is a permutation $a_1, a_2, ..., a_n$ of the numbers $1, 2, ..., n$, where $n$ divides one of the numbers $a_k^2 - a_{k+1} - 1$ and $a_k^2 - a_{k+1} + 1$ for each $k, 1 \le k \le n$ (we assume $a_{n+1} = a_1$).
