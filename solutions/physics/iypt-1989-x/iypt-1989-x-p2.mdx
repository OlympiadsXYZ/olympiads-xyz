Is it possible to call “noon” the moment in the middle of the time interval between sunrise and sunset? Using a calendar, you will easily notice that throughout the year this moment “drifts” relatively to a certain moment of time. Explain the cause of this effect. [6]

<ProblemSection id="note-6">

### Note [6]

In [Jordens 1989], the problem reads: “Noon. Can we call "noon” the moment exactly at the middle of the time- stretch between sunrise and sunset? With the help of a calendar you can easily make sure that this moment wanders during the year. Explain the cause of this effect”
