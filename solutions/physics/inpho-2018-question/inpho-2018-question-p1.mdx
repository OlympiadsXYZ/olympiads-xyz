## Условие

In a nucleus, the attractive central potential which binds the proton and the neutron is called the Yukawa potential. The associated potential energy $U(r)$ is

$$
U(r)=-\alpha\frac{e^{-r/\lambda}}r
$$

Here $\lambda=1.431\ \mathrm{fm}$ ($\mathrm{fm}=10^{-15}\ \mathrm m$), $r$ is the distance between nucleons, and $\alpha=86.55\ \mathrm{MeV\cdot fm}$ is the nuclear force constant ($1\ \mathrm{MeV}=1.60\times10^{-13}\ \mathrm J$). Assume nuclear force constant $\alpha$ to be $A\hbar c$. Here $\hbar=h/2\pi$ and $h$ is Planck’s constant. In order to compare the nuclear force to other fundamental forces of nature within the nucleus of Deuterium ($\,{}^2\mathrm H$), let the constants associated with the electrostatic force and the gravitational force to be equal to $B\hbar c$ and $C\hbar c$ respectively. Here $A$, $B$ and $C$ are dimensionless. State the expression and numerical values of $A$, $B$ and $C$.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

$$
A=\frac\alpha{\hbar c}=0.44
$$

$$
B=\frac{e^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0\hbar c}=7.31\times10^{-3}\quad\text{or}\quad\frac{q_pq_n}{4\pi\epsilon_0\hbar c}=0
$$

$$
C=\frac{Gm_p^2}{\hbar c}\quad\text{or}\quad\frac{Gm_nm_p}{\hbar c}=5.60\times10^{-39}\ \text{to}\ 6.00\times10^{-39}
$$


</Spoiler>
