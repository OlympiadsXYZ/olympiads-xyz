## Условие

A small circular hole of diameter $d$ is punched on the side and the near the bottom of a transparent cylinder of diameter $D$. The hole is initially sealed and the cylinder is filled with water of density $\rho_w$. It is then inverted onto a bucket filled to the brim with water. The seal is removed, air rushes in and height $h(t)$ of the water level (as measured from the surface level of the water in the bucket) is recorded at different times ($t$). The figure below and the table in part (c) illustrates this process. Assume that air is an incompressible fluid with density $\rho_a$ and its motion into the cylinder is a streamline flow. Thus its speed $v$ is related to the pressure difference $\Delta P$ by the Bernoulli relation. Take the outside pressure $P_0$ to be atmospheric pressure $=1.00\times10^5\ \mathrm{Pa}$.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

### (a)

at time $t$ from pressure balance equation

$$
P_0=p+h\rho_wg
$$

$$
\Delta P=P_0-p=h\rho_wg
$$

From Bernoulli’s principle

$$
\frac12v^2\rho_a=h\rho_wg=\Delta P
$$

$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{2\rho_wg}{\rho_a}}\sqrt h
$$

The speed of air gushing in through the small hole is related to speed $v_w$ of fall of water level by

$$
\frac{\pi d^2}4v=\frac{\pi D^2}4v_w\qquad (28)
$$

$$
v_w=\frac{d^2}{D^2}\sqrt{\frac{2\rho_wg}{\rho_a}}\sqrt h\qquad (29)
$$

### (b)

$$
v_w=-\frac{dh}{dt}=\frac{d^2}{D^2}\sqrt{\frac{2\rho_wg}{\rho_a}}\sqrt h
$$

$$
\sqrt h=\sqrt{h_0}-\frac{d^2}{D^2}\sqrt{\frac{\rho_wg}{2\rho_a}}\,t
$$

### (c)

The appended original handwritten graph sheet is retained in full.

**Native handwritten annotations:** The horizontal axis is $t$ (in sec), the vertical values are in $\sqrt{\mathrm{cm}}$, the marked origin is $(0,3.25)$, and the intercept is $4.78$. The vertical handwritten label uses $\sqrt{h_0}$ even though the plotted values vary with $t$.

Scale: On Xaxis $\to1\ \mathrm{cm}=0.33\ \mathrm{sec}$; Yaxis $\to1\ \mathrm{cm}=0.062\sqrt{\mathrm{cm}}$.

Marked coordinates: $(1.48,4.40)$ and $(4.42,3.63)$.

$$
\text{Slope}=\frac{4.40-3.63}{1.48-4.42}=-2.61\times10^{-1}\ \frac{\sqrt{\mathrm{cm}}}{\mathrm{sec}}
$$

$$
\Rightarrow\sqrt{h_0}=4.78
$$

$$
h_0=22.85\ \mathrm{cm}
$$
