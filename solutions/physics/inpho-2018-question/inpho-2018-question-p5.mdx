## Условие



**a)** Consider two short identical magnets each of mass $M$ and each of which maybe considered as point dipoles of magnetic moment $\vec\mu$. One of them is fixed to the floor with its magnetic moment pointing upwards and the other one is free and found to float in equilibrium at a height $z$ above the fixed dipole. The magnetic field due to a point dipole at a distance $r$ from it is

$$
\vec B(\vec r)=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi r^3}\left(3(\vec\mu\cdot\hat r)\hat r-\vec\mu\right)
$$

Obtain an expression for the magnitude of the dipole moment of the magnet in terms of $z$ and related quantities.

**Summary answer field:** $\vec\mu=$ [blank]. **[3 т.]**

**b)** **i. [1½ marks]** Consider a ring of mass $M_r$ rotating with uniform angular speed about its axis. A charge $q$ is smeared uniformly over it. Relate its angular momentum $\vec S_r$ to its magnetic moment ($\vec\mu_r$).

**Summary answer field:** $\vec\mu_r=$ [blank].

**ii. [1 mark]** Assume that the electron is a sphere of uniform charge density rotating about its diameter with constant angular speed. Also assume that the same relation as in the previous part holds between its angular momentum $\vec S$ and its magnetic dipole moment ($\vec\mu_B$). Further assume that $S=h/(2\pi)$ where $h$ is Planck’s constant. Calculate $\mu_B$.

**Summary answer field:** $\mu_B=$ [blank].

**iii. [3 marks]** Assume that the sole contribution to the dipole moment of a $\mathrm{ZnFe_2O_4}$ molecule comes from an unpaired electron. Also assume that the magnets in the part (5a) are $0.482\ \mathrm{kg}$ each of $\mathrm{ZnFe_2O_4}$ and the unpaired electrons of the molecules are all aligned. Calculate the height $z$. (Note: The molecular weight of $\mathrm{ZnFe_2O_4}=211$)

**Summary answer field:** $z=$ [blank].

**iv. [1½ marks]** In an experiment $\mu_B$ is aligned along a magnetic field of $1\ \mathrm T$. It is flipped in a direction anti-parallel to the magnetic field by an incident photon. What should be the wavelength of this photon?

**Summary answer field:** Wavelength = [blank].

Detailed answers can be found on page numbers: [blank]. **[7 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

### (a)

Given

$$
\vec B(\vec r)=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi r^3}\left[3(\vec\mu\cdot\hat r)\hat r-\vec\mu\right]
$$

$$
\hat r=\hat k,\quad r=z
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}\vec B(z)&=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi z^3}[3\mu\hat k-\mu\hat k]\\&=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi z^3}[2\mu\hat k]\end{aligned}
$$

$$
B(z)=\frac{\mu_0\mu}{2\pi z^3}
$$

The magnets are oppositely facing along $\hat z$. The force on the upper magnet due to the lower fixed magnet is

$$
\vec F=\frac{3\mu_0\mu^2}{2\pi z^4}
$$

The repulsive magnetic force is balanced by the weight acting down.

$$
Mg=\frac32\frac{\mu_0}{\pi z^4}\mu^2\qquad (16)
$$

$$
\mu^2=\frac23\frac{\pi z^4}{\mu_0}Mg
$$

$$
\mu=\left(\frac{Mg\,4\pi}{6\mu_0}\right)^{1/2}z^2\qquad (17)
$$

### (b) i.

Let radius of the ring to be $R$ and it is rotating with the angular speed $\omega\hat z$. Dipole moment can be expressed as

$$
\vec\mu_r=\text{current}\times\text{area}=\frac{q\omega}{2\pi}\pi R^2\hat z
$$

$$
\vec S_r=M_r\omega R^2\hat z
$$

$$
\Rightarrow\vec\mu_r=\frac q{2M_r}\vec S_r
$$

### (b) ii.

$$
\mu_B=\frac{e\hbar}{2m_e}=9.20\times10^{-24}\ \text{to}\ 9.30\times10^{-24}\ \mathrm{A\cdot m^2}
$$

### (b) iii.

The system (dipole) is made of $\mathrm{ZnFe_2O_4}$ and has one electron/molecule. The number of molecules is

$$
=\frac{\text{Mass of }\mathrm{ZnFe_2O_4}\text{ in gm}}{\text{Molecular weight of }\mathrm{ZnFe_2O_4}}\times N_A\qquad (18)
$$

$$
=\frac MA\times N_A\qquad (19)
$$

Assuming that each molecule of $\mathrm{ZnFe_2O_4}$ has one unpaired electron and each contributes to dipole moment. Further all of them are perfectly aligned. Hence dipole moment is

$$
\mu=\frac{MN_A\mu_B}A\qquad (20)
$$

Using Eq. (17)

$$
6.60\le z\ (\mathrm{cm})\le6.80
$$

### (b) iv.

$$
\text{Wavelength}=\frac{hc}{2\mu_BB}=1.00\ \text{to}\ 1.10\ \mathrm{cm}\qquad (21)
$$

**Source note:** The key writes a vector $\vec F$ equal to a scalar magnitude, and in Eq. (19) uses $M$ for mass in grams and $A$ for molecular weight without separately defining $A$; the question gives the magnet mass in kilograms. The preceding “in gm” wording, formulas and accepted ranges are preserved.
