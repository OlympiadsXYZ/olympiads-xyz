## Условие

An opaque sphere of radius $R$ lies on a horizontal plane. On the perpendicular through the point of contact, there is a point source of light at a distance $R$ above the top of the sphere (i.e. $3R$ from the plane).

**a)** Find the area of the shadow of the sphere on the plane.

**Summary answer field:** Area = [blank]. **[2 т.]**

**b)** A transparent liquid of refractive index $\sqrt3$ is filled above the plane such that the sphere is just covered with liquid. Find the area of the shadow of the sphere on the plane now.

**Summary answer field:** Area = [blank].

Detailed answers can be found on page numbers: [blank]. **[6 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

### (a)

[[figure:p2-key-shadow-air]]

MP is the radius $r$ of the shadow on ground. Let, $\angle\mathrm{MSP}=\theta$. Triangles OSQ and SMP are similar.

$$
\frac{\mathrm{MP}}{\mathrm{MS}}=\frac{\mathrm{OQ}}{\mathrm{SQ}}
$$

$$
\Rightarrow\frac r{3R}=\frac R{\sqrt{\mathrm{OS}^2-\mathrm{OQ}^2}}=\frac R{\mathrm{SQ}}=\frac R{R\sqrt3}
$$

$$
r=R\sqrt3
$$

Area of the shadow $=3\pi R^2$.

### (b)

[[figure:p2-key-shadow-liquid]]

Ray from $S$ is refracted at $N$. New radius of shadow is $\mathrm{MP'}=r'$. Also, $\angle\mathrm{N'NP'}=\theta$. In $\triangle\mathrm{NP'N'}$

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{\mathrm{NN'}}{\mathrm{NP'}}=\frac{2R}{r'+R\tan i}
$$

$$
r'+R\tan i=\frac{2R}{\cos\theta}=2R\sec\theta
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}r'&=\mathrm{P'N'}+\mathrm{N'M}\\&=2R\tan\theta+R\tan i\end{aligned}
$$

which yields

$$
\sec\theta-\tan\theta=\tan i
$$

$$
\Rightarrow2i=\frac\pi2-\theta
$$

$$
\Rightarrow\sin\theta=\frac{\sin i}{\sqrt3}=\frac13
$$

$$
r'=2R\tan\theta+R\tan i=2R\frac1{\sqrt8}+R\frac1{\sqrt2}=R\sqrt2
$$

Area of the shadow $=2\pi R^2$.

**Source note:** The native key prints the denominator $\mathrm{NP'}$ and $r'+R\tan i$ in the cosine equality, then $r'+R\tan i=2R\sec\theta$. These printed intermediate equalities do not agree with the later relations for $r'$, $\sec\theta-\tan\theta$ and the pictured geometry. All signs, symbols and the final answer remain as printed.
