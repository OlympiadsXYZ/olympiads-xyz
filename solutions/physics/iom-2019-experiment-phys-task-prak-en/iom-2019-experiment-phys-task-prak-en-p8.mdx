*The 4th International Olympiad of Metropolises — Carbon Dioxide Solubility in Water*

<details>

</details>

## Условие

Based on the data obtained and the results of previous experiments, calculate the change in vapor pressure in the flask $\Delta p_w$ due to its transfer from the box to the water bath. Write down basic formulas that should be used for this calculation.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 65.7 mmHg

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

*Exercise 2.2*

Change of vapor pressure in the flask: $\Delta p_v = 65.7$ mmHg

Formulas for calculating the change of vapor pressure in the flask:

$$
\Delta p_v = \Delta p_2 - \Delta p_1 - p_0\frac{t_2 - t_1}{T_0 + t_1}
$$


</Spoiler>
