*The 4th International Olympiad of Metropolises — Carbon Dioxide Solubility in Water*

<details>

</details>

## Условие

**Part 2: Solubility-Temperature Relationship**

Pour 30 ml of plain water into one of the flasks. Close the flask with a thermometer plug. Place the flask in water at room temperature. Wait a few minutes and connect a manometer to the flask. Wait until pressure in the flask stops changing. Write down the manometer reading and the water temperature in the box. Transfer the flask to the water bath. Completely immerse the flask in hot water by placing it inside a metal cylinder with holes, and secure it with the support stand. Wait until a thermal equilibrium is established; record the pressure in the flask and the water temperature in the water bath.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

*Exercise 2.1*

Temperature of water in the box: $t_1 = 23.9\ {}^{\mathrm{o}}\mathrm{C}$

Temperature of water in the water bath: $t_2 = 50.8\ {}^{\mathrm{o}}\mathrm{C}$

Manometer reading at room temperature: $\Delta p_1 =$ –0.2 mmHg

Manometer reading corresponding to the pressure in the flask placed in the water bath: $\Delta p_2 = 132.0$ mmHg


</Spoiler>
