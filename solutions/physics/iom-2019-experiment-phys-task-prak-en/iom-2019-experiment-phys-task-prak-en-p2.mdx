## Условие

Use a 20-ml syringe to measure the inner volume $v_0$ of the tube. Also measure the length $L$ of the tube. Calculate the cross-section area $s_0$ of the tube channel.

If additional pressure is created inside the plastic tube then the cross-section area of the tube channel changes. Suggest a method to reliably measure the relative change in cross-section area $\Delta s / s_0$ as a function of additional pressure $\Delta p$ in the tube. Explain the proposed method with the help of drawings similar to those shown in Fig. 1; make annotations to all the elements in your drawings. Measure the above relationship by obtaining at least 7 experimental points in the whole possible range of positive additional pressures in the tube. Draw a graph of the relationship obtained. Determine the slope of the graph.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- Slope of the graph: 8.3 mmHg⁻¹

</Spoiler>
