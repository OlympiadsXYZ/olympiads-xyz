*The 4th International Olympiad of Metropolises — Carbon Dioxide Solubility in Water*

## Условие

Using the carbon dioxide solubility at room temperature as the reference point, calculate the carbon dioxide solubility at the water bath temperature from the data obtained. Write down basic formulas used for such calculation. Does the solubility of carbon dioxide increase or decrease with increasing temperature?

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 0.5

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

*Exercise 2.5*

Solubility of gas at the water bath temperature: 0.5

Does the solubility rise or fall as the temperature rises? (Underline the correct answer)

| Rise | <u>Fall</u> |
|---|---|

Formulas for calculations:

$$
\frac{p_1V'}{RT_1} + \frac{\alpha_1 p_1 V_w'}{RT_0} = \frac{p_2V'}{RT_2} + \frac{\alpha_2 p_2 V_w'}{RT_0}
$$

$$
\alpha_2 = \frac{T_0}{p_2V_w'}\left(\frac{\alpha_1 p_1 V_w'}{T_0} + \frac{p_1V'}{T_1} - \frac{p_2V'}{T_2}\right)
$$
