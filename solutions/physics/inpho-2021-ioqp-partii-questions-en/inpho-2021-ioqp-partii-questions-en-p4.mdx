## Условие

Three thin convex lenses $\mathrm{L_1}$, $\mathrm{L_2}$, and $\mathrm{L_3}$ with focal lengths $f_1$, $f_2$, and $f_3$, respectively, are arranged in order ($\mathrm{L_1}$ followed by $\mathrm{L_2}$, followed by $\mathrm{L_3}$ from left to right) with their principal axes coincident. The distance $d_{12}$ between $\mathrm{L_1}$ and $\mathrm{L_2}$, and the distance $d_{23}$ between $\mathrm{L_2}$ and $\mathrm{L_3}$ are such that $d_{12} + d_{23} \geq f_1 + 4f_2 + f_3$. If a parallel beam of light incident on $\mathrm{L_1}$ at a small angle to the principal axis remains parallel to itself when leaving the system after passing through $\mathrm{L_2}$ and $\mathrm{L_3}$, draw the appropriate ray diagram and determine $d_{12}$ and $d_{23}$ in terms of $f_1$, $f_2$, and $f_3$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- $d_{12} = f_1 + f_2 + \frac{f_1 f_2}{f_3},\quad d_{23} = f_2 + f_3 + \frac{f_2 f_3}{f_1}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Solution:**

An incoming parallel beam falling on the thin lens $\mathrm{L_1}$ will converge to a certain point A on the focal plane of $\mathrm{L_1}$. The point A serves as the point source for $\mathrm{L_2}$ whose image is formed on the other side of $\mathrm{L_2}$ at a certain point B. The line AB must intersect the principal axis at the pole $\mathrm{O_2}$ of $\mathrm{L_2}$. For a parallel beam to emerge from $\mathrm{L_3}$, B must lie in the focal plane of $\mathrm{L_3}$. The necessary ray diagram is drawn below.

Since $\alpha \approx 0$, we make the approximations

$$\mathrm{AA_1} = f_1 \tan\alpha \approx f_1\alpha$$

$$\mathrm{BB_1} = f_2 \tan\alpha \approx f_2\alpha$$

From magnification formula for lens $\mathrm{L_2}$,

$$\frac{\mathrm{BB_1}}{\mathrm{AA_1}} = \frac{f_3\alpha}{f_1\alpha} = \frac{v_2}{-u_2} = \frac{d_{23} - f_3}{d_{12} - f_1}$$

$$\implies \frac{d_{12}}{f_1} = \frac{d_{23}}{f_3} = k(\text{say})$$

$$\implies d_{12} = kf_1 \quad \text{and} \quad d_{23} = kf_3$$

From the lens equation for lens $\mathrm{L_2}$,

$$\frac{1}{v_2} - \frac{1}{u_2} = \frac{1}{f_2}$$

$$\frac{1}{(d_{23} - f_3)} - \frac{1}{-(d_{12} - f_1)} = \frac{1}{f_2}$$

$$\frac{1}{f_3(k-1)} + \frac{1}{f_1(k-1)} = \frac{1}{f_2}$$

$$\implies \quad k = 1 + \frac{f_2}{f_1} + \frac{f_2}{f_3}$$

Then,

$$\boxed{d_{12} = f_1 + f_2 + \frac{f_1 f_2}{f_3}}$$

$$\boxed{d_{23} = f_2 + f_3 + \frac{f_2 f_3}{f_1}}$$


</Spoiler>
