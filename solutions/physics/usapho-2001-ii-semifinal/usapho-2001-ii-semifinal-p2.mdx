## Условие

Point charges $q_1$, $q_2$, and $Q$ are located at three corners of an $r_1$ by $r_2$ rectangle as shown in the diagram to the right.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '38%', '--fig-max': '244px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2001-ii-semifinal/p2-fig1.png" alt="Rectangle with q1 top left, Q top right, q2 bottom right and X bottom left; sides r1 and r2." width="761" height="529" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

**a.** Write a general expression for the electrostatic field at the point marked X in the diagram. Define any quantities you introduce. **[10 т.]**

**b.** For what values of $q_1$ and $q_2$ (expressed in terms of $r_1$, $r_2$, and $Q$) does the field at X vanish? **[5 т.]**

**c.** Write a general expression for the electrostatic potential at X and evaluate it for the charges found in Part b. Assume the potential vanishes an infinite distance from the charges. **[5 т.]**

**d.** How much total work is done by the electric force as a fourth charge $q_4$ moves from infinity to the point marked X? Assume $q_1$ and $q_2$ are the charges found in Part b. **[5 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-a">

### a. (10 т.)

We will write the fields $\vec E_1$, $\vec E_2$, and $\vec E_3$ due to $q_1$, $q_2$, and $Q$, respectively, in terms of the usual unit vectors $\hat i$ and $\hat j$. See accompanying diagram.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '41.8%', '--fig-max': '268px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2001-ii-semifinal/p2-sol-fig1.png" alt="Charge rectangle with unit vectors i and j, angle theta and field arrows E1,E2,E3." width="837" height="622" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


$$
\vec E_1=-\hat j k\frac{q_1}{r_1^2}
$$

where $k=$ Coulomb's constant

$$
\vec E_2=-\hat i k\frac{q_2}{r_2^2}
$$

$$
\vec E_3=-(\hat i\cos\theta+\hat j\sin\theta)k\frac Q{(r_1^2+r_2^2)}.
$$

Noting that $\cos\theta=\dfrac{r_2}{\sqrt{r_1^2+r_2^2}}$ and $\sin\theta=\dfrac{r_1}{\sqrt{r_1^2+r_2^2}}$, the total field becomes

$$
\vec E=-k\left(\frac{q_2}{r_2^2}+\frac{Qr_2}{(r_1^2+r_2^2)^{3/2}}\right)\hat i-k\left(\frac{q_1}{r_1^2}+\frac{Qr_1}{(r_1^2+r_2^2)^{3/2}}\right)\hat j.
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-b">

### b. (5 т.)

The field at X will vanish if each component vanishes:

$$
\left(\frac{q_2}{r_2^2}+\frac{Qr_2}{(r_1^2+r_2^2)^{3/2}}\right)=0\quad\text{or}\quad q_2=-\frac{Qr_2^3}{(r_1^2+r_2^2)^{3/2}}
$$

$$
\left(\frac{q_1}{r_1^2}+\frac{Qr_1}{(r_1^2+r_2^2)^{3/2}}\right)=0\quad\text{or}\quad q_1=-\frac{Qr_1^3}{(r_1^2+r_2^2)^{3/2}}
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-c">

### c. (5 т.)

The electrostatic potential at X is

$$
V=k\frac{q_1}{r_1}+k\frac{q_2}{r_2}+k\frac Q{(r_1^2+r_2^2)^{1/2}}.
$$

For the charges found in Part b

$$
V=-k\frac{Qr_1^2}{(r_1^2+r_2^2)^{3/2}}-k\frac{Qr_2^2}{(r_1^2+r_2^2)^{3/2}}+k\frac Q{(r_1^2+r_2^2)^{1/2}}=k\frac{Q(-r_1^2-r_2^2+r_1^2+r_2^2)}{(r_1^2+r_2^2)^{3/2}}=0
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-d">

### d. (5 т.)

The total work is done by the electric force as a fourth charge $q_4$ is moved from infinity to the point marked X is

$$
W=-\Delta U=-q_4\Delta V=-q_4(V_X-V_\infty)=-q_4(0-0)=0
$$

</ProblemSection>
