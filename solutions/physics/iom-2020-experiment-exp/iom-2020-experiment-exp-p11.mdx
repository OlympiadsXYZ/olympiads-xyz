**Q.11 e)** What wave, ordinary or extraordinary, is the second harmonic radiation for this crystal?

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **Q.11 a)** 354 - 355 nm
- **Q.11 b)** In vertical, qOr, rOq
- **Q.11 c)** In vertical , qOr, rOq
- **Q.11 d)** Extraordinary
- **Q.11 e)** Ordinary

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer 11 a) 354 - 355 nm

$\frac{\lambda_1}{3} = \frac{1064}{3} = 354.666\ldots$ нм

Answer 11 b) In vertical, qOr, rOq

Answer 11 c) In vertical , qOr, rOq
