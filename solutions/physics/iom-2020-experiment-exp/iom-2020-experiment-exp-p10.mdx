**Q.10 c)** In what plane does the first harmonic polarization lie? In the figure, indicate the direction.

**Q.10 d)** In what plane does the second harmonic polarization lie? In the figure, indicate the direction.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **Q.10 a)** 532 nm
- **Q.10 b)** In horizontal plane, qOp, pOq, …
- **Q.10 c)** qOr, rOq
- **Q.10 d)** qOp, pOq

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer 10 a) 532 нм

$\frac{\lambda_1}{2} = \frac{1064}{2} = 532$ нм

Answer 10 b) In horizontal plane, qOp, pOq, …

Answer 10 c) qOr, rOq

Vertical arrow, anywhere drawn

Answer 10 d) qOp, pOq

Horizontal arrow, anywhere drawn
