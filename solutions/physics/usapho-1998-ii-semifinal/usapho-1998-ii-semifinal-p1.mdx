## Условие

Two moles of monatomic ideal gas are taken through the reversible cycle shown on the $PV$ diagram below. The cycle forms a rectangle, starting from State A at $(V_o,P_o)$, to State B at $(V_o,2P_o)$, to State C at $(2V_o,2P_o)$, to State D at $(2V_o,P_o)$, and back to State A. Express all answers to the following questions in terms of $V_o$, $P_o$, and the ideal gas constant $R$.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '37.5%', '--fig-max': '240px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-1998-ii-semifinal/p1-fig1.png" alt="Clockwise rectangular pressure-volume cycle A, B, C, D between Vo and2Vo and Po and2Po." width="751" height="533" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

**a.** Calculate the net work done by the gas during the cycle. **[5 т.]**

**b.** Calculate the net heat transferred between the gas and the surroundings during one cycle. **[5 т.]**

**c.** Calculate cycle’s thermodynamic efficiency when operating as an engine. **[10 т.]**

**d.** Calculate the change in entropy of the gas as the system goes from State A to State D. **[5 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

$PV=nRT$, $c_v=\tfrac32R$.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '53.6%', '--fig-max': '343px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-1998-ii-semifinal/p1-fig2-v2.png" alt="Official-key PV cycle with segments1,2,3,4." width="1072" height="585" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-a">

### a. (5 т.)

$$
W=\int P\,dV=P_oV_o.
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-b">

### b. (5 т.)

$$
Q=\underbrace{\Delta U}_{0}+W=P_oV_o.
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-c">

### c. (10 т.)

$$
e=\frac{W}{Q_{in}}
$$

where $Q_{in}=$ heat put into system in cycle (all positive heat).

Segments 1 and 3: $V=\text{const}$

$$
Q=\Delta U+\underbrace{W}_{0}=nc_v\Delta T=\frac{c_v}{R}\Delta(PV)=\frac32V\Delta P
$$

$$
\text{Segment }Q_1=\frac32V_o(2P_o-P_o)=\frac32P_oV_o>0
$$

$$
Q_3=\frac32(2V_o)(P_o-2P_o)<0
$$

2 and 4: $P=\text{const}$.

$$
Q=\Delta U+W=\frac32V\Delta P+P\Delta V=\frac52P\Delta V
$$

$$
Q_2=\frac52(2P_o)(2V_o-V_o)=5P_oV_o
$$

$$
Q_4=\frac52P_o(V_o-2V_o)<0
$$

$$
\therefore Q_{in}=Q_1+Q_2=\frac{13}{2}P_oV_o
$$

and $e=2/13$.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-d">

### d. (5 т.)

$$
\Delta S=\int_A^D\frac{dQ}{T}=\int_A^D\frac{dU+P\,dV}{T}=\int_A^D\left[nc_v\frac{dT}{T}+nR\frac{dV}{V}\right]
$$

$$
=nc_v\ln\frac{T_D}{T_A}+nR\ln\frac{V_D}{V_A}
$$

$$
\frac{T_D}{T_A}=2=\frac{V_D}{V_A}
$$

$$
\Delta S=n(c_v+R)\ln2=5R\ln2.
$$

</ProblemSection>


</Spoiler>
