*EIGHTH BALKAN PHYSICS OLYMPIAD (BPO-8) · 29 юни 2026 г. · 25 т.*

## Условие

There is a planet without an atmosphere. The acceleration of free fall on its surface is $a = 10{,}0\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$. Two balls are thrown simultaneously from its surface from a point with Cartesian coordinates $(x, y) = (0{,}0)$ with one and the same speed $v_0$ ($x$ is the horizontal axis, $y$ is the vertical axis). The angles of throw for the first and second balls are respectively $\alpha$ and $\beta$. Two photos are made during their flight. The coordinates of the first ball on the first photo and on the second photo are respectively $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$.

**A)** Obtain a formula $y = f(x)$ for the trajectory of the first ball expressed in terms of the given quantities $x_1, y_1, x_2, y_2$. **[6 т.]**

**B)** If $(x_1, y_1) = (5{,}0\ \mathrm{m}, 9{,}0\ \mathrm{m})$ and $(x_2, y_2) = (15{,}0\ \mathrm{m}, 21{,}0\ \mathrm{m})$, find the values of $\alpha$ (or its trigonometric function) and $v_0$. **[3 т.]**

**C)** Obtain the formula for the horizontal distance $L$ of the flight (expressed in terms of $a$, $v_0$ and $\alpha$) of the first ball and calculate its value. **[3 т.]**

**D)** It turned out that the flight distance $L$ of the second ball is the same. Express $\beta$ through $\alpha$. **[2 т.]**

**E)** Calculate the moments $t_1$ and $t_2$ (after the throw) when the first and second photo were taken. **[3 т.]**

**F)** Calculate the coordinates $(x'_1, y'_1)$ and $(x'_2, y'_2)$ of the second ball on the first and second photo, respectively. **[5 т.]**

**G)** Calculate how long it took the first ball to fall after the second (the time delay $T$). **[3 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **A)** $y = \frac{y_1x_2^2 - y_2x_1^2}{x_1x_2(x_2 - x_1)}x - \frac{y_1x_2 - y_2x_1}{x_1x_2(x_2 - x_1)}x^2$
- **B)** tan α = 2, α ≈ 63.4°; v0 = 25,0 m/s
- **C)** 50 m
- **D)** $\beta = \frac{\pi}{2} - \alpha$
- **E)** t1 = 1/√5 s ≈ 0.45 s; t2 = 3/√5 s ≈ 1,34 s
- **F)** (x'1, y'1) = (10,0 m; 4,0 m); (x'2, y'2) = (30,0 m; 6,0 m)
- **G)** 2.2 s

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**A)** The motion of the first ball along the $x$-axis is uniform: $= v_0\cos\alpha\, t$. (1) **(0.5 p)** The motion of the first ball along the $y$-axis is with constant acceleration $a$: $y = v_0\sin\alpha\, t - \frac{1}{2}at^2$. (2) **(0.5 p)** Expressing $t$ from Eq. (1) and substituting the result in (2): $= \tan\alpha\, x - \frac{ax^2}{2{v_0}^2\cos^2\alpha}$. **(1.0 p)** (3) Lets $A = \tan\alpha$ and $= \frac{a}{2{v_0}^2\cos^2\alpha}$. Then (3) is transformed to $y = Ax - Bx^2$. (4) Substituting in (4) the two positions of the first ball on the photos: $y_1 = Ax_1 - B{x_1}^2$ (5) and $y_2 = Ax_2 - B{x_2}^2$ (6). Multiplying Eq. (5) by ${x_2}^2$ and Eq. (6) by ${x_1}^2$ and subtracting them, we obtain $y_1{x_2}^2 - y_2{x_1}^2 = Ax_1x_2(x_2 - x_1)$. Then $A = \frac{y_1{x_2}^2 - y_2{x_1}^2}{x_1x_2(x_2 - x_1)}$ (7). **(1.5 p)** Similarly, multiplying Eq. (5) by $x_2$ and Eq. (6) by $x_1$ and subtracting them, we obtain $y_1x_2 - y_2x_1 = Bx_1x_2(x_2 - x_1)$. Then $B = \frac{y_1x_2 - y_2x_1}{x_1x_2(x_2 - x_1)}$. **(1.5 p)** (8) Finally, the formula for the trajectory is $y = \frac{y_1{x_2}^2 - y_2{x_1}^2}{x_1x_2(x_2 - x_1)}x - \frac{y_1x_2 - y_2x_1}{x_1x_2(x_2 - x_1)}x^2$. (9) **(1.0 p)**

**B)** $\tan\alpha = A = \frac{y_1{x_2}^2 - y_2{x_1}^2}{x_1x_2(x_2 - x_1)} = \frac{9.15^2 - 21.5^2}{5.15(15 - 5)} = 2$. $\alpha \approx 63.4^{\circ}$. **(1.0 p)** As $\frac{1}{\cos^2\alpha} \equiv \tan^2\alpha + 1$, then $B = \frac{a}{2{v_0}^2}(A^2 + 1)$ and $v_0 = \sqrt{\frac{a(A^2+1)}{2B}}$. (10) $B = \frac{y_1x_2 - y_2x_1}{x_1x_2(x_2 - x_1)} = \frac{9.15 - 21.5}{5.15(15 - 5)} = 0.0040\ \mathrm{m^{-1}}$. Then using (10), $v_0 = \sqrt{\frac{10(2^2+1)}{2.0{,}04}} = 25{,}0\ \mathrm{m/s}$. **(2.0 p)**

**C)** From (3) it follows $0 = \tan\alpha\, L - \frac{aL^2}{2{v_0}^2\cos^2\alpha} = L\left(\tan\alpha - \frac{aL}{2{v_0}^2\cos^2\alpha}\right)$. **(0.5 p)** Then $L = \frac{2\tan\alpha\cos^2\alpha\, {v_0}^2}{a} = \frac{2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha\, {v_0}^2}{a}$. (11) **(0.5 p)** As $\tan\alpha = 2$, then $\cos\alpha = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}$ and $\sin\alpha = \frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}$. Substituting in (11), $L = \frac{2\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}25^2}{10} = 50.0\ \mathrm{m}$. **(2.0 p)**

**D)** From Eq. (11) it follows $= \frac{2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha\, {v_0}^2}{a} = \frac{\sin(2\alpha){v_0}^2}{a}$. **(0.5 p)** As $L(\alpha) = L(\beta)$, then $\sin(2\alpha) = \sin(2\beta)$, $2\beta = \pi - 2\alpha$, $\beta = \frac{\pi}{2} - \alpha$. (12) **(1.5 p)**

**E)** From (1) $t_1 = \frac{x_1}{v_0\cos\alpha} = \frac{5}{25.\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\ \mathrm{s} \approx 0.45\ \mathrm{s}$, **(1.5 p)** $t_2 = \frac{x_2}{v_0\cos\alpha} = \frac{15}{25.\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}} = \frac{3}{\sqrt{5}}\ \mathrm{s} \approx 1{,}34\ \mathrm{s}$. **(1.5 p)**

**F)** Calculate the coordinates $(x'_1, y'_1)$ and $(x'_2, y'_2)$ of the second ball on the first and second photo, respectively.

From (1) and (12) $x'_1 = v_0\cos\beta\, t_1 = v_0\sin\alpha\, t_1 = \frac{v_0\sin\alpha\, x_1}{v_0\cos\alpha} = \tan\alpha\, x_1 = 2.5 = 10{,}0\ \mathrm{m}$. **(1.0 p)**

From (2) and (12) $y'_1 = \tan\beta\, {x_1}' - \frac{a{x_1'}^2}{2{v_0}^2\cos^2\beta} = \frac{{x_1}'}{\tan\alpha} - \frac{a{x_1'}^2}{2{v_0}^2\sin^2\alpha} = \frac{10}{2} - \frac{10.10^2}{2.25^2\left(\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}\right)^2} = 4{,}0\ \mathrm{m}$. **(1.5 p)**

From (1) and (12) $x'_2 = v_0\cos\beta\, t_2 = v_0\sin\alpha\, t_2 = \frac{v_0\sin\alpha\, x_2}{v_0\cos\alpha} = \tan\alpha\, x_2 = 2.15 = 30{,}0\ \mathrm{m}$. **(1.0 p)**

From (2) and (12) $y'_2 = \tan\beta\, {x_2}' - \frac{a{x_2'}^2}{2{v_0}^2\cos^2\beta} = \frac{{x_2}'}{\tan\alpha} - \frac{a{x_2'}^2}{2{v_0}^2\sin^2\alpha} = \frac{30}{2} - \frac{10.30^2}{2.25^2\left(\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}\right)^2} = 6{,}0\ \mathrm{m}$. **(1.5 p)**

**g) (3 points)** Calculate how long it took for the first ball to fall after the second (the time delay $T$).

From (1) $T = \frac{L}{v_0\cos\alpha} - \frac{L}{v_0\cos\beta}$ $\mathbf{(0.5\ p)}$ $= \frac{L}{v_0\cos\alpha} - \frac{L}{v_0\sin\alpha} = \frac{L}{v_0}\left(\frac{1}{\cos\alpha} - \frac{1}{\sin\alpha}\right)$ $\mathbf{(1.0\ p)}$ $= \frac{50}{25}\left(\sqrt{5} - \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}\right) \approx 2{,}2\ \mathrm{s}$. **(1.5 p)**


</Spoiler>
