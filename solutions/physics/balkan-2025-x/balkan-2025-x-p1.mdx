*BPO7 Problems 26-30 June 2025, Ohrid · 26 юни 2025 г. · 25 т.*

## Условие

The famous Ohrid Swimming Marathon is an international open water marathon taking place in the waters of Ohrid Lake every year, usually in August. Professional swimmers from all over the world take part in this marathon, swimming the 25 km course. The swimmers start from the place "St. Naum" and finish at the Ohrid port, near the city center. The swimmers' achieved times are measured at three control points, the first is 13 km from the start near the village of Peshtani, the second is 19 km near the hotel "Metropol" and the third one is at the finish line, i.e. 25 km from the start.

**Ohrid Marathon 2024 - average swimming speed.** The table below shows the officially measured times at the three control points for five randomly selected swimmers in the men's competition at last year's Ohrid Marathon.

**c)** Is the winner of last year's marathon among the five swimmers in the table, if it is known that he swam the distance to the first checkpoint at an average speed of 1.15 m/s? Explain your answer. **[1 т.]**

At the Ohrid Swimming Marathon, each swimmer participating in the competition is joined by a team, which follows the swimmer by a boat.

**d)** The boats move at a certain distance from the competitors to ensure the smooth running of the competition. At a given moment, one of the swimmers stops and signals (by raising his hand) to his team to give him some water to drink. At that moment, the swimmer is at a distance $l$ from the second control point. After giving the signal, he starts swimming again with an acceleration $a_1$ towards the control point. At the moment when the swimmer gives the signal, the boat is at a distance $d$ relative to the swimmer's direction of motion, i.e. at a distance $d$ from the control point, as shown in the figure. When the team in the boat sees the signal, the boat starts moving from rest with acceleration $a_2$. Determine the minimum time $t_{\mathrm{min}}$, for which the boat with the team will reach the swimmer before the swimmer reaches the control point. Assume that the lake water is still. **[15 т.]**

**e)** Express the distances travelled by the boat and the swimmer to the meeting point in terms of the ratio of accelerations, $r = a_2/a_1$, $l$ and $d$. What is the remaining distance for the swimmer to reach the control point? **[2 т.]**

**f)** Derive a condition under which it is impossible for the boat to meet the swimmer before the control point? **[2 т.]**

**Note:** The solution of the quadratic equation of the form $ax^2+bx+c=0$ is given by $x_{1,2}=\dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **c)** No: 1.15 m/s (1.2 m/s) is not among the first-section speeds in Table 1, so the winner is not among the five swimmers.
- **d)** $t_{\min}=\sqrt{\frac{2la_1-2\sqrt{l^2a_1^2-\left(a_1^2-a_2^2\right)\left(l^2+d^2\right)}}{a_1^2-a_2^2}}$
- **e)** $l_1=\frac{l-\sqrt{l^2-\left(1-r^2\right)\left(l^2+d^2\right)}}{1-r^2},\quad l_2=\frac{lr-r\sqrt{l^2-\left(1-r^2\right)\left(l^2+d^2\right)}}{1-r^2},\quad l-l_1=\frac{\sqrt{l^2-\left(1-r^2\right)\left(l^2+d^2\right)}-lr^2}{1-r^2}$
- **f)** $r \lt \sin\alpha,\quad \sin^2\alpha=\frac{d^2}{l^2+d^2}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Solution:**

According to the definition, the average speed can be calculated when we divide the distance travelled $\Delta s$ with the total time $\Delta t$ needed to pass the distance, i.e.

$$
v_{av}=\frac{\Delta s}{\Delta t}
$$

a) We will solve this part by using the tabulated data to find the time needed for each swimmer to pass the distance between the control points, 13 km, 6 km и 6 km, correspondingly. Let us first convert the time in second. The results are given in the table below:

d) The boat will reach the swimmer before he/she gets to the control point in a least possible time if it moves along a straight line as it is shown in the following figure:

Correct sketch of the situation (**2.0 points**)

From the law of cosines, we have

$$
l_2^2=l_1^2+\left(l^2+d^2\right)-2l_1\sqrt{l^2+d^2}\cos\alpha
$$

**2.0 points** (1)

Moreover,

$$
\cos\alpha=\frac{l}{\sqrt{l^2+d^2}}
$$

**2.0 points**

and, thus, equation (1) becomes

$$
l_2^2=l_1^2+\left(l^2+d^2\right)-2l_1l
$$

**0.5 point** (2)

Since

$$
l_1=\frac{a_1t^2}{2},\ \ l_2=\frac{a_2t^2}{2},
$$

**2.0 points** (3)

for equation (2), we have

$$
\frac{a_2^2t^4}{4}=\frac{a_1^2t^4}{4}+\left(l^2+d^2\right)-la_1t^2,\quad \frac{\left(a_1^2-a_2^2\right)t^4}{4}+\left(l^2+d^2\right)-la_1t^2=0.
$$

**1.0 point** (4)

By substitution $y=t^2$, this biquadratic equation transforms to the following quadratic equation

$$
\frac{\left(a_1^2-a_2^2\right)y^2}{4}-la_1y+\left(l^2+d^2\right)=0.
$$

**1.0 point** (5)

which solutions are

$$
y_{1,2}=\frac{2la_1\pm2\sqrt{l^2a_1^2-\left(a_1^2-a_2^2\right)\left(l^2+d^2\right)}}{a_1^2-a_2^2}.
$$

**0.5 points** (6)

From here, in order to have a real solution (the discriminant to be positive or equal to zero), it should be satisfied

$$
l^2a_1^2-\left(a_1^2-a_2^2\right)\left(l^2+d^2\right)\ge0,\ \ \text{i.e.,}\ \ a_1^2d^2\le a_2^2\left(l^2+d^2\right).
$$

**2.0 points** (7)

Note that the Eq. (6) gives the solution for $t^2$, so the right hand side should be positive or equal to zero in order to get a real solution for the time. This will be satisfied for the solution with the minus sign in both cases, $a_1\le a_2,\ a_2\ge a_1$, So the required solution for the minimum time is $y_1$ (the one with the minus sign in (6)), i.e.

$$
t_{\min}=\sqrt{\frac{2la_1-2\sqrt{l^2a_1^2-\left(a_1^2-a_2^2\right)\left(l^2+d^2\right)}}{a_1^2-a_2^2}}.
$$

**2.0 points** (8)

e) Express the distances travelled by the boat and the swimmer to the meeting point in terms of the ratio of accelerations $r=a_2/a_1$, $l$ and $d$. What is the remaining distance for the swimmer to reach the control point? (**2 points**)

From (3) and (8) we find

$$
l_1=\frac{la_1^2-a_1\sqrt{l^2a_1^2-\left(a_1^2-a_2^2\right)\left(l^2+d^2\right)}}{a_1^2-a_2^2}=\frac{l-\sqrt{l^2-\left(1-r^2\right)\left(l^2+d^2\right)}}{1-r^2},
$$

**0.5 points**

$$
l_2=\frac{la_1a_2-a_2\sqrt{l^2a_1^2-\left(a_1^2-a_2^2\right)\left(l^2+d^2\right)}}{a_1^2-a_2^2}=\frac{lr-r\sqrt{l^2-\left(1-r^2\right)\left(l^2+d^2\right)}}{1-r^2},
$$

**0.5 points**

$$
l-l_1=\frac{a_1\sqrt{l^2a_1^2-\left(a_1^2-a_2^2\right)\left(l^2+d^2\right)}-la_2^2}{a_1^2-a_2^2}=\frac{\sqrt{l^2-\left(1-r^2\right)\left(l^2+d^2\right)}-lr^2}{1-r^2},
$$

**1.0 point**

where $r=a_2/a_1$.

f) Derive a condition under which it is impossible for the boat to meet the swimmer before the control point? (**2 points**)

If we analyze the condition (7), under which real solutions for the time exist, we can conclude that if this condition is not satisfied, then the equation (6) will have only imaginary solutions. So the required condition is opposite to condition (7), i.e.

$$
a_1^2d^2 \gt a_2^2\left(l^2+d^2\right)\Rightarrow\frac{a_2^2}{a_1^2} \lt \frac{d^2}{l^2+d^2}=\sin^2\alpha,
$$

**1.0 point**

$$
r^2 \lt \sin^2\alpha\Rightarrow r \lt \sin\alpha\ \left(0 \lt \alpha \lt \pi/2\right).
$$

**1.0 point**
