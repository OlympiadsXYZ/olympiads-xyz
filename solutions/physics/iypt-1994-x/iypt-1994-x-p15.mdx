*7th IYPT (1994)*

## Условие

Transmit without wires to a distance of 3 meters the largest possible part of the energy stored in a capacitor having capacity of $C=10\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ charged to voltage $U=100\,\mathrm{V}$. Measure this energy. Your device should not contain energy sources. Naturally the capacitor itself must not be transported.
