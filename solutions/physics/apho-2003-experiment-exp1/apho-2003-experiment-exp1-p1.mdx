*4th Asian Physics Olympiad — Experimental Competition — 25 April 2003. I. Determination of Capacitance · 10 т.*

## Условие

### Background

It is known that capacitors play a significant role in the electrical circuits. There are several methods of measurements of the capacitance of a capacitor. In this experiment you are required to perform the experiment in order to determine the capacitance of an AC capacitor using a simple electrical circuitry.

In Fig. 1.1 (a), a capacitor of capacitance $C$ and a resistor of resistance $R$ are connected in series to the alternating voltage source of mains frequency. The electrical power which is dissipated at the resistor $R$ depends on the values of $\varepsilon_0$, $C$, $R$ and frequency of the mains $f$. Graphical analysis of this relationship can be used to determine $C$.

### Materials and apparatus

1. capacitor
2. three resistors of known values with ±5% errors ( $R_A = 680\,\Omega$, $R_B = 1500\,\Omega$ and $R_C = 3300\,\Omega$ ) as shown in figure 1.1 (b).
3. step-down isolation transformer for alternating voltage source of $f = 50\ \mathrm{Hz}$
4. digital voltmeter
5. electrical connectors
6. linear graph papers

**Warning**: **The digital multimeter in this experiment will be used for measuring the rms voltage ($\tilde{V}$) across $R$ only. Do not use it to measure in other modes.**

**a)** Derive the expression for the average power dissipation $\overline{P}$ in resistor $R$ in terms of $\varepsilon_o$, $R$, $C$ and $\omega$. **[1 т.]**

**b)** Deduce the condition for which $\overline{P}$ is a maximum. **[1 т.]**

**c)** Convert the dependence found in a) into a linear dependence of certain quantities $\alpha$ and $\beta$. **[1 т.]**

**d)** Measure the root mean square (effective) voltage $V$ across $R$ for each of all possible combinations of $R_A$, $R_B$ and $R_C$. **[2,5 т.]**

**e)** Plot $\overline{P}$ versus $R$ and from this graph compute the value of capacitance $C$. **[2 т.]**

**f)** From c), draw the graph of $\alpha$ versus $\beta$ and determine capacitance $C$. **[2 т.]**

**g)** Estimate the uncertainties in the values of $C$ obtained in e) and f). **[0,5 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

a)

$$
\overline{P} = I^2 R = \frac{\tfrac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0^{\,2}}{R^2 + \left(\dfrac{1}{\omega C}\right)^2}\,R \qquad \text{(1.0 point)}
$$

b)

$$
\frac{d}{dR}\overline{P} = 0 \qquad \text{(0.3 point)}
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dR}\overline{P} = \frac{d}{dR}\left(\frac{\tfrac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0^{\,2}}{R^2 + \left(\dfrac{1}{\omega C}\right)^2}\,R\right) \qquad \text{(0.4 point)}
$$

$$
= \tfrac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0^{\,2}\,\frac{R^2 + \left(\dfrac{1}{\omega C}\right)^2 - R(2R)}{\left(R^2 + \left(\dfrac{1}{\omega C}\right)^2\right)^2}
$$

condition for $\overline{P}_{\max}$: $$R = \frac{1}{\omega C} \qquad \text{(0.3 point)}$$

c. ( 1 point)

$$
\overline{P} = \frac{\dfrac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0^{\,2}}{R^2 + \left(\dfrac{1}{\omega C}\right)^2}\,R = \frac{\dfrac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0^{\,2} R}{R^2\left[1 + \left(\dfrac{1}{R\omega C}\right)^2\right]} = \frac{\dfrac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0^{\,2}}{R\left[1 + \left(\dfrac{1}{R\omega C}\right)^2\right]}
$$

$$
\Rightarrow \frac{1}{R\overline{P}} = \frac{2}{\varepsilon_0^{\,2}}\left[1 + \frac{1}{R^2}\,\frac{1}{\omega^2 C^2}\right]
$$

$$
\frac{1}{R\overline{P}} = \frac{1}{V^2} = \frac{2}{\varepsilon_0^{\,2}} + \frac{2}{\varepsilon_0^{\,2}}\left(\frac{1}{\omega C}\right)^2 \frac{1}{R^2}
$$

Note: The linear graph will be $\dfrac{1}{R\overline{P}}$ or $\dfrac{1}{V^2}$ versus $\dfrac{1}{R^2}$. If $a$ is the slope and $b$ is the intercept with the Y axis, then

$$
\frac{1}{\omega^2 C^2} = \frac{a}{b} \quad\Rightarrow\quad C = \frac{1}{\omega}\sqrt{\frac{b}{a}}
$$

An alternative method:

$$
\frac{V^2}{R^2} = \frac{\tfrac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0^{\,2}}{R^2 + \left(\dfrac{1}{\omega C}\right)^2}
$$

$$
\frac{R^2}{V^2} = \left(R^2 + \left(\frac{1}{\omega C}\right)^2\right)\frac{2}{\varepsilon_0^{\,2}}
$$

$$
\frac{1}{V^2} = \left[1 + \left(\frac{1}{\omega C}\right)^2 \frac{1}{R^2}\right]\frac{2}{\varepsilon_0^{\,2}} = \frac{2}{\varepsilon_0^{\,2}} + \frac{2}{\varepsilon_0^{\,2}}\left(\frac{1}{\omega C}\right)^2 \frac{1}{R^2}
$$

$$
R^2 = \left(\frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0^{\,2}\right)\left(\frac{R}{V}\right)^2 - \left(\frac{1}{\omega C}\right)^2
$$

Note: The graph will be $R^2$ versus $\left(\dfrac{R}{V}\right)^2$ and $C$ is determined from the Y-intercept.

e) (1.5 points):good graph (0.5 point): correct value

$$
R \text{ at } \overline{P}_{\max} = 1600\,\Omega \Rightarrow C = \frac{1}{\omega R} = \frac{1}{2\pi \times 50 \times 1600} = 1.9 \times 10^{-6}\ \mathrm{F} = 1.9\,\mu\mathrm{F}
$$
