## Условие

Let’s consider a beam of electrons, which is propagating in a vacuum with a speed $\vec{V}$, electronic density $\rho$ and radius R.

<figure>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/balkanski-2008-cgp-en/p2-fig1.png" alt="Sketch of a cylindrical electron beam: red dots (electrons) with arrows pointing right inside a cylinder; above it an arrow labelled 'Sens de propagation'." />
</figure>

**1.)** Considering only the electromagnetic interactions, calculate the expression of the magnetic and electrostatic fields created by the electrons as a function of $\varepsilon_0$, $\mu_0$, $\rho$, R, $\vec{V}$ and r (the distance to the center of the beam). We can ignore the side effects and suppose that the beam has the form of an infinite cylinder.

**2.)** Making a balance of all forces try to find out the sense of evolution of the radius of the electronic beam in the time.

***Hint***: The speed of light in the vacuum is $c = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\varepsilon_0\times\mu_0}}$, with $\varepsilon_0 = 8{,}85.10^{-12}\ SI$, $\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}\ SI$
