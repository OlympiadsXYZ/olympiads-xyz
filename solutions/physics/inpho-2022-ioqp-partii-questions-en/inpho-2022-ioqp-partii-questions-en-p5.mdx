## Условие

A circuit consists of an emf source and five resistors with unknown resistances. When an ideal ammeter is connected between points 1 and 2, its reading is $I_A$. If instead a resistor $R$ is connected to the same two points, the current through that resistor is $I_R$. If instead an ideal voltmeter is connected between points 1 and 2, its reading is $V$. Obtain $V$ in terms of $I_A$, $R$ and $I_R$ only.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- $V = \frac{I_A I_R}{I_A - I_R} R$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Resistances, currents and emf are shown in the diagram. Imagine also there is a resistance $R_M$ between terminals 1 and 2. We can set later $R_M = 0$ for an ideal ammeter and $I_M = 0$ for an ideal voltmeter placed between two terminals. Applying Krichoff’s law

$$
\varepsilon = (I_1 + I_2)R_0 + I_1 R_1 + (I_1 - I_M)R_3 = (R_0 + R_1 + R_3)I_1 + I_2 R_0 - I_M R_3 \qquad (5.1)
$$

$$
= R_{013} I_1 + R_0 I_2 - R_3 I_M \qquad (5.2)
$$

$$
\varepsilon = (I_1 + I_2)R_0 + I_2 R_2 + (I_2 + I_M)R_4 = (R_0 + R_2 + R_4)I_2 + I_1 R_0 + I_M R_4 \qquad (5.3)
$$

$$
= R_{024} I_2 + R_0 I_1 + R_4 I_M \qquad (5.4)
$$

Solving above equations

$$
I_1 = \frac{\varepsilon R_{24} + I_M (R_0 R_{34} + R_3 R_{24})}{R_0 R_{1234} + R_{13} R_{24}} \quad \text{and} \quad I_2 = \frac{\varepsilon R_{13} - I_M (R_0 R_{34} + R_4 R_{13})}{R_0 R_{1234} + R_{13} R_{24}} \qquad (5.5)
$$

Potential difference between terminal 1 and 2 is

$$
V_{12} = I_2 R_2 - I_1 R_1 = \varepsilon A - I_M B \qquad (5.6)
$$

where the coefficients $A$ and $B$ depends only on resistances in the circuit. When an ideal ammeter is placed between terminals $V_{12} = 0$ and $I_M = I_A$.

$$
\varepsilon A = I_A B
$$

When a resistance $R$ is placed between terminals, $I_M = I_R$ and $V_{12} = I_R R$. This yields

$$
I_R R = \varepsilon A - I_R B = I_A B - I_R B \Rightarrow B = \frac{I_R R}{I_A - I_R} \qquad (5.7)
$$

When an ideal voltmeter is placed between terminals, $I_M = 0$. Hence $V_{12} = \varepsilon A - 0 = I_A B$ or

$$
V_{12} = \frac{I_A I_R}{I_A - I_R} R
$$
