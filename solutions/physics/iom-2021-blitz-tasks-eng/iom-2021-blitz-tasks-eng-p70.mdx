*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

Inside a given region in space there are two uniform fields: an electric field, with an intensity $E = 0.01$ V/m, and a magnetic field, with an intensity $B = 0.2$ T. These fields are placed in such a way that they are perpendicular to each other, just as shown in the figure. A charged particle $q$ enters the region with a constant velocity $v$, which is perpendicular to both fields. The particle goes through the region without its velocity being altered. Find the speed of the particle. Give answer in mm/s, rounded to integer.
