*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

The point charge $+q$ is located at a distance $L = 3R$ from the center of the metallic sphere of radius $R$. At what values of the positive charge $+Q$ of sphere the sphere and charge will attract?

- $\frac{Q}{q} < \frac{15}{184}$
- $\frac{Q}{q} < \frac{17}{192}$
- $\frac{Q}{q} < \frac{19}{191}$
- $\frac{Q}{q} < \frac{21}{197}$
- $\frac{Q}{q} < \frac{23}{214}$
