*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

A steady current $I = 0.2$ A is maintained in the conducting path $ACDE$. Find the strength of the magnetic field induction at $P$ located at the center of the circular arc $CD$ of radius $R = 2$ cm subtending an angle of $150^{\circ}$. The number should be given in microTesla and rounded to two decimal places.

## Отговори

## Решение
