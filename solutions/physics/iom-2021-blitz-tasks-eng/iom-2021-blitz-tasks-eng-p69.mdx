*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

A red substance consists of two elements. It is soluble in aqueous ammonia, concentrated hydrochloric acid, and concentrated nitric acid. In the latter case, a brown gas is released and a blue solution is formed. Determine the substance, write down its chemical formula.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- Cu2O

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Chemistry(18).** Answer: Cu2O
