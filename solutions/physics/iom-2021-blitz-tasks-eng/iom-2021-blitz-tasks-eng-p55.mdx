*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

Consider the reaction sequence.

$$
\mathrm{Cr_2O_3} \xrightarrow{1} \mathrm{CrCl_3(anhydrous)} \xrightarrow{2} \mathrm{Na_3[Cr(OH)_6]} \xrightarrow{3} \mathrm{Na_2CrO_4}
$$

Find the correspondence between the reaction number and the reaction conditions.

- $\mathrm{HCl/H_2O}$
- $\mathrm{NH_3/H_2O}$
- $\mathrm{NaOH(excess)/H_2O}$
- $\mathrm{Cl_2/NaOH}$
- $\mathrm{C,\ Cl_2},\ t$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 1 — C, Cl2, t; 2 — NaOH(excess)/H2O; 3 — Cl2/NaOH

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Chemistry(16).** Answer: 1 — C, $\mathrm{Cl_2}$, t

2 — $\mathrm{NaOH(excess)/H_2O}$

3 — $\mathrm{Cl_2/NaOH}$


</Spoiler>
