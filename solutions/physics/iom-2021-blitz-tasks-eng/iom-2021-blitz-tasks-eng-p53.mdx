*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

During the complete combustion of 50 L of a mixture of methane and ethane, 80 L of carbon dioxide were obtained. How many liters of oxygen have been consumed? Gas volumes are measured under the same conditions. Give the integer answer.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 145 L

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Chemistry(15).** Answer: 145


</Spoiler>
