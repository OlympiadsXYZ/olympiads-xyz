*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

The police stopped a driver and sent him for blood sampling. The analysis showed that $10.0$ mL of blood reacted with $2.0 \cdot 10^{-5}$ mol of potassium dichromate. Calculate the mass concentration of ethanol (g/L) in the blood. Assume that only ethanol reacts with $\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7}$ and the only oxidation product is acetic acid.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 0.138 g/L

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Chemistry(2).** Answer: 0.138


</Spoiler>
