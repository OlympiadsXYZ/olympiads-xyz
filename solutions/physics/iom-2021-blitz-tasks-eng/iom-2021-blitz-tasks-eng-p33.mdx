*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

A metallic equipment needs to be galvanized with a thin layer of silver on its surface. The equipment is put into a solution of sodium dicyanoargentate(I) and the electrolysis of the solution starts with the equipment attached to the cathode. How many seconds will it take to obtain the 20μm layer of silver with a current of 10 A and the surface area of the equipment of 50 cm$^2$? The density of silver is 10.49 g/cm$^3$, the Faraday constant is 96500 C/mol.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 93.7 s

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Chemistry(10).** Answer: 93.7


</Spoiler>
