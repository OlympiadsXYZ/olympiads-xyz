*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

The organic substance **X** is a sulfur analogue of the oxygen-containing compound **Y**. 5.50 g of **X** is burned in an excess of oxygen. As a result, 16.63 dm$^3$ of a gas mixture forms (400 K, 1 bar). When the mixture is cooled down to 20°C, a colorless, odorless liquid with the mass of 2.70 g condenses from this mixture. If the remaining gases are passed through a column with calcium hydroxide, a precipitate weighing 36.0 g forms. On air, the precipitate increases in weight by 0.80 g. Determine the formulas of **X** and **Y**. Write down the relative molecular mass of **Y** (as an integer number). Use the integer values of all atomic masses.
