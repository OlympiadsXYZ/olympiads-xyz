*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

What should be the speed of a car on an icy road (no friction) in a roundabout? The road is inclined at an angle of $5^{\circ}$ to the horizontal (towards the center of the roundabout). The diameter of the roundabout is $200$ m, $g = 10$ m/s$^2$. Give number in m/s, round the result to the first decimal.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 9.35 m/s

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Physics(14).** Answer: 9.3-9.4


</Spoiler>
