*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

A dielectric plate with dielectric permeability $\varepsilon = 1.5$ is placed in a uniform electric field so that its normal makes an angle $\alpha_0 = 30^{\circ}$ with electric field lines. Strength of the electric field outside the plate is equal to $E_0 = 1.0\ \frac{\mathrm{V}}{\mathrm{m}}$. Find the strength of the electric field inside the plate. Give your number in V/m, rounded to two decimal.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 0.76 V/m

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Physics(3).** Answer: 0.76


</Spoiler>
