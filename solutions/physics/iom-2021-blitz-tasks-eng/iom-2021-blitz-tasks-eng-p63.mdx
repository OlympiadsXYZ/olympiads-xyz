*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

Let $ABCD$ be a rectangle with area $1$ and let point $E$ lie on the side $CD$. What is the area of the triangle formed by the centroids of the triangles $ABE$, $BCE$ and $ADE$?

If the answer is a fraction, it can be entered either as a decimal fraction or as a ratio “$r/s$”. Rounding is not allowed.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- $\frac{1}{9}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Mathematics(16).** Answer: 1/9


</Spoiler>
