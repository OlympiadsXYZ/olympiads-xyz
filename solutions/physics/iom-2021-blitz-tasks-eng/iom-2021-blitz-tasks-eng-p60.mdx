*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

Peter observes an image of the Moon on a screen after the light passes through a convex lens. What is the diameter of the image on the screen if the focal length of the lens is $200$ mm? The apparent diameter of the Moon is $0.50^{\circ}$. Answer should be given in millimeters and rounded to the first decimal.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 1.7 mm

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Physics(15).** Answer: 1.7


</Spoiler>
