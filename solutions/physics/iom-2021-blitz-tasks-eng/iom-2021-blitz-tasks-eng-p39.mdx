*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

Determine the area of triangle $ABC$ if point $O$ is the origin of the coordinate system with basis unit vectors $\vec{i}$ and $\vec{j}$, the vector $\overrightarrow{OA} = -2\vec{i} + \vec{j}$, the vector $\overrightarrow{AB} = 5\vec{i} - 3\vec{j}$, the vector $\overrightarrow{AC}$ is parallel to the vector $\vec{i}$, and the scalar product $\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC} = 0$.

If the answer is a fraction, it can be entered either as a decimal fraction or as a ratio “$r/s$”. Rounding is not allowed.
