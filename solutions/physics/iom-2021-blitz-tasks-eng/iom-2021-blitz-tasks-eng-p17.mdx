*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

Unpolarized light of intensity $I_0$ is incident on a polarizer. The transmitted light is then incident on a second polarizer. The axis of the second polarizer makes an angle of 60° to the axis of the first polarizer.

An arrow labelled “unpolarized light” enters the first polarizer from the lower left." width="1329" height="905" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

What is the intensity of the light transmitted through the second polarizer?

- $I_0$
- $\frac{I_0}{2}$
- $\frac{I_0}{4}$
- $\frac{I_0}{8}$
- $\frac{I_0}{16}$
