*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

You have 2000 bottles of wine and exactly 1 of them is poisonous. You also have $x$ mice and you can make each of them drink from a subset of bottles. After 1 day if a mouse drinks from a poisonous bottle it dies. What is the smallest $x$ for which you can be sure you can find the poisonous bottle in 2 days.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 7

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Informatics(14).** Answer: 7


</Spoiler>
