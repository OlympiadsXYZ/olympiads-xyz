*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

$100$ students came to a camp for $7$ days. On each day, for each student, a bicycle was provided. Find the smallest possible number of bicycles in the camp, given that each bicycle has been used at most for $6$ days.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 117

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">
