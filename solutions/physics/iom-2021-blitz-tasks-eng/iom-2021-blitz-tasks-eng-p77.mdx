*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

How many different sets of values of boolean variables $x_1$, $x_2$, ..., $x_{10}$ satisfy all of the following conditions?

$(x_1 \to x_2) \to (x_3 \to x_4) = 1$

$(x_3 \to x_4) \to (x_5 \to x_6) = 1$

$(x_5 \to x_6) \to (x_7 \to x_8) = 1$

$(x_7 \to x_8) \to (x_9 \to x_{10}) = 1$

</Spoiler>
