*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2021*

## Условие

Numbers $x_1$ and $x_2$ are the roots of the equation $4x^2 - 10x - 3 = 0$. Find the value of the expression $x_1^2 + 2.5x_2$.

If the answer is a fraction, it can be entered either as a decimal fraction or as a ratio “$r/s$”. Rounding is not allowed.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 7

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**Mathematics(9).** Answer: 7
