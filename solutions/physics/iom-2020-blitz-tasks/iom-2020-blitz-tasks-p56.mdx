*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2020*

## Условие

Two mols of iron were dissolved in hot 40% $HNO_3$ and the mixture of $NO$ and $NO_2$ with a density equal to that of fluorine was released. How many mols of $HNO_3$ were consumed? Give the integer value.
