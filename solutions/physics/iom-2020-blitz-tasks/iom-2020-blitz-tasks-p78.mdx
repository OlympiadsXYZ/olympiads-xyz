## Условие

The voltmeter connected in parallel to resistor $R_1 = 4$ kOhm show a reading of $U_v = 36$ V. A source with terminals of constant potential difference of $U = 100$ V is connected to the circuit. Find the ratio of the currents flowing through voltmeter and resistor $R_2 = 6$ kOhm.
