*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2020*

## Условие

Calculate $f^{2020}(2020)$, where function $f\colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfies

$(x - 1) \cdot f(x) + f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = \frac{1}{x-1}$

for all real $x \ne 0, 1$.

(Here $f^{2020}$ denotes the $2020$th application of $f$, i.e., $\underbrace{f(f(\dots(f(x))\dots))}_{2020}$, not numerical power.)

Fractional numbers can be entered either as a decimal fraction or as $m/n$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- $-\frac{1}{2019}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Warning title="Непълно решение">
The source contains only the answer key, no worked solution.
</Warning>

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: -1/2019


</Spoiler>
