*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2020*

## Условие

An adult absorbs about 8000 kJ of energy per 1 day from food. Half of it is used for ATP production. Given that the Gibbs energy of ATP hydrolysis is -52 kJ/mol and the molar mass of ATP is 503 g/mol, calculate the mass of ATP (in kg) produced in the human body every day. Round the answer to the nearest integer.
