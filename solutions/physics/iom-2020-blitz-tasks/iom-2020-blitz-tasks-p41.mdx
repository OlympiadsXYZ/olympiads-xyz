## Условие

Hydrogen gas can dissolve in some transition metals such as palladium at room temperature. Palladium has the face-centered cubic crystal structure with the lattice parameter 389 pm. The radius of hydrogen atom is 54 pm. What is the maximum number of hydrogen atoms that can fit in a unit cell of metal palladium?
