*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2020*

## Условие

Anhydrous salt $X$ is a white powder, which gives a white precipitate when it gets into water, but easily dissolves in dilute nitric acid without gas evolution. On strong heating, $X$ decomposes with the release of brown gas, leaving no solid residue. When solution $X$ is exposed to a solution of potassium iodide, a red precipitate is formed, which dissolves in the excess of the reagent to form a pale yellow solution. Determine $X$. Write down its molar mass (in g/mol) rounded to the nearest integer.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 325 g/mol

</Spoiler>
