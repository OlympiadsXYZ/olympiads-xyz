*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2020*

## Условие

A particle is projected with the speed 40 m/s from a point on horizontal ground, in the direction making an angle of $\alpha$ above the horizontal. The particle reaches the ground again after 4 s. Find the horizontal distance travelled by the particle.

$g = 10$ m/s$^2$.

*The answer should be given in the SI units unless otherwise stated. If the numerical result turns out to be an integer, it should be given as the answer. Otherwise round the result to the first decimal.*

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 138.6 m

</Spoiler>
