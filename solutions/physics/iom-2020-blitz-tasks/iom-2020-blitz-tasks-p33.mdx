*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2020*

## Условие

Let $a$ and $b$ be real numbers such that $\frac{ab}{a^2+b^2} = \frac{1}{6}$.

Find the value of $\left(\frac{a^2-b^2}{a^2+b^2}\right)^2$.

Fractional numbers can be entered either as a decimal fraction or as $m/n$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- $\frac{8}{9}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Warning title="Непълно решение">
The source contains only the answer key, no worked solution.
</Warning>

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 8/9


</Spoiler>
