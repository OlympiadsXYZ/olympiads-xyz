*The Blitz-contest of IOM-2020*

## Условие

The stability constant of $CdL^{4-}$ at 298 K is $a\cdot10^b$ ($a$ and $b$ are integers, $1 \le a < 10$). Given the standard potentials $E^{\circ}(Cd^{2+}/Cd) = -0{,}403$ V, $E^{\circ}(CdL^{4-}/Cd) = -0{,}958$ V, determine $a$ and $b$.

Write $a$ in the first field, and $b$ in the second.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 6;18

  Answer key: 6;18 — that is, $a = 6$, $b = 18$.

</Spoiler>
