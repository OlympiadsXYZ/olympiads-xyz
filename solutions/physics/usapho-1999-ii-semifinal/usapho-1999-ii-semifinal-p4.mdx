*1999 Semi-Final Exam*

## Условие

A pendulum bob is constructed by taking a thin, uniform-density circular ring of mass $M$ and radius $R$ and affixing a straight, thin, uniform density rod of mass $m$ and length $2R$ across its diameter as shown in the diagram. The pendulum hangs in a vertical plane from a frictionless pivot that can be attached to the ring at any point. The pivot allows the bob to swing either in the plane of the bob or in the plane perpendicular to the bob. Assume the angular amplitude is small.

<figure>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-1999-ii-semifinal/p4-fig1.png" alt="A circular ring labelled M with a rod labelled m along a diameter; an arrow labelled R from the centre to the ring." />
</figure>

**a.** What swinging configurations give the maximum period? **[5 т.]**

**b.** What swinging configurations give the minimum period? **[5 т.]**

**c.** Find the ratio of the maximum period to the minimum period. **[10 т.]**

*(contributed by Leaf Turner)*

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a.** Swinging in the plane of the bob.
- **b.** Swinging perpendicular to the plane of the bob with the rod horizontal.
- **c.** $\frac{T_{\max}}{T_{\min}} = \sqrt{4/3}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The only torque acting on the system is due to the gravitational force.

$$
I\alpha = \sum\tau = \left(M + m\right)gR\sin\theta
$$

For small angles

$$
\alpha \cong \left(\frac{\left(M + m\right)gR}{I}\right)\theta = \omega^2\theta
$$

We will use the parallel axis theorem to find the moment of inertia of the system. Swinging in the plane of the bob, pivot on rim

$$
I_{tot} = I_{ringcm} + MR^2 + I_{rodcm} + mR^2
$$

$$
= MR^2 + MR^2 + \tfrac{1}{12}m\left(2R\right)^2 + mR^2 = \left(2M + \tfrac{4}{3}m\right)R^2
$$

Swinging perpendicular to the bob, we expect the extreme cases to be rod horizontal and rod vertical

vertical: $I_{rod} = \tfrac{4}{3}mR^2$ horizontal: $I_{rod} = mR^2$

For the ring:

$$
I_{ring} = I_{ringcm} + MR^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}MR^2 + MR^2 = \tfrac{3}{2}MR^2
$$

vertical:

$$
I_{vtot} = \tfrac{3}{2}MR^2 + \tfrac{4}{3}mR^2 = \left(\tfrac{3}{2}M + \tfrac{4}{3}m\right)R^2
$$

horizontal:

$$
I_{htot} = \tfrac{3}{2}MR^2 + mR^2 = \left(\tfrac{3}{2}M + m\right)R^2
$$

The maximum period occurs when ω is smallest or $I$ is largest. Since $I_{tot} \ge I_{vtot} \ge I_{htot}$ the maximum period occurs when it swings in the plane of the bob. The minimum period occurs when it swings perpendicular to the plane of the bob and the wire is horizontal.

$$
\frac{T_{\max}}{T_{\min}} = \sqrt{\frac{I_{\max}}{I_{\min}}} = \sqrt{\frac{2M + \tfrac{4}{3}m}{\tfrac{3}{2}M + m}} = \sqrt{\tfrac{4}{3}}
$$


</Spoiler>
