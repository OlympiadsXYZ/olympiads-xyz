<details>
<summary>Porous membrane</summary>

You are going to study a membrane of anodic aluminum oxide. The membrane is transparent; it has thickness $h$ and cylindrical channels of diameter $d$ as shown in figure. Your goal is to determine parameters $h$, $d$ and porosity $p$ (the volume fraction of channels in the membrane). Assume the membrane to be optically homogeneous and please **do not touch** the surface of the membrane!

</details>

## Условие

**Task** Using results from previous parts and making additional measurements if necessary, estimate the diameter $d$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 6 nm

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

In this part, we can determine diameter of pores, using results, obtained in previous parts:

$$
\tau = 1204 \pm 10
$$

$$
h = 74.6 \pm 0.2\ \text{мкм}
$$

$$
p = 13.5 \pm 0.5\%
$$

In part A we derived the equation

$$
\tau = \frac{3Vh}{pS_0 vd}
$$

It yields

$$
d = \frac{3Vh}{pS_0\,v\tau}
$$

Where $v$ is thermal velocity of $CO_2$ ($T \simeq 300K$, more accuracy isn't needed):

$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{8RT}{\pi\mu}} = 376\ m/s
$$

$V$ is volume of the cylindrical vessel, it can be measured ruler:

$$
V = \pi\frac{D_{in}^2}{4}L
$$

$$
L = 5.0\ cm
$$

$$
D_{in} = 7.4\ cm
$$

$$
V = 215 cm^3
$$

Numerical answer is:

$$
d = 6.0 \pm 0.5\ nm
$$

Here you can see the surface image of the membrane studied in this experimental problem. This image was obtained with scanning electron microscope (SEM). Average diameter of channels is about $15\ nm$. The reason of difference between these experimental results is that diffusion inside pores is not the only limiting stage even with two fans are turned on. Moreover, some fraction of channels is branched and terminated (A.A. Noyan et al. / Electrochimica Acta 226 (2017) 60–68).
