*Experimental competition. Thursday, 17 July 2014 — Experiment. To see invisible! (20 points) · 17 юли 2014 г. · 4,5 т.*

## Условие

**Light transmission through LCC**

Place the LCC between the polarizers as shown in Fig. 6B.

***The experimental dependence under investigation is strongly nonmonotonic with domains of quite abrupt changes. Take this into account when taking measurements.***  
***To measure the AC voltage of the cell power supply and the DC voltage of the photodetector, connect the appropriate leads directly to the multimeter.***

**2.3.1** Measure the intensity of the transmitted light as a function of the voltage across the LCC. Plot the corresponding graph. (2.0 points)

**2.3.2** Calculate the phase difference between the ordinary and extraordinary waves $\Delta\varphi_0$ when the power supply is disconnected with the LCC. (1.5 points)

**2.3.3** In a sufficiently wide range of the voltage drop across the LCC, the phase difference between the ordinary and extraordinary waves depends on the voltage applied by the power law

$$
\Delta\varphi = CU^{\beta}.
$$

Using the data obtained, plot the graph which allows one to determine the applicability range of the above formula and calculate the exponent $\beta$. Specify that range of applicability and evaluate the numerical value of the parameter $\beta$. (1.0 point)

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **2.3.2** 10.6 rad
- **2.3.3** 1.75

</Spoiler>

2.3.2 The value of the phase shift at zero voltage is $\Delta\varphi_0 \approx 10.6$.

2.3.3 In order to check applicability of the power function $\Delta\varphi = CU^{\beta}$ it is recommended to redraw the last graph logarithmically, as shown in the figure below.

It can be seen from the graph that in the range of 1 V to 5 V the function is almost linear, which justifies the applicability of the power law. The power in that equation is equal to the slope of the graph, its numerical value is $\beta \approx 1.75$.
