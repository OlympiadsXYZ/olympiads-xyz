*ORANGE — 38th International Physics Olympiad, Isfahan-Iran, July 2007*

## Условие

**3** Now assume that the switch is in state $\beta$ i.e. the movable plate is connected to the ground through a capacitor, the capacitance of which is $C_S$ (there is no initial charge on the capacitors). If the movable plate is displaced by an amount $x$ from its central position,

**3.1** Find $V_S$ the electrical potential difference across the capacitor $C_S$ as a function of $x$. **[1,5 т.]**

**3.2** Again assume that $d \gg x$ and ignore terms of order $x^2$ compared to terms of order $d^2$. Simplify your answer to the previous part. **[0,2 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **3.1** $V_S = V\,\frac{\frac{2\varepsilon_0 A x}{d^2 - x^2}}{C_S + \frac{2\varepsilon_0 A d}{d^2 - x^2}}$
- **3.2** $V_S = V\,\frac{2\varepsilon_0 A x}{d^2 C_S + 2\varepsilon_0 A d}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**3.1)**

Starting with Kirchhoff’s laws, for two electrical circuits, we have

$$
\begin{cases} \dfrac{Q_S}{C_S} + V - \dfrac{Q_2}{C_2} = 0 \\[2ex] -\dfrac{Q_S}{C_S} + V - \dfrac{Q_1}{C_1} = 0 \\[2ex] Q_2 - Q_1 + Q_S = 0 \end{cases}
$$

(Each has (0.3), Note: the sings may depend on the specific choice made)

Noting that $V_s = \frac{Q_S}{C_S}$ one obtains

$$
\Rightarrow V_S = V\,\frac{\dfrac{2\varepsilon_0 A\,x}{d^2 - x^2}}{C_S + \dfrac{2\varepsilon_0 A\,d}{d^2 - x^2}}.
$$

((0.4) + (0.2): (0.4) for solving the above equations and (0.2) for final result)

Note: Students may simplify the above relation using the approximation $d^2 \gg x^2$. It does not matter in this section.

---

**3.2)**

Ignoring terms of order $x^2$ in the answer to 3.1., we get

$$
V_S = V\,\frac{2\varepsilon_0 A\,x}{d^2 C_S + 2\varepsilon_0 A\,d}.
$$

(0.2)


</Spoiler>
