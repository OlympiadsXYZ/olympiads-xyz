*5th Balkan Physics Olympiad, BPO 5 - Albania, 24 June 2023, Durrës, ALBANIA — Problems · 24 юни 2023 г.*

## Условие

**A.** A conservative variable force $F$ is slowly pulling a body of weight $W$ as illustrated in the figure, along a smooth semi-sphere with a radius $R$. The force acts tangential to the semi-sphere. When the body is in the position A the spring has its natural length.

Calculate the work done by $F$, to move the body from А to Г. **[10 т.]**

**B.** For a particular horizontal spring, the intensity of the elastic force, depends on the deformation $x$ as follows: $F(x) = \alpha \cdot x + \beta$, where $\alpha = 50\ \mathrm{N/m}$ and $\beta = 10\ \mathrm{N}$. **[15 т.]**

**B1.** Calculate the potential energy function $U(x)$ for this spring. Assume that $U(0) = 0$. **[5 т.]**

**B2.** An object of mass 2 kg is attached to the end of this spring and it’s elongated with 1.5 m on a smooth horizontal surface and then released. Determine the speed of the object when the elongation is 1.0 m for the first time. **[10 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **A.** $\Delta U = mgR\sin\theta + \frac{1}{2}K(R\theta)^2$
- **B1.** $U(x) = \beta x + 0.5\alpha x^2$
- **B2.** 6 m/s

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**A. Solution:**

In position A where the spring has its natural length we considered that the gravitational potential energy and the potential energy of the spring is zero.

The body moves very slowly. Consequently its kinetic energy does not change. **(2 p)**

If x is the strain of the spring at position Γ, then, for the spring-body system we have:

$\Delta U = U_2 - U_1 = U_g + U_s - 0 = mg\ R\sin\theta + 1/2\ K\ (R\ \theta)^2$ **(4 p)**

$U_g = mgh = mg\ (R\sin\theta)$ is the gravitational potential energy at position Γ **(2 p)**

$U_s = 1/2\ K\ x^2 = 1/2\ K\ (R\theta)^2$. the potential energy of the spring at position Γ **(2 p)**

**B1. Solution:**

$$
W = -(U_2 - U_1)
$$
**(1 p)**

By plotting the force F versus position x and calculating the area of the trapezoid formed from $x = 0$ to position x we will find

$$
W = -\beta x - 0.5\alpha\, x^2,
$$
**(2 p)**

since at $x = 0$, $U_1 = 0$ **(1 p)**

then

$$
U(x) = \beta x + 0.5\alpha\, x^2.
$$
**(1 p)**

**B2. Solution:**

We apply the principle of conservation of mechanical energy for the two positions $x_1 = 1.5$ m and $x_2 = 1.0$ m of the object and we have

$$
K_1 + U_1 = K_2 + U_2 \quad (1)
$$
**(4 p)**

$$
K_1 = 0 \text{ and } K_2 = 1/2\ m\ v^2 \quad (2)
$$
**(3 p)**

For x=1.5 m

$$
U_1 = \beta x + 0.5\alpha\, x^2 = 71{,}25\ J \qquad (3)
$$
**(0.5 p)**

For x=1.0 m

$$
U_2 = 35\ J \qquad (4)
$$
**(0.5 p)**

Hence

$$
v = 6.0\ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}.
$$
**(2 p)**
