*5th Balkan Physics Olympiad, BPO 5 - Albania, 24 June 2023, Durrës, ALBANIA — Problems · 24 юни 2023 г.*

## Условие



**A.** **Warming up.**

During warm up, a tennis ball (A) is dropped from Novak Djokovic’s pocket at a height *H*. At the same time, a grasshopper (B) just underneath Novak’s pocket, jumps from the ground, vertically towards the ball with initial velocity $v_0$. When they collide, the ball has twice the speed of the grasshopper. The collision occurs at height *h*. **[15 т.]**

**A1.** Write down the equation of motion for the ball, $y_A$ as a function of time, in terms of *H*, $v_0$ and gravitational acceleration *g*. **[1 т.]**

**A2.** Write down the equation of motion for the grasshopper, $y_B$ as a function of time, in terms of $v_0$ and *g*. **[1 т.]**

**A3.** Derive the expression for the velocity of the ball $v_A(t)$ in terms of *H*, *g* and $y_A(t)$. **[3 т.]**

**A4.** Derive the expression for the velocity of the grasshopper $v_B(t)$ in terms of $v_0$, *g* and $y_B(t)$. **[1 т.]**

**A5.** Derive the expression for the initial velocity $v_0$ of the grasshopper in terms of *h*, *H* and *g*. **[3 т.]**

**A6.** Derive the expression for the moment of the collision $t_c$ in terms of $v_0$ and *g*. **[3 т.]**

**A7.** Calculate the numerical value of the ratio $h/H$. **[3 т.]**

**B.** **Match**

During match, at serve, Novak aims to hit tennis ball horizontally. **[10 т.]**

**B1.** Write down the expression for the equation of motion of the ball in the horizontal direction $x(t)$. **[1 т.]**

**B2.** Write down the expression for the equation of motion of the ball in the vertical direction $y(t)$. **[1 т.]**

**B3.** Calculate the numerical value of the minimal initial velocity $v_i$ required for the ball to pass just above the 0.9 m high net, 15 m in front of Novak, if the ball is launched (horizontally) from a height of 2.5 m. **[3 т.]**

**B4.** Where will the ball land in the case given under B3? **[4 т.]**

**B5.** How long will the ball be in the air before it lands in the case given under B3? **[1 т.]**

*The gravitational acceleration is* $g = 9.8\ m/s^2$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **A1.** $y_{\mathrm{A}}(t) = H - \frac{1}{2}gt^2$
- **A2.** $y_{\mathrm{B}}(t) = v_0 t - \frac{1}{2}gt^2$
- **A3.** $v_A(t) = \sqrt{2g(H - y_{\mathrm{A}}(t))}$
- **A4.** $v_B(t) = \sqrt{v_0^2 - 2gy_B(t)}$
- **A5.** $v_0^2 = \frac{1}{2}g(H + 3h)$
- **A6.** $t_C = \frac{2v_0}{3g}$
- **A7.** $\frac{h}{H} = \frac{2}{3}$
- **B1.** $x(t) = v_i t$
- **B2.** $y(t) = y_0 - \frac{1}{2}gt^2$
- **B3.** 26.3 m/s
- **B4.** 18.8 m
- **B5.** 0.71 s

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**A. Warming up**

During warm up, a tennis ball (A) is dropped from rest from Novak Djokovic pocket of height *H*. At the same instant of time grasshopper (B) stands on the ground below Novak’s pocket and starts to move vertically towards the ball with initial velocity $v_0$. When they collide, the ball has twice the speed of the grasshopper. The collision occurs at height *h*.

**A1.**

$$
y_{\mathrm{A}}(t) = H - \frac{1}{2}gt^2.
$$

**A2.**

$$
y_{\mathrm{B}}(t) = v_0 t - \frac{1}{2}gt^2.
$$

**A3.**

$$
v_A(t) = -gt \;\Rightarrow\; t = \left|-\frac{v_A(t)}{g}\right| = \frac{v_A(t)}{g},
$$

$$
\Rightarrow y_{\mathrm{A}}(t) = H - \frac{1}{2}g\left(\frac{v_A(t)}{g}\right)^2,
$$

$$
\Rightarrow v_A^2(\mathrm{t}) = 2g(H - y_{\mathrm{A}}(t)) \;\Rightarrow\; v_B(t) = \sqrt{2g(H - y_{\mathrm{A}}(t))}.
$$

**A4.**

$$
v_B^2(\mathrm{t}) = v_0^2 - 2gy_B(t) \;\Rightarrow\; v_B(t) = \sqrt{v_0^2 - 2gy_B(t)}.
$$

**A5.**

Using condition $v_A(t_C) = 2v_B(t_C)$ at the moment of collision $t_C$ and $y_{\mathrm{A}}(t_C) = y_{\mathrm{B}}(t_C) = h$ we can write

$$
2g(H - h) = 4v_0^2 - 8gh,
$$

$$
\Rightarrow v_0^2 = \frac{1}{2}g(H + 3h).
$$

**A6.**

Using again condition $|v_A(t_C)| = 2v_B(t_C)$ we can write

$$
gt_C = 2v_0 - 2gt_C,
$$

$$
\Rightarrow t_C = \frac{2v_0}{3g}.
$$

**A7.**

$$
H = v_0 t_C = v_0\frac{2v_0}{3g} = \frac{2}{3g}v_0^2 = \frac{2}{3g}\frac{1}{2}g(H + 3h),
$$

$$
\Rightarrow \frac{h}{H} = \frac{2}{3}.
$$

**B. Match**

During match, at serve, Novak aims to hit tennis ball horizontally.

**B1.**

$$
x(t) = v_i t.
$$

**B2.**

$$
y(t) = \mathrm{y}_0 - \frac{1}{2}gt^2.
$$

**B3.**

For $x_1(t_1) = 15\ m$, $\mathrm{y}_1(t_1) = 0.9\ m$ and $\mathrm{y}_0 = 2.5\ m$, where $t_1$ is instant of time when the ball passed just above the net, we can write

$$
x_1(t_1) = v_i t_1 \;\Rightarrow\; v_i = \frac{x_1(t_1)}{t_1},
$$

$$
\mathrm{y}_1(t_1) = \mathrm{y}_0 - \frac{1}{2}gt_1^2 \;\Rightarrow\; t_1 = \sqrt{\frac{2}{g}(\mathrm{y}_0 - \mathrm{y}_1(t_1))},
$$

$$
\Rightarrow v_i = x_1(t_1)\sqrt{\frac{g}{2(\mathrm{y}_0 - \mathrm{y}_1(t_1))}} = 26.3\ \frac{m}{s}.
$$

**B4.**

For $x_2(t_2) = v_i t_2$ and $\mathrm{y}_2(t_2) = \mathrm{y}_0 - \frac{1}{2}gt_2^2 = 0$, where $t_2$ is instant of time when the ball lands, we can write

$$
t_2 = \sqrt{\frac{2}{g}\mathrm{y}_0},
$$

$$
\Rightarrow x_2(t_2) = v_i\sqrt{\frac{2}{g}\mathrm{y}_0} = x_1(t_1)\sqrt{\frac{g}{2(\mathrm{y}_0 - \mathrm{y}_1(t_1))}}\sqrt{\frac{2}{g}\mathrm{y}_0},
$$

$$
\Rightarrow x_2(t_2) = x_1(t_1)\sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{y}_0}{(\mathrm{y}_0 - \mathrm{y}_1(t_1))}} = 18.8\ m.
$$

**B5.**

Gravitational acceleration is $g = 9.8\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$.

$$
t_2 = \sqrt{\frac{2}{g}\mathrm{y}_0} = 0.71\ m.
$$


</Spoiler>
