## Условие

A homogeneous disc A of mass $m$ and radius $R_A$ moves translationally on a smooth horizontal $x$-$y$ plane in the $x$ direction with a velocity $V$ (see the figure on the next page). The center of the disk is at a distance $b$ from the $x$-axis. It collides with a stationary homogeneous disc B whose center is initially located at the origin of the coordinate system. The disc B has the same mass and the same thickness as A, but its radius is $R_B$. It is assumed that the velocities of the discs at their point of contact, in the direction perpendicular to the line joining their centers, are equal after the collision. It is also assumed that the magnitudes of the relative velocities of the discs along the line joining their centers are the same before and after the collision.

**1)** For such a collision determine the $X$ and $Y$ components of the velocities of the two discs after the collision, i.e. $V'_{AX}$, $V'_{AY}$, $V'_{BX}$ and $V'_{BY}$ in terms of $m$, $R_A$, $R_B$, $V$ and $b$. **[8 т.]**

**2)** Determine the kinetic energies $E'_A$ for disc A and $E'_B$ for disc B after the collision in terms of $m$, $R_A$, $R_B$, $V$ and $b$. **[2 т.]**

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-1">

### 1)

When disc A collides with disc B, let $n$ be the unit vector along the normal to the surfaces at the point of contact and $t$ be the tangential unit vector as shown in the figure. Let $\varphi$ be the angle between $n$ and the $x$ axis. Then we have

$$
b=(R_A+R_B)\sin\varphi.
$$

The momentum components of A and B along $n$ and $t$ before collision are:

$$
mV_{An}=mV\cos\varphi,\quad mV_{Bn}=0,\qquad mV_{At}=mV\sin\varphi,\quad mV_{Bt}=0.
$$

Denote the corresponding momentum components of A and B after collision by $mV'_{An}$, $mV'_{Bn}$, $mV'_{At}$, and $mV'_{Bt}$. Let $\omega_A$ and $\omega_B$ be the angular velocities of A and B about the axes through their centers after collision, and $I_A$ and $I_B$ be their corresponding moments of intertia. Then,

$$
I_A=\frac12mR_A^2,\qquad I_B=\frac12mR_B^2.
$$

The conservation of momentum gives

$$
mV\cos\varphi=mV'_{An}+mV'_{Bn},\qquad (1)
$$

$$
mV\sin\varphi=mV'_{At}+mV'_{tn}.\qquad (2)
$$

The conservation of angular momentum about the axis through O gives

$$
mVb=mV'_{At}(R_A+R_B)+I_A\omega_A+I_B\omega_B.\qquad (3)
$$

The impulse of the friction force exerted on B during collision will cause a momentum change of $mV'_{At}$ along $t$ and produces an angular momentum $I_B\omega_B$ simultaneously. They are related by.

$$
mV'_{Bt}R_b=I_B\omega_B.\qquad (4)
$$

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '28.4%', '--fig-max': '182px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ipho-1994-theory-94/p3-solution.png" alt="Original contact geometry with all normal/tangential arrows, angle phi and both disc radii." width="569" height="513" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


During the collision at the point of contact A and B acquires the same tangential velocities, so we have

$$
V'_{At}-\omega_AR_A=V'_{Bt}-\omega_BR_B.\qquad (5)
$$

It is given that the magnitudes of the relative velocities along the normal direction of the two discs before and after collision are equal, i.e.

$$
V\cos\varphi=V'_{Bn}-V'_{An}.\qquad (6)
$$

From Eqs. 1 and 6 we get

$$
V'_{An}=0,\qquad V'_{Bn}=V\cos\varphi.
$$

From Eqs. 2 to 5, we get

$$
V'_{At}=\frac56V\sin\varphi,\qquad V'_{Bt}=\frac16V\sin\varphi,\qquad\omega_A=\frac{V\sin\varphi}{3R_A},\qquad\omega_B=\frac{V\sin\varphi}{3R_B}.
$$

The $x$ and $y$ components of the velocities after collision are:

$$
V'_{Ax}=V'_{An}\cos\varphi+V'_{At}\sin\varphi=\frac{5Vb^2}{6(R_A+R_B)^2},\qquad (7)
$$

$$
V'_{Ay}=-V'_{An}\sin\varphi+V'_{At}\cos\varphi=\frac{5Vb\sqrt{(R_A+R_B)^2-b^2}}{6(R_A+R_B)^2},\qquad (8)
$$

$$
V'_{Bx}=V'_{Bn}\cos\varphi+V'_{Bt}\sin\varphi=\left[1-\frac{5b^2}{6(R_A+R_B)^2}\right],\qquad (9)
$$

$$
V'_{By}=-V'_{Bn}\sin\varphi+V'_{Bt}\cos\varphi=-\frac{5Vb\sqrt{(R_A+R_B)^2-b^2}}{6(R_A+R_B)^2}.\qquad (10)
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-part-2">

### 2)

After the collision, the kinetic energy of disc A is

$$
E'_A=\frac12m\left({V'_{Ax}}^2+{V'_{Ay}}^2\right)+\frac12I_A\omega_A^2=\frac{3mV^2b^2}{8(R_A+R_B)^2},\qquad (11)
$$

while the kinetic energy of disc B is

$$
E'_B=\frac12m\left({V'_{Bx}}^2+{V'_{By}}^2\right)+\frac12I_B\omega_B^2=\frac12mV^2\left[1-\frac{11b^2}{12(R_A+R_B)^2}\right].\qquad (12)
$$

</ProblemSection>
