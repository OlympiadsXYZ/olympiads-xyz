*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 3 · 3 септември 2018 г.*

## Условие

There is an unlimited supply of chips of three colours: red, green and blue. Find the number of ways to arrange 9 chips in a circle such that each colour is present. Arrangements are considered identical if and only if they can be obtained from each other by a rotation.
