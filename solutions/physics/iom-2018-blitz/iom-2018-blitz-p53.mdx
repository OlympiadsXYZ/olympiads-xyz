*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 3 · 3 септември 2018 г.*

## Условие

Which of the following mixtures is a buffer solution?

1) $CH_3COOH$ (50 mL; 0.1 mol/L) + $NaOH$ (50 mL; 0.1 mol/L)
2) $CH_3COOH$ (50 mL; 0.1 mol/L) + $NaOH$ (50 mL; 0.05 mol/L)
3) $CH_3COOH$ (50 mL; 0.05 mol/L) + $NaOH$ (50 mL; 0.1 mol/L)
4) $CH_3COOH$ (50 mL; 0.05 mol/L) + $NaOH$ (50 mL; 0.05 mol/L)

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 2

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 2


</Spoiler>
