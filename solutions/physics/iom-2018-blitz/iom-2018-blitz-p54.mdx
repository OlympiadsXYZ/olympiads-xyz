*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 3 · 3 септември 2018 г.*

## Условие

When 3.00 g of mineral M is treated with an excess acid, 0.4313 L of carbon dioxide escape (at 298 K and 1 bar). M contains 55.31% copper (by mass) and consists of four elements. Assume that all copper ions are in oxidation state +2. How many moles of oxygen are in one mole of M? Take $A_r(Cu) = 63.5$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 8

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 8


</Spoiler>
