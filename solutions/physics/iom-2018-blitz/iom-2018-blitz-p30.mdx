*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 3 · 3 септември 2018 г.*

## Условие

100 ml of 30% perchloric acid solution ($\rho = 1.11$ g/mL) and 300 ml of 20% sodium hydroxide solution ($\rho = 1.10$ g/mL) were mixed. How many milliliters of water should be added to this mixture, so that the mass fraction of sodium hydroxide in the resulting solution be 11%? Take $A_r(Cl) = 35.5$.

1) 11.2 2) 38.5 3) 123 4) 159

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 2

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 2


</Spoiler>
