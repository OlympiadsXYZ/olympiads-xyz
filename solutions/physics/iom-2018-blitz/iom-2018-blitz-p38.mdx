*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 3 · 3 септември 2018 г.*

## Условие

Chris has an unlimited number of 2-dollar, 7-dollar and 10-dollar coins. He wants to pay an amount of 48 dollars exactly. What is the minimum number of coins required?

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 6

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 6


</Spoiler>
