*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 3 · 3 септември 2018 г.*

## Условие

A solution of hydrochloric acid is added to 10 mL of a 0.2 M solution of sodium aluminate. The amount of precipitate produced when 10 mL of the $HCl$ solution is added is equal to the amount of the precipitate when 20 mL of the $HCl$ solution is added. What is the molar concentration (in M) of the $HCl$ solution? Give the answer with 2 digits after decimal point and without units. Assume that only $Al(OH)_3$ precipitates.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 0.16

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 0,16


</Spoiler>
