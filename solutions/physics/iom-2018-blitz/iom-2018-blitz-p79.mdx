*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 3 · 3 септември 2018 г.*

## Условие

The substance X decomposes at a rate of 5% per minute at 318 K and 15% per minute at 328 K. Decomposition is a first-order reaction. Determine the activation energy.

1) 9.6 kJ/mol 2) 86.7 kJ/mol 3) 95.3 kJ/mol 4) 100 kJ/mol

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 4

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 4


</Spoiler>
