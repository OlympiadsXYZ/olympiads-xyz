*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 3 · 3 септември 2018 г.*

## Условие

How many typewriter pages of text can be stored on a 360KB floppy disk, if each character occupies 7 bits and each page is filled with 60 lines of 80 characters? Round the result up to the nearest integer.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 88

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 88


</Spoiler>
