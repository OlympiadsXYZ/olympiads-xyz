*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 3 · 3 септември 2018 г.*

## Условие

Mr. Bagas bought 654 red candies, 101 blue candies, and 256 green candies for his son. His son doesn’t want all the candies, he just wants to take 119 candies of the same color. Mr. Bagas wants his son to take as much as possible, so he puts all of them in one bag and asks his son to take them one by one from the bag, until his son has 119 candies of some color. How many candy can Mr. Bagas’s son take, at most?

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- 338

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Answer: 338


</Spoiler>
