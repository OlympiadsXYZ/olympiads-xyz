*OLYMPIAD OF METROPOLISES. Moscow, September 3 · 3 септември 2018 г.*

## Условие

A small mass is sliding without friction along the looped track shown in the Figure. It detaches from the loop at some point $A$ and during the flight passes through the center $O$ of the loop. What is the angle $\alpha$ (in degrees)?
