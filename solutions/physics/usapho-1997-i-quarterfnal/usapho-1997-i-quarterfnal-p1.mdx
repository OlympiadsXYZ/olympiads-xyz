## Условие

A collection of $N$ identical blocks, each of mass $m$, are connected by unstretchable ropes of negligible mass.  The blocks are on a horizontal surface.  An external force $F_o$ acts on Block 1, pulling horizontally to the right.  What is the tension in the rope connecting Block $n$ to Block $n+1$, if:

<figure>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-1997-i-quarterfnal/p1-fig1.png" alt="A row of blocks on a horizontal surface, labelled from left to right N, N-1, …, n+1, n, …, 2, 1, joined by ropes (with dashes marking omitted blocks); an arrow F0 pulls block 1 to the right." />
</figure>

**(a)** There is no friction between the blocks and the surface? **[8 т.]**

**(b)** The coefficient of kinetic friction between each block and the surface is $\mu_k$?  ($F_o$ is large enough to accelerate the blocks despite the friction.) **[12 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **(a)** $T = F_o(1 - n/N)$
- **(b)** $T = F_o(1 - n/N)$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**(a, 8)**

The force $F_o$ must accelerate $N$ blocks each of mass $m$:
$F_o = Nma$, so that $a = F_o/Nm$. (4)

Let $T$ be the tension dragging $N$-$n$ blocks to the right.  $F = ma$ gives
$T = (N-n)ma$ (2)

Using $a$,
$T = (N-n)\,F_o/N = F_o\,(1 - n/N)$. (2)

**(b, 12)**

Each block has exerted on it the friction force $\mu mg$. (2)

$F = ma$ applied the the entire $N$-block system gives
$F_o - N\mu mg = Nma$ (2)

Solving for acceleration,
$a = (F_o/Nm) - \mu g$ (2)

Let $T$ be the tension dragging $N$-$n$ blocks to the right.  $F=ma$ gives
$T - (N-n)\mu mg = (N-n)ma$ (2)

so that upon substituting for $a$,
$T = (N-n)m\,[\mu g + (F_o/Nm) - \mu g\,]$ (2)

which gives the final answer,
$T = F_o(1 - n/N)$, the same as without friction. (2)


</Spoiler>
