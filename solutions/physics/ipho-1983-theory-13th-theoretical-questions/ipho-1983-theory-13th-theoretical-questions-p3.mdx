*IPhO 1983 Theoretical Questions · 7 т.*

## Условие

Two dispersive prisms having apex angles $\hat{A}_1 = 60^{\circ}$ and $\hat{A}_2 = 30^{\circ}$ are glued as in the figure below ( $\hat{C} = 90^{\circ}$ ). The dependences of refraction indexes of the prisms on the wavelength are given by the relations

$$
n_1(\lambda) = a_1 + \frac{b_1}{\lambda^2};
$$

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '25.4%', '--fig-max': '163px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ipho-1983-theory-13th-theoretical-questions/p3-fig1-v3.png" alt="Two glued prisms forming the rectangle ABCD-like shape: triangle ADC with apex angle A1 at A, refractive index n1, and right triangle ABC with angle A2 at C and index n2, right angle at C; an incident ray arrives from the left on face AD." width="509" height="472" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

$$
n_2(\lambda) = a_2 + \frac{b_2}{\lambda^2}
$$

were

$a_1 = 1{,}1$ , $b_1 = 1 \cdot 10^5\ \mathrm{nm}^2$ , $a_2 = 1{,}3$ , $b_2 = 5 \cdot 10^4\ \mathrm{nm}^2$ .

**a.** Determine the wavelength $\lambda_0$ of the incident radiation that pass through the prisms without refraction on $AC$ face at any incident angle; determine the corresponding refraction indexes of the prisms.

**b.** Draw the ray path in the system of prisms for three different radiations $\lambda_{red}$ , $\lambda_0$ , $\lambda_{violet}$ incident on the system at the same angle.

**c.** Determine the minimum deviation angle in the system for a ray having the wavelength $\lambda_0$ .

**d.** Calculate the wavelength of the ray that penetrates and exits the system along directions parallel to DC.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

<ProblemSection id="solution-solution-1">

### a.

The ray with the wavelength $\lambda_0$ pass trough the prisms system without refraction on $AC$ face at any angle of incidence if:

$$
n_1(\lambda_0)=n_2(\lambda_0).
$$

Because the dependence of refraction indexes of prisms on wavelength has the form:

$$
n_1(\lambda)=a_1+\frac{b_1}{\lambda^2}\qquad (3.1)
$$

$$
n_2(\lambda)=a_2+\frac{b_2}{\lambda^2}\qquad (3.2)
$$

The relation (3.1) becomes:

$$
a_1+\frac{b_1}{\lambda_0^2}=a_2+\frac{b_2}{\lambda_0^2}.\qquad (3.3)
$$

The wavelength $\lambda_0$ has correspondingly the form:

$$
\lambda_0=\sqrt{\frac{b_1-b_2}{a_2-a_1}}.\qquad (3.4)
$$

Substituting the furnished numerical values

$$
\lambda_0=500\,\mathrm{nm}.\qquad (3.5)
$$

The corresponding common value of indexes of refraction of prisms for the radiation with the wavelength $\lambda_0$ is:

$$
n_1(\lambda_0)=n_2(\lambda_0)=1.5.\qquad (3.6)
$$

The relations (3.6) and (3.7) represent the answers of question **a**.

</ProblemSection>

### c.

In the figure 1.2 is presented the path of ray with wavelength $\lambda_0$ at minimum deviation (the angle between the direction of incidence of ray and the direction of emerging ray is minimal).

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '31.7%', '--fig-max': '203px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ipho-1983-theory-13th-theoretical-questions/p3-sol-fig2-v2.png" alt="Prism ray path, Figure 3.2." width="633" height="879" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<figcaption>Figure 3.2</figcaption>
</figure>


In this situation

$$
n_1(\lambda_0)=n_2(\lambda_0)=\frac{\sin\frac{\delta_{\min}+A'}2}{\sin\frac{A'}2}.\qquad (3.7)
$$

where $m(\hat A')=30^\circ$, as in the figure 1.1. Substituting in (3.8) the values of refraction indexes the result is

$$
\sin\frac{\delta_{\min}+A'}2=\frac32\sin\frac{A'}2\qquad (3.8)
$$

or

$$
\delta_{\min}=2\arcsin\left(\frac32\sin\frac{A'}2\right)-\frac{A'}2.\qquad (3.9)
$$

Numerically

$$
\delta_{\min}\simeq30.7^\circ.\qquad (3.10)
$$

The relation (3.11) represents the answer of question **c**.

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-solution-4">

### d.

Using the figure 1.3 the refraction law on the $AD$ face is

$$
\sin i_1=n_1\sin r_1.\qquad (3.11)
$$

The refraction law on the $AC$ face is

$$
n_1\sin r_1'=n_2\sin r_2.\qquad (3.12)
$$

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '56.4%', '--fig-max': '361px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/ipho-1983-theory-13th-theoretical-questions/p3-sol-fig3-v2.png" alt="Prism ray path, Figure 3.3." width="1128" height="800" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<figcaption>Figure 3.3</figcaption>
</figure>


As it can be seen in the figure 1.3

$$
r_2=A_2\qquad (3.13)
$$

and

$$
i_1=30^\circ.\qquad (3.14)
$$

Also,

$$
r_1+r_1'=A_1.\qquad (3.15)
$$

Substituting (3.16) and (3.14) in (3.13) it results

$$
n_1\sin(A_1-r_1)=n_2\sin A_2\qquad (3.16)
$$

or

$$
n_1(\sin A_1\cos r_1-\sin r_1\cos A_1)=n_2\sin A_2.\qquad (3.17)
$$

Because of (3.12) and (3.15) it results that

$$
\sin r_1=\frac1{2n_1}\qquad (3.18)
$$

and

$$
\cos r_1=\frac1{2n_1}\sqrt{4n_1^2-1}.\qquad (3.19)
$$

Putting together the last three relations it results

$$
\sqrt{4n_1^2-1}=\frac{2n_2\sin A_2+\cos A_1}{\sin A_1}.\qquad (3.20)
$$

Because $\hat A_1=60^\circ$ and $\hat A_2=30^\circ$ relation (3.21) can be written as

$$
\sqrt{4n_1^2-1}=\frac{2n_2+1}{\sqrt3}\qquad (3.21)
$$

or

$$
3n_1^2=1+n_2+n_2^2.\qquad (3.22)
$$

Considering the relations (3.1), (3.2) and (3.23) and operating all calculus it results:

$$
\lambda^4(3a_1^2-a_2^2-a_2-1)+(6a_1b_1-b_2-2a_2b_2)\lambda^2+3b_1^2-b_2^2=0.\qquad (3.23)
$$

Solving the equation (3.24) one determine the wavelength $\lambda$ of the ray that enter the prisms system having the direction parallel with $DC$ and emerges the prisms system having the direction again parallel with $DC$. That is

$$
\lambda=1194\,\mathrm{nm}\qquad (3.24)
$$

or

$$
\lambda\simeq1.2\,\mu\mathrm m.\qquad (3.25)
$$

The relation (3.26) represents the answer of question **d**.
