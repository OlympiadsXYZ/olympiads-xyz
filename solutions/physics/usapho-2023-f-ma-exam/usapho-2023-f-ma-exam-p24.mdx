## Условие

A very heavy plate continuously moves up and down with a small speed $v$, switching directions after each time $t$. At a random time, a ball is dropped from rest far above the plate, then bounces elastically off of it. How does the ball’s speed change during this collision?

- (A) The ball always slows down.
- (B) The ball is more likely to slow down than to speed up.
- (C) The ball is equally likely to slow down or speed up.
- (D) The ball is more likely to speed up than to slow down.
- (E) The ball always speeds up.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- D

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

When the lowest point of the ball enters the collision region, the plates move up or down with equal probability. if the plate moves up, then the speed of the ball will increase. If the plate moves down, it still has time to turn around and collide with the ball as it will move up.

This is a simple model for how a gas heats up in a container with hot walls, or more exotically, the Fermi acceleration mechanism for cosmic rays.


</Spoiler>
