## Условие

An archer takes aim at a target that is $100\ \mathrm{m}$ away. Assuming she holds the bow at the same height as the center of the target and shoots an arrow with velocity $v = 100\ \mathrm{m/s}$, at what angle above the horizontal should she aim the bow so that the arrow hits the center of the target?

- (A) $\dfrac{\arccos(1/5)}{2}$
- (B) $\dfrac{\arcsin(1/5)}{2}$
- (C) $\dfrac{\arccos(1/10)}{2}$
- (D) $\dfrac{\arcsin(1/10)}{2}$
- (E) $\dfrac{\arctan(2/5)}{2}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- D

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Since the initial and final heights are the same, we can apply the range equation $R = v^2\sin(2\theta)/g$. Setting $R = 100\ \mathrm{m}$ and $v = 100\ \mathrm{m/s}$ and solving for $\theta$ gives the answer. Of course, an analysis using the usual kinematic equations would also give the same answer, after some more effort.


</Spoiler>
