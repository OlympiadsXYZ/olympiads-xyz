## Условие

A helium balloon is released from the floor in a room at rest, then slowly rises and comes to rest touching the ceiling. During this process, the gravitational potential energy of the balloon has increased. Since energy is conserved, the energy of something else must have decreased during this process. Which of the following is the main contribution to this decrease?

- (A) The kinetic energy of the balloon decreased.
- (B) The elastic potential energy of the balloon decreased.
- (C) The thermal energy of the air in the balloon decreased.
- (D) The thermal energy of the air in the room decreased.
- (E) The gravitational potential energy of the air in the room decreased.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- E

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The main contribution is choice (E). In terms of energy, objects that are lighter than air float upward because the air can move downward to where the object originally was, thereby reducing its gravitational potential energy by more than the gravitational potential energy of the object went up.
