## Условие

A ball at the end of a rope of length $0.5\ \mathrm{m}$ is swung in a horizontal circle, with a speed of $15\ \mathrm{m/s}$. The other end of the rope is fixed in place. What is the height difference between the ends of the rope?

- (A) $1.1\ \mathrm{cm}$
- (B) $2.2\ \mathrm{cm}$
- (C) $3.8\ \mathrm{cm}$
- (D) $4.9\ \mathrm{cm}$
- (E) $7.5\ \mathrm{cm}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- A

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

For all of the answer choices, the rope is close to horizontal, so we can use the small angle approximation. The angle $\theta$ the rope makes with the horizontal must obey

$$
\theta \approx \tan\theta = \frac{mg}{mv^2/r}
$$

where the radius of the horizontal circle is $r = \ell\cos\theta \approx \ell$. Thus, the height difference is $\ell\sin\theta \approx \ell\theta = g\ell^2/v^2$, and plugging in the numbers gives the answer.


</Spoiler>
