## Условие

A bead attached to a string of length $\ell = 10\ \mathrm{m}$ is released from a very small angle $\theta_0$ to the vertical. A wall is placed in the path of the bead such that the bead collides elastically with the wall when the string is at an angle $\theta_0/2$ to the vertical, as shown.

What is the time interval between the bead’s collisions with the wall?

- (A) $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\ \mathrm{s}$
- (B) $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\ \mathrm{s}$
- (C) $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\ \mathrm{s}$
- (D) $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\ \mathrm{s}$
- (E) $2\pi\ \mathrm{s}$

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

If the wall was not there, then the angle of the pendulum would be $\theta(t) = \theta_0\cos(t\sqrt{g/L})$, which corresponds to period $2\pi$ seconds. When the bead collides with the wall, it effectively skips from a phase angle $120^{\circ}$ in its oscillation to a phase angle $240^{\circ}$, which removes $1/3$ of the oscillation cycle. Thus, the period is $(2/3)(2\pi) = 4\pi/3$ seconds.


</Spoiler>
