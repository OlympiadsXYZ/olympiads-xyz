## Условие

A uniform box with mass $m$ is at rest on a horizontal surface, and the coefficient of static friction between them is $\mu_s$. A force directed at an angle of $85^{\circ}$ above the horizontal is applied to the center of the box, with a linearly increasing magnitude $F = \beta t$.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '19.8%', '--fig-max': '127px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2023-f-ma-exam/p13-fig1.png" alt="A box resting on the ground with an arrow F starting at the centre of the box and pointing up, tilted slightly to the right of vertical." width="396" height="344" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

The box will eventually slide or lift off the ground. Which of the following is correct?

- (A) If $\mu_s < \tan 85^{\circ}$, the box will lift off the ground first.
- (B) If $\mu_s < \tan 85^{\circ}$, the box will slide first.
- (C) For any value of $\mu_s$, the box will lift off the ground first.
- (D) For any value of $\mu_s$, the box will slide first.
- (E) The answer depends on the values of $\beta$, $g$, and $m$.

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- D

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The box will slide first, no matter what the other parameters are. The box will only lift off the ground once the normal force goes to zero, which occurs when the vertical component of the applied force exceeds the weight of the box. Right before that moment, the normal force will be very small, so the maximal possible friction force is also very small, and cannot balance the horizontal component of the applied force.


</Spoiler>
