## Условие

A mass on an ideal pendulum is released from rest at point I. When it reaches point II, which of the following shows the direction of its acceleration?

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

At this point, this is both an inward centripetal force because the mass is moving in a circle, and a tangential force because the mass is speeding up. Thus, the answer has to be a positive sum of vectors directed along (A) and (C), so it must be (B).
