## Условие

A bead on a circular hoop with radius $2\ \mathrm{m}$ travels counterclockwise for $10\ \mathrm{s}$ and completes $2.25$ rotations, at which point it reaches the position shown.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '16.4%', '--fig-max': '106px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2023-f-ma-exam/p1-fig1.png" alt="A circle (the hoop) with a radius arrow from the centre to the right edge labelled 2 m; the bead is drawn as a small ball at the bottom of the circle." width="330" height="354" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

In the past $10\ \mathrm{s}$, what were its average speed and the direction of its average velocity?

- (A) $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{5}\,\dfrac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}, \swarrow$
- (B) $\dfrac{2\pi}{5}\,\dfrac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}, \swarrow$
- (C) $\dfrac{9\pi}{10}\,\dfrac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}, \swarrow$
- (D) $\dfrac{2\pi}{5}\,\dfrac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}, \searrow$
- (E) $\dfrac{9\pi}{10}\,\dfrac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}, \searrow$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- E

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

The total distance traveled is $(2.25)(2\pi \cdot 2\ \mathrm{m}) = 9\pi\ \mathrm{m}$, and the average speed is the distance per time, $(9\pi/10)\ \mathrm{m/s}$. This rules out all choices but (C) and (E). To find the direction we note that 10 seconds ago, the bead was initially at the left side of the hoop, so the direction of the average velocity is $\searrow$.


</Spoiler>
