## Условие

Consider a perfectly conducting disk of radius $r_0$ in a constant magnetic field $B$ perpendicular to the plane of the disk. Sliding contacts are provided at the edge of the disk ($C_1$) and at its axle ($C_2$) ( cf. ***Figure 2*** ). This system is Faraday’s “homopolar generator.” When turned at constant angular velocity, it provides a large direct current with no ripple. A torque is produced by a mass $M$ hung on a long string wrapped around the perimeter of the disk.

**(a)** Explain. how and why a current flows. Give a quantitative expression for the current as a function of angular velocity.

**(b)** Given a long enough string, this system will reach a constant angular velocity $\omega_f$. Find this $\omega_f$ and the associated current.
