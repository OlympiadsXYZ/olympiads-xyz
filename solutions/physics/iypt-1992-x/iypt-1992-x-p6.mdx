### Note [17]

The 2006 English version places here an entirely different problem No. 6 that reads:

 6. Flame

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="note-18">

### Note [18]

“Minimum amount”, according to [RMF 1992]. The Russian text reads literally, “With what minimum quantity of “sulfur” a match is still valid for igniting?”
