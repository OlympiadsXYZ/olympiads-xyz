A laser beam is directed [27] perpendicularly to the wall of a transparent glass tank [28] filled with water. If the beam passes through the tank above or below the level of the water in the tank, we can observe a spot on the screen behind the tank. If the beam passes along the level of the water we observe a vertical line. Explain the origin of the line and calculate its parameters.

<ProblemSection id="note-27">

### Note [27]

“Directed horizontally”, in the Russian text

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="note-28">

### Note [28]

“Flat transparent cuvette with water”, not mentioning the material of the walls, in the Russian text. “Glass tank (aquarium)” in the 1992 English text.

</ProblemSection>
