A special swing (trapeze) is used to train air and space pilots. [11] This device is able to make a loop around the horizontal axis. [12] What minimum time is necessary to build up the motion of the swing from the rest at the equilibrium position, to an amplitude of 180°? [13]

<ProblemSection id="note-11">

### Note [11]

A special term looping («лопинг») is omitted in all translations. This term, a noun, is not colloquial in Russian language and describes a rotating swing driven solely by the person who uses it

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="note-12">

### Note [12]

”This device is able to make a loop around horizontal axis around which it swings”, according to [Kopelevich 1992]

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="note-13">

### Note [13]

The last part of the phrase, introducing the distance L, is in the Russian text, “for a person who is trained on the swing with a distance L between the axis of revolution and the deck?”

</ProblemSection>
