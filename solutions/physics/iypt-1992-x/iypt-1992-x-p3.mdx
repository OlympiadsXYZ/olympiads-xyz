There is a saying in Russian, [8] “money goes like water through sand.” However, sand dams hold water. What should be the thickness [9] of the dam in order to retain water whose level [10] behind the dam is 10 m?

<ProblemSection id="note-7">

### Note [7]

The Slovak text places here an entirely different problem No. 3 that reads:

 3. Floating ball

 A ping-pong ball floats up from a depth x in a vessel with water.

- Finally, the ball will jump out of water. What will be the altitude of the jump?

- Describe the process of ascending if the vessel rotates about a vertical axis

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="note-8">

### Note [8]

Literally, “There is an expression, “money leaks out like water leaks through sand” in the 1992 Russian text

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="note-9">

### Note [9]

“How long has to be the barrier in order to retain the water with height 10 m above the surface”, according to the 2006 English version

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="note-10">

### Note [10]

“Depth” in [Kopelevich 1992], “level” in the 1992 Russian text

</ProblemSection>
