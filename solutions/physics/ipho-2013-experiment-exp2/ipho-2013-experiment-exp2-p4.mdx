## Условие



**2.4a** The maximum power that the solar cell can deliver to the external circuit is denoted $P_{\max}$. Determine $P_{\max}$ for your solar cell through a few, suitable measurements. (You may use some of your previous measurements from question 2.2). **[0,5 т.]**

**2.4b** Estimate the optimal load resistance $R_{\mathrm{opt}}$, i.e. the total external resistance when the solar cell delivers its maximum power to $R_{\mathrm{opt}}$. State your result with uncertainty and illustrate your method with suitable calculations. **[0,5 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **2.4a** 12.7 mW
- **2.4b** 15.3 Ω

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

| I | U | P |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| mA | V | mW |  |
| 26.8 | 0.458 | 12.2744 | 1 |
| 27.8 | 0.454 | 12.6212 | 2 |
| 29.2 | 0.435 | 12.7020 | 3 |
| 30.6 | 0.410 | 12.5460 | 4 |
| 31.1 | 0.395 | 12.2845 | 5 |

$$
P_{\max} = (12.7 \pm 0.1)\mathrm{mW}\ \text{at}\ I = (28.8 \pm 0.2)\mathrm{mA}
$$

$$
R_{\mathrm{opt}} = \frac{P_{\max}}{I_{\mathrm{opt}}^2} = \frac{12.71\mathrm{mW}}{(28.8\mathrm{mA})^2} = (15.3 \pm 0.3)\Omega
$$
