## Условие

A massless wheel of radius $R$ has four small masses $M$ placed evenly along its rim. Let the moment of inertia for rotations about the center of wheel, in the plane of the wheel, be $I_0$.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '24.6%', '--fig-max': '158px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2022-fma-exam-b/p2-fig1-v5.png" alt="A circle (the wheel) with four point masses labelled m at the top, bottom, left and right of the rim. </figure>

Now consider rotations about the two axes shown, with corresponding moments of inertia $I_1$ and $I_2$. Which of the following is true?

(A) $I_1 = I_2 = I_0/2$

(B) $I_1 = I_2 = I_0/\sqrt{2}$

(C) $I_1 = I_2 = I_0$

(D) $I_1 = I_0/2, I_2 = I_0/\sqrt{2}$

(E) $I_1 = I_0, I_2 = I_0/\sqrt{2}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- A

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

By the definition of the moment of inertia, $I_0 = 4MR^2$, $I_1 = 2MR^2$, and $I_2 = 4M(R/\sqrt{2})^2 = 2MR^2$. Therefore, $I_1 = I_2 = I_0/2$.


</Spoiler>
