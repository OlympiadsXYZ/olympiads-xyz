## Условие

A firework explodes, sending shells in all directions in a vertical plane, as shown.

Suppose the shells are all launched with the same speed, and ignore air resistance, but not gravity. A long time later, but before any of the shells hit the ground, what shape do the shells make?

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

In the absence of gravity, the shells would always form a circle. Adding gravity simply shifts all of their locations downward by $gt^2/2$, so the shape is still always a circle.
