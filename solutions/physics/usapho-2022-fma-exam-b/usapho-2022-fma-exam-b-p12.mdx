## Условие

Two identical bricks of mass $m$ are attached to two frictionless pulleys by a massless string, on a fixed slope of angle $\theta$ to the horizontal, as shown.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '31.5%', '--fig-max': '202px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/usapho-2022-fma-exam-b/p12-fig1-v5.png" alt="A slope inclined at angle θ rising to the right. Two stacked bricks, each labelled m, rest on the slope; a string from the bricks runs up the slope around two small pulleys attached to a bar fixed at the top of the slope." width="631" height="440" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

Suppose that the coefficient of static friction between the lower block and the slope is $\mu$, and all other surfaces are frictionless. What is the minimum value of $\mu$ so that the blocks can stay static?

(A) $\dfrac{1}{2}\tan\theta$

(B) $\dfrac{2}{3}\tan\theta$

(C) $\tan\theta$

(D) $\dfrac{3}{2}\tan\theta$

(E) $2\tan\theta$

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

Let the tension in the string that wraps around both pulleys be $T$. Applying force balance to the upper brick, we must have $T = mg\sin\theta$. The pulley applies a force $2T$ to the lower brick, so applying force balance gives

$$
2T = mg\sin\theta + f_{\mathrm{fric}}.
$$

When the friction is just barely sufficient, we have

$$
f_{\mathrm{fric}} = \mu N = 2\mu mg\cos\theta
$$

and combining these two equations gives $\mu = (\tan\theta)/2$.


</Spoiler>
