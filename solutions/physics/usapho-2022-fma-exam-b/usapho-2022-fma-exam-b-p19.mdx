## Условие

Next, the rod is brought back to equilibrium. It is slightly rotated about its center of mass, then released from rest.

What is the frequency $f$ of its oscillations?

(A) $\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{M}}$

(B) $\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\dfrac{2k}{M}}$

(C) $\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\dfrac{3k}{M}}$

(D) $\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\dfrac{4k}{M}}$

(E) $\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\dfrac{6k}{M}}$

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- E

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

If the rod is rotated by a small angle $\theta$, the net torque on the rod is $\tau = 2(L/2)(kL\theta/2) = kL^2\theta/2$. The rotational form of Newton’s second law, $\tau = I\alpha$, gives

$$
\frac{1}{12}ML^2\alpha = -\frac{kL^2}{2}\theta
$$

or equivalently $\alpha = -(6k/M)\theta$, which has the form of a simple harmonic motion equation with $\omega = \sqrt{6k/M}$.


</Spoiler>
