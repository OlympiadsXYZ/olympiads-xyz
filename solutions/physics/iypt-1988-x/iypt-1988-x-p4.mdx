**В тази задача**

- [Note [5]](#note-5)

</nav>

Several knots ($n\le10$) [5] are interconnected with batteries of known EMF and $r$. Create a computer program to calculate the potential difference between the first and the second knot. Consider the time from the start of data input (tables of EMF and $r$ values) to the moment of the correct result output, as the quality criterion of the program.

<ProblemSection id="note-5">

### Note [5]

It was stated “n≤10” in [MGU 1988], but “n\<10” in [Kvant 1987]. In the temporary 2002 publication of the problems on the Evgeny Yunosov's Faraday Tournament website [Farad 2002], it was stated “n>10”. Both 1996 Czech and 2006 English versions read here “nj10”, while the 1996 Slovak translation does not introduce the n variable at all (“\<10”.) It may be speculated that a handwritten formula in a book published shortly before the event, and most commonly used by the participants and jurors at the competition, is somewhat more authentic

</ProblemSection>
