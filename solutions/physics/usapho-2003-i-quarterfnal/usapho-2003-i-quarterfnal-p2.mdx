## Условие

Suppose that Galileo is in the Leaning Tower of Pisa.  He throws a cannonball straight down from height $H$ above the ground.  The ball leaves his hands with an initial speed $v_o$.  At the same instant, his friend throws a cannonball straight up from ground level with an initial speed of $2v_o$. When the cannonballs collide, they are traveling in the same direction and Galileo's cannonball is traveling with 7 times the speed of his friend's.  Ignore air resistance.

**a.** At what time does the collision occur?  Express your answer in terms of $H$ and $v_o$ only. **[10 т.]**

**b.** At what height above the ground does the collision occur?  Express your answer in terms of $H$ only. **[15 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a.** $t = \frac{H}{3v_o}$
- **b.** $y = \frac{H}{4}$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**2. a.** Selecting down to be negative and the ground to be the zero of position, the position of Galileo’s cannonball is

$$
y_G = H - v_o t - \tfrac{1}{2}gt^2. \qquad (2-1)
$$

**[3]**

The position of his friend’s cannonball is

$$
y_F = 0 + 2v_o t - \tfrac{1}{2}gt^2. \qquad (2-2)
$$

**[3]**

The cannonballs collide when they are at the same position at the same time.  Setting the equations equal

$$
2v_o t - \tfrac{1}{2}gt^2 = H - v_o t - \tfrac{1}{2}gt^2.
$$

**[2]**

Solving for $t$,

$$
3v_o t = H,
$$

or

$$
t = \frac{H}{3v_o}. \qquad (2-3)
$$

**[2]**

**b.** The velocities at the time of collision are:

for Galileo’s cannonball

$$
v_G = -v_o - gt
$$

**[3]**

and for the friend’s cannonball

$$
v_F = 2v_o - gt.
$$

**[3]**

The velocities are related

$$
v_G = 7v_F.
$$

**[2]**

Combining

$$
-v_o - gt = 7(2v_o - gt)
$$

or

$$
6gt = 15v_o. \qquad (2-4)
$$

**[2]**

There are many ways of combining equations (2-3) and (2-4) with either (2-1) or (2-2) to get $y$ in terms of only $H$.  One of the shortest is to multiply (2-4) by $t$ and use (2-3) to simplify.

$$
6gt^2 = 15v_o t = 15v_o\left(\frac{H}{3v_o}\right) = 5H
$$

**[3]**

Subbing into equation (2-2), the collision occurs at

$$
y_F = 0 + 2v_o t - \tfrac{1}{2}gt^2 = 2\left(\frac{H}{3}\right) - \tfrac{1}{2}\left(\frac{5H}{6}\right) = \frac{H(8-5)}{12} = \frac{H}{4}.
$$

**[2]**


</Spoiler>
