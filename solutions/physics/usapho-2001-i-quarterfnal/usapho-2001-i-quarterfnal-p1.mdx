## Условие

A mass $m$ attached to a massless string moves around a circle of radius $r_0$ with speed $v_0$ on a frictionless table as shown in the accompanying diagram. The string passes through a small hole in the table. Starting at time $t=0$, the string is pulled downward with a constant speed $V$. Determine:

</figure>

**a** The tangential speed $v$ of the mass $m$ as a function of the radius $r$. **[7 т.]**

**b** The tangential speed $v$ of the mass $m$ as a function of time $t$. **[6 т.]**

**c** The force with which the string must be pulled as a function of time $t$. **[6 т.]**

**d** The work done on the mass by the string from time $0$ to time $t$. **[6 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a** $v=v_0r_0/r$
- **b** $v=\frac{v_0r_0}{r_0-Vt}$
- **c** $F=\frac{mv_0^2r_0^2}{(r_0-Vt)^3}$
- **d** $W=\frac12mv_0^2\left(\frac{r_0^2}{(r_0-Vt)^2}-1\right)$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**a.** The string force is radial, so it exerts no torque about the hole. Angular momentum is conserved: $L_0=L$. [2]

For a particle of mass $m$ moving with speed $v$ in a circle of radius $r$, $L=mvr$. [2]

Equating the angular momenta at times $0$ and $t$, $mv_0r_0=mvr$. [2]

$$
v=\frac{v_0r_0}{r}\qquad\text{Eq. 1-1}
$$

[1]

**b.** The string and mass are pulled radially inward with constant speed $V$:

$$
-V=\frac{\Delta r}{\Delta t}=\frac{r-r_0}{t-0}.
$$

[2] The outward direction is positive. Thus

$$
r=r_0-Vt\qquad\text{Eq. 1-2}
$$

[2] Substituting in Eq. 1-1,

$$
v=\frac{v_0r_0}{r_0-Vt}\qquad\text{Eq. 1-3}
$$

[2]

**c.** The string is massless, so the net force on it is zero. [1] The pulling force $F$ equals the tension $T$: $F=T$. [1] The tension supplies the centripetal force:

$$
F=\frac{mv^2}{r}.
$$

[2] Substituting Eqs. 1-3 and 1-2,

$$
F=\frac{mv_0^2r_0^2}{(r_0-Vt)^3}.
$$

[2]

**d.** Use the work-energy theorem: $W_{net}=\Delta K$. [2]

$$
W_{net}=\frac12mv^2-\frac12mv_0^2.
$$

[2] Combining with Eq. 1-3,

$$
W_{net}=\frac12m\left(\frac{v_0r_0}{r_0-Vt}\right)^2-\frac12mv_0^2=\frac12mv_0^2\left(\frac{r_0^2}{(r_0-Vt)^2}-1\right).
$$

[2]


</Spoiler>
