## Условие

A projectile is launched in a vertical plane from a fixed site. It has an initial velocity $v_0$ at an angle of $45^\circ$ above the horizontal. A second projectile is simultaneously launched at an angle of $30^\circ$ from a second site a distance $S$ directly behind the first one. See diagram above. They collide when the first projectile attains its maximum height. Ignore any effects of air resistance. Express your answers in terms of in terms of $v_0$ and $g$.

**a** Find the separation $S$. (Hint: first find the initial velocity of the second projectile.) **[15 т.]**

**b** Find the velocities of the projectiles immediately before they collide. You may leave your answers in component form for this and the next part. **[5 т.]**

**c** If the projectiles are identical small mass spheres and the collision is elastic, find their velocities immediately after the collision. **[5 т.]**

## Отговори

<Spoiler title="Покажи отговорите">

- **a** $S=\frac{v_0^2}{2g}(\sqrt3-1)$
- **b** $v_x=v_0/\sqrt2,\quad u_x=v_0\sqrt{1.5},\quad v_y=u_y=0$
- **c** $v_{Fx}=v_0\sqrt{1.5},\quad u_{Fx}=v_0/\sqrt2,\quad v_{Fy}=u_{Fy}=0$

</Spoiler>

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи официалното решение">

**a.** The two projectiles are simultaneously launched at $t=0$ from $x_{20}=0$, $y_{20}=0$ and $x_{10}=S$, $y_{10}=0$. Let $u_0$ = the initial velocity of the second projectile. The components of the initial velocities are

$$
v_{0x}=v_0\cos45^\circ=\frac{v_0}{\sqrt2},\qquad v_{0y}=v_0\sin45^\circ=\frac{v_0}{\sqrt2}
$$

[1]

$$
u_{0x}=u_0\cos30^\circ=u_0\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\qquad u_{0y}=u_0\sin30^\circ=\frac{u_0}{2}.
$$

[1] Projectile 1 reaches its highest point when $v_y=0$. [1]

$$
v_y=v_{0y}-gt
$$

[1] or

$$
t=\frac{v_{0y}}{g}=\frac{v_0}{g\sqrt2}.
$$

[1] At this time, $x_1=x_2$ and $y_1=y_2$. [2] With

$$
y=y_0+v_{0y}t-\frac12gt^2
$$

[1] and $x=x_0+v_{0x}t$. [1] Equating $y$'s at time $t$,

$$
y_{10}+v_{0y}t-\frac12gt^2=y_{20}+u_{0y}t-\frac12gt^2
$$

[1]

$$
v_{0y}t=u_{0y}t
$$

[1] Thus $u_0=\sqrt2v_0$. [1] Equating $x$'s at time $t$,

$$
x_{10}+v_{0x}t=x_{20}+u_{0x}t.
$$

[1] Substituting in for $t$ and $v_0$'s,

$$
S+\frac{v_0}{\sqrt2}\left(\frac{v_0}{g\sqrt2}\right)=0+u_0\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\left(\frac{v_0}{g\sqrt2}\right)
$$

$$
S+\frac{v_0}{\sqrt2}\left(\frac{v_0}{g\sqrt2}\right)=\sqrt2v_0\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\left(\frac{v_0}{g\sqrt2}\right)
$$

[1]

$$
S+\frac{v_0^2}{2g}=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\frac{v_0^2}{g}
$$

$$
S=\frac{v_0^2}{2g}(\sqrt3-1).
$$

[1]

**b.** The $x$- and $y$-components of the velocity are $v_x=v_{0x}$ and $v_y=v_{0y}-gt$. [1] Therefore

$$
v_x=v_{0x}=\frac{v_0}{\sqrt2},\qquad v_y=v_{0y}-gt=\frac{v_0}{\sqrt2}-g\frac{v_0}{g\sqrt2}=0
$$

[2] and

$$
u_x=u_{0x}=u_0\frac{\sqrt3}{2}=\sqrt2v_0\frac{\sqrt3}{2}=v_0\sqrt{1.5}
$$

[1]

$$
u_y=u_{0y}-gt=\frac{u_0}{2}-g\frac{v_0}{g\sqrt2}=\frac{v_0\sqrt2}{2}-\frac{v_0}{\sqrt2}=0.
$$

[1] Both projectiles are at their highest points.

**c.** Since immediately before the collision the velocities are totally in the $x$-direction, the collision is one-dimensional. [1] The collision is elastic so total momentum and total kinetic energy are unchanged. [2] Because the masses are equal, this requires the velocities to interchange.

$$
v_{Fx}=v_0\sqrt{1.5}
$$

[1]

$$
u_{Fx}=\frac{v_0}{\sqrt2}.
$$

[1]


</Spoiler>
