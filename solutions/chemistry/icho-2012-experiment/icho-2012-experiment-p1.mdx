## Условие

Discoveries of reaction mechanisms underlie advances in catalysis/synthesis. Kinetics is powerful because rate dependence on conditions follows directly from mechanism. Isotopically substituted molecules provide another tool: reactivity is similar, but rates differ slightly with nuclear mass. Use kinetics and isotope effects to investigate acetone iodination in acidic aqueous solution.

Rate = k[acetone]ᵐ[I₃⁻]ⁿ[H⁺]ᵖ. Determine k and integer orders m,n,p. Compare acetone with acetone-d₆, whose six protium(¹H) atoms are replaced by deuterium(²H,D), obtaining kH/kD and inferring mechanism. Read the whole task and plan before beginning.

**Chemicals**

- HCl aqueous, c≈2mol dm⁻³*,50cm³.
- KI₃ aqueous, c≈0.01mol dm⁻³*,10cm³, labelled “I₂”.
- Acetone(CH₃)₂CO, M58.08g mol⁻¹,density0.791g cm⁻³,10cm³.
- Acetone-d₆(CD₃)₂CO, M64.12g mol⁻¹,density0.872g cm⁻³,3.0cm³.

*Exact concentration is indicated on its label.

**Procedure**

Rates depend on temperature. Record room temperature, asking the room assistant. Choose and measure HCl, distilled water and potassium triiodide labelled“I₂” into the reaction vessel. Initial concentrations should fall within: [H⁺]0.2–1.0mol dm⁻³, [I₃⁻]0.0005–0.002mol dm⁻³, [acetone]0.5–1.5mol dm⁻³. You need not explore full ranges, but values should not be significantly outside them.

Start by adding chosen acetone volume, quickly cap, start timer, shake vigorously once and set the vial on a white background. Record volumes in Table1.1. Do not hold/touch below liquid level while setting up/running. Watch yellow-brown triiodide colour disappear; record disappearance time. Leave completed vessels sealed to avoid iodoacetone vapours.

Repeat as desired at different concentrations; record in Table1.3. Hint: change one concentration at a time.

After acetone, investigate acetone-d₆. Acetone is ample, but expensive acetone-d₆ is limited to3.0cm³; each refill costs one point. Raise your hand for the supervisor to open the sealed ampoule. Deuterium-substituted compounds usually react slower; choose conditions promoting faster reactions with(CD₃)₂CO.

After finishing: (a) empty water bottle and return it with unused equipment to“Kit#1”; (b) place used pipettes/sealed used vials in designated hood containers; (c) dispose of every empty-ampoule part in“Broken Glass Disposal”. You may clean up after STOP.
