*The best ICHO-tasks of the last years — Amsterdam workshop, October 1990 (selection from 1980–1990)*

<Warning title="Бележка към темата">
This archive copy is the 1990 Amsterdam workshop top-twelve selection, containing eight theoretical tasks and four independent practical experiments from earlier Olympiads. 1990 identifies the workshop, not the year of every selected task. Solutions are attributed to the archive copy; no established official authorship is inferred. Practical1,2 and4 have no supplied keys. Separate report/answer forms referenced by Practical1,2 and3 are absent; Practical4 includes its blank answer sheet. Printed scientific and numerical discrepancies are retained.
</Warning>

## Условие

<nav className="problem-sections-toc" aria-label="Части на задачата">

**В тази задача**

- [Halle 1989 source edition — question and diagram variants](#volume2-edition-note)

</nav>

The course of chemical reactions can be followed by using labelled compounds. ³²P labelled phosphorus pentachloride (half life $t_{1/2}=14.3$ days) is employed to establish reaction (1) as an electrophilic attack on a $\mathrm{PCl}_4^+$ cation on nitrogen or on oxygen.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '89.6%', '--fig-max': '574px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/icho-best-1980-1990/p6-fig1-v2.png" alt="Original labelled phosphorus reaction (1), compounds I–IV and all atom labels." width="1794" height="342" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


The reaction is carried out in tetrachloromethane and then the solvent and IV is distilled off.

Individually, samples of:

- III, remaining in the distillation flask.
- IV, in the distillate, and
- the radio-labelled starting material, II

are hydrolysed by heating with sodium hydroxide solution. The phosphate ions formed are precipitated as ammonium magnesium phosphate. The compounds are then recrystallised and dried. Exactly weighed samples of the three precipitates are dissolved in known volumes. The radioactivity is then determined and the specific radioactivities of the phosphates are calculated (per unit mass).

**1)** Write the reaction equation for labelled red phosphorus forming phosphorus pentachloride.

**2)** Write the reaction equations for the complete hydrolysis of the compounds II and III using sodium hydroxide solution.

**3)** After how many days (an integer) does the radioactivity decay to 10⁻³ of the initial value?

**4)** Write two alternative mechanisms for the reaction of labelled $\mathrm{PCl}_4^-$ with the anion of I.

**5)** After hydrolysis, the precipitated ammonium magnesium phosphates possess the following values of radioactivity:

- II. 2380 Bq for 128 mg of Mg(NH₄)PO₄.
- III. 28 Bq for 153 mg of Mg(NH₄)PO₄.
- IV. 2627 Bq for 142 mg of Mg(NH₄)PO₄.

Using a calculation based on these data, what can you say about the nucleophilic centre attacked by $\mathrm{PCl}_4^+$?

**6)** Given the following data,

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathrm{H_3PO_4}:&\mathrm{p}K_{a1}=2.2,\quad\mathrm{p}K_{a2}=7.2,\quad\mathrm{p}K_{a3}=12.4,\\
\mathrm{p}K_{sp}\text{ of }\mathrm{Mg(NH_4)PO_4}&=12.6,
\end{array}
$$

and the equilibrium concentration of $\mathrm{NH}_4^+=0.1\,\mathrm{mol\,dm^{-3}}$. Calculate the solubility for Mg(NH₄)PO₄ in mol dm⁻³ under idealised conditions (activity coefficient equals one) at pH equals 10.

### 3)

In the time dt dN nuclei are degradated. With λ as radio-active constant follows

$$
\frac{dN}{dt}=-\lambda N(t).\qquad (4)
$$

Solution of equation(4) gives

$$
N(t)=N_0e^{-\lambda t}\quad\left(\text{ie}\ \ln\frac{N(t)}{N_0}=-\lambda t\right).\qquad (5)
$$

N₀ is the number of atoms present at time t=0. With half-life value

$$
\tau_{1/2}=\frac{\ln2}{\lambda},
$$

from equation(5) results

$$
\ln\frac{N(t)}{N_0}=-\frac{t}{\tau_{1/2}}\ln2,
$$

$$
t=-\frac{\tau_{1/2}\ln(N(t)/N_0)}{\ln2}.\qquad (6)
$$

Set in the quantities

$$
t=\frac{14.3\,\mathrm d\times\ln10^{-3}}{\ln2}=143\,\mathrm d.
$$

Source note: the last printed numerical substitution omits the preceding leading minus sign, while still printing 143 d.

</ProblemSection>

### 5)

Specific activities:

$$
A_{sp}(\mathrm{II})=18.6\,\mathrm{Bq\,mg^{-1}},\quad A_{sp}(\mathrm{III})=0.18\,\mathrm{Bq\,mg^{-1}},\quad A_{sp}(\mathrm{IV})=18.5\,\mathrm{Bq\,mg^{-1}}.
$$

Because $A_{sp}(\mathrm{II})\approx A_{sp}(\mathrm{IV})$, reaction(8) is right. $\mathrm{PCl_4^+}$ attacks the O-atom!

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-key-6">

### 6)

$$
K_L=c_{\mathrm{Mg^{2+}}}c_{\mathrm{NH_4^+}}c_{\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}}.\qquad (9)
$$

$$
\mathrm{H_3PO_4}+\mathrm{H_2O}\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{H_2PO_4^-}+\mathrm{H_3O^+},\quad\mathrm{p}K_{a1}=2.2.\qquad (10)
$$

$$
\mathrm{H_2PO_4^-}+\mathrm{H_2O}\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}+\mathrm{H_3O^+},\quad\mathrm{p}K_{a2}=7.2.\qquad (11)
$$

$$
\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}+\mathrm{H_2O}\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}+\mathrm{H_3O^+},\quad\mathrm{p}K_{a3}=12.4.\qquad (12)
$$

At pH=10 the main component is HPO₄²⁻. It results

$$
L=c_{\mathrm{Mg^{2+}}}=c_{\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}}.\qquad (13)
$$

From(12) follows

$$
c_{\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}}=K_{a3}\frac{c_{\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}}}{c_{\mathrm{H_3O^+}}}.\qquad (14)
$$

With(9),(13) and(14) results

$$
K_L=c_{\mathrm{NH_4^+}}LK_{a3}\frac L{c_{\mathrm{H_3O^+}}},\qquad L^2=\frac{K_Lc_{\mathrm{H_3O^+}}}{K_{a3}c_{\mathrm{NH_4^+}}},
$$

$$
\mathrm{p}L=\frac12(\mathrm{p}K_L+\mathrm{pH}-\mathrm{p}K_{a3}+\log c_{\mathrm{NH_4^+}}),
$$

$$
\mathrm{p}L=\frac12(12.6+10.0-12.4-1.0)=4.6,\qquad L=2.5\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{mol\,dm^{-3}}.
$$

</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-volume2-key-6">

### Volume 2 — 4.6

Given data:

$$
K_{\mathrm{sp}}=[\mathrm{Mg^{2+}}][\mathrm{NH_4^+}][\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}]=10^{-12.6};
\quad[\mathrm{NH_4^+}]=0.1;\quad\mathrm{pH}=10;
\quad pK_1=2.2;\quad pK_2=7.2;\quad pK_3=12.4.
$$

Exact solution:

$$
2[\mathrm{Mg^{2+}}]+[\mathrm{NH_4^+}]+[\mathrm{H_3O^+}]
=[\mathrm{H_2PO_4^-}]+2[\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}]+3[\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}]+[\mathrm{OH^-}],
$$

$$
[\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}]=\frac{[\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}][\mathrm{H^+}]}{K_3},
$$

$$
[\mathrm{H_2PO_4^-}]
=\frac{[\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}][\mathrm{H^+}]}{K_2}
=\frac{[\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}][\mathrm{H^+}]^2}{K_2K_3},
\qquad
[\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}]=\frac{K_{\mathrm{sp}}}{[\mathrm{NH_4^+}][\mathrm{Mg^{2+}}]}.
$$

The final exact-solution line is retained as printed, including $K_1K_3$:

$$
\Longrightarrow\quad
2[\mathrm{Mg^{2+}}]
=\left(\frac{[\mathrm{H^+}]^2}{K_1K_3}
+\frac{2[\mathrm{H^+}]}{K_3}+3\right)
\frac{K_{\mathrm{sp}}}{[\mathrm{NH_4^+}]}
-[\mathrm{Mg^{2+}}]\left([\mathrm{NH_4^+}]+[\mathrm{H^+}]-[\mathrm{OH^-}]\right),
$$

etc.

A simpler solution: At $\mathrm{pH}=10$ the main component is $\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}$:

$$
[\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}]=\frac{[\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}][\mathrm{H^+}]}{K_3}
=10^{2.4}[\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}],
$$

$$
[\mathrm{H_2PO_4^-}]
=\frac{[\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}][\mathrm{H^+}]}{K_2}
=10^{-2.8}[\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}].
$$

The collection prints the following literal definition and relation:

$$
S=[\mathrm{Mg^{2+}}][\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}],
\qquad
K_{\mathrm{sp}}=[\mathrm{NH_4^+}]\times S\times K_3\times\frac{S}{[\mathrm{H^+}]}.
$$

Its next line has an unclosed parenthesis before the first equals sign:

$$
pS=0.5(pK_{\mathrm{sp}}+\mathrm{pH}-pK_3-p[\mathrm{NH_4^+}]
=0.5(12.6+10.0-12.4-1.0)=4.6;
\qquad S=2.5\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{mol\,dm^{-3}}.
$$

</ProblemSection>
