## Условие

Extraction of gold using sodium cyanide, a very poisonous chemical, causes environmental problems and serious public concern about the “cyanide process”. Thiosulfate leaching is an alternative. The main reagent, ammonium thiosulfate (NH₄)₂S₂O₃, is relatively non-toxic. Although environmentally benign, the chemistry is complex and needs thorough study. The leaching solution contains S₂O₃²⁻, Cu²⁺, NH₃ and dissolved O₂, and must have pH greater than 8.5 for free ammonia.

The proposed mechanism forms a local voltaic micro-cell on gold particles:

**Anode:**

Au(s) + 2 NH₃(aq) → [Au(NH₃)₂]⁺(aq) + e⁻;

[Au(NH₃)₂]⁺(aq) + 2 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) → [Au(S₂O₃)₂]³⁻(aq) + 2 NH₃(aq).

**Cathode:**

[Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺(aq) + e⁻ → [Cu(NH₃)₂]⁺(aq) + 2 NH₃(aq);

[Cu(NH₃)₂]⁺(aq) + 3 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) → [Cu(S₂O₃)₃]⁵⁻(aq) + 2 NH₃(aq).

**6.1** Write the overall cell reaction for this voltaic cell.

**6.2** In the presence of ammonia [Cu(S₂O₃)₃]⁵⁻ is oxidized by O₂ back to [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺. Write a balanced equation for this oxidation-reduction in basic solution.

**6.3** In this leaching process [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺ functions as a catalyst and speeds dissolution of gold. Write the net overall oxidation-reduction reaction for dissolution of gold metal catalyzed by this ion.

**6.4** Draw the coordination geometries of the metal in [Au(NH₃)₂]⁺ and [Au(S₂O₃)₂]³⁻, indicating the coordinating atoms.

Their formation constants Kf are 1.00·10²⁶ and 1.00·10²⁸, respectively. Consider equilibrium concentrations [S₂O₃²⁻] = 0.100; [NH₃] = 0.100 and total concentration of gold(I) species = 5.50·10⁻⁵ mol dm⁻³.

**6.5** Calculate the percentage of gold(I) existing as thiosulfate complex.

When O₂ concentration is not high enough and pH > 10, S₂O₃²⁻ reduces [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺ to [Cu(S₂O₃)₃]⁵⁻ with tetrathionate S₄O₆²⁻ formation:

2 [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺(aq) + 8 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) → 2 [Cu(S₂O₃)₃]⁵⁻(aq) + S₄O₆²⁻(aq) + 8 NH₃(aq).

In basic solution tetrathionate disproportionates to trithionate S₃O₆²⁻ and thiosulfate.

**6.6** Write a balanced equation for this disproportionation reaction.

**6.7** When O₂ concentration is too high, S₂O₃²⁻ is oxidized by oxygen to yield trithionate and sulfate ions. Write a balanced equation.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

**6.1** Net anode half reaction:

Au(s) + 2 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) → [Au(S₂O₃)₂]³⁻(aq) + e⁻.

Net cathode half reaction:

[Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺(aq) + 3 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) + e⁻ → [Cu(S₂O₃)₃]⁵⁻(aq) + 4 NH₃(aq).

Overall cell reaction (native heading “erall cell reaction”):

Au(s) + [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺(aq) + 5 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) → [Au(S₂O₃)₂]³⁻(aq) + [Cu(S₂O₃)₃]⁵⁻(aq) + 4 NH₃(aq).

**6.2** Oxidation half reaction:

[Cu(S₂O₃)₃]⁵⁻(aq) + 4 NH₃(aq) → [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺(aq) + 3 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) + e⁻.

Reduction half reaction: O₂(g) + 2 H₂O(l) + 4 e⁻ → 4 OH⁻(aq).

Redox reaction:

4 [Cu(S₂O₃)₃]⁵⁻(aq) + 16 NH₃(aq) + O₂(g) + 2 H₂O(l) → 4 [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺(aq) + 12 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) + 4 OH⁻(aq).

**6.3** Sum the equations:

Au(s) + [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺(aq) + 5 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) → [Au(S₂O₃)₂]³⁻(aq) + [Cu(S₂O₃)₃]⁵⁻(aq) + 4 NH₃(aq);

4 [Cu(S₂O₃)₃]⁵⁻(aq) + 16 NH₃(aq) + O₂(g) + 2 H₂O(l) → 4 [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺(aq) + 12 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) + 4 OH⁻(aq).

Summarizing:

4 Au(s) + 8 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) + O₂(g) + 2 H₂O(l) → 4 [Au(S₂O₃)₂]³⁻(aq) + 4 OH⁻(aq).

**6.4** Coordination geometry: [H₃N–Au–NH₃]⁺ and [O₃S–S–Au–S–SO₃]³⁻, as the native straight-line drawings indicate.

**6.5** Au⁺(aq) + 2 NH₃(aq) → [Au(NH₃)₂]⁺(aq), Kf,1 = 1.00·10²⁶.

Au⁺(aq) + 2 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) → [Au(S₂O₃)₂]³⁻(aq), Kf,2 = 1.00·10²⁸.

Thus [Au(NH₃)₂]⁺(aq) + 2 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) → [Au(S₂O₃)₂]³⁻(aq) + 2 NH₃(aq), with Keq = Kf,2/Kf,1 = 1.00·10².

$$
[\mathrm{Au(NH_3)_2^+}]+[\mathrm{Au(S_2O_3)_2^{3-}}]=5.50\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{mol\,dm^{-3}}.
$$

$$
K_{\rm eq}=\frac{(0.100)^2x}{(5.50\cdot10^{-5}-x)(0.100)^2}=1.00\cdot10^2,\quad x=5.445\cdot10^{-5}.
$$

$$
\frac{5.445\cdot10^{-5}}{5.50\cdot10^{-5}}\times100=99.0\%.
$$

Thus 99.0% of Au(I) is [Au(S₂O₃)₂]³⁻.

**6.6** S₄O₆²⁻(aq) + 2 e⁻ → 2 S₂O₃²⁻(aq);

12 OH⁻(aq) + 3 S₄O₆²⁻(aq) → 4 S₃O₆²⁻(aq) + 6 H₂O(l) + 10 e⁻.

Disproportionation:

4 S₄O₆²⁻(aq) + 6 OH⁻(aq) → 5 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) + 2 S₃O₆²⁻(aq) + 3 H₂O(l).

**6.7** 2 S₂O₃²⁻(aq) + 2 O₂(g) → SO₄²⁻(aq) + S₃O₆²⁻(aq).
