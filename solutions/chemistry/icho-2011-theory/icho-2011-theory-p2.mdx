## Условие

Anhydrous ammonia is an ultra-clean, energy-dense alternative liquid fuel. It produces no greenhouse gases on combustion. In an experiment, gaseous NH₃ is burned with O₂ in a container of fixed volume according to the equation:

$$
4\,\mathrm{NH_3}(g)+3\,\mathrm{O_2}(g)\to2\,\mathrm{N_2}(g)+6\,\mathrm{H_2O}(l).
$$

The initial and final states are at 298 K. After combustion with 14.40 g of O₂, some of NH₃ remains unreacted.

**2.1** Calculate the heat released during the process. ΔfH°(NH₃(g)) = −46.11 kJ mol⁻¹ and ΔfH°(H₂O(l)) = −285.83 kJ mol⁻¹.

To determine the amount of NH₃ gas dissolved in water, produced during the combustion process, a 10.00 cm³ sample of the aqueous solution was withdrawn from the reaction vessel and added to 15.0 cm³ of a H₂SO₄ solution (c = 0.0100 mol dm⁻³). The resulting solution was titrated with a standard NaOH solution (c = 0.0200 mol dm⁻³) and the equivalence point was reached at 10.64 cm³. Kb(NH₃) = 1.8·10⁻⁵; Ka(HSO₄⁻) = 1.1·10⁻².

**2.2** Calculate pH of the solution in the container after combustion.

**2.3** At the end point of titration, NH₄⁺ and SO₄²⁻ ions are present in the solution. Write the equations for the relevant equilibria to show how the presence of these two ions affects the pH and calculate their equilibrium constant(s).

**2.4** Tick the correct statement for the pH of the solution at the equivalence point: pH > 7; pH = 7; pH < 7.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

**2.1** $q_v=\Delta E=\Delta H-\Delta n_gRT$. For 1 mole of NH₃:

$$
\Delta H=\frac32(-285.83)-(-46.11)=-382.64\,\mathrm{kJ},\qquad\Delta n_g=-1.25\,\mathrm{mol}.
$$

$$
\Delta E=-382.64\,\mathrm{kJ}-[(-1.25\,\mathrm{mol})\times8.314\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}\times298\,\mathrm K]=-379.5\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$

$$
n(\mathrm{O_2})=\frac{14.4\,\mathrm g}{32.0\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}}=0.450\,\mathrm{mol},\quad n(\mathrm{NH_3})_{\rm reacted}=0.450\times\frac43=0.600\,\mathrm{mol}.
$$

$$
q_v=\Delta E=0.600\,\mathrm{mol}\times(-379.5\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}})=-227.7\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$

**2.2** Total $n(\mathrm{H_2SO_4})=15.00\,\mathrm{cm^3}\times0.0100\,\mathrm{mol\,dm^{-3}}=0.150\,\mathrm{mmol}$.

H₂SO₄ + 2 NaOH → Na₂SO₄ + 2 H₂O. After back titration with NaOH:

$$
n(\mathrm{H_2SO_4})_{\rm reacted}=\tfrac12n(\mathrm{NaOH})_{\rm reacted}=\tfrac12(0.01064\,\mathrm{dm^3}\times0.0200\,\mathrm{mol\,dm^{-3}})=1.064\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{mol}=0.1064\,\mathrm{mmol}.
$$

$n(\mathrm{H_2SO_4})$ reacted with NH₃ = 0.0436 mmol. 2 NH₃ + H₂SO₄ → (NH₄)₂SO₄.

$$
n(\mathrm{NH_3})=2\times0.0436=0.0872\,\mathrm{mmol},\quad c(\mathrm{NH_3})=\frac{0.0872\,\mathrm{mmol}}{0.0100\,\mathrm{dm^3}}=8.72\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol\,dm^{-3}}.
$$

NH₃(aq) + H₂O(l) ⇌ NH₄⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq), with equilibrium amounts c−x, x, x.

$$
K_b=1.8\cdot10^{-5}=\frac{x^2}{0.00872-x},\quad x=[\mathrm{OH^-}]=3.96\cdot10^{-4},\quad\mathrm{pOH}=3.41,\quad\mathrm{pH}=10.59.
$$

**2.3** SO₄²⁻(aq) + H₂O(l) ⇌ HSO₄⁻(aq) + OH⁻(aq).

$$
K_b=\frac{K_w}{K_a}=\frac{1.0\cdot10^{-14}}{1.1\cdot10^{-2}}=9.1\cdot10^{-13}.
$$

NH₄⁺(aq) + H₂O(l) ⇌ NH₃(aq) + H₃O⁺(aq).

$$
K_a=\frac{K_w}{K_b}=\frac{1.0\cdot10^{-14}}{1.8\cdot10^{-5}}=5.6\cdot10^{-10}.
$$

**2.4** Correct answer: pH < 7.0.
