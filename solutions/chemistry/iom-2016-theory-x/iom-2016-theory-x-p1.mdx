**Problem 1. Circulation of carbon dioxide (12 marks)**

| Question | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Points | 2 | 6 | 4 | 8 | **20** |

Moscow is one of the greenest metropolises of the world. Green area in Moscow occupies $340\ \mathrm{km^2}$, that is about $1/3$ of the total area (without the new territories). Under the action of light green plants absorb carbon dioxide and evolve oxygen in a photosynthesis process, which is described by the simplified equation:
$$
\mathrm{H_2O(l)+CO_2(g)\rightarrow CH_2O(aq)+O_2(g)}.
$$
where $\mathrm{CH_2O}$ denote all carbohydrates formed.

**1** Determine the signs of enthalpy and Gibbs energy for the above reaction.

| Function | $\Delta_\mathrm{r}H^\circ$ | $\Delta_\mathrm{r}G^\circ$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign (+, −, 0) |  |  |

**[2 т.]**

**2** Calculate the volume of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ (in $\mathrm{m^3}$, at $25^\circ\mathrm C$ and $1\ \mathrm{atm}$) and the mass of carbohydrates (in kg) formed during a sunny summer day (8 h) in Moscow. The necessary information:

- solar energy absorbed by Moscow region in summer time – $150\ \mathrm{W\cdot m^{-2}}$;
- the Gibbs energy of photosynthesis is $480\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$ of $\mathrm{CO_2}$;
- green plants absorb ~10% of the available solar energy;
- 25% of the absorbed energy is used for the photosynthesis process.

Calculations:

$V(\mathrm{CO_2})=$ _______ $\mathrm{m^3}$

$m(\mathrm{CH_2O})=$ ______ kg **[6 т.]**

At the same time there are 4 million of cars in Moscow that strongly pollute the air of the city.

**3** How many kilometers (in average) can a typical Moscow car run during one day so that all carbon dioxide from the car exhaust be completely absorbed by green plants within the city? Assume that the formula of car fuel is $\mathrm{C_8H_{18}}$, the fuel density is $0.7\ \mathrm{kg/L}$, the fuel consumption by an average car in the city is $10\ \mathrm L/100\ \mathrm{km}$.

Calculations:

The car run = _____ km **[4 т.]**

In addition to natural photosynthesis, $\mathrm{CO_2}$ can be also utilized by artificial chemical processes designed by chemical engineers. ### Official answers and grading scheme

**1** Determine the signs of enthalpy and Gibbs energy for the above reaction.

| Function | $\Delta_\mathrm rH^\circ$ | $\Delta_\mathrm rG^\circ$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign (+, −, 0) | + | + |

**1 pt** for each correct answer, total – **2 pts**.

**2** Calculate the volume of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ consumed (in $\mathrm{m^3}$, at $25^\circ\mathrm C$ and $1\ \mathrm{atm}$) and the mass of carbohydrates formed (in kg) during a sunny summer day (8 h) in Moscow. The necessary information:

- solar energy absorbed by Moscow region in summer time – $150\ \mathrm{W\cdot m^{-2}}$;
- the Gibbs energy of photosynthesis is $480\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$ of $\mathrm{CO_2}$;
- green plants absorb ~10% of the available solar energy;
- 25% of the absorbed energy is used for the photosynthesis process.

Calculations:

Energy absorbed by the plants:
$$
150\frac{\mathrm J}{\mathrm{s\cdot m^2}}\cdot8\cdot3600\ \mathrm s\cdot340\cdot10^6\ \mathrm{m^2}\cdot\frac{10\%}{100\%}=1.47\cdot10^{14}\ \mathrm J.
$$
**2 pts**

The quantity of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ and $\mathrm{CH_2O}$:
$$
n(\mathrm{CO_2})=n(\mathrm{CH_2O})=\frac{1.47\cdot10^{14}\ \mathrm J\cdot\frac{25\%}{100\%}}{480\cdot10^3\ \mathrm{J/mol}}=7.65\cdot10^7\ \mathrm{mol}.
$$
**2 pts**

The molar volume of a gas at $25^\circ\mathrm C$ and $1\ \mathrm{atm}$:
$$
V_\mathrm m=\frac{RT}{P}=\frac{8.314\cdot298}{1.013\cdot10^5}=0.0245\ \mathrm{m^3/mol}.
$$
$$
V(\mathrm{CO_2})=7.65\cdot10^7\cdot0.0245=\mathbf{1.87\cdot10^6\ \mathrm{m^3}}.
$$
**1 pt**
$$
m(\mathrm{CH_2O})=7.65\cdot10^7\cdot0.030=\mathbf{2.30\cdot10^6\ \mathrm{kg}}.
$$
**1 pt**

**Total – 6 pts**

**3** Calculations:
$$
\mathrm{C_8H_{18}\rightarrow8CO_2}
$$
**1 pt**
$$
n(\mathrm{C_8H_{18}})=n(\mathrm{CO_2})/8=9.56\cdot10^6\ \mathrm{mol}
$$
**1 pt**
$$
m(\mathrm{C_8H_{18}})=9.56\cdot10^6\ \mathrm{mol}\cdot0.114\ \mathrm{kg/mol}=1.09\cdot10^6\ \mathrm{kg}
$$
$$
V(\mathrm{C_8H_{18}})=1.09\cdot10^6\ \mathrm{kg}/0.7\ \mathrm{kg/L}=1.56\cdot10^6\ \mathrm L
$$
**1 pt**

The average run for one car is:
$$
\frac{1.56\cdot10^6\ \mathrm L}{4\cdot10^6}\cdot\frac{100\ \mathrm{km}}{10\ \mathrm L}=3.9\ \mathrm{km}
$$
**1 pt**

The car run = $3.9\ \mathrm{km}$

**Total – 4 pts**

**4** a) Substance – $\mathrm{CaCO_3}$

Reaction equation: $\mathrm{CaO+CO_2=CaCO_3}$

b) Substance – urea, $\mathrm{(NH_2)_2CO}$

Reaction equation: $\mathrm{2NH_3+CO_2=(NH_2)_2CO+H_2O}$

c) Substance – $\mathrm{CH_3OH}$

Reaction equation: $\mathrm{CO_2+3H_2=CH_3OH+H_2O}$

d) Substance – $\mathrm{CH_4}$

Reaction equation: $\mathrm{CO_2+4H_2=CH_4+2H_2O}$

Each substance – **1 pt**

Each balanced equation – **1 pt** (0.5 pts – if not balanced, but with correct substances)

Total – **8 pts**
