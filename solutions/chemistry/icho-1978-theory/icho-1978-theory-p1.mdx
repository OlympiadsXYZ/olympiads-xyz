in particular solutions of the problems set in the first years of the IChO competition that 
were often available in a brief form and necessary extent only, just for the needs of

## Условие


- a) A chromium ore which does not contain water, consists of: Fe(CrO₂)₂, Mg(CrO₂)₂, MgCO₃, and CaSiO₃.
- b) It was found by analysis the ore contains 45.6% of Cr₂O₃, 7.98% of Fe₂O₃, and 16.12% of MgO.
- c) When the ore was treated with a concentrated hydrochloric acid, chromium compounds being present in the ore did not react with the acid.
- d) When the reaction was finished, the ore was thoroughly washed with water (till the reaction with Cl⁻ was negative) and the solid residue was dried to a constant mass.

**1.1**   
Write stoichiometric and ionic equations for the reactions taking place when the ore is treated with the hydrochloric acid as given in c).



**1.2**   
Calculate:

- the content of the compounds (in mass%) present in the ore,
- amounts of substances of the compounds present in the ore.



**1.3**   
Calculate the content of Cr₂O₃ (in mass%) in the dried residue obtained according to d).



**1.4**   
A glass tube was filled with a sufficient amount of granulated CaO, the total mass of the filled tube having been 412.02 g. A gas formed by the reaction as given in c), was dried and then transmitted through the glass tube. Calculate the mass of the glass tube with its filling after the reaction was finished.

Relative atomic masses:

$$A_r(\mathrm{Cr})=52.01;\quad A_r(\mathrm{Fe})=55.85;\quad A_r(\mathrm{Mg})=24.32;\quad A_r(\mathrm{Ca})=40.08;$$
$$A_r(\mathrm{Si})=28.09;\quad A_r(\mathrm C)=12.01;\quad A_r(\mathrm O)=16.00.$$



### Source notes

The source question gives 45.6% Cr₂O₃; the key line prints 45.5 − 15.19 = 30.41. Native numerical values, the 223.7 g table entry and the missing unit after 107.65 are retained literally.




## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">




<ProblemSection id="solution-native-key-1">

### 1.1


$$\mathrm{MgCO_3}+2\mathrm{HCl}\rightarrow\mathrm{MgCl_2}+\mathrm{CO_2}+\mathrm{H_2O}$$
$$\mathrm{MgCO_3}+2\mathrm{H^+}\rightarrow\mathrm{Mg^{2+}}+\mathrm{CO_2}+\mathrm{H_2O}$$
$$\mathrm{CaSiO_3}+2\mathrm{HCl}\rightarrow\mathrm{CaCl_2}+\mathrm{SiO_2}+\mathrm{H_2O}$$
$$\mathrm{CaSiO_3}+2\mathrm{H^+}\rightarrow\mathrm{Ca^{2+}}+\mathrm{SiO_2}+\mathrm{H_2O}$$



</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-native-key-2">

### 1.2


The total amount of iron is in the form of Fe(CrO₂)₂.

Since:

$$2\mathrm{Fe(CrO_2)_2}\longleftrightarrow\mathrm{Fe_2O_3}$$
$$M_r(\mathrm{Fe_2O_3})=159.70,\quad M_r(\mathrm{Fe(CrO_2)_2})=223.87,\quad\%\mathrm{Fe_2O_3}=7.98$$
$$\%\mathrm{Fe(CrO_2)_2}=\frac{2\times223.87}{159.70}\times7.98=22.37$$

The difference between the total amount of Cr₂O₃ and that being contained in Fe(CrO₂)₂ corresponds to the amount of Cr₂O₃, having been in the form of Mg(CrO₂)₂.

% Cr₂O₃ in Fe(CrO₂)₂:

$$\mathrm{Fe(CrO_2)_2}\longleftrightarrow\mathrm{Cr_2O_3}$$

$M_r$: 223.87; 152.02.

$$\%\mathrm{Cr_2O_3}=\frac{152.02}{223.87}\times22.37=15.19$$

% Cr₂O₃ in Mg(CrO₂)₂: $45.5-15.19=30.41$.

Content of Mg(CrO₂)₂:

$$\mathrm{Cr_2O_3}\longleftrightarrow\mathrm{Mg(CrO_2)_2}$$

$M_r$: 152.02; 192.34.

$$\%\mathrm{Mg(CrO_2)_2}=\frac{192.34}{152.02}\times30.41=38.47$$

The difference between the total amount of MgO in the ore and that corresponding to Mg(CrO₂)₂, is contained in MgCO₃. % MgO and % MgCO₃ can be calculated analogously as it is given above.

$$\mathrm{Mg(CrO_2)_2}\longleftrightarrow\mathrm{MgO}$$

$M_r$: 192.34; 40.32.

$$\%\mathrm{MgO}=\frac{40.32}{192.34}\times38.47=8.06$$

$$\mathrm{MgO}\longleftrightarrow\mathrm{MgCO_3}$$

$M_r$: 40.32; 84.32.

$$\%\mathrm{MgCO_3}=\frac{84.32}{40.32}\times8.06=16.86$$

Content of CaSiO₃ is obtained as complementary value to 100%.

$$\%\mathrm{CaSiO_3}=100-(22.37+38.47+16.86)=22.30$$

One kilogram of the ore contains:

| Compound mass | Amount |
|---|---|
| 223.7 g of Fe(CrO₂)₂ | 1 mol |
| 384.7 g of Mg(CrO₂)₂ | 2 mol |
| 168.6 g of MgCO₃ | 2 mol |
| 223.0 g of CaSiO₃ | 2 mol |



</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-native-key-3">

### 1.3


In order to simplify the problem we can assume that the hydrochloric acid reacts with 1 kg of the ore, i.e. with 168.6 g of MgCO₃ and with that CaO which is contained in 223.0 g CaSiO₃, i.e. with 107.65 of CaO.

Thus, 276.25 g of the ore (168.6 g + 107.65 g) reacted while 723.75 g remain unreacted.

One kilogram of the ore contains 456 g of Cr₂O₃ (45.6%) and the same amount remains in the unreacted part that represents:

$$\%\mathrm{Cr_2O_3}=\frac{456}{723.75}\times100=63.0$$



</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-native-key-4">

### 1.4


The mass of the filling in the tube is increased by the mass of CO₂ formed by decomposition of MgCO₃ with hydrochloric acid. From 168.6 g of MgCO₃ 87.98 g of CO₂ are formed and thus, the mass of the tube after reaction is 500 g.



</ProblemSection>
