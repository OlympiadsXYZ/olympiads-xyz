## Условие


Chromium plating is usually made by electrolysis in a solution of chromic acid. The chromium plated objects form the cathode. The anode is an alloy that is inert under given conditions, i.e. it does not react either chemically or electrochemically.

An electrolytic cell was filled with 100.0 dm³ of an aqueous solution which contained 0.230 kg of chromium acid anhydride in 1 dm³ of the solution.

In electrolysis a current of 1500 A passed through the electrolyte for 10.0 hours. After electrolysis an increase of the mass of the cathode was 0.679 kg.

The ratio of gas volumes:

$$\frac{V_C}{V_A}=1.603$$

where $V_C$ is a volume of gases evolved at the cathode, whereas that marked as $V_A$ is the volume of gases which are evolved at the anode. Both volumes were measured at the same conditions.

**3.1**   
What part of the total charge (in%) was used for a deposition of 0.679 kg of chromium?



**3.2**   
Calculate:

- a) the volume ratio of both gases (at STP) which are evolved as by-products at the cathode and anode,
- b) current efficiency for the corresponding reactions taking place separately at the cathode and anode when the gases are evolved.

If you find any disproportion between the data calculated and those given in the task, try to explain what process would take place in the electrolytic cell which has not been considered till now.

Write the corresponding summary equation for the reactions at electrodes and correct your previous calculations if possible.



## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">




<ProblemSection id="solution-native-key-1">

### 3.1


The total electric charge passed through the electrolyte:

$$Q=\frac{1500\times3600\times10}{96500}=559.6F$$

Reaction at the cathode:

$$\mathrm{Cr^{VI}}+6e^-\rightarrow\mathrm{Cr^0}$$

or

$$\mathrm{CrO_4^{2-}}+8\mathrm{H^+}+6e^-\rightarrow\mathrm{Cr}+4\mathrm{H_2O}$$

Deposited:

$$\frac{679\ \mathrm g}{51.996\ \mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}}=13.06\ \text{mol of chromium}$$

A charge of 78.36 $F$ was required to deposit the above chromium.

Current efficiency:

$$\frac{78.36F}{559.6F}\times100=14.0\%$$



</ProblemSection>

<ProblemSection id="solution-native-key-2">

### 3.2


The simplest assumption: Only hydrogen is evolved at the cathode and at the same time oxygen at the anode. On this assumption the amounts of substances of the evolved oxygen and hydrogen are as follows:

$$n(\mathrm{H_2})=\frac{559.6\times0.86}{2}=240.63\ \mathrm{mol}$$
$$n(\mathrm{O_2})=\frac{559.6}{4}=139.9\ \mathrm{mol}$$

The molar ratio is:

$$\frac{n(\mathrm{H_2})}{n(\mathrm{O_2})}=\frac{V(\mathrm{H_2})}{V(\mathrm{O_2})}=\frac{240.63\ \mathrm{mol}}{139.9\ \mathrm{mol}}=1.720$$

This value is different from that given in the task. Thus, beyond the mentioned reactions also other processes take place at the electrodes. The current efficiency may be calculated from the volume ratio of gases evolved, without making any investigation of what kind the processes are.

**Balance of the processes**

The main process:

$$\mathrm{CrO_3}\rightarrow\underbrace{\mathrm{Cr}}_{\text{cathode}}+\underbrace{\tfrac32\mathrm{O_2}}_{\text{anode}}$$
$$\eta_1=14.0\%$$

The by process (electrolysis of water):

$$2\mathrm{H_2O}\rightarrow\underbrace{2\mathrm{H_2}}_{\text{cathode}}+\underbrace{\mathrm{O_2}}_{\text{anode}}$$
$$\eta_2=?$$

The amount of substance of the hydrogen evolved at the cathode is equal to:

$$n(\mathrm{H_2})=\frac{Q\eta_2}{2}$$

The amount of substance of the oxygen evolved at the anode is equal to:

$$n(\mathrm{O_2})=\frac{Q(\eta_1+\eta_2)}4$$

According to the data given in the task:

$$
\frac{V(\mathrm{H_2})}{V(\mathrm{O_2})}=\frac{n(\mathrm{H_2})}{n(\mathrm{O_2})}
=\frac{Q\eta_2/2}{Q(\eta_1+\eta_2)/4}=1.603
$$

In solving the equation for $\eta_2$ we get a value:

$$\eta_2=0.565\quad(56.5\%)$$

Volumes of the hydrogen and oxygen evolved:

$$n(\mathrm{H_2})=\frac{559.6\times0.565}{2}=158.1\ \mathrm{mol}$$
$$V(\mathrm{H_2})=22.41\ \mathrm{dm^3\ mol^{-1}}\times158.1\ \mathrm{mol}=3543\ \mathrm{dm^3}$$
$$n(\mathrm{O_2})=\frac{559.6\times(0.140+0.565)}4=98.6\ \mathrm{mol}$$
$$V(\mathrm{O_2})=22.41\ \mathrm{dm^3\ mol^{-1}}\times98.6\ \mathrm{mol}=2210\ \mathrm{dm^3}$$

The current efficiency when the hydrogen is evolved at the cathode is equal to 56.5%. The current efficiency when the oxygen is evolved at the anode is equal to 70.5%.

Thus, 29.5% of the electric charge is used without an apparent effect. Therefore some cyclic process is taking place in the electrolytic cell which causes that anion CrO₄²⁻ is reduced incompletely. One of the reactions which causes a decrease of the current efficiency value, is the following:
