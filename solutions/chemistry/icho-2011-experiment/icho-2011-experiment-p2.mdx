## Условие

Hydrogen is considered a clean and environmentally benign new energy carrier towards a sustainable future. Effective, safe hydrogen storage is a key issue. Among potent solid chemical-hydride storage materials, ammonia-borane H₃N·BH₃ attracts attention because of high hydrogen content and stability under fuel-cell conditions. Hydrolysis releases hydrogen:

H₃N·BH₃(aq) + 2 H₂O(l) → NH₄BO₂(aq) + 3 H₂(g). (1)

Aqueous ammonia-borane is stable and hydrolysis occurs only with a suitable catalyst. Water-soluble-polymer-stabilized palladium(0) nanoclusters are highly active. They are generated in situ by reducing potassium tetrachloropalladate(II) with ammonia borane in poly(4-styrenesulfonic acid-co-maleic acid).

The experiment starts with potassium tetrachloropalladate(II) precatalyst in polymer solution. Ammonia borane reduces it; polymer-stabilized palladium(0) nanoclusters form and catalyze hydrolysis.

**Chemicals**

- Solution A: H₃NBH₃, 29.5 mg in 10 cm³ H₂O.
- Solution B: poly(4-styrenesulfonic acid-co-maleic acid), 137.7 mg in 9 cm³ H₂O.
- Solution C: potassium tetrachloropalladate(II), K₂[PdCl₄], 6.7 mg in 1 cm³ H₂O.

**I. Preparation of the Experimental Set-up**

1. Check that the setup shown is held on a support, the graduated tube is connected to the Schlenk tube with Tygon tubing and a stir bar is inside the Schlenk tube.
2. Make sure the septum is off and valve open.
3. Change bulb height to adjust the graduated-tube water level to zero.
4. Close the Schlenk-tube valve.

Experimental Set-up.

**II. Hydrolysis of ammonia borane**

**A. In the absence of catalyst**

1. Transfer all Solution A from its glass vial through the funnel to the Schlenk tube.
2. Add Solution B likewise through the funnel.
3. Close with septum, stir at 600 rpm as marked and open the valve to the graduated tube. Record water level V₀ at time zero. Start timer.
4. Every minute read total gas volume and enter the answer-sheet table, for 10 minutes. Stop timer.

**B. In the presence of catalyst**

1. While stirring, inject all Solution C from its glass vial through septum with a 2.0 cm³ syringe. Keep syringe inserted throughout. Start timer.
2. Every minute read total gas volume and enter the answer-sheet table, for 10 minutes. Stop timer.

**Treatment of Data — A. Reaction without catalyst**

**2.1** Plot recorded gas volume against time in Graph2.1.

**2.2** Report the gas volume evolved as Vuncatalyzed.

**B. Reaction with catalyst**

**2.3** Plot generated gas volume against time in Graph2.2.

**2.4** Calculate maximum theoretical hydrogen amount in mol and volume in cm³ from hydrolysis of 29.5 mg ammonia borane of purity 97% w/w at 25 °C and atmospheric pressure 690 torr.

**2.5** Calculate hydrogen-generation rate in your experiment:

(i) In cm³ H₂/min.

(ii) In mmol H₂/min, assuming 25 °C and atmospheric pressure 690 torr.

**2.6** Based on your experiment calculate hydrogen-production rate per mole of palladium in (mol H₂)(mol Pd)⁻¹(min)⁻¹. Potassium tetrachloropalladate(II) purity is 98% w/w.
