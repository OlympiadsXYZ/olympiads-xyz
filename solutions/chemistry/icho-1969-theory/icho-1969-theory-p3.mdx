## Условие

A 10 cm³ sample of an unknown gaseous hydrocarbon was mixed with 70 cm³ of oxygen and the mixture was set on fire by means of an electric spark. When the reaction was over and water vapours were liquefied, the final volume of gases decreased to 65 cm³. This mixture then reacted with a potassium hydroxide solution and the volume of gases decreased to 45 cm³.

What is the molecular formula of the unknown hydrocarbon if volumes of gases were measured at standard temperature and pressure (STP) conditions?

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

The unknown gaseous hydrocarbon has the general formula: CₓHᵧ

$$
n(\mathrm{C_xH_y})=\frac{0.010\,\mathrm{dm^3}}{22.4\,\mathrm{dm^3\,mol^{-1}}}=\frac{0.010}{22.4}\,\mathrm{mol}.
$$

Balance of oxygen:

- Before the reaction: 70 cm³, i.e. $\frac{0.070}{22.4}\,\mathrm{mol}$
- After the reaction: 45 cm³, i.e. $\frac{0.045}{22.4}\,\mathrm{mol}$

Consumed in the reaction: $\frac{0.025}{22.4}\,\mathrm{mol}$ of O₂

According to the equation:

$$
\mathrm{C_xH_y}+\left(x+\frac y4\right)\mathrm{O_2}=x\mathrm{CO_2}+\frac y2\mathrm{H_2O}.
$$

Hence, $\frac{0.020}{22.4}$ mol of O₂ reacted with carbon and $\frac{0.020}{22.4}$ mol of CO₂ was formed (C + O₂ = CO₂), $\frac{0.005}{22.4}$ mol O₂ combined with hydrogen and $\frac{0.010}{22.4}$ mol of water was obtained (2 H₂ + O₂ = 2 H₂O).

$$
3n(\mathrm C)=n(\mathrm{CO_2})=\frac{0.020}{22.4}\,\mathrm{mol},
$$

$$
n(\mathrm{H_2})=2n(\mathrm{H_2O})=\frac{0.020}{22.4}\,\mathrm{mol},
$$

$$
x:y=n(\mathrm C):n(\mathrm{H_2})=0.020:0.020=1:1.
$$

From the possible solutions C₂H₂, C₃H₃, C₄H₄, C₅H₅.only C₂H₂ satisfies to the conditions given in the task, i.e. the unknown hydrocarbon is acetylene.

Source note: The initial 3 before n(C), the hydrogen amount notation and the printed candidate formulas are retained literally from native page 19.
