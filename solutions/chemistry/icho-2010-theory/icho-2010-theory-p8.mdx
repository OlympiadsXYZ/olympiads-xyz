## Условие

The esterification reaction between bi-functional molecules gives one of the typical linear chain polymers, as shown in equation (1), by polycondensation (often called “condensation polymerization”). The control of polymerization conditions and procedures determines the length of polymer strands, i.e. the average degree of polymerization, $\bar X$ (note that $\bar X=2\bar n$ in the present instance). Because $\bar X$ (and also $\bar n$) is an averaged number, it is not always an integer but a value with decimal figures.

$$
n\,\mathrm{HOOC{-}R^1{-}COOH}+n\,\mathrm{HO{-}R^2{-}OH}\longrightarrow\mathrm{HO{-}[COR^1CO{-}OR^2O]_n{-}H}+(2n-1)\mathrm{H_2O}.\qquad (1)
$$

$\bar X$ can be estimated from the consumption of functional groups (here, −COOH and −OH). Let us define the degree of reaction, p, as $p=(N_0-N)/N_0\le1$, where N₀ and N denote the total numbers of functional groups before and after the polymerization, respectively. For each functional group of the dicarboxylic acid molecules (A) and diol molecules (B), we add the suffixes of “A” or “B” such as Nₐ₀, Nᵦ₀, Nₐ or Nᵦ, respectively, i.e. N₀ = Nₐ₀ + Nᵦ₀ and N = Nₐ + Nᵦ. When the initial feed is unbalanced such as Nₐ₀ ≤ Nᵦ₀, $\bar X$ is expressed by pₐ and r as shown in equation 2, where r = Nₐ₀/Nᵦ₀ ≤ 1 and pₐ = (Nₐ₀ − Nₐ)/Nₐ₀. If r = 1, pₐ is identical to p and equation 2 becomes the same to the Carothers equation.

$$
\bar X=\frac{1+r}{1+r-2p_Ar}.\qquad (2)
$$

Some nylon-6,6 sample was prepared by polycondensation between an equimolar mixture of adipic acid (hexanedioic acid) and hexamethylenediamine (hexane-1,6-diamine).

**8.1** Show the chemical structure of this nylon-6,6 sample. [Caution: What are the end groups when polycondensation was started from the equimolar mixture?]

**8.2** When an average molar weight, $\bar M$, of this nylon-6,6 sample is 5507.25 (g mol⁻¹), calculate its $\bar X$ value to the second decimal place.

**8.3** Give the p value to the fifth decimal place that is necessary to prepare the above nylon-6,6 sample. If you get no numerical answer in 8.2 use the value 52.50 instead.

The low-molecular-weight polyester (oligoester) is prepared from the mixture of 36.54 g of adipic acid (hexanedioic acid) and an unknown amount [W (g)] of butane-1,4-diol (Bdiol). Under the condition of $p_A\to1$, the oligoester with $\bar X=11.00$ carrying Bdiol units in both chain ends is obtained.

**8.4** Show the precise chemical structure of this oligoester of $\bar X=11.00$.

**8.5** Calculate the unknown amount, W (g), to the first decimal place.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

**8.1** HO–[CO(CH₂)₄CO–NH(CH₂)₆NH]ₙ–H or equivalent structures.

**8.2** The unit molecular weight, Mᵤ, is calculated as follows:

$$
M_u=\frac{12.01\times12+1.01\times22+14.01\times2+16.00\times2}{2}=\frac{226.36}{2}=113.18,
$$

$$
\bar X=\frac{5507.25-18.02}{M_u}=\frac{5507.25-18.02}{113.18}=48.50,
$$

or $\bar X=2\bar n=2\times(5507.25-18.02)/226.36=48.50$.

**8.3** From equation 2 at r = 1 (Carothers eq.), $\bar X=48.50=1/(1-p)$. Thus p = 0.97938. (p = 0.98095 when $\bar X=52.50$ was used instead of $\bar X=48.50$.)

**8.4** [HO(CH₂)₄O]₁.₀₀₀–[CO(CH₂)₄CO–O(CH₂)₄O]₅.₀₀₀–H or HO(CH₂)₄O–[CO(CH₂)₄CO–O(CH₂)₄O]₅.₀₀₀–H is accurate. However, HO(CH₂)₄O–[CO(CH₂)₄CO–O(CH₂)₄O]₅–H is acceptable.

**8.5** M(adipic acid) = 146.16 g mol⁻¹; M(Bdiol) = 90.14 g mol⁻¹.

**Answer 1:** Since $\bar X=11.00$, the oligoester contains 5.00 units of adipate and 6.00 units of the Bdiol. When pₐ → 1, the initial molar feed ratio of the monomers is equal to the molar composition of the resulting oligoester.

$$
\frac{[\text{adipic acid}]_0}{[\mathrm{Bdiol}]_0}=\frac{5.00}{6.00},\qquad W=90.14\times\frac{6.00}{5.00}\times\frac{36.54}{146.16}=27.0\,\mathrm g.
$$

**Answer 2:** From eq. 2, when pₐ → 1, $\bar X=(1+r)/(1-r)$. Therefore, the printed slash-form expression is

$11.00 = \{1 + [(36.54 / 146.16) / W / 90.14)]\} / \{1 - [(36.54 / 146.16) / W / 90.14)]\}.$

= [(W / 90.14) + 0.2500] / [(W / 90.14) − 0.2500].

11.00 × [(W / 90.14) − 0.2500] = [(W / 90.14) + 0.2500];

10.00 × [(W / 90.14)] = 3.000;

W = 3.000 × 90.14 / 10.00 = 27.0 g.

Source note: The original unparenthesized slash expression and extra closing bracket in Answer 2 are retained alongside its printed rearrangement.
