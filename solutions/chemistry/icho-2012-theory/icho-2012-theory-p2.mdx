## Условие

**A. Platinum(II) Compounds, Isomers, and the Trans Effect**

Platinum and other Group 10 metals form square planar complexes; their reaction mechanisms have been extensively studied. Substitution proceeds with retention of stereochemistry:

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '46.6%', '--fig-max': '299px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/icho-2012-theory/p2-fig1-v2.png" alt="Complete square planar substitution scheme showing ligand T trans to departing X and incoming Y." width="933" height="240" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>


The rate of replacing X by Y depends on ligand T trans to X (the trans effect). For the following T ligands the trans substitution rate decreases left to right:

CN⁻ > H⁻ > NO₂⁻, I⁻ > Br⁻, Cl⁻ > pyridine, NH₃, OH⁻, H₂O.

Preparations of cis- and trans-Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂ depend on this effect (source writes “trans affect”). The cis isomer, the cancer chemotherapy agent cisplatin, is prepared by reacting K₂PtCl₄ with ammonia.

**2.1** Draw all possible stereoisomers of square planar Pt(py)(NH₃)BrCl, with py = pyridine C₅H₅N.

**2.2** Write aqueous reaction schemes, including any intermediates, preparing each stereoisomer of [Pt(NH₃)(NO₂)Cl₂]⁻ from PtCl₄²⁻, NH₃ and NO₂⁻, controlled kinetically by the trans effect.

**B. Kinetic Studies of Substitution Reactions of Square Planar Complexes**

ML₃X + Y → ML₃Y + X can proceed by either or both mechanisms:

i) Direct substitution: Y attaches to the central metal to form five-coordinate ML₃XY, then rapidly eliminates X. Formation is rate-determining with rate constant kY.

ML₃X —(+Y; rate-determining, kY)→ [ML₃XY] —(−X)→ ML₃Y.

ii) Solvent-assisted substitution: solvent S attaches to form ML₃XS, which eliminates X to give ML₃S; Y rapidly displaces S. Solvent attachment is rate-determining with constant kS.

ML₃X —(+S; rate-determining, kS)→ [ML₃XS] —(−X)→ [ML₃S] —(+Y; −S)→ ML₃Y.

Rate = kS[ML₃X] + kY[Y][ML₃X]. When [Y] ≫ [ML₃X], Rate = kobs[ML₃X]. Values kS/kY depend on reactants/solvent. One example is displacement of Cl⁻ in square planar ML₂X₂ by pyridine; the ML₃X scheme also applies to ML₂X₂.

<figure className="problem-figure problem-figure--sized" style={{'--fig-w': '86.5%', '--fig-max': '554px'}}>
<img src="https://pub-43290baaaff14857b5dd59610ea438c7.r2.dev/problems/icho-2012-theory/p2-fig3.png" alt="Native platinum bipy/pyridine substitution scheme with charge, CH₃OH, structures and chloride." width="1731" height="421" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

|---|---|
| 0.122 | 7.20×10⁻⁴ |
| 0.061 | 3.45×10⁻⁴ |
| 0.030 | 1.75×10⁻⁴ |

- Most forms by direct (kY) substitution.

To better target cisplatin to cancer cells, Professor Lippard’s MIT group attached a platinum(IV) complex to oligonucleotides bound to gold nanoparticles.
