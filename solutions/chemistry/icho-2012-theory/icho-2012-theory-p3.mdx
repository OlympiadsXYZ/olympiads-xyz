## Условие

Thiomolybdate ions derive from molybdate MoO₄²⁻ by replacing oxygen with sulfur. They occur in deep Black Sea water where biological sulfate reduction generates H₂S. Transformation rapidly removes dissolved Mo from seawater into sediments, depleting an essential trace element. Relative concentrations in dilute solution obey:

MoS₄²⁻ + H₂O(l) ⇌ MoOS₃²⁻ + H₂S(aq), K₁ = 1.3×10⁻⁵.

MoOS₃²⁻ + H₂O(l) ⇌ MoO₂S₂²⁻ + H₂S(aq), K₂ = 1.0×10⁻⁵.

MoO₂S₂²⁻ + H₂O(l) ⇌ MoO₃S²⁻ + H₂S(aq), K₃ = 1.6×10⁻⁵.

MoO₃S²⁻ + H₂O(l) ⇌ MoO₄²⁻ + H₂S(aq), K₄ = 6.5×10⁻⁶.

**3.1** At equilibrium [MoO₄²⁻] = 1×10⁻⁷ and [H₂S] = 1×10⁻⁶, find [MoS₄²⁻].

Solutions containing MoO₂S₂²⁻, MoOS₃²⁻ and MoS₄²⁻ absorb at 395/468 nm. Other ions and H₂S absorb negligibly. Molar absorptivities ε:

| Ion | ε468 / dm³ mol⁻¹ cm⁻¹ | ε395 / dm³ mol⁻¹ cm⁻¹ |
|---|---|---|
| MoS₄²⁻ | 11870 | 120 |
| MoOS₃²⁻ | 0 | 9030 |
| MoO₂S₂²⁻ | 0 | 3230 |

**3.2** A nonequilibrium mixture contains only MoS₄²⁻, MoOS₃²⁻, MoO₂S₂²⁻ as Mo species, total 6.0×10⁻⁶ mol dm⁻³. A 10.0 cm cell gives absorbances 0.365 at 468 nm and 0.213 at 395 nm. Calculate all three anion concentrations.

Initial MoS₄²⁻ concentration 2.0×10⁻⁷ mol dm⁻³ hydrolyzes in a closed system; H₂S accumulates to equilibrium. Calculate H₂S and all five Mo anion concentrations, ignoring possible H₂S → HS⁻ ionization. The printed parenthetic list is MoO₄²⁻, MoOS₃²⁻, MoO₂S₂²⁻, MoOS₃²⁻, MoS₄²⁻. Source note: its fourth species repeats MoOS₃²⁻; the preceding equilibria and key print MoO₃S²⁻.

**3.3** Write six independent equations determining the system.

**3.4** Calculate all six concentrations with reasonable approximations, giving two significant figures.

## Решение

<Spoiler title="Покажи решението от архива">

**3.1** Multiply four mass-action laws: [MoO₄²⁻][H₂S]⁴/[MoS₄²⁻] = 10⁻⁷(10⁻⁶)⁴/[MoS₄²⁻] = 1.4×10⁻²⁰. Thus [MoS₄²⁻] = 7×10⁻¹².

**3.2** At 468 nm, 0.365 = 11870×10.0×c(MoS₄²⁻); c = 3.08×10⁻⁶ mol dm⁻³. Mo conservation gives c(MoOS₃²⁻) + c(MoO₂S₂²⁻) = 6.0×10⁻⁶ − 3.08×10⁻⁶ = 2.9×10⁻⁶ mol dm⁻³; hence c(MoO₂S₂²⁻) = 2.9×10⁻⁶ − c(MoOS₃²⁻).

At 395 nm (equation quantities without dimensions):

0.213 = (120×10.0×3.08×10⁻⁶) + (9030×10.0×c(MoOS₃²⁻)) + (3230×10.0×c(MoO₂S₂²⁻)).

Substitute the conservation relation: last term = 3230×10.0×(2.9×10⁻⁶ − c(MoOS₃²⁻)). Thus c(MoOS₃²⁻) = 2.0×10⁻⁶ and c(MoO₂S₂²⁻) = 0.9×10⁻⁶ mol dm⁻³.

**3.3** Mo balance (1):

2.0×10⁻⁷ = c(MoS₄²⁻) + c(MoOS₃²⁻) + c(MoO₂S₂²⁻) + c(MoO₃S²⁻) + c(MoO₄²⁻).

S balance (2):

8.0×10⁻⁷ = 4c(MoS₄²⁻) + 3c(MoOS₃²⁻) + 2c(MoO₂S₂²⁻) + c(MoO₃S²⁻) + (H₂S).

Source notation: the last term is printed (H₂S) without c. Equilibrium relations (3)–(6):

1.3×10⁻⁵ = [MoOS₃²⁻][H₂S]/[MoS₄²⁻]; 1.0×10⁻⁵ = [MoO₂S₂²⁻][H₂S]/[MoOS₃²⁻]; 1.6×10⁻⁵ = [MoO₃S²⁻][H₂S]/[MoO₂S₂²⁻]; 6.5×10⁻⁶ = [MoO₄²⁻][H₂S]/[MoO₃S²⁻].

**3.4** Multiple approaches are possible. Maximum H₂S is 8.0×10⁻⁷ mol dm⁻³ for complete hydrolysis. At this concentration MoO₃S²⁻ is about 12% of MoO₄²⁻ and remaining thio anions much less abundant. Two-significant-figure accuracy permits truncation:

2.0×10⁻⁷ = [MoO₃S²⁻] + [MoO₄²⁻] (Mo balance); 8.0×10⁻⁷ = [MoO₃S²⁻] + [H₂S] (S balance).

Subtract to obtain [MoO₄²⁻] = [H₂S] − 6.0×10⁻⁷; S balance gives [MoO₃S²⁻] = 8.0×10⁻⁷ − [H₂S].

6.5×10⁻⁶ = ([H₂S] − 6.0×10⁻⁷)[H₂S]/(8.0×10⁻⁷ − [H₂S]).

Rearrange and solve quadratically for H₂S, then back-substitute:

| Species | Concentration |
|---|---|
| H₂S | 7.8×10⁻⁷ |
| MoO₄²⁻ | 1.8×10⁻⁷ |
| MoO₃S²⁻ | 2.1×10⁻⁸ |
| MoO₂S₂²⁻ | 1.0×10⁻⁹ |
| MoOS₃²⁻ | 8.1×10⁻¹¹ |
| MoS₄²⁻ | 4.9×10⁻¹² |


</Spoiler>
