I12 - T02 - A

Условие

Calculate the length of the sidereal day on Earth. What would be the length of the solar and sidereal days, in the current time measures (our solar hours, minutes and seconds), if the Earth would rotate in the opposite direction, but with the same rotation speed?

Условие: PDF 31

Печатно решение / форма

The length of solar day is exactly 24 hrs. But sidereal day is a little less than solar day because of the yearly motion of the Sun.

Now, in one solar year the Earth completes 365.25 solar days but it completes one additional rotation around itself. Thus number of sidereal days will be 366.25.

Tsid=TSol×nn+1(13.20)=24h×365.25365.25+1=23h56m4s.(13.21)\begin{aligned} T_{sid}&=T_{Sol}\times\frac n{n+1} \qquad\text{(13.20)}\\ &=24^h\times\frac{365.25}{365.25+1}\\ &=23^h56^m4^s. \qquad\text{(13.21)} \end{aligned}Tsid​​=TSol​×n+1n​(13.20)=24h×365.25+1365.25​=23h56m4s.(13.21)​

Now, if Earth rotates in opposite direction then exactly opposite thing will happen i.e. the Earth will complete one rotation less than number of solar days in a solar year.

On the other hand, the Sidereal day will remain the same as it doesn’t depends on the rotation direction. (neglecting the precession)

TSol′=Tsid×n−1n(13.22)=TSol×nn+1×n−1n=24h×365.25−1365.25+1=23h52m8s.(13.23)\begin{aligned} T'_{Sol}&=T_{sid}\times\frac{n-1}n \qquad\text{(13.22)}\\ &=T_{Sol}\times\frac n{n+1}\times\frac{n-1}n\\ &=24^h\times\frac{365.25-1}{365.25+1}\\ &=23^h52^m8^s. \qquad\text{(13.23)} \end{aligned}TSol′​​=Tsid​×nn−1​(13.22)=TSol​×n+1n​×nn−1​=24h×365.25+1365.25−1​=23h52m8s.(13.23)​
